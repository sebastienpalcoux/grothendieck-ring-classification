\section{Complete classification and its boundary}
All 336 supplied multiplicity-one rings at ranks nine and ten now have
an accepted general exclusion or a unitary model. The counts are 102
exclusions and 40 models at rank nine, and 136 exclusions and 58 models
at rank ten. The last cyclic ring is realized in
Section~\ref{sec:r189-model}; its structural restrictions remain valid.
This classifies Grothendieck rings with at least one model, not all
inequivalent fusion categories. Independent human verification remains
pending, and the enumeration qualification is unchanged.
