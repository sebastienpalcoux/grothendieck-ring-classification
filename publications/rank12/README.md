# Rank twelve, multiplicity one

**632 rings: 480 excluded over C, 152 modeled, none unresolved.** Of the
modeled rings, 151 have unitary realizations; R12_1_623 has a complex and
pseudo-unitary realization with unitarity unproved.

The manuscript gives the classification proof guide and the final unified
conductor-saturation exclusion and cyclic near-group quotient construction.
All earlier family proofs, exact categorical constructor data, tensor maps,
code and independent assistant audits are preserved in the proof archive.
Its historical intermediate ledgers are not current status records.

The [separate census](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/fusion-ring-census/tree/main/verification/rank12)
retains the raw input, completed producer run, matching complete enumeration
source, exact validity and based-isomorphism checks. Full enumeration was not
independently repeated. Independent human verification and Lean formalization
remain pending. “New” constructions have the limited meaning stated in the
[construction catalogue](https://sebastienpalcoux.github.io/grothendieck-ring-classification/constructions.html).

The exact census publication is pinned to [commit `a35155aa`](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/fusion-ring-census/tree/a35155aae3b190112d2679ad14d2097d13c4dda0/verification/rank12).
