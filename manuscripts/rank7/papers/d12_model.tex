\subsection{The unitary \texorpdfstring{$D_{12}$}{D12} model}
\label{sec:d12}
The ring $R7\_2\_240$ is the even part of the $D_{12}$ subfactor,
equivalently the local bosonic modularization of $PSU(2)_{20}$.
Existence and the split-object fusion rules follow from
\cite[Theorem~4.14]{MPSskein} and
\cite[Theorem~2.2 and Proposition~2.4]{MPSknots}.
The resulting category is unitary modular. In the model basis
$f_0,f_2,f_4,f_6,f_8,P,Q$, bulk products are obtained by folding the
$SU(2)_{20}$ rules under $f_{2t}=f_{2(10-t)}$ and setting
$f_{10}=P+Q$. The remaining rules are
\[
\begin{gathered}
P f_{2j}=\sum_{k=5-j}^{4}f_{2k}
 +\begin{cases}P&j\text{ even},\\Q&j\text{ odd},\end{cases}
\qquad Q f_{2j}\text{ interchanges }P,Q,\\
P^2=Q+f_2+f_6,\quad Q^2=P+f_2+f_6,
\quad PQ=f_0+f_4+f_8,\quad P^*=Q.
\end{gathered}
\]
The model-to-census permutation is $(0,5,3,6,4,1,2)$. It matches all
$343$ ordered structure constants; the separate associativity check has
$2401$ equations. The category is supplied by the cited construction,
not inferred from those finite ring checks.
