\subsection{Excluding the conductor-eight quadratic case}
\label{sec:m5-cyclic-application}
\begin{proposition}
\label{prop:m5-5039-cyclic-exclusion}
The based ring $R7\_5\_5039$ has no categorification over $\mathbb C$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The source-bound signed reduction covers all four oriented signed
quotients. The reduction establishes sphericality and a dimension
character in the Frobenius--Perron Galois orbit. After Galois
conjugation, any categorification would therefore give one of the
pseudo-unitary center systems considered below. The incompatible
signed branches are excluded by their quadratic character fields.
The independent domain enumeration reconstructs all four induced-unit
frames and all $142\,510$ possible high restriction rows. Character
reciprocity leaves two eligible frames. One has twist-one
conductor-eight difference-square weight $8$, exceeding its upper
bound $2$. The other has twelve label assignments, comprising two
classes of identical character-to-row assignments. Its second-indicator
vector is uniquely $(1,0,0,1,1,1,1)$.

The sign-safe conductor-eight argument gives $c\in\{0,2\}$ and
exact difference-square masses $c,c,2c,4c$ at two-primary twist
orders $1,2,4,8$. Use the complete $61$-record inventory of
Proposition~\ref{prop:m5-power-phase-inventory} and its source-fixed
packet divisibilities, together with the fourth and eighth rotation
equations. For either label class with $c=2$, an integral row
functional modulo $256$ annihilates every one of the $1700$ columns
but takes value $128$ on the right-hand side. This excludes both
complete $c=2$ systems.

For $c=0$, complete aggregate and inactive-lattice enumeration reduces
$615$ phase allocations to four. The five integral possibilities for
the fourth rotation of source object $1$ reduce to the unique
multiplicity vector $(3,1,0,0)$. The conductor-five allocation has
$1004$ raw possibilities, exactly $50$ of which satisfy the residual
source Gram constraints. Exact rational separators exclude $184$ of
the resulting $200$ combinations in label class~$0$, and $192$ of
$200$ in label class~$1$.

For the remaining $24$ combinations, the $\sqrt{10}$ and
$i\sqrt{10}$ eighth-indicator equations and exact Gram capacities
force one order-$320$ packet of multiplicity $16$ in the designated
restriction orbit (orbit~$50$ in the certificate). There are two
packet choices for each active phase, giving $48$ final branches.
Other phase contributions in that orbit remain allowed. The verifier
reconstructs the complete packet domains and all capacity inequalities
from the original induction Gram matrix; it checks each capacity by
an integer scan and rounds only by the gcd of the packet units.

Every final branch has two exact separating leaves. If $u$ is the
multiplicity of $-i$ in the fourth rotation for source object $5$,
the leaves respectively exclude $u\leq18$ and $u\geq19$.
They cover every integer $u$, giving $96$ exact leaves in total.
These separators use the Gram, first/second moment, modular $STS$,
prime-budget, integral fusion-norm, fourth-rotation and capacity
constraints. Their eighth-rotation coefficients are zero: the
eighth indicators enter earlier through the forced packet domain.
No numerical infeasibility result is used as a certificate.
All possible categorical branches therefore give a contradiction.
\end{proof}

\path{evidence/rank7_m5_20261020/models/sqrt5/}
binds the original tensor, reconstructs every domain and system, and

disabled. The proof uses the integer inequality in
Proposition~\ref{prop:m5-integral-fusion-norm}, rather than treating
failure of a trial necessary-data witness as a categorical exclusion.
