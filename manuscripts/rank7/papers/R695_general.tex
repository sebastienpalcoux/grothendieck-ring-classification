\subsection{A parity contradiction at the compression boundary}
\label{subsec:r695-general}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:r695-general}
The ring $R7\_3\_695$ has no categorification over $\mathbb C$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Its source basis is self-dual and its Casimir polynomial is
\[
 P(x)=(x-7)^3(x-5)^2(x^2-840x+4900).
\]
The irrational roots are $D=420+70\sqrt{35}$ and its conjugate.
Their field $L=\mathbb Q(\sqrt{35})$ has discriminant $140$, and
\[
 Q_L(R)=140\frac{840^2-4\cdot4900}{4900^2}=4.
\]
For a hypothetical categorification, the boundary case of
Corollary~\ref{cor:compression-boundary} therefore says that its
sphericalization has dimension field $K=L$ and all formal codegrees
in the signed sector are rational.

Both lifts of each source simple are self-dual, so the signed quotient
is commutative with self-dual basis. Its seven codegrees are eigenvalues
of its integer Casimir matrix $B_T$. They are thus positive integers.
Let $d=(d_i)$ be its spherical dimension character and
$\delta=\sum_i d_i^2$. The field generated by the $d_i$ is $K=L$,
so $d$ and its quadratic conjugate are distinct. Both have the same
rational integer codegree $\delta$. Formal-codegree divisibility gives
\[
 \frac\delta D=\frac\delta{70}(6-\sqrt{35})\in\mathcal O_L.
\]
Since $\mathcal O_L=\mathbb Z[\sqrt{35}]$, we obtain $70\mid\delta$.

The signed coefficients agree with the source coefficients modulo two.
Hence $B_T\equiv B_N\pmod2$ and
\[
 \det(xI-B_T)\equiv P(x)\equiv x^2(x+1)^5\pmod2.
\]
Exactly two signed codegrees are even, namely the two occurrences of
$\delta$. Write the other five, which are odd, as $h_1,\ldots,h_5$.
Induction integrality gives $\delta/h_i\in\mathbb Z$, and the
reciprocal-codegree identity yields
\[
 \frac2\delta+\sum_{i=1}^5\frac1{h_i}=1,
 \qquad \sum_{i=1}^5\frac\delta{h_i}=\delta-2.
\]
Since $\delta$ is even and every $h_i$ is odd,
$\delta/h_i\equiv\delta\pmod4$. The last equality would therefore
give $5\delta\equiv\delta-2\pmod4$, a contradiction.
\end{proof}

This argument uses the individual dimension-character field throughout.
It does not infer a nontrivial grading from rational spherical global
dimension. Its exact source verifier checks the Casimir polynomial,
compression equality, inverse formula, and both even residue classes
modulo four.
