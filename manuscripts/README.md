# Manuscripts

**24 November 2026: revised ranks 2–5 series.** The four current papers are *Grothendieck rings of complex fusion categories: rank 2*, *rank 3*, *rank 4* and *rank 5*. They have 3, 10, 50 and 72 pages. All four classify based rings without assuming a pivotal structure and prove sphericality. Ranks two and three are computer-free; ranks four and five use exact computations. Rank five also proves Galois pseudo-unitarity and the sharp bounds μ ≤ 4 and FPdim ≤ 48 + 24√3. [Series submission sources and revision letters](hexagon/README.md) · [Rank-four verifier](../supplements/rank4/sphericality/README.md) · [Rank-five reading guide](rank5/README.md) · [Rank-five exact replay](../supplements/rank5/sharp/README.md).

**Current classification: six unresolved cases.** R8_3_1768 is excluded over the complex numbers by pivotal conjugation parity, using the established existence and complete center-domain reductions. The current continuation adds branch exclusions and conditional constructions, including the exact identification of Anyon Atlas B20-02148. No additional categorification is claimed.

## Spherical structures in rank six

[*Spherical structures on fusion categories of rank six*](rank6/sphericality/Sphericality_in_Rank_Six.pdf) proves that every complex fusion category of rank six admits a spherical structure. The theorem holds at every fusion multiplicity.

## Scope

Ranks 2–5 have unrestricted classification claims. Ranks 6–8 use explicit census bounds 9, 5 and 3. Two rank-six and three rank-eight cases remain open. Rank nine through multiplicity two has 2,148 general exclusions, 76 unitary models and one unresolved case. Ranks 10–12 at multiplicity one have no unresolved cases; R12_1_623 has a complex/pseudo-unitary model with unitarity unproved.
