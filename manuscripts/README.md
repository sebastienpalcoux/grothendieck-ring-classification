# Manuscripts

The papers are TeX/PDF pairs. Rank three is an unrestricted sphericality
and ring-classification argument; ranks four to six use the pinned
fixed-rank census and explicit criteria for categorification.

| Manuscript | TeX source | Reading copy | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|

| Rank three | [TeX](rank3.tex) | [PDF](rank3.pdf) | Unrestricted rank-three sphericality and Grothendieck rings |
| Rank four, finite range | [TeX](rank4/papers/finite.tex) | [PDF](rank4/papers/finite.pdf) | Complete general classification through multiplicity 128 |
| Rank four, companion | [TeX](rank4/papers/companion.tex) | [PDF](rank4/papers/companion.pdf) | Criteria, positive models and frontier proofs |
| Rank four, verification guide | [TeX](rank4/papers/verification_guide.tex) | [PDF](rank4/papers/verification_guide.pdf) | Software and certificate checks |
| *Rank-five Grothendieck rings through multiplicity twenty: two general residuals at eighteen* | [TeX](rank5/papers/manuscript.tex) | [PDF](rank5/papers/manuscript.pdf) | Complete bands through 17 and at 19–20; two general residuals at 18 |
| Rank six | [TeX](rank6/papers/manuscript.tex) | [PDF](rank6/papers/manuscript.pdf) | Complete through 2, analyzed through 8 with 47 general residuals |

Rank-four, rank-five and rank-six TeX files use sections and generated tables
from their sibling source trees. [Provenance](../docs/PROVENANCE.md) explains
the separately versioned census and classification ledgers. These manuscripts
are working research releases, with unresolved rings identified explicitly.
