Rank-four through rank-eight TeX files use sections and generated tables
from their sibling source trees. [Provenance](../docs/PROVENANCE.md) explains
the separately versioned census and classification ledgers. These manuscripts
are working research releases, with unresolved rings identified explicitly.


The current rank-six paper retains the completed `R6_3_215` proof and adds
12 general exclusions, including the former first residual `R6_4_6`. Its
new forced-associator, signed-spectrum, Morita and central-induction criteria
leave 10 unresolved rings: 16,548 general exclusions and 54 realizations among
16,612 census tensors. General classification is complete through multiplicity
three and separately at exact multiplicity seven; multiplicities four through
six remain partial. [Revision notes](../supplements/rank6/REVISION_NOTES.md)
distinguish the new 12-record third continuation from the unchanged original
campaign and the previous 7- and 18-record continuations. The current exact
replay is `verification/round3/release_replay.json`; earlier campaigns retain
their recorded verification evidence. The previous v2 paper and archive
represent the superseded 22-residual snapshot.
