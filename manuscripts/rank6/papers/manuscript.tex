\begin{abstract}
We study complex categorifiability of the $\CensusCount$ rank-six fusion
rings of multiplicity at most eight in the \emph{Fusion Ring Census}.
Combining arithmetic and subring obstructions with a unified
central-induction framework, we obtain $\NegativeCount$ general
exclusions, $\PositiveCount$ categorifiable rings, and $\UnresolvedCount$
unresolved cases. Categorifiability is completely classified through
multiplicity two: $43$ of $193$ rings admit spherical models, including
$42$ with unitary models. Our necessary criteria and model identifications
are applied automatically, with exact certificates and a stepwise
exclusion table. For the first unresolved ring, at multiplicity three,
we prove and test a fourth-order product-Gram condition for pseudo-unitary
centers. It survives
this test but admits no braided spherical categorification, by the
Dong--Schopieray catalogue; its general categorifiability remains open.
\end{abstract}
