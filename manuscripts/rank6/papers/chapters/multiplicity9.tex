\subsection{The exact-multiplicity-nine extension}
\label{sec:multiplicity-nine}
The extension contains $8\,809$ new rank-six based rings whose largest
structure coefficient is exactly nine: $8\,808$ commutative rings and one
noncommutative ring. The source-bound campaign certifies general
nonexistence for $8\,798$ of them and supplies no new positive model.
All accepted general certificates were independently replayed in optimized
Python with numerical optimizers disabled. Eleven general existence
questions remain open. The actual ordered campaign has seventeen stages,
including the additional arithmetic/Morita stage; the detailed stage
counts and all deadlines are retained in the verification manifests.

The additional arithmetic stage removes nine rings by the signed
trace-ideal obstruction, one by the biquadratic trace obstruction, and
$\RR{9}{530}$ by Theorem~\ref{thm:full-morita-gram}. These eleven
certificates belong to the main campaign only. A separate ten-stage
advanced campaign covers precisely its eleven final residuals, with
$110$ recorded attempts and no further general exclusion. A timeout
records an unfinished search and has no mathematical force.

For the second row, exact pseudo-unitary obstructions were extended to
complete spherical dimension-character proof trees. Five use global
codegree moment or dimension-field obstructions; the sixth,
$\RR{9}{8200}$, belongs to the quadratic Gauss family with parameter
eight. Its all-orders Gauss argument requires at least $3200$ root
contributions where only $770$ are available. The optimized exact replay
checks every spherical character branch and the underlying
pseudo-unitary witness.

The upgrade from spherical to pivotal nonexistence uses the following
simple lemma, with a tensor-checked hypothesis. In a pivotal category,
\[
 \dim_{\mathrm{left}}(X)=\dim_{\mathrm{right}}(X^*).
\]
Thus a pivotal fusion category in which every simple object is self-dual
is spherical: the two dimensions agree on simples, and semisimplicity
extends equality to the two traces of every endomorphism. This is the
usual duality identity for pivotal traces \cite[Section~4.7]{EGNO}.
All six tensors have identity dual map. The released pivotal obstruction
wrapper checks this map and replays the entire spherical certificate.
It makes no assumption that an arbitrary fusion categorification admits
a pivotal structure.

\paragraph{A separate braided obstruction for $\RR{9}{3748}$.}
This ring has a dual-closed rank-three subring on labels $\{0,1,5\}$,
with basis $1,a,b$ and rules
\[
 a^2=1+b,\qquad ab=a+b,\qquad b^2=1+a+b.
\]
It has no nontrivial proper fusion subring. The Frobenius--Perron
dimension of $a$ satisfies $t^3-t^2-2t+1=0$, which has no rational root;
in particular this subring is not integral. Suppose the ambient rank-six category were
braided, and let $\mathcal A$ denote this fusion subcategory. Its M\"uger
center is either trivial or all of $\mathcal A$. The latter possibility
would make $\mathcal A$ symmetric, contradicting integrality of symmetric
fusion categories in characteristic zero. Hence $\mathcal A$ is
nondegenerate. The decomposition theorem for a nondegenerate braided
subcategory then forces a rank-two fusion centralizer in the ambient
category. No such based subring exists: for labels $j=1,\ldots,5$, the
supports of $j^2$ are respectively
\[
 \{0,5\},\quad \{0,2,3,4\},\quad \{0,2,3,4,5\},\quad
 \{0,1,2,3,4,5\},\quad \{0,1,5\};
\]
none is contained in $\{0,j\}$. This proves that
$\RR{9}{3748}$ has no braided categorification, without any pivotal
assumption. The invoked results are \cite[Corollary~2.46 and Theorem~3.13]{DGNO}.
Braided nonexistence is recorded separately from the five existence
columns above.

\paragraph{Coverage and remaining limitations.}
The audit distinguishes verified exclusions, failed mathematical
hypotheses, bounded searches, and missing prerequisite data. None of the
eleven residuals contains a Fibonacci, Ising, half-$E_6$, or cyclic
order-three subring, so the corresponding historical projection
criteria do not apply. The field of $\RR{9}{3748}$ has degree six,
outside the inherited rational, quadratic, and degree-at-most-four
center implementations. More elaborate full-center FS, balancing, and
Vafa constraints require complete fusion, twist, or induction data that
have not been constructed; their absence is not a passed test.

No registered positive construction matches a new tensor: the sixty-four
inherited model records all have largest coefficient at most seven, an
invariant of based-ring isomorphism. Conditional relative-center
calculations and bounded model searches are retained as research notes,
with their hypotheses and search ranges; they establish no further
existence decision. The release establishes the conclusions of
its implemented, exactly checked criteria within the recorded limits,
not an exhaustion of every possible categorical obstruction.
