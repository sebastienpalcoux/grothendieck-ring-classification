\subsection{A spherical reduction for the two $\sqrt{10}$ cases}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:hi6-pair-galois-pu}
Every complex categorification of $R8\_2\_630$ or $R8\_2\_671$
admits a spherical structure and has a pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Both tensors are self-dual. Choose the positive-second-indicator
lifts in the canonical sphericalization. These lifts are self-dual,
because their duals have the same nonzero spherical dimensions,
whereas the other lifts have the opposite dimensions. Their signed
quotient tensor $T$ is fully symmetric and associative, with
$|T_{ij}^k|\leq N_{ij}^k$ and $T_{ij}^k\equiv N_{ij}^k\pmod2$.
The odd-channel parity theorem \cite[Theorem~3.9]{TPEParity} further gives
$N_{jj}^i-T_{jj}^i\equiv0\pmod4$.

Thus repeated coefficients one are positive, repeated coefficients
two have signs $\pm2$, and all-distinct coefficients one have signs
$\pm1$. There are $27$ Boolean signs for each tensor. Only
$R8\_2\_671$ has an all-distinct coefficient two, with possible values
$-2,0,2$. The source-bound exact checker regenerates every ordered
associativity equation. For $R8\_2\_630$, exhaustive branching on the
$157$ distinct equations visits $195$ nodes and leaves only $T=N$.
For the three values of the extra coefficient of $R8\_2\_671$, it
visits $57,5,103$ nodes; the only solution is again $T=N$, in the
last branch. Every rejection evaluates an integer associativity
equation exactly. Consequently the chosen actual lifts are tensor
closed, and restriction of the forgetful functor identifies their
spherical fusion category with the original category.

The same checker verifies the complete tables of eight distinct
characters over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3,\sqrt{10})$. The formal codegrees are
\[
120\pm36\sqrt{10},\quad 4,6,6,12,12,12.
\]
All six characters outside the FP conjugate pair vanish on a simple
basis object. Since spherical dimensions of simple objects are
nonzero, the spherical dimension character belongs to that pair.
A Galois automorphism therefore takes it to the positive FP
character and produces a pseudo-unitary category.
\end{proof}
