\section{The open case \texorpdfstring{$R8\_2\_24$}{R8\_2\_24}}
\label{sec:r24-open}
The ring $R8\_2\_24$ has no unitary categorification by
Proposition~\ref{prop:scoped-eight}, but its general complex
categorifiability remains open. The following necessary conditions
reduce the question to a specific tensor-autoequivalence problem.

\begin{lemma}\label{lem:2d2-neutral-indicators}
Let $\mathcal B$ be pseudo-unitary with simple objects $1,g,r,gr$
and rules
\[
 g^2=1,\qquad gr=rg,\qquad r^2=1+2r+2gr.
\]
Then $\nu_2(g)=-1$, $\nu_2(r)=\nu_2(gr)=1$, and the cubic
rotations on $\operatorname{Hom}(1,r^3)$ and
$\operatorname{Hom}(1,(gr)^3)$ are identities.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $d=2+\sqrt5$ and $D=4+8d=20+8\sqrt5$.
The formal codegrees are $D,D',4,4$, and
$1/D-1/D'=-1/\sqrt5$. The proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:q17-conductor-equality}, with the
primitive character modulo five in place of that modulo seventeen,
gives the same equality conclusion at conductor five: every central simple with nonrational dimension
ratio $z/D$ has twist of order dividing five.

We use the signed version of the saturated indicator identity.
Let $\ell(z)$ be a rational coordinate of the nontrivial quadratic
part of $z/D$, scaled so that its values on $(1,g,r,gr)$ are
$(2,2,-1,-1)$. For a cyclotomic automorphism $\sigma$ with residue
$k$ modulo five, write $\pi$ and $\varepsilon_A$ for its modular
permutation and signs. The Chinese remainder theorem lifts each chosen
unit residue to an exponent coprime to the full cyclotomic conductor.
The dimension transformation gives
\[
 z_{\pi A}=\varepsilon_A\frac D{\sigma D}\sigma(z_A),
 \qquad \ell(z_{\pi A})=\varepsilon_A\chi_5(k)\ell(z_A).
\]
The first induction identity, weighted by $\ell$, is
\[
 \sum_A\ell(z_A)z_A\theta_A=D\ell(1).
\]
The signs in the two preceding transformations cancel, giving
\[
 \sigma\bigl(\ell(z_A)z_A\bigr)
 =\chi_5(k)\frac{\sigma D}{D}\ell(z_{\pi A})z_{\pi A}.
\]
Apply $\sigma$ to the weighted identity and reindex by $\pi$.
If $h$ is the inverse of $k$ modulo five, its nonzero terms obey
$\sigma(\theta_A)=\theta_{\pi A}^{h}$, since their twists divide
five and $\theta_{\pi A}=\sigma^2(\theta_A)$. The terms belonging
to $I(1)$ have twist one and satisfy the same equation.
Cancel $\sigma D$ and use the induction formula for the $h$th
indicator. Inversion permutes the units modulo five and
$\chi_5(h)=\chi_5(k)$, proving
\[
 \sum_i\ell(\dim X_i)\nu_k(X_i)=\chi_5(k)\ell(1)
 \qquad (k,5)=1.
\]
At $k=2$ this reads
$2+2\nu_2(g)-\nu_2(r)-\nu_2(gr)=-2$.
All three unknowns are signs, forcing the stated values.
At $k=3$ it reads $\nu_3(r)+\nu_3(gr)=4$.
Each rotation acts on a two-dimensional space and has order
three. Equality in the trace bound forces both rotations to be
identities.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Necessary inner-square condition]\label{prop:r24-reduction}
A complex categorification of $R8\_2\_24$ has a pseudo-unitary Galois
conjugate with a faithful $C_2$ grading. Its neutral subcategory
$\mathcal B$ has the fusion rules and indicators of
Lemma~\ref{lem:2d2-neutral-indicators}. There is a tensor autoequivalence
$T$ of $\mathcal B$ fixing every simple label and satisfying
\[
 T^2\simeq_{\otimes}\operatorname{Ad}(g).
\]
The pointed subcategory generated by $g$ has nontrivial associator class
in $H^3(C_2,\C^\times)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The source has exactly two FP dimensions, $1$ and $2+\sqrt5$.
Apply \cite[Lemma~3.1]{SchNear} to the whole category to obtain a
pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate. The source tensor has neutral labels
$\{0,5,6,7\}$, with $r=X_5$, $g=X_7$, and the displayed rank-four
fusion rules. The odd invertible $a=X_1$ has $a^2=g$.
Commutativity of the source ring makes
$T=\operatorname{Ad}(a)|_{\mathcal B}$ fix every neutral simple label.
The coherent composition of inner tensor functors gives
$T^2\simeq_{\otimes}\operatorname{Ad}(a^2)=\operatorname{Ad}(g)$.
The neutral category inherits pseudo-unitarity, so
Lemma~\ref{lem:2d2-neutral-indicators} applies. In the canonical spherical
structure on a pointed $C_2$ category, $\nu_2(g)=-1$ is precisely the
nontrivial associator class \cite{NgIndicators}.
\end{proof}

This is a necessary condition, not an exclusion. In particular,
the nontrivial pointed class on $\langle g\rangle$ is compatible with
restriction of an odd associator class from $C_4=\langle a\rangle$.
Pseudo-unitarity alone does not identify $\mathcal B$ with the known
unitary $2D2$ category.

\subsection{The categorical transport question}
\label{sec:r24-transport}
A possible local approach uses the composition-paired spaces
\[
 H^+=\operatorname{Hom}(r^2,r),\quad
 H^-=\operatorname{Hom}(r,r^2),\qquad
 J^+=\operatorname{Hom}(r^2,gr),\quad
 J^-=\operatorname{Hom}(gr,r^2).
\]
Each has dimension two. For any tensor functor, the two induced
maps on opposite Hom spaces preserve composition. Consequently their
matrices obey $K^-=(K^+)^{-\mathsf t}$. This inverse-transpose identity
is intrinsic; identifying a numerical partial-bend formula with these
maps requires a common choice of oriented charts.

In a proposed paired right-bend frame, write
$E=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ and
$\bar i=1-i$. If the decorated outgoing cubic rotation is
$\Omega=\operatorname{diag}(\omega_0,\omega_1)$, the oriented left bends
are
\[
 L^-=E\Omega,\qquad L^+=\Omega^{-1}E.
\]
Thus the incoming bend on the composition-dual vector with index $i$
has coefficient $\omega_{\bar i}^{-1}$. The two decorated phases cannot
be cancelled by identifying that coefficient with
$\omega_i^{-1}$. No equality of the two phases is available from these
matrix identities alone.

A route avoiding decorated left bends retains all eight even slots in
one tetrahedral block:
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}
 t&0&0&a_U\\0&t&t&0\\0&t&t&0\\a_L&0&0&t
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 D_0=\begin{pmatrix}
 x&0&0&Z\\0&X&y&0\\0&Y&W&0\\z&0&0&w
 \end{pmatrix}.
\]
Here $W,w$ are independent. For clarity, let
$F_0=F^{r,r,r}_r$ and $F_1=F^{r,r,r}_{gr}$, and let $A,B$ be the
$p=r,gr$ blocks of $F_0$ over $q=r$ and $C,D_0$ the corresponding
blocks of $F_1$, in the convention of \cite[pp.~528--530]{EIP}.
A superscript $\vee$ denotes the opposite-tree coefficient of the
inverse associator, not complex conjugation. For $v=(i,j,k,l)$ put
\[
\begin{split}
 p(v)&=(k,\bar j,i,\bar l),\\
 c(v)&=(j,\bar k,\bar i,l),\qquad
 c^2(v)=(\bar k,i,\bar j,l),\qquad b_l=-i(-1)^l.
\end{split}
\]
The desired common-frame identities are
\[
 A^\vee_v=A_{p(v)},\qquad (D_0)^\vee_v=(D_0)_{p(v)},
\]
and, crucially, the four oriented transports
\begin{equation}\label{eq:r24-transport-goal}
\begin{aligned}
 B_v&=b_l(D_0)_{c(v)},&
 B^\vee_v&=b_l^{-1}(D_0)^\vee_{c(v)},\\
 C_v&=b_l(D_0)_{c^2(v)},&
 C^\vee_v&=b_l^{-1}(D_0)^\vee_{c^2(v)}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:r24-transport-goal} is an \emph{unproved transport
requirement}, not a lemma applied in the classification. In particular,
the opposite $B,C$ transports cannot be inferred by applying $p$ to
the forward equations: the slot maps $p$ and $c$ do not commute.

The unresolved task is to derive all four transports in a single
oriented pivotal frame, including the evaluation of $g$, the chosen
$g$--$r$ crossing and the mixed-channel charts. Reversing a $g$ cap
can contribute a sign because $\nu_2(g)=-1$. The unitary numerical
formulas of \cite{EIP} therefore do not establish these identities
for an arbitrary pseudo-unitary category. The general tetrahedral
action of \cite[Lemma~3.13(ii), Proposition~3.22 and Corollary~4.9]{FGPartialDual}
retains partial-dual maps on its tensor factors; those maps need not
preserve chosen bases. It does not by itself supply the required
numerical transport table.

The finite algebra explains the relevance of this question. If the
paired coefficient identities and \eqref{eq:r24-transport-goal} hold,
three column contractions of $F$ with its inverse give, with
$S=xX+Ww$ and $d=2+\sqrt5$,
\[
 1=2t^2+S+1/d,\qquad
 1=S+2zZ,\qquad 1=S+2yY.
\]
The unit term is obtained from normalized cup and cap contractions.
If the annular-corner parameter has the compatible value
$t=(2-\sqrt5+\tau)/4$, $\tau=\pm1$, subtraction gives
\[
 yY=zZ=t^2+\frac1{2d}
 =\begin{cases}(\sqrt5-1)/8,&\tau=1,\\
 (3\sqrt5-5)/8,&\tau=-1.
 \end{cases}
\]
Both values are nonzero. In a compatible diagonal gauge the weights
of $y,z$ are $u^{-2}v^2,u^{-2}v^{-2}$; their invariance would force
$u^4=1$, preventing the required projective square on $H^+$.
This is a conditional coefficient calculation. Its exact replay in
\texttt{verify\_eight\_slot\_algebra.py} verifies the slot algebra
and the two nonzero field values, but proves none of the four
categorical identities in \eqref{eq:r24-transport-goal}. It contributes
no exclusion of $R8\_2\_24$.
