## An additional exact necessary test

Let B be a hypothetical positive ribbon categorification of the retained rank-26 datum. For simple objects a,b, the braid group B3 acts on Hom(b,a^3). The eigenvalues of the first generator are obtained by concatenating the eigenvalues of the self-braidings on Hom(c,a^2), each with multiplicity N_ca^b. These self-braiding multiplicities are fixed by the modular data and second generalized indicators (Ng–Rowell–Wen, arXiv:2408.02748, Theorem 3.1).

Put z=(sigma1 sigma2)^3. The ribbon axiom gives its scalar action theta_b/theta_a^3. On an irreducible B3 constituent whose generator eigenvalues are lambda_1,...,lambda_r, Tuba–Wenzl gives:

- r=1: z=lambda_1^6;
- r=2: z=-(lambda_1 lambda_2)^3;
- r=3: z=(lambda_1 lambda_2 lambda_3)^2;
- r=4: z^2=(lambda_1 lambda_2 lambda_3 lambda_4)^3.

The last equation is only a necessary central-character condition. It does not include Tuba–Wenzl's nonvanishing irreducibility conditions. Every finite-dimensional B3 representation has a Jordan–Hölder series, so for total dimension at most four its generator spectrum must partition into multisets satisfying these conditions with the same central scalar. This argument does not presume complete reducibility or unitarity.

`verify_braid3.py` performs this finite partition test using integer exponents modulo 48 and the previously audited exact second-indicator multiplicities. **All 70 nonzero blocks with dimension at most four pass.** The script hashes its three inputs, checks the fusion and braiding multiplicity counts, and writes `braid3_small.json`. A passing partition is not a B3 representation or a categorification witness.
