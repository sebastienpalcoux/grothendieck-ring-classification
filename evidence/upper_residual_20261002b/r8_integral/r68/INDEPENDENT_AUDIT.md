# Independent mathematical audit of R8_2_68

Verdict: **approved as a general complex-fusion-category exclusion**. Both adjoint branches are covered. No pivotal, spherical, pseudo-unitary, braided, or unitary assumption enters the proof.

The exact source tensor and its dimension108 character were independently replayed with `verify_r68.py` in normal Python and with `python3 -O`. Both receipts are identical and pass. This executable verifies the source hypotheses and finite arithmetic; the categorical argument was reviewed separately below.

1. Solvability and the final prime equivariantization descend the original type `1^4,4^2,6^2` to dimension54 type `1^2,2^4,3^4`. The unique order-two invertible in the source fixes every noninvertible, so the outer action has two free pairs in each noninvertible dimension. All outer-stable possible adjoint dimensions are1,2,9,18,27,54. Pointed1/2 cannot support a dimension3 square. The strong Frobenius property excludes dimension2 objects in adjoint dimensions9/27. Thus both and only the18 and54 branches remain.

2. In the54 branch, the last cyclic step is another C2 equivariantization. Its integral dimension27 category has only dimensions1 and3, and equivariantization counting forces type `1^9,3^2`. The central sign lift is unique because the dimension54 category has trivial universal grading.

3. In the18 branch, the neutral category has type `1^2,2^4` and trivial universal grading. The possible order-two grading would have a solvable dimension9 neutral subcategory with dimension2 simples, prohibited by the strong Frobenius property. The remaining grading orders fail the component inventory. Consequently this category is `(Vec_A^omega)^C2`, with `A=C9` or `C3^2` and inversion action. Its central invertible group is exactlyC2: forgetting a central lift has kernel the tensor automorphisms of the identity, which are trivial here.

4. The faithful C3 grading of the dimension54 category allows Gelaki–Naidu–Nikshych Theorems3.3/3.5 to act braidedly on the neutral center. Its unique nontrivial central invertible is invariant. The obstruction to its equivariant structure lies in `H^2(C3,C*)=0`. The six central lifts form an abelian groupC6, whose unique order-two element restricts to the electric boson. Self-braiding is still+1 because the object is in the neutral part of the crossed center. This gives a central Tannakian C2 in the whole category, without assuming the dimension18 category is an ordinary group representation category. De-equivariantization gives an integral dimension27 category of type `1^9,3^2`; a sign-fixed dimension3 object would otherwise descend to rational dimension3/2.

5. In both branches the central order-two subgroup is unique and is preserved by the outer tensor action. 6. The order-nine invertible group fixes both dimension3 simples. Their adjoint squares therefore contain all nine invertibles once, so the dimension27 category is faithfully C3-graded over `Vec_A^omega`, with component ranks9,1,1. Rank-one module categories force omega to be a coboundary. The C4 generator must exchange the nonneutral components; otherwise equivariantization retains a nontrivial C3 grading, contradicting the original full adjoint subcategory.

7. For C9 no automorphism squares to inversion. For `C3^2`, the square-root condition gives determinant1. The induced action on `H^2(C3^2,C*)=C3` is trivial. This is the final contradiction.
