# A unitary categorification of R8_3_982 from the E21 exceptional category

**The fusion ring R8_3_982 is the triality-zero component of the unitary
E21 fusion category associated with the conformal embedding
SU(3)_21 into E7_1.** In the alternative Coxeter-number notation this
graph/category is denoted E^(24). This supplies an actual known
construction for the formerly unresolved ring; the identification of
its eight-object component with the census ring is the new result here.

## Categorical existence and identification of the graph

Böckenhauer–Evans, *Modular invariants, graphs and alpha-induction for
nets of subfactors III*, Commun. Math. Phys. **205** (1999), 183–228,
[arXiv:hep-th/9812110v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9812110), Section 6.4,
the E^(24) example on printed pages 51–54, constructs the two chiral
systems for SU(3)_21 into E7_1. These are systems of finite-index
endomorphisms of the extension factor, hence actual unitary fusion
categories. The source lists their 24 simple sectors on page 52,
establishes that the alpha-induction homomorphisms are surjective, and
gives their fundamental fusion graph in Figure 13 on page 53.
Thus we use a fusion graph of a genuine chiral category, rather than
inferring monoidal existence from a nimrep or an integral graph algebra.

For the relationship between the graph descriptions, Evans–Pugh,
*SU(3)-Goodman–de la Harpe–Jones subfactors and the realisation of SU(3)
modular invariants*, [arXiv:0906.4252v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0906.4252),
pages 4–5 and 26, explicitly explains that these conformal inclusions
are local/type I, so the chiral graphs agree with their nimreps; the
SU(3)_21 example is E^(24).

We use the convenient labeled diagram in Coquereaux–Isasi–Schieber,
*Notes on TQFT Wire Models and Coherence Equations for SU(3) Triangular
Cells*, SIGMA **6** (2010), 099,
[doi:10.3842/SIGMA.2010.099](https://doi.org/10.3842/SIGMA.2010.099),
[arXiv:1007.0721v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.0721), Section 4.6,
Figure 43, printed page 37. The same source gives exact dimensions and
cells. Coquereaux–Hammaoui–Schieber–Tahri, *Comments about quantum
symmetries of SU(3) graphs*, J. Geom. Phys. **57** (2006), 269–292,
[arXiv:math-ph/0508002v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0508002),
Section 6.3.5, pages 25–26, separately records the 24-vertex E21 graph's
self-fusion and its triality grading.

## Reconstructing all fusion coefficients from the source graph

Let A be the row adjacency matrix, oriented from triality t to t+1
modulo three. It is the fundamental fusion matrix. Its transpose is
the matrix of the dual fundamental. We order the triality-zero vertices
as

    1_1, 3_1, 6_1, 6_2, 7_1, 7_2, 3_2, 1_2.

The tensor unit is 1_1, index zero. The other two triality blocks and
all 60 directed edges are recorded in `e21_graph.json`. They were
extracted from the source PDF's vector paths, with vertex positions
and source SHA-256 recorded. The largest distance between an edge
endpoint and its assigned circle center is below 0.281 PDF points;
each endpoint has a unique circle within one point. The read-only
`extract_e21_graph.py` independently checks all positions, colors, and
edges against the original PDF. The labels themselves are transcribed
from Figure 43.

For all monomials A^a(A^t)^b of total degree at most d, the dimensions
of the span of their unit rows, for d=0,...,6, are

    1, 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 24.

Choose 24 independent monomials H_j, and put their unit rows into a
matrix B. The source graph is normal, so the algebra generated by A
and A^t is commutative. Evaluation on the unit row is injective in a
commutative algebra with a cyclic unit row: an element killing that
row kills all its translates, and hence the entire space. It is also
surjective by the displayed rank calculation. Consequently the
simple fusion matrices are forced uniquely by

    F_i = sum_j (B^(-1))[i,j] H_j.

In the actual source fusion category, each H_j is the multiplication
matrix of a polynomial in the fundamental object and its dual.
Its unit row is that object's coefficient vector. Thus the displayed
linear combinations are exactly the multiplication matrices of the
source's simple objects. This identifies the actual fusion rules, not
just a possible compatible based ring.

The exact computation verifies every entry of the full 24-object
tensor, all nonnegativity and integrality, unit and duality, all
331,776 entries of the associativity matrix identities, and the
triality support of every product. The latter establishes a faithful
Z/3Z grading at the level of the actual category. In particular the
eight triality-zero objects form a unitary fusion subcategory C.

## The previously necessary algebra is now realized

Let V be the fundamental simple object, the vertex 2^1_1 in triality
one. The exact source product is

    V tensor V* = 1_1 + 3_1 = X0 + X5.

The canonical internal-end algebra on V tensor V* is a connected
separable unitary algebra object, and its underlying object lies in C.
Consequently the previously only necessary candidate algebra 1+X5
really exists in this realization. Its dimension is

    (1+(sqrt(2)+sqrt(6))/2)^2 = 3+sqrt(2)+sqrt(3)+sqrt(6).

Both previously necessary nonregular based module types are also
realized. Restricting the neutral objects' fusion matrices to the eight
vertices of triality one gives class 2 in the complete prior module
classification; triality two gives class 1. The certificate checks
all 512 action coefficients in each case, with local-source-to-prior
vertex permutations `[0,2,7,4,5,6,3,1]` and
`[0,3,6,5,4,7,2,1]`, respectively. Together with the neutral regular
component these realize all three *based* module types. This does not
assert uniqueness of a module category with any one such based type.

The homogeneous components of a faithful fusion-category grading are
invertible bimodule categories over the neutral component. Here they
give an order-three subgroup of the Brauer–Picard group of this
realization: their multiplication is the Z/3Z grading, and neither
nontrivial component can represent the identity because its left
based module is nonregular. This is an actual source of Morita
autoequivalences with the same underlying fusion ring.

The general Morita restrictions proved in `MORITA_OBJECT.md` and
`MORITA_RING.md` remain valid and are now nonvacuous: every Morita
partner of every categorification of this ring has exactly the same
based ring R8_3_982, with no rank or multiplicity cutoff. This does
not classify associators, module algebra structures, or the entire
Brauer–Picard group. In particular it does not identify the other
quartic census rings R8_3_928 or R8_3_941 as Morita partners.
