# Independent check of the proposed R1660 spin-branch obstruction

Date: 27 October 2026.

**Verdict: the proposed exclusion of the entire (44,2) branch is NOT
established.** The corrected argument does exclude that branch in low
frame 0, but it does not exclude it throughout frame 1 and does not
exclude R6_8_1660.

I reread the exact phase-mass and graded-Gram inputs in the rank-six
manuscript and reproduced the character-table transition averages.

## Valid short deduction

In frame 0, the low asymmetric columns exhaust the asymmetric norm, so
all high columns have b=c. At phase12 the exact moments are
sum q²=sum qa=sum a²=4, forcing q=a individually. Since q=b+c is even,
there is exactly one column, with q=a=2 and b=c=1. It is selfdual because
duality preserves the restriction column and phase and there is no other
simple there. The negative charge-conjugation space at phase12 is zero.
The irreducible (44,2) requires a one-dimensional negative space at that
phase, so it cannot occur in frame0.

This elementary deduction does not need a Verlinde calculation.

## Why the stronger argument failed

The total Gram has H_XX=142, H_X,gX=138. Consequently the sum over all
central columns of (b-c)² is 142+142-2*138=8. In frame1 all low columns
are symmetric, so the high-column budget is **8**, not4. The manuscript's
quantity Delta=4 counts one member per even h-pair, not every column.
Four q=a=1 columns at phase12 consume only half of this total budget.
It is therefore invalid to infer b=c at phase36 merely from that phase12
configuration.

The missing inference is essential. The exact S-transition probabilities
from the one-dimensional phase9 line of (44,2) are 1/4 at each of phases
12,15,36,39, and zero at9,33. The relevant Galois-unit eigenvalues are
-1 on the spin phases, +1 on homogeneous q=a columns, and -1 on q=2a
columns. If phase36 is forced to the last kind, the diagonal would indeed
be -1/2 and impossible in the normalized dual-difference basis. But that
forcing is not proved. If its contributing line has q=a instead, the
same exact expectation is **0**, which is integral. I independently
reproduced both the transition probabilities and the latter value.

In particular, even-charge h-partners of asymmetric phase12 columns lie
at phase36 and use the other half of the asymmetry budget; this possibility
must be analyzed rather than silently removed. This observation alone is
not an assertion that a full modular completion exists.

## Other negative representations

The original two-branch statement classified only negative irreducibles
meeting the four spin phases. There are additional admissible negative
irreducibles with phase12 or phase36 support and no spin phases, including
(2,2), (0,2), and (6,2). Thus one must also avoid treating the negative
space at those nonspin phases as automatically one-dimensional merely
because (44,2) occurs once.

No status change for the whole ring follows from this audit. Any repaired
full-branch argument must control these remaining possibilities explicitly.
