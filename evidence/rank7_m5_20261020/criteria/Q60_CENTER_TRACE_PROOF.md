# A saturated quadratic two-moment obstruction at conductor 60

This extends the repository's conductor-12 and conductor-24 argument in
`manuscripts/rank7/papers/quadratic_center_trace.tex` to the common
conductor-60 family R7_5_443 and R7_5_1214.

The exact certificate here excludes **pseudo-unitary** categorifications.
The independent reduction in `../sqrt15_reduction/` closes the general
complex scope: each source has only its original canonical signed
quotient, and every spherical dimension character outside the FP Galois
orbit vanishes on a simple object. Hence every hypothetical categorification
is Galois conjugate to a pseudo-unitary one. That reduction is essential
to the final general conclusion and is not assumed by this verifier.

## Shared quadratic and character data

Put s=sqrt(15), eta=4+s. The dimension vectors are

* R443: `(1,1,4+s,5+s,5+s,4+s,6+2s)`;
* R1214: `(1,1,4+s,4+s,5+s,5+s,6+2s)`.

Their global dimension is `D=60 eta=240+60s`, with `eta*bar(eta)=1`.
Both have quadratic Casimir factor `x²-480x+3600`, giving `Q_60=1`.
The other formal codegrees are respectively `(12,5,4,6,6)` and
`(5,12,12,4,4)`. The simple source characters are all one-dimensional.

The separating matrix `sum_i w_i N_i`, with
`w=(1,3,11,37,101,307,997)`, has characteristic polynomial

```
R443:  (x-683)(x-422)(x-230)(x-184)(x+298)
       (x²-15896x-26866796),
R1214: (x-953)(x-24)(x+28)
       (x²-16436x-22501976)(x²+712x-572).
```

These factors have distinct roots. The first quadratic discriminant is
`4900²*15`; the final R1214 quadratic discriminant is `412²*3`.
Thus an automorphism sigma exists that flips s and fixes every other
source character: the only other nonrational character field is Q(sqrt(3)),
disjoint from Q(sqrt(15)).

Write `d_i=a_i+b_i s`, `a(v)=sum_i a_i v_i`, and similarly for b(v).
The full forgetful-row transport formula, obtained from modular Galois
symmetry and character orthogonality as in the earlier manuscript, becomes

`T(v) = v + (b_i)_i (a(v)-4 b(v))`.

Every actual restriction row has partner T(v) or -T(v), with a single
sign making its coordinates nonnegative. Mixed signs rule out that row.
The transform is an integral involution. In particular the Galois image
of the central unit has forced restriction row `(1,0,1,1,1,1,2)` and
dimension `eta²=31+8s`.

## Compression equality and the required trace refinement

Let J be that Galois image of the central unit and put `w=e_1-e_J`.
On the known induced-unit coordinates,

`P_(I(1)) S w = (D^-1-bar(D)^-1) w = -w/sqrt(60)`.

This exhausts the full compression bound. Its equality refinement places
the cyclic modular subrepresentation generated by w at level 60 and
selects negative primitive quadratic-character twist modes. Therefore,
for a central simple of dimension z,

`z/D not in Q  =>  theta^60=1`,

and a primitive-60 twist `theta=exp(2*pi*i*k/60)` has `chi_60(k)=-1`.
This is the same local equality and primitive-mode argument already proved
in the earlier manuscript; no assertion is made about the twist order of
the rational normalized-dimension rows.

One refinement is needed at conductor 60: units modulo 60 do not all
square to one, so paired individual twists need not agree. Instead take
normalized relative trace `t=t_(Q(sqrt(15)))`. Modular Galois symmetry
sends a twist theta to `theta^(u²)`. Because every square automorphism
fixes the quadratic field, **t(theta^(u²))=t(theta)**, and the same holds
for the second moment. Thus paired restriction-row types have equal
multiplicities at each pair of relative-trace values. This is exactly
the equality needed by the moment columns; equality of individual twists
is unnecessary.

## Universal two-moment hull

Write `(a,b,c,d)` for `(a+b*s,c+d*s)`. For every root of unity, the pair
`(t(theta),t(theta²))` lies in the convex hull of these nine points:

```
( 1,0, 1,0), (-1,0, 1,0),
( 1/2,0,-1/2,0), (-1/2,0,-1/2,0), (0,0,-1,0),
(0, 1/8,-1/8,0), (0,-1/8,-1/8,0),
(0,0,0, 1/8), (0,0,0,-1/8).
```

For the nonrational normalized-dimension rows, the first five points and
`(0,-1/8,-1/8,0)` suffice by the saturated twist constraint.

Here is the uniform justification, including all possible root orders.
If the order n is not divisible by 60, the first relative trace is the
Ramanujan value `mu(n)/phi(n)`. For n divisible by 60 its rational part
vanishes. Its irrational part is nonzero precisely when `n=60m`, where
m is squarefree and coprime to 30. In that case the pair has the form

`(±s*mu(m)*chi_60(m)/(8 phi(m)), -mu(m)/(8 phi(m)))`.

At m=1 these are the two primitive-60 points. Otherwise
`w=1/phi(m)<=1/6`; give the matching primitive point weight w and absorb
the rational second-coordinate correction into the vertical segment
`(0,[-1,1])`, which belongs to the rational five-point hull. If only the
second moment has an irrational part, n=120m with the same restrictions,
and the pair lies on the segment between the primitive-120 points.
All remaining pairs are rational. Odd squarefree orders give `(u,u)`,
twice odd squarefree orders give `(-u,u)`, with `-1/2<=u<=1`; orders
divisible by four give `(0,u)`, with `-1<=u<=1`. These lie in the same
five-point hull. This proves the unrestricted claim. Among orders
dividing 60 only the negative-character primitive-60 point is additionally
needed for the restricted claim.

## Complete integer and moment domains

The source Gram matrix is `B_ij=sum_A v(A)_i v(A)_j` for central simple
restriction rows v(A). Each central dimension `z=a+b*s` satisfies D/z
algebraically integral. Since D/60 is a unit and the ring of integers is
Z[s], this gives the exact necessary divisibilities

`a²-15b² divides 60a` and `a²-15b² divides 60b`.

The six nonunit induced-unit rows have the prescribed dimensions D/f,
include the forced Galois-unit row, sum to `B_(0,*)-e_0`, and leave a
positive semidefinite residual Gram matrix. Exhaustive bounded enumeration
gives **110 frames for R443** and **157 for R1214**. All bounds come from
B. Independently enumerating possible remaining rows gives respectively
3,670 and 3,741 rows before the frame-specific Gram constraints.

Every residual row is tested against the full exact condition
`v v^T <= R`, including singular R, and against its Galois partner.
The odd-isotypic pivotal forms on `X Y X*` and `X³` force all seven
second indicators to +1 in either source.

For each frame there are 21 Gram equations, 12 first-trace equations and
12 second-trace equations. The identities used are

`Tr(theta | I(X_i))=0`,
`Tr(theta² | I(X_i))=D*nu_2(X_i)` for nonunit X_i.

All coefficients are multiplied by eight to give integer columns. Paired
rows share a single hull vertex, justified by the relative-trace
observation above. Thirteen R443 frames and twenty R1214 frames already
fail a necessary integral Gram-span condition modulo a small prime. The
remaining 97 and 137 frames are separated by respectively nine and thirteen
integer covectors y satisfying `y*column>=0` for every allowed column and
`y*target<0`. These exact inequalities exclude a nonnegative real solution,
and hence exclude every possible center and source category.
