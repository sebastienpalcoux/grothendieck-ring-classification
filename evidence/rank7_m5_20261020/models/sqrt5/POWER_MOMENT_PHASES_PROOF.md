# Relative phase inventories for power-of-two moments

Let F = Q(sqrt(5),sqrt(2),i) = Q(sqrt(5),zeta_8). We use the ordered basis
(1,sqrt(5),sqrt(2),sqrt(10),i,i sqrt(5),i sqrt(2),i sqrt(10)). For k>=1,
record the normalized relative traces of theta^(2^j), 0<=j<=k, for all roots
of unity theta of order 2^a 5^b. This is the twist-order prime restriction
already established in the surrounding argument; this note proves the
complete inventory at every possible exponent a,b, rather than imposing a
bound on the order.

## Uniform finite inventory

Write xi=zeta_8. The normalized 5-primary trace has three possibilities:
(c_0,c_1)=(1,1), or
c_0=(-1+delta sqrt(5))/4, c_1=(-1-delta sqrt(5))/4, delta=+1 or -1.
For b=0 these are the first pair. For b=1 the two nontrivial cases alternate
under squaring. For b>=2, the normalized relative trace of every
2^j-th power is zero: averaging the subgroup congruent to1 modulo5 already
cancels each primitive 5^b-th root, and 2^j is coprime to5.

The 2-primary relative trace of a primitive 2^a-th root raised to2^j is
zero when a>j+3. This follows by averaging the kernel of reduction from
units modulo2^a to units modulo8. When a<=j+3 the root after powering lies
in Q(zeta_8), so its relative trace equals itself. The CRT decomposes the
relative Galois group over F into the independent 2- and 5-primary factors;
therefore these two descriptions multiply.

Consequently all nonzero moment vectors have the following form. Choose
h=0,...,k. For h=0 choose ell=0,...,7; for h>0 choose odd ell modulo8.
The first h moments are zero, and the remaining moments are

    mu_j = xi^(2^(j-h) ell) c_((j-h) mod2),  h<=j<=k.

For h>0 this describes exact 2-primary order 2^(h+3). The 5-primary sign
can absorb the h earlier squarings, since both signs are included.
There are24 records at h=0 and12 at each h=1,...,k. All are distinct.
The additional all-zero vector occurs precisely when a>=k+4 or b>=2.
Thus the complete inventory contains25+12k records. Every nonzero record
is realized by a root of unity of order dividing M=40*2^k.

All coordinates have denominator dividing8. The implementation
`phase_records_powers(k)` independently compares this closed list with
normalized Galois averages of every M-th root, computed in Q[x]/Phi_M(x).
The F-fixing subgroup consists of u=1 modulo8 and u=+/-1 modulo5. Its
order is2^(k+1). The implementation checks the embedded field generators,
exact field membership, all phase fibers, unique exact-order provenance,
and all source-field-fixing square-action packets. It also checks projection
to both historical inventories. These are exact rational calculations.

## Packet sizes and signs

Choose L=Q(sqrt(5),sqrt(-2)). It is the full source-character field for
R5039 and contains the full source-character field Q(sqrt(5)) for R3895.
Its fixing subgroup on roots of order2^a5^b has square image

    {1 modulo8} on the 2-primary part,
    {1 modulo5} on the 5-primary part.

At low exponents the first group is interpreted as trivial. Its orbit on a
primitive root has exactly

    2^max(a-3,0) * 5^max(b-1,0)

members. For the 2-primary claim, the square map from the union of classes
1 and3 modulo8 onto the principal subgroup1 modulo8 is surjective at each
level; for the 5-primary claim, squaring is bijective on the principal
5-group, and the classes+/-1 modulo5 give this entire image. CRT makes the
two actions independent.

Automorphisms fixing the complete source field fix source characters,
source dimensions, and center restriction rows. The relevant normalized
Galois signs are positive because they fix the positive dimension quotient;
this is the sign-safe argument in FULL_SOURCE_PHASES_PROOF.md. Thus these
square-action cycles genuinely stay within one restriction row. No assertion
about the sign of the raw sqrt(5)-transport matrix is used here.

All finite visible records have b<=1 and a<=k+3, so their required
per-row packet sizes are1,2,...,2^k. For the truezero phase, cycles from
high2 order have size divisible by2^(k+1), while cycles from high5 order
have size divisible by5. A union of such cycles has multiplicity in the
additive monoid generated by2^(k+1) and5. This is a necessary relaxation,
not a claim that every such multiplicity is categorically realizable.

If the *actual positive* sqrt(5)-Galois transport fixes a restriction row up
to source duality, categorical duality can return to the same row while
preserving its twist. The sqrt(5)-nontrivial square action inverts the
primitive5 component. For b>=1 it exchanges two disjoint source-fixed
packets; hence the per-row packet divisor doubles. The truezero divisors,
conditioned on exact5-primary part1,5,or at least25, are therefore

    2^(k+1), 2^(k+2), 10.

The conditional statement must not be applied merely from unsigned raw
transport: the sign issue and correct positive-row action remain essential.

## Eighth moments and the conductor-eight split

For k=3 the finite modulus is320 and the inventory contains61 records:
24 ordinary records,12 second-only onset records,12 fourth-only onset
records,12 eighth-only onset records, and the genuine all-zero record.
The latter means a>=7 or b>=2; its per-row monoid is16N+5N.
Its three conditional exact5-primary divisors are16,32,10.

The independent conductor-eight argument restricts a chi_{-8}-active row
in the c=0 branch to orders16,32,64 times1 or5. Every such order divides320,
and its eighth moment is nonzero. Thus no chi_{-8}-active object in that
branch can have the all-zero four-moment record. This conclusion uses the
proved conductor-eight restriction, not the phase enumeration alone.

## Replay and compatibility

Run `full_source_phases.py --max-power 3` normally and with Python -O.
The receipt `power_3_moment_phases_certificate.json` records all320 fibers
and all61 phase vectors. The old `phase_records('F8')`,
`phase_records_fourth()`, and their receipt files retain their former
interfaces and bytes. The generic k=2 calculation is additionally checked
against the fourth-moment receipt including exact exponent fibers and
packet multiplicities. The generic API includes old phase indices to make
projection explicit without relying on a common sorting order.

An additional independently implemented replay, `verify_power_phase_inventory.py`,
uses only the Python standard library and Fraction arithmetic. It uses the CRT
factorization zeta_(5n)^k=zeta_n^(5^(-1)k) zeta_5^(n^(-1)k), n=2^(k+3),
and explicit multiquadratic multiplication; it imports neither SymPy nor the
generator. Its normal and -O receipts also test17 packet sizes through
order2000,22 higher2 cancellation instances and12 higher5 cancellation
instances. Deliberately altered phase coordinates are rejected.
