# Independent audit of conductor-five equality and the primitive residue sign

Status: **PASS** for the mathematical implication below. Every application must still check its exact induced-unit compression and the torus character. No categorification is constructed by these necessary conditions.

Let `S,T` be the unitary modular representation of a pseudo-unitary center, with its anomaly-one normalization. Let `P` project onto all twist-one coordinates, and let `L` be the known induced-unit subspace. Suppose `w in L` has quadratic diagonal-torus character `chi_5`, and

`projection_L(Sw) = delta*w/sqrt(5)`, where `delta=+1` or `-1`.

Then:

1. `PSw=delta*w/sqrt(5)`, so every additional twist-one coordinate of `Sw` outside `L` is zero.
2. The cyclic representation generated by `w` factors through level five.
3. A nonzero coordinate of `Sw` outside `L` has primitive fifth-root twist `zeta_5^k` with `chi_5(k)=delta`.
4. Its normalized relative first/second trace pair is the single point `P_delta=(-1/4,delta/4,-1/4,-delta/4)`.

## Exact equality space at every 5-primary level

Here is an independent direct derivation specialized to the character actually needed. Work first at modulus `M=5^a`. Let `Omega_a` be the primitive column vectors `(p,q)` over `Z/M`, and use the permutation representation on its functions. The stabilizer of `(1,0)` is precisely the upper unipotent group `U`, and the group is transitive on primitive vectors. Thus every irreducible with a nonzero `U`-fixed vector embeds in this permutation representation, by Frobenius reciprocity; the embedding may be rescaled to an isometry.

In the `U`-fixed, `chi_5` diagonal-torus eigenspace, the functions supported on `q` a unit form the single line generated by

`F(p,q)=chi_5(q) [5 does not divide q]`.

Write `Sigma` for its orthogonal complement, supported on `q` a nonunit (where primitivity forces `p` to be a unit). Let `A=PSP` be the Weyl compression. Since `chi_5(-1)=1`, the Weyl inverse acts as the Weyl element on this character space, so `A` is self-adjoint. Averaging the Weyl translate of `F` over `U` gives

`AF=G`, with `G(p,q)=chi_5(-p) [5 divides q]`.

Indeed, if `q` is divisible by five, the relevant values `chi_5(p+tq)` are constant; otherwise their sum is zero. A vector of `Sigma`, after the same Weyl/unipotent averaging, vanishes on the small cell and is a torus-character vector on the big cell, so `A(Sigma)` lies in `C F`. Self-adjointness therefore determines this entire map from `AF=G`: its adjoint image is `C G` and it vanishes on `Sigma orthogonal to G`.

The supports of `F,G` are disjoint and their squared norms have ratio `||G||^2/||F||^2=1/5`. Thus `AG=F/5`, the norm of `A` is `1/sqrt(5)`, and its complete maximal-singular subspace is exactly `span(F,G)`. Both functions are pullbacks modulo five, and the space of all such pullbacks is invariant under the entire group. Hence the cyclic representation of an equality vector factors through level five. In an irreducible summand, nonzero intersection with that pullback subrepresentation forces the entire summand to descend.

At a prime different from five the local character is trivial and the compression bound is one. Equality means that both `w` and `Sw` are upper-unipotent-fixed. Thus `w` is fixed by the upper and lower unipotents, which generate the local `SL_2`, and that factor is trivial. Chinese remaindering and orthogonal irreducible decomposition give the global result: equality in the product norm forces equality at every factor. Equivalently, diagonalize the positive local operators `A* A`; the top eigenspace of their tensor product is the tensor product of their top eigenspaces. This supplies the all-level justification, not just a numerical check at a few moduli.

The known projection on `L` already has squared norm `||w||^2/5`, equal to the global upper bound. Since `L` lies in the full twist-one space, Pythagoras forces all unknown twist-one coordinates to vanish and makes the full projection equal to the known one.

## Primitive sign from STS and norms

For the applications `||w||^2=2`. The full twist-one component has squared norm `x_0=2/5`. Level-five descent means that all remaining support lies on primitive fifth-root eigenspaces. Let `x_+` and `x_-` be their total squared norms for quadratic-residue and nonresidue exponents respectively. Then `x_++x_-=8/5`.

The diagonal torus sends the exponent `k` to `u^2 k`, and its action is unitary. Because `w`, hence `Sw`, has torus character `chi_5`, the two exponents within each residue class have equal squared norm. Their averaged twist values are `(-1+sqrt(5))/4` and `(-1-sqrt(5))/4`.

The vector `Sw` is real in the categorical application: its coordinates are the difference of the two real formal-codegree-five characters evaluated on a nonnegative integral restriction row, divided by five. Alternatively, charge conjugation fixes `w`, so `S^{-1}w=Sw`. Using `Tw=w` and the anomaly-one identity `STS=T^{-1}ST^{-1}` gives

`<Sw,T Sw> = <w,S w> = 2 delta/sqrt(5)`.

Therefore

`2/5 + x_+*(-1+sqrt(5))/4 + x_-*(-1-sqrt(5))/4 = 2 delta/sqrt(5)`.

The rational part cancels, so `x_+-x_-=8 delta/5`. Nonnegativity now gives `x_{-delta}=0`. This proves the primitive sign without a classification of Weil representations or a convention-sensitive Fourier sign. Additional twist-one support was already ruled out by saturation.

## Application to the two rank-seven rings

The torus character must be checked for the difference of the two induced-unit constituents whose formal codegree is five. Modular Galois symmetry preserves the induced-unit support and has positive signs there: the integral induced-unit multiplicity vector is fixed by `S`, hence by its signed Galois permutation. The two relevant real characters are exchanged exactly by the automorphisms nontrivial on `Q(sqrt(5))`, so their difference has character `chi_5`.

The source character calculation makes `(Sw)_Y` a nonzero constant times the difference of restriction coordinates 4 and 5 for `R7_5_3895`, and 3 and 4 for `R7_5_5039`. Once the exact low compression has eigenvalue `delta/sqrt(5)` on this difference vector, every high row with unequal indicated coordinates is therefore restricted to the single phase `P_delta`. Rows with equal coordinates have zero `Sw` coordinate and receive no such restriction.

More precisely, write `(p,q)` for this pair of coordinates. The exact characters give `(Sw)_Y=(v_p-v_q)/sqrt(5)`, up to the global sign of `w`. The known induced-unit rows have differences `1,-1` and zero otherwise. Hence all additional center simples satisfy the small integral budget

`sum_Y (v_p-v_q)^2 = 8`.

Each of the two permitted primitive fifth-root eigenvalues contributes exactly four to this sum. In particular every additional row has `|v_p-v_q|<=2`. The normalized row transport reverses this difference, so any orbit containing difference two contributes at least eight and exhausts the budget. This is a necessary count of actual center simples, with their integral multiplicities; it does not by itself prove that any compatible list can be realized categorically.
