# Independent categorical audit of the positive rank-nine M2 ledger

The seven baseline models and the fourteen phase-two models have actual
categorical existence arguments. None of the positive entries reviewed here
is justified merely by a dimension match, an associative integer tensor, or
a permutation of simple objects. This review found no reason to withdraw a
positive model. Numerical tensor replay is a separate check; the root audit
also replays the complete source identifications.

The claims are existential: each ring has a **unitary** realization, hence
also a complex fusion-category realization. They do not assert that every
categorification of that ring is unitary or equivalent to the displayed one.

## Actions with explicit coherence

**21, 748, 1211: PASS.** Gelaki–Naidu–Nikshych,
[0905.3117](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.3117), Proposition 4.8 supplies the
actual orthogonal-group action on `TY(A,χ,τ)`. Inversion preserves each
displayed bicharacter. The TY associator is unitary for the stated
unit-modulus bicharacter and `τ=|A|^{-1/2}`.

The parity transformation `η_m=-1`, `η_a=1` is a **monoidal natural
automorphism** of the identity, not an arbitrary sign on simples. Thus
modifying action composition by an invariant, normalized, parity-valued
group two-cocycle preserves the action pentagon. For 748/1211 the cocycle
is `ε(g)ε(h)` on `S3`; the projective multiplier at `m` is trivialized
by `i^{ε(g)}`, producing the documented extra sign exactly on odd-odd
fusion channels. For 21 it is `bc` on `V4`, and the odd stabilizer's
twisted group algebra has anticommuting generators squaring to one,
hence is `M2(C)`. There is exactly one odd simple, of dimension `2√5`.
Frobenius reciprocity then determines its products with the integral
subcategory and its square. Every coherence map has modulus one.

**1373: PASS.** The factor permutation on `Fib ⊠ Fib` is an actual unitary
tensor action; pullback through `S3→C2` is a categorical action. The
fixed simple pairs and the free two-element orbit have the stabilizers
used in the character average. On a fixed multiplicity-one factor line
the tensorator is the ordinary interchange of two vector-space factors,
with no super sign. This establishes the fusion traces used by the code.

**1857 and 2029: PASS, with the separately recorded detailed audit.** The
seed action is the order-three Dynkin automorphism of the quantum-group
construction, descending through tilting semisimplification. The zest
requires an equivariant pointed gauge, supplied by the split group
extension `V4→E→C3`. The cocycle is equivariant as categorical data, and
the new tensorator preserves the zested associator. This is covered in
`phase2/integral_theory/d4_zesting_audit.md`; the present review does not
replace an action-lifting proof by tensor invariance. Root assigned an
additional independent audit of 2029 in phase three.

## Central algebra quotients

**515: PASS.** In `Ising ⊠ SU(2)_10,even`, each factor has a specified
fermionic lift in its Drinfeld center. The product `ψ ⊠ V10` has central
order two and self-braiding `+1`, so it generates `Rep(C2)` **in the
center**. Its regular algebra is connected, commutative there, separable,
and unitary. This is sufficient for the fusion category of its ordinary
modules. One does not require the Ising fermion to be transparent in
the original Ising braiding. The action on the 18 simples is free:
`j→10−j` has no fixed even label. Thus the free-orbit fusion rule used
in the constructor is the actual module fusion rule.

**389: PASS.** The proof in `phase2/weak_integral/mr_orbifold.md` is an
explicit pointed associator, inversion tensorator, central half-braiding,
and equivariant algebra construction. In particular it verifies the
central **tensor square**, including its associator correction, and the
transport of the central lift under inversion. The identity algebra
equivariance respects multiplication because its tensorator on `(8,8)`
is one. These are the coherence conditions that would be missing in a
ring-only orbifold proposal. All scalars are phases and the quotient is
by a free central boson. The independent closed-form audit is in
`phase2/models/additional_model_audit.md`. A second phase-three audit
was separately assigned by root.

**1983: PASS.** The half-E6 input is an existing unitary category with a
specified central fermion. Its existence and half-braiding are published
in Aasen–Lake–Walker,
[1709.01941](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.01941), Sections 7.1–7.2 and
Appendix D.1, in particular equation (240). The equal-factor product
must use the same local rotation phase in both inputs. The ordinary
factor swap preserves the central diagonal algebra and its identity
equivariance, hence descends coherently. It preserves both fixed-point
resolutions: an ordinary Deligne swap introduces no super interchange
sign. The balanced multiplication coefficients `1,b,−b,−1` show that
the lifted residual fermion squares to the sign representation.
The centrality argument is valid: the two original central fermions
centralize the diagonal algebra, become isomorphic as central modules,
and their one-dimensional module-isomorphism space makes the swap
isomorphism central. This justifies all left/right products used in the
fusion-rule reconstruction. The fuller audit is
`phase2/signed/ordinary_swap_audit.md`. A second phase-three audit was
separately assigned by root.

## Published exotic category and the two pullbacks

**1372: PASS.** Grossman–Snyder,
[1202.4396v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1202.4396), Section 4.3 explicitly
identifies `AH3` as the principal even part of the `AH+1` subfactor.
This is an actual unitary even part; Table 3 and the ancillary
`AH3Modules` data provide its fusion rules. It is not merely one of the
paper's enumerated candidate fusion modules. The reconstruction uses the
regular multiplication table, its displayed duality, and restores the
unit coefficient. The source digest and table interpretation are retained.

**1396 and 1397: PASS.** I rechecked the full proof in
`phase2/group_models/PULLBACK_MODELS.md`, not only the preceding audit.
The LPR dimension-24 non-self-dual seed is explicitly categorified
unitarily in [2010.10264v5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.10264), Section 4.4.
The actual cyclic-prime construction characterization of solvability
in [ENO 0809.3031](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.3031), Definition 1.2 and
Proposition 4.4, justifies the forced equivariantization steps because the
universal grading is trivial. Comparing two half-braidings on a fixed
invertible gives a monoidal scalar system, hence a character of the
universal grading group. Its triviality proves uniqueness of the central
`Rep(C3)` lift, which therefore is preserved coherently by the outer
action. Its equivariantization is a **central** `Rep(S3)`, and genuine
de-equivariantization supplies the pointed category with an actual `S3`
action. Pullback along `Dih(A)→S3` introduces no new action obstruction.
The inflated seed fixes the two nontrivial channel traces, so the
complete tensor calculation is not choosing them freely.

These pullbacks are group-theoretical, being Morita equivalent to the
pointed crossed product. Their unitarizability also follows independently
from Galindo–Hong–Rowell,
[Theorem 5.20](https://people.tamu.edu/~rowell/GHRarx1.pdf).

**812 and 813: PASS.** The explicit groups `V4 ⋊ Dih(A)` exist for
`A=C9,C3×C3`; the quotient action factors through `S3=Aut(V4)`.
Their representation categories provide immediate unitary existence.

## Separation from the negative-ledger repair

This positive audit explicitly accepts the published half-E6 category
`NG(C2,2)`. Root subsequently found that an incomplete lower-rank positive
registry had been used in some **negative** baseline certificates. That
is a separate defect requiring withdrawal and re-evaluation of those
exclusion certificates. No exclusion is endorsed here merely because a
subring is absent from a list of models.
