# Independent review of six new general exclusions

## Cyclic internal-end family

The internal end algebra is an actual algebra, supplied by the hypothetical
module category. Its multiplication between stabilizing invertibles is
nonzero because it composes isomorphisms to the chosen simple. The free-module
adjunction identifies the endomorphism algebra, including multiplication,
with a twisted group algebra of the right stabilizer of a simple object.
A subgroup of a cyclic group is cyclic, so its twisted group algebra over C
is generated by a single element with nonzero power equal to the identity.
It is commutative. This contradicts any repeated simple summand.

This proof uses no spherical structure, positivity, or TY associator table.
I wrote a separate general implementation from the original input tensors,
without importing the producer. It checks every fusion subring and every
candidate simple in all2083 rank-nine M2 inputs. It finds289 witnesses;
285 are independently excluded already, and exactly1025,1709,1759,1988
were unresolved. All32 prior model tensors pass as negative controls.
The full independent receipts are `cyclic_internal_end.json`.

## Dimension-120 family

I checked the categorical sequence in `../weak_b/DIMENSION120_A5_OBSTRUCTION.md`:

* Trivial universal grading prohibits an extension as the last step of a
  solvable construction. The alternative is an actual Cp equivariantization,
  whose canonical Rep(Cp) embeds in the category; exactly two invertibles
  forces p=2.
* De-equivariantizing the stated fixed and exchanged simple objects gives
  precisely the rank-six type1^5,5 at dimension30. The fixed two-dimensional
  objects split into distinct simple modules of dimension1, because their
  free-module endomorphism algebra is semisimple of dimension2. EGO's
  general-complex cyclic near-group criterion rules out this type.
* ENO Theorem9.16 supplies weak group-theoreticity at dimension60. A
  nilpotent Morita representative of dimension60 is built from group
  extensions whose orders multiply to60. If all groups were solvable the
  representative and source would be solvable. Thus one group must beA5
  of order60, making the representative pointed Vec_A5^omega. No cocycle
  is assumed trivial.
* Naidu–Nikshych Example2.2 covers arbitrary omega and forces the subgroup
  of a pointed-dual module category to be normal abelian. A5 admits only
  the trivial such subgroup. Each invertible bimodule is therefore regular
  on the left, with60 simples. Equal component dimensions then force all
  its simple FP dimensions to1 in every graded extension.
* ENO Lemma3.4 explicitly transports a group extension along Morita
  equivalence. Hence every extension of the neutral category in question
  is group-theoretical and integral. The dimension2sqrt5 odd objects in
 585 and1303 contradict this.

The relevant ENO and Naidu–Nikshych statements were opened and checked in
primary full texts. Exact source/neutral/grading checks were rerun and pass.
The proof never identifies categories from a mere match of their fusion rings.

**Accepted:** 585,1025,1303,1709,1759,1988 are excluded in general-complex
scope. This adds six decisions to the phase-three ledger.
