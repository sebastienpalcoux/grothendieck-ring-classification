# The forced pointed associator excludes the nonidentity quotient action

## Result and precise scope

The TY-origin Morita reduction in `THEOREM.md` forces a pointed subcategory
P = Vec(D8, omega) whose associator has **class 2 on the rotation subgroup C4**.
For either sign of the TY parameter tau, a simple object X cannot satisfy

    r X = X s,    |r|=4, |s|=2,

where r and s are invertibles of P. This is a module-associativity obstruction
over C. It requires no pivotal, pseudo-unitary or unitary structure on the full
category containing P.

Both labelled completions 26 and 27 for each of R9_2_137, R9_2_1607 and
R9_2_1976 have precisely this forbidden relation. They constitute one common
based-ring isomorphism type and are therefore eliminated **as TY-origin Morita
duals**. The earlier three-type frontier reduces to the two identity-action
types, cases 12 and 25. The three original source rings remain unresolved.

This statement is not an unconditional exclusion of all categorifications of
the abstract rank-twelve based ring: the forced pointed associator is essential.
The earlier elimination of twenty other completions by formal-codegree fields
still uses the separately stated source-to-PU existence reduction. The new
elimination here has no such extra hypothesis.

## 1. The rotation subgroup has nontrivial class 2

Write D=TY(V4,chi,tau), with chi the nonalternating form forced in `THEOREM.md`.
Let Y be its dimension-two simple. In coordinates V4=F2^2,

    chi(a,b)=(-1)^(a1*b1+a2*b2).

Thus the four diagonal values of chi are 1,-1,-1,1. Shimizu's fourth-indicator
formula (Theorem 3.4 and its immediately following specialization) gives

    nu_4(Y)=(1/2) sum_a chi(a,a)^(-1)=0.

This formula uses the canonical positive spherical structure on the TY category
and holds for both tau=+1/2 and tau=-1/2. All four invertibles have nu_4=1.
Consequently the weighted indicator of D is

    v_4(D):=sum_T FPdim(T)*nu_4(T)=4.

The pointed dual P=Bimod_D(1+g), with g the isotropic involution, is Morita
equivalent to D. The weighted indicator is invariant under this Morita
equivalence. For clarity, this does not assert that every object indicator is
Morita invariant. Ng--Schauenburg Theorem 4.1 gives, for a positive spherical
category B,

    v_n(B) = (1/FPdim(B)) sum_{Z simple in Z(B)} FPdim(Z)^2 * theta_Z^n.

This follows by multiplying their formula for nu_n(T) by FPdim(T) and summing
over T. Morita-equivalent fusion categories have braided-equivalent centers;
the canonical positive pivotal structure is unique and is preserved by that
equivalence, so their twists and the displayed sum agree. Here both D and P
have integral dimension eight and possess these canonical positive structures.
No positive structure on a larger ambient category has been invoked.

The D8 group has six elements of order at most two and two inverse rotations
r,r^{-1} of order four. For every invertible t of order two in any pointed
category with its positive structure, nu_4(t)=nu_2(t)^2=1. The two rotations
have equal fourth indicators (they are dual, or conjugate). Therefore

    4 = v_4(P) = 6 + 2*nu_4(r),
    nu_4(r) = -1.

For a cyclic pointed category Vec(C_N,omega_k), the calculation immediately
before Ng--Schauenburg Lemma 9.3 gives

    nu_{ell*N}(t)=exp(2*pi*i*k*ell/N)

for a generator t and class k in H^3(C_N,C*)=Z/N. Hence nu_4(r)=-1 means

    res_<r>[omega] = 2 in H^3(C4,C*)=Z/4.

In contrast, the pullback of any class in H^3(C2,C*) along the quotient
C4 -> C2 is zero. One can verify this without a cohomology-ring convention:
the nontrivial C2 cocycle pulls back to

    w(a,b,c)=(-1)^((a mod2)*(b mod2)*(c mod2)),  a,b,c in Z/4,

and w is the coboundary of

    beta(a,b)=(-1)^(floor(a/2)*(b mod2)).

The certificate checks all 64 values of this equality. Reversing the monoidal
structure or inverting the cocycle leaves the zero pullback class unchanged.

## 2. The cyclic stabilizer obstruction

Suppose a complex fusion category B contains P and has a simple X with rX=Xs,
where |r|=4 and |s|=2. Regard B as a module category over
P tensor P^rev, using the action

    (a,b) . X = a tensor X tensor b.

The group of invertibles of this acting category is D8 times D8^op. Its element
t=(r,s) has order four and fixes X, because rXs = X. Thus all of <t>=C4 fixes
the simple X. The full additive subcategory generated by X is a rank-one module
category for the pointed cyclic subcategory generated by t.

A rank-one module category over Vec(K,eta) exists only if [eta]=0 in H^3(K,C*).
Indeed, choose nonzero isomorphisms k.X -> X. Their composition scalars give a
2-cochain whose coboundary, by the module pentagon, is eta. This is also the
stabilizer condition in Ostrik, Example 2.1.

But on <t>, the first projection pulls back omega from <r> by an isomorphism,
giving class 2. The second projection factors through the quotient C4 -> <s>=C2,
so its contribution is zero by the explicit coboundary above. The external
product associator therefore restricts to class 2+0=2, a contradiction.

This proves the obstruction for arbitrary complex fusion categories.

## 3. Exact source-labelled applications

All indices below are zero-based in the stored rank-twelve dual tensors.
The selected simple is X=8. Each equality is checked as equality of the full
twelve-component product vectors, not only their dimensions.

| Source | Candidate indices | Rotation r | Reflection s | ord(r) | ord(s) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| R9_2_137 | 26,27 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 2 |
| R9_2_1607 | 26,27 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 |
| R9_2_1976 | 26,27 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 2 |

In each case rX=Xs, and the same relation holds for all four noninvertible
simples. These are exactly the nonidentity theta cases: theta interchanges
the rotation coset in D8/Z(D8) with one reflection coset.

As a supplementary check, the exact induction overlap is

    dim Hom(I(r),I(s)) = [r : sum_Y Y s Y*] = 4.

If the ambient category is pseudo-unitary, there is a second proof. The
indicator trace formula and positivity force every central summand of I(r) to
have theta_Z^4=-1, because nu_4(r)=-1 and FPdim(r)=1. Since nu_4(s)=1, every
summand of I(s) has theta_Z^4=1. Their positive overlap is impossible. This
alternative is not needed for the general-complex proof in Section 2.

## 4. Additional forced cyclic restrictions

The even pointed sector E=(V4/<g>) times <g>-hat has trivial associator: the
original pointed V4 splits over <g>, and its subgroup-algebra dual is the
untwisted pointed category on E. Its three involutions, including the central
z=r^2, therefore have nu_2=+1. The two remaining reflections have equal nu_2,
by conjugacy. Morita invariance of the weighted second indicator gives

    4+2*sgn(tau) = v_2(D) = v_2(P) = 4+2*nu_2(s_odd),

so their second indicator is sgn(tau). These additional sign restrictions do
not by themselves eliminate the two identity-action survivors.
