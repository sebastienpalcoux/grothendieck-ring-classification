The new criterion applies, but excludes **none** of the three remaining

## The categorical premise is satisfied

The integral subcategory has basis {0,5,6,7,8}; its four invertibles are
V4 and its dimension-two object is Y=b8. The dimension-d objects form
two left V4-orbits:

| Orbit | Stabilizer |
|---|---|
| {b1,b3} | {1,b7} |
| {b2,b4} | {1,b6} |

Tensoring by Y takes either simple in one orbit to the sum of the two in
the other orbit. The two internal-end algebras inside the TY subcategory
are consequently 1+b7 and 1+b6. The perpendicular-stabilizer calculation
in the sibling `quadratic_comm/ty_module_subtask/THEOREM.md` applies to
this left module category just as in the commutative cases. Commutativity
of the full source ring is not used. It forces the nonalternating TY
bicharacter, with b7 and b6 anisotropic and b5=b7*b6 the unique isotropic
involution. The sign of tau remains unrestricted.

Thus A=1+b5 is exactly the isotropic subgroup algebra used in the phase-four
Morita computation. Its pointed eight-object dual is D8, with the associator
restriction of class 2 on the rotation subgroup C4. This is the conclusion
of `../quadratic_comm/ty_module_subtask/FORCED_D8_ASSOCIATOR.md`, based on
the Morita-invariant weighted fourth indicator of TY. It is a restriction
on this TY-origin pointed category; no classification of arbitrary spherical
categories or unitary reconstruction is assumed.

## Every remaining quotient action is the identity

In the phase-four group labeling, the center is {0,1}, the four cosets are
{0,1}, {2,3}, {4,5}, {6,7}, and the two rotations are 6 and 7. The other
nonidentity elements are involutions. All ten original completions, hence
all three surviving representatives, have identity quotient action: gX=Xg.
In particular, rX=Xs can hold only when r and s lie in the same central
coset. The rotation coset contains two rotations and no involution.
Therefore the forbidden relation with ord(r)=4 and ord(s)=2 never occurs.

## Stronger cyclic stabilizer check

The four group-supported A-bimodules have indices E={0,1,2,3}; their
pointed associator is trivial. The two odd reflections 4 and 5 have
restriction class 0 in H^3(C2,C*) when tau is positive, and class 1 when
tau is negative. Rotations 6 and 7 both have restriction class 2 in
H^3(C4,C*). These are the additional restrictions derived in Section 4
of the sibling forced-associator proof.

For each remaining tensor, the verifier lists all 64 equalities gX=Xh
with g,h invertible and X noninvertible. For both signs of tau, it checks
the associator on the cyclic stabilizer generated by (g,h^-1). If its
order is n, the pullback of the class k on a factor of order m is
(n/m)^2*k modulo n. The two factor contributions cancel in every case.
Equivalently, rotation relations pair the same order-four class; reflection
relations stay within a central coset with the same order-two class; and
the stabilizing central involution has trivial restriction.

The rotation-duality relation rX=X* in the last row does not strengthen
this result: dualization is contravariant, so this is not an ordinary
rank-one module for a cyclic pointed subgroup. No contradiction is
inferred from that relation without an additional theorem.
