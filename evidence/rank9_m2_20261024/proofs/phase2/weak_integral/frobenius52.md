# A prime-kernel rigidity and bimodule-rank obstruction

**Conclusion.** The two rings `R9_2_1071` and `R9_2_1724` admit no complex
fusion categorification. The argument does not assume a braiding, pivotal
structure, sphericality, or unitarity.

Both have a faithful C2 grading whose neutral component has dimension52,
rank7, dimension type (1^4,4^3), invertible group C4, and trivial universal
grading. Every neutral invertible fixes each dimension-four simple. The odd
component has rank2, with both simples of FP dimension sqrt26. These are
finite tensor statements checked in `frobenius52.py` and recorded in
`frobenius52_certificates.json`.

## 1. Rigidity of the neutral category

We prove a categorical statement: a fusion category D with the neutral
properties just listed is tensor equivalent to Rep(C13 semidirect C4), where
a generator of C4 acts faithfully on C13 (e.g. multiplication by5 modulo13).

By ENO, *Weakly group-theoretical and solvable fusion categories*, Theorem1.6,
D is solvable. The equivalent prime cyclic extension/equivariantization
construction (Definition1.2 and Proposition4.4) ends in an equivariantization,
because D_ad=D forbids a nontrivial grading. The character group of the final
prime cyclic group embeds into Inv(D)=C4. Thus D=B^C2, FPdim(B)=26. The unique
order-two invertible is the sign object. It pairs the four invertibles and
fixes the three dimension-four simples. The equivariant-simple
orbit/stabilizer formula forces B to have dimensions (1,1,2,2,2,2,2,2): a
sign-fixed simple comes from a free orbit of length2, while a sign-exchanged
pair comes from a fixed simple. There are no nontrivial projective
representation dimensions for a cyclic stabilizer.

B has trivial universal grading. Indeed, the order of any faithful grading
group divides26 and does not exceed rank8. The only possible nontrivial order
is2; its neutral component would have prime dimension13 and therefore be
pointed of rank13, impossible inside rank8. Solvability again gives
B=P^C2, with P=Vec_C13^omega. The inner C2 action is inversion on C13: a
trivial object action would make all26 simples invertible.

The central Rep(C2) in B is unique over the two invertibles. The kernel of
Inv(Z(B))->Inv(B) is Aut_tensor(Id_B), which is trivial because B_ad=B.
Therefore the outer C2 action preserves this central Tannakian subcategory.
Equivariantization gives a central Tannakian Rep(K) in D, with fully faithful
underlying embedding, where 1->C2->K->C2->1. This is Tannakian, rather than
merely super-Tannakian: the symmetric forgetful functors give a fiber functor
to Vec. All four simples have dimension1 and exhaust Inv(D)=C4. Therefore
K=C4. De-equivariantization in stages gives

    D = (Vec_C13^omega)^C4.

The action of C4 on C13 is faithful: otherwise nonidentity pointed objects
have orbits of size at most2, with cyclic stabilizers, and all resulting
simples have dimension at most2, contrary to the dimension-four simples.
Let u in (Z/13)^* be the order-four automorphism. Then u²=-1 modulo13.
The action induced on H³(C13,C^*)=H⁴(C13,Z)=Z/13 is multiplication by u²:
H*(BC13,Z) is generated by a degree-two class t, and u acts by t->u t.
Consequently invariance of [omega] gives [omega]=-[omega], so [omega]=0.

Finally, the action on Vec_C13 with this object permutation is unique up to
categorical equivalence. The tensor-structure ambiguity for each functor is
H²(C13,C^*)=0. After making the functors the standard strict permutation
functors, composition constraints differ by a 2-cocycle with values in
Aut_tensor(Id)=C13-hat. H²(C4,C13-hat)=0 by coprime averaging. Hence the action
is equivalent to the ordinary semidirect-product action. This proves the
claimed tensor equivalence to Rep(G), G=C13 semidirect C4.

This extends the previously proved dimension52 descent argument in the
rank-seven manuscript: there the invertible group was V4 and the orbit sizes
contradicted dimension4; here C4 is permitted, and the coprime cohomology
calculation identifies the category uniquely.

## 2. The rank-two homogeneous component cannot be invertible

Every homogeneous component of a faithful fusion-category grading is an
invertible bimodule category over the neutral category (ENO, *Fusion
categories and homotopy theory*, Section6). Thus the odd component M would
be an invertible Rep(G)-bimodule category of rank2. In particular its dual
fusion category End_Rep(G)(M) must be tensor equivalent to Rep(G), of rank7.

Indecomposable module categories over Rep(G) are Rep^psi(H), for subgroups
H of G and 2-cocycles psi, up to conjugacy and coboundary (Ostrik's module
category classification). Here every subgroup is cyclic, dihedral of
order26, or G itself. All their Schur multipliers vanish. For the noncyclic
cases this follows from the Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre spectral sequence for
1->C13->H->Cd->1, d=2 or4: the three total-degree-two terms vanish since
H²(C13,C^*)=H²(Cd,C^*)=0 and H¹(Cd,C13-hat)=0 by coprimality.

The irreducible-count possibilities are respectively 1,2,4,13,8,7 for subgroup
orders1,2,4,13,26,52. Thus a rank-two module category must be Rep(C2), with
trivial multiplier. Its dual is the group-theoretical category C(G,1,C2,1).
The normalizer of this C2 is the complement C4. There are two double cosets
of size2, each with stabilizer C2 and hence two simples of dimension1. The
remaining48 group elements form twelve double cosets of size4, each with
trivial stabilizer and one simple of dimension2. Therefore its dual has

    dimension type (1^4,2^12), rank16,

contradicting the required rank7. This excludes both source rings.

The verifier reconstructs G as pairs (a,b) in Z13 x Z4 with
(a,b)(c,d)=(a+5^b c,b+d), enumerates all30 subgroups, computes their conjugacy
