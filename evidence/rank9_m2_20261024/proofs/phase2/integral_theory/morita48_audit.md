# Independent audit of the 396/1265 Morita exclusions

The mathematical scope and the extended anti-module enumeration pass independent audit. The criterion is necessary for arbitrary complex fusion categorifications; it assumes no pivotal, spherical, pseudo-unitary, or braided structure.

The fixed-simple argument trivializes the associator on the order-two pointed subgroup and supplies the connected separable algebra `A=1+g`. Its right-module category carries the involution obtained by changing the algebra action's sign on `g`. The difference of the two structures on each fixed simple gives the anti-invariant basis. Bounds, parity, transpose, and all fusion-product relations hold for any number of fixed simples. There is no mathematical restriction to fixed-block size at most two.

The new `general_anti.py` uses the full bounded matrix domain for three fixed simples. Its Boolean encoding was inspected term by term: the scalar sentinel represents one, parity domains are exactly `{-1,1}` or `{-2,0,2}`, squares and mixed products are exact, negative pseudo-Boolean weights are complemented with the correct right-hand-side shift, and transpose identifications merge equal entries only. No Frobenius–Schur sign, matrix commutativity beyond the source fusion relations, or auxiliary trace condition is imposed. Every solution is blocked only along its diagonal-sign gauge orbit. Completion is claimed only after an unsatisfiability result.

An independent enumeration is supplied in `audit_morita48_anti.py`. It uses distinct eight-bit variables for **every** matrix entry and direct matrix-product equations, rather than the discovery program's Boolean-product encoding or variable identifications. Arithmetic modulo 256 is exact on this finite domain: a three-by-three product entry has absolute value at most 12 and each nine-term fusion-linear entry has absolute value at most 36, so no modular equality can introduce an integer mismatch. Both complete enumerations terminate and find exactly 18 diagonal-sign gauge classes, matching all saved branches. The receipt is `morita48_anti_audit.json`.

The partial and full Gram implementations were checked against the existing categorical proofs in `manuscripts/rank7/papers/module_dual_gram.tex` and `manuscripts/rank6/papers/chapters/full_morita_gram.tex`. Their identities are Morita-bicategory associativity and evaluation identities. Dimension constraints use positive Frobenius–Perron module dimensions only. The fixed first-column decomposition of `I+M_g` is exhaustive, since each simple bimodule has a nonzero underlying right module; it fixes the dual rank and dimension multiset. The candidate searches enumerate all bounded nonnegative integral columns, using exact dimension equations or exact rational image coordinates. Their additional entrywise Gram and commuting-action constraints are necessary, and the later linear relaxation only enlarges the allowable domain.
