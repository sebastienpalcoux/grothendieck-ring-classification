## Positive construction review

The 16 registry models use pointed/Tambara–Yamagami categories, finite-group representation categories, published noncommutative integral examples, SU(2)/PSU(2), metaplectic SO(7)₂, and the cited SU(2)₆ zesting. Full tensors are generated or sourced with explicit based permutations; dimension coincidence is not accepted as a match. The seven residual registry models are A6, C13⋊C4, adjoint SU(3)₅, two TY equivariant constructions, Vec(Q8)^S3, and the twisted Q8⋊S3 group-theoretical model. Three AnyonWiki associators add direct general-complex existence witnesses.

For R7_4_2019, I checked the global Chern-class construction and its restriction to C2×S3, the cocycle triviality on the selected S3 subgroup, the projective lift whose square is the sign character, and the resulting C4 of invertibles. The independent Hamilton-coordinate fiber computation and actual U(2) relations both pass. This is stronger than merely obtaining an associative numerical tensor.

For R7_4_35 and R7_4_2665, `inversion_orbifold_model.tex:29–126` was scrutinized for the potentially dangerous jump from a based-ring involution to a coherent categorical action. The argument uses an actual C* near-group category; the regular algebra self-Morita equivalence; uniqueness of its relevant algebra structure; the explicit central inversion; central algebra preservation and base change; identification of the descended module as the regular left module; and the monodromy identity relating its square to an inner automorphism. Trivial universal grading removes the final coherence ambiguity. The reflection algebra 1+s therefore exists in the coherent crossed product. The associator-dependent C4 versus V4 distinction is made with Frobenius–Schur exponent/indicator information, not inferred from the same D8 multiplication table. The exact replay honestly checks only finite tensors and induction identities; categorical existence rests on the proof just reviewed and on the cited starting categories.

The C16 input relies on the specific Budinski near-group existence data and Izumi’s self-duality/automorphism machinery; the C4² input is the unitary near-group realization of Yu–Zhang. I checked the latter primary proposition and Izumi’s relevant theorem statements, but did not independently reconstruct every starting category’s F-symbols. This remains a literature dependency, as with standard quantum-group constructions.

## Primary references consulted and version caveats

- Schopieray, *Categorification of integral group rings extended by one dimension*, JLMS108 (2023), DOI [10.1112/jlms.12789](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1112/jlms.12789): the **published** Lemma3.1 on p1628 gives a Galois conjugate that is pseudo-unitary; it does not claim the original category is pseudo-unitary. The earlier arXiv-v1 wording is different. Theorem1.1 is a general categorifiability classification and excludes NG(V4,8). The project’s applications use the published scope.
- Schopieray, [*The dimension block of a spherical fusion category*](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35287v1): local compression Lemma4.1 and the representation-theoretic part of the proof of Theorem5.1.
- Izumi, [*A Cuntz algebra approach to the classification of near-group categories*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.04288): Theorem11.3, Section12, Theorem13.4 and Corollary13.5. Its C* scope is appropriate for the positive starting constructions, not a license to exclude all complex categorifications.
- Grossman–Jordan–Snyder, [*Cyclic extensions of fusion categories via the Brauer–Picard groupoid*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.6414): Lemma4.7 and Remark4.8. The rank-seven proof uses the explicit unique-algebra part of the module argument and retains the automorphism ambiguity.
- Yu–Zhang, [arXiv:2412.20763v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.20763v2): Proposition3.1 and the specified C4² bicharacter input.

No claim is made that every external cited theorem was reproved, that every line of every transitive software dependency was independently reimplemented, or that all possible general fusion-category obstructions were searched. Input-census completeness is the producer’s enumerative claim; the audit verifies the supplied tensors and accepted decisions, not a new enumeration of all rank-seven fusion rings.
