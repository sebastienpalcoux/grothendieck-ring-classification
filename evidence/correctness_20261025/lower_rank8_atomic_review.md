## Proof-to-encoding checks

The reviewed source was compared with the partial modular spectrum, character-labeled induction, integer cubic reconstruction and joint Galois propositions in `manuscripts/rank8/paper/m3_frontier.tex`, the dedicated joint-Galois section and integer-system proof, and the current R3472 complete-domain argument. These predicates are initially **pseudo-unitary** necessary conditions; an independent complete sphericalization/Galois gate is required for a general complex exclusion. The current accepted atomic callers require their general-lift certificates rather than treating a failed pseudo-unitary relaxation alone as general nonexistence. The detailed signed-gate proof audit remains the rank-eight agent's coverage.

1. **Full character and field verification.** The real, complex-quartic and real-sextic tables verify all eight distinct characters, the FP dimension character, every source multiplication relation, duality/conjugation and orthogonality. The nonreal adapter checks an actual field involution. Field embeddings and positive dimensions are exact. The integrality test is the characteristic polynomial of rational multiplication by the candidate algebraic number; it does not incorrectly identify the primitive power basis with an integral basis. The cyclotomic adapter legitimately uses the integral power basis of the full cyclotomic field.

2. **Atomic low choices.** For each non-FP character, `AtomicSpectrum` collects every surviving old low column having the required distinguished dimension. Taking the union of dimension-compatible columns across the old groups and using independent one-hot choices can enlarge the possible domain, but cannot omit an actual labeled frame. The omitted FP symmetry equation follows identically from the dimension equation. Every non-FP pair has the correct full-field equation `beta_E chi_E(v_F) - beta_F chi_F(v_E) = 0`. Complex conjugation is correctly absent from this symmetric S equation. All rational coordinates are retained and denominators cleared exactly.

3. **Complete cone pairing.** The streaming implementation represents each high duality orbit by the sum of its vectors and rank-one Gram forms, while the per-member common dimension is used in its phase factors. This is consistent with dual central simples having equal twists and dimensions. The direct-column and factored-score implementations have identical Gram, indicator and rotation contributions. Added atomic S rows occur only on low columns and are included in `low_scores`; they do not accidentally disappear from the Farkas oracle. The oracle traverses every low, high-phase and rotation column before accepting a separator.

4. **Exact Galois automorphisms.** `automorphisms` requires an irreducible degree-d defining polynomial, all d distinct roots in the same field, exact root verification, multiplicativity on basis products, and permutation of the complete root set. Thus the maps are the complete automorphism group of a normal characteristic-zero field, not a subset selected numerically.

5. **Individual and joint support pruning.** A variable represents either one labeled low restriction or a complete high duality orbit. The unit is included as a fixed constituent. A missing conjugate forces *every* nonnegative variable containing the offending eigenvalue to vanish. The same rule applies to the complete joint tuple, with one field automorphism acting on every coordinate and with distinguished matrix labels held fixed. The algorithms count all members of a high orbit, retain the fixed unit throughout, iterate deletions, and detect an empty distinguished domain or a fixed constituent with missing conjugate. There is no first deleted actual column: an actual integer fusion spectrum has all conjugates. Using only currently present support and removing several justified columns in a round is therefore sound. The filtered object deliberately exposes only the data interface and is not reused as an unchanged phase cone.

6. **Galois multiplicity equations.** Separate and joint rows use true per-orbit multiplicities, including repeated eigenvalues in the same high orbit. Fixed constituents move to the right-hand side with the correct sign. A missing conjugate still creates a row against zero support; it is not silently omitted. Fusion-coefficient auxiliary variables contribute no spectral multiplicity. The joint-tuple necessity proof follows by a separating integer linear combination of commuting integral center fusion matrices. Omitting the FP coordinate only weakens the system.

7. **Cubic/integer reconstruction.** The third character is dualized, the normalization is `D^2 dim(v)`, and all 196 specified non-FP triples are included. Constant coordinates equal actual nonnegative integer fusion coefficients; all other field coordinates vanish. This use of power coordinates does not assume an integral basis. Low-duality equations pair both the character and the restriction dual. High variables count actual central simples per restriction-duality orbit, not relative-trace weights. Their diagonal-Gram bounds are conservative; the fusion-variable upper bound uses the fixed constant term plus all positive constant-coordinate contributions, so negative contributions are not incorrectly counted as positive. The additional FP ratio bound uses exact sign comparisons in the positive embedding and takes the minimum with the earlier valid bound.

8. **Integer reductions and arithmetic.** Box propagation updates exact minimum/maximum contributions with the correct reversed inequalities for negative coefficients and applies only necessary residual-gcd conditions. Reduction shifts all variables by their lower bounds and lifts them back exactly. Equation compression divides both sides by their common content, normalizes signed rows, and merges only equal normalized equations. Modular/Hermite negative conclusions are checked by annihilators on every original column; HNF membership alone is explicitly a signed-lattice condition. Exact integer witnesses are checked against every original equation and finite bound. No numerical negative is accepted as an obstruction.
