### Particularly important reasoning checks

* The Casimir eigenvalue on a matrix block is `r_E f_E`, with multiplicity `r_E^2`. The manuscript and oriented/weighted implementations distinguish this from the formal codegree `f_E`. In low induction, `r_E` weights the unit multiplicity and restricted row sum, while each **distinct** central-simple column occurs once in the Gram subtraction.
* General nonexistence is not inferred merely from a positive-dimension obstruction. The relevant gates use weak integrality, the cited two-FP-dimension Galois reduction, or exhaustive sphericalization/sign-character analysis. `signed.signed_problem` explicitly checks commutative descent before imposing symmetric signed tensors. Noncommutative sectors retain oriented coefficients.
* The signed algebra is semisimple using its positive coefficient-of-unit inner product and adjoint duality. Its real dimension character, field containment and original/signed induction quotients were traced through the arguments; no unsupported assumption that every original category is pivotal was found.
* Finite relative-twist trace domains include the zero contribution of sufficiently high prime-power layers. They are not finite cutoffs on actual categorical twist orders. Missing eligibility, unsplit fields, resource limits and failed search are not themselves exclusion proofs.
* The anti-invariant module obstruction uses a genuine algebra `1+g`, with trivialization of the pointed associator forced by a fixed simple. The rotation constraints use naturality across leg operators; a ring symmetry alone is not treated as a tensor autoequivalence.
* Conformal constructions use existing connected étale algebras from actual embeddings, followed by full module or neutral-module reconstruction as appropriate. A modular invariant or associative fusion tensor alone is not offered as an existence proof.
* The relative Fibonacci-center construction starts with the unitary `TT_3` category and an actual Fibonacci subcategory. Diagonal-one Gram pivots force all restrictions, then linear uniqueness forces the whole tensor. Existence is supplied before the Gram calculation.
