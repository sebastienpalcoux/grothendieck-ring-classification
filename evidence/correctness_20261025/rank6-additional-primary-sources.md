For every even A index the adjoint Brauer–Picard group is trivial, regardless of the full A-category modular-braiding branch. Substituting index6 gives the precise BrPic(Ad(A6))=1 used by the manuscript.

This supplies the stated general-complex/Galois scope, not just a unitary Brauer–Picard computation.

The local rank-six proof separately establishes that the complementary bimodule is an actual twisted regular invertible bimodule, using the ambient left-module equivalence d→dX and transported right action.
