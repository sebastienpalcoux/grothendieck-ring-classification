# Classification audit — 25 October 2026

**Status: completed audit of the pinned current release, with corrections required.**

No current exclusion or realization needs reversal on the evidence obtained in this audit. **Proof gaps were found in the source chains identifying R8_2_820, R8_2_869 and R6_2_143. New exact replacement arguments have now passed independent cross-review and support retaining all three models.** Their original manuscript proofs still need explicit revision. A further exact completion proof identifies R8_3_721 without assuming the upstream census is complete. Reproducibility, implementation and documentation defects were also found. The scoped proof reviews, accepted-decision replays and case reconciliation are complete. The execution qualifications and remaining external theorem dependencies are stated below; this is not a formal proof that no undiscovered error exists.

## Fixed sources and meaning of the audit

The audited classification repository is [commit 8014c749c17ddd2ad3d1b99a4359d49a8be921c5](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/grothendieck-ring-classification/tree/8014c749c17ddd2ad3d1b99a4359d49a8be921c5). The census repository is [commit a69cd7e10047a7d86f3f03a7c43cb52dbc73c2f0](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/fusion-ring-census/tree/a69cd7e10047a7d86f3f03a7c43cb52dbc73c2f0). Fresh clones were used. All 98 restored large data files matched their released SHA-256 values. No public repository or website has been changed by this audit.

The current release has eleven main mathematical manuscripts, totaling 657 PDF pages. Their substantive included proof sections were divided among six auditors. The review asks, for every exclusion, whether the premise really applies to an arbitrary complex fusion category; for every model, whether an actual category/action/algebra exists before the fusion tensor is computed. A matching dimension vector, feasible Gram matrix, ring automorphism, or successful scalar calculation is not treated as a categorification.

“Proof reviewed” means mathematical reading and checking of the argument, with independent reconstruction of especially delicate deductions and selected finite calculations. It does not mean proof-assistant certification, human peer review, or a fresh proof of every theorem in the external literature. Fresh replay means a new execution on the pinned inputs, with its actual outcome retained. A timeout, process kill, missing file, or failed assertion is never counted as a pass.

Rank-six execution requires a particular qualification. The original verifier ran all 25,362 workers and reached its exact final “seven negative certificates failed” check. Every failure is printed by that unchanged loop; all seven complete workers subsequently passed with `SYMPY_GROUND_TYPES=python`. During the run, the raw JSONL pathname was replaced by a 3889-row copy; the still-open original inode could not be recovered through the available read interfaces. A separately reviewed finalizer therefore binds the complete log, exact terminal control-flow location, unchanged work list/source/data, all seven successful retries, consistent surviving prefix, and both original post-loop checks. It records 25355 passing original verdicts by control-flow inference plus 7 successful full retries, without inventing result rows or timings. This is a **log-derived composite replay**, not a successful unchanged single invocation or complete retained raw log of every row. The final receipt is `rank6-current-reconstructed/final-reconstructed-coverage.json`.

The full R8_3_3472 replay also passed: 298,865,973 general sphericalization-gate nodes, complete high/low domains, the exact terminal integer system, rounding cuts and source bindings. This is not merely the package's quick check.

Current realized categories include one rank-six spherical, non-pseudo-unitary example. The audit therefore does **not** identify “realized” with “unitarily realized.”

## Independent checks beyond rerunning existing programs

- Full lower-rank positive catalogues imported into ranks six, seven and eight were compared using independent exhaustive unit-fixing permutations. There are exactly the required 15 rank-four and 17 rank-five classes; the rank-eight rank-six snapshot has exactly all 43 current models through multiplicity two, including the non-pseudo-unitary one.
- Native signed enumeration was compared with independent rectangular brute force and independent Python enumeration, including zero-denominator/singular branches. The coefficient-two global test gives 1832 signed tables and 48 sign/permutation orbits in both implementations.
- Exact trace averages, affine integer solutions, Gram principal-minor enumerations and native Schur-complement tests were independently compared on deterministic complete small domains.
- Exclusion criteria were applied directly to known realized rings without skipping known models. Completed controls produced no false exclusion. Eligibility failures and bounded search timeouts are separately identified in the rank reports.
- Three rank-seven models with supplied exact associators have independently verified global dimensions 16, 28, 16. Published solvability and complete-unitarity theorems establish unitarizability; this does not claim that their supplied gauges are orthonormal.
- All 27 rank-eight multiplicity-one/two pointer controllers were read in full, together with their critical dependencies. The final targeted reviews also covered the active atomic-spectrum filters, original multiplicity-three dispatch, biquadratic kernels and native helpers; exact source inventories distinguish new reviews from byte-identical shared kernels already reviewed.
- R8_3_721 now has a solver-free exact completion: the four duality branches have 0, 0, 2 and 0 integral solutions, with one based-ring class. All accepted coefficients are verified before census lookup.
- R8_2_868’s exact external center theorem is explicitly identified as Theorem A(2)/5.1 of Gannon–Schopieray–Yadav, arXiv:2609.25185v1. The separately checked generator matrix is not treated as a proof of the full tensor.
- Several sourced rank-eight tensors were rebuilt from categorical fusion formulas or actual equivariant-bundle character convolution, rather than accepted from a matching dimension vector or imported tensor.
- The main sphericalization, signed-dimension, Galois pseudo-unitarity, cyclic-isotypic and full-I(1) compression scope gates were checked explicitly. In particular, the general compression bound is not replaced by the stronger spherical-only bound.
