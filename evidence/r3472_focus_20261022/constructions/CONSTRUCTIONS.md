# R3472: seven-invertible rules and Radchenko–Wheeler parent obstruction

These are necessary conditions and obstructions to a specified construction
route. They neither construct associators nor exclude general categorification
of R8_3_3472. Put r=sqrt(2), delta=7+5r and D=700+490r.

## 1. Two possible seven-invertible based rings

Suppose a hypothetical Morita partner has dimensions 1^7, delta^7. Its
invertibles form C7, generated by g. Since delta^2=1+14delta, reciprocity
shows that each delta-dimensional simple has trivial left and right
stabilizers. Write these simples X_i=g^i X. There is a unit a modulo 7
such that Xg=g^a X, and the unique invertible summand of X^2 is g^b.
Associating X^2g in two ways gives a^2=1. Reciprocity gives
X*=g^(-b)X, and applying duality twice gives b=ab. Thus a is +1 or -1.
If a=-1 then b=0. If a=+1,
replacing X by g^c X allows b=0 since 2 is invertible modulo 7. Hence X is
self-dual in either case.

If a=-1, comparing X^2g and gX^2 forces all seven noninvertible
coefficients of X^2 to agree. Their sum is 14, so each is 2. If a=+1,
write X^2=1+m(g)X, with m(g)=sum m_j g^j, m_j=m_{-j}>=0 and sum m_j=14.
The symmetry follows by duality.

For the next step use the two structural properties established for the
hypothetical Morita class: after a Galois conjugacy its categories are
pseudo-unitary; every simple in their common Drinfeld center has FP dimension
in Q(r), and this center has no nontrivial invertible. Consequently all
formal codegrees belong to Q(r). Indeed the induction theorem associates to
each irreducible representation E a central simple A_E with
FPdim(A_E)=D/f_E. This formula has NO extra factor dim(E); its multiplicity
in I(1) is dim(E).

For a=+1 take a nontrivial character g->zeta of C7. Its extensions have
X->x, where x^2-tx-1=0 and t=m(zeta). Since t is real, x is real and the
formal codegree is f=7(1+x^2). Thus x^2 is in Q(r), and
t^2=(x^2-1)^2/x^2 belongs to Q(r). But t belongs to the real cubic field
Q(zeta+zeta^-1), whose intersection with Q(r) is Q. Therefore t^2 is
rational, and the odd degree of this cubic field makes t rational. The
cyclotomic polynomial Phi_7 then forces m_0=A and m_j=B for 1<=j<=6,
with A+6B=14. The only choices are (A,B)=(14,0),(8,1),(2,2).
The middle choice has t=7, and its nontrivial codegrees
(371 +/- 49sqrt(53))/2 do not belong to Q(r). The first choice has t=14
and contains the rank-two fusion subring X^2=1+14X, excluded by the
rank-two categorification theorem. Alternatively, the root x=delta gives
codegree D for nontrivial C7 characters and hence nontrivial central
invertibles, a contradiction. Thus A=B=2.

The two surviving based rings, with i,j modulo 7, are exactly

    g_i g_j = g_(i+j),
    g_i X_j = X_(i+j),
    X_i g_j = X_(i+a*j),
    X_i X_j = g_(i+a*j) + 2 sum_k X_k,       a in {+1,-1}.

Their dualities are g_i*=g_-i and X_i*=X_(-a*i). Both are genuine
associative based fusion rings: the complete 14x14x14 tensors and all
associativity equations are checked by `verify_constructions.py`.
The + ring is commutative; the - ring is not. This does not establish
categorification or a Morita equivalence to R3472.

## 2. A nonunitary-safe necessary half-braiding formula

Let C be one of the Radchenko–Wheeler categories constructed from
gamma in SL_2(Z), trace gamma=N+2, as in arXiv:2609.21892v2,
Sections 4.3 and 5.4. Its group is G=Z^2/Z^2(gamma-I), |G|=N, with the
nondegenerate symmetric bicharacter

    chi(u,v)=exp(2 pi i * omega(u(gamma-I),v)/N).

Use RW's strict Leavitt-algebra realization. Equations (46)-(47) and the
paragraph “Compositions with alpha_g” give

    alpha_h rho = rho,
    rho alpha_g = Ad(U_g) rho,
    alpha_h(U_g)=chi(h,g) U_g.

If alpha_g has a central lift, its half-braiding with rho must be a nonzero
scalar times U_g, since rho is simple. The half-braiding hexagon at
alpha_h rho=rho therefore forces its half-braiding with alpha_h to be
chi(h,g)^(-1). In particular its self-braiding is chi(g,g)^(-1).
This derivation uses no star operation, positivity, or unitarity.

The forgetful map from central invertibles to G is injective. Its kernel
is a tensor automorphism of the identity, hence a character of the universal
grading. That grading is trivial: rho occurs in rho^2 and every alpha_g
also occurs in rho^2. Thus all possible central invertibles embed, preserving
self-braiding, into the premetric group (G,q), q(g)=chi(g,g)^(-1).
We do not need to assert that every g has a central lift.

## 3. The odd-square two-primary obstruction

Let N=4s^2, with s odd, for any RW input gamma of trace 4s^2+2. The
polarization of q is chi^-2, whose radical is R=G[2]. Since G is a quotient
of Z^2, its 2-primary part (of order 4) is either C4 or C2 x C2. In the
first case |R|=2. In the second, gamma-I=2A, with

    tr A=2s^2 == 2 mod 4,       det A=-s^2 == 3 mod 4.

The classes of the two rows of A are a basis of G[2]. If
A=[[a,b],[c,d]], evaluating chi on these classes gives diagonal values
(-1)^b and (-1)^c. Thus q restricted to R can be trivial only if b,c
are both even. But det A odd then makes a,d odd, and tr A==2 mod4 makes
ad==1 mod4. This gives det A=ad-bc==1 mod4, a contradiction.
Therefore q|R is nontrivial in the C2 x C2 case, and its kernel has size 2.
In all cases the bosonic part of the radical has size at most 2.
This proof applies to EVERY gamma of the required trace, not a finite
sample of representatives. The certificate additionally checks its modulo-4
implication exhaustively as an independent arithmetic check.

## 4. Arbitrary pointed stabilization does not remove the obstruction

Let P be any pointed fusion category, FPdim(P)=m. Suppose C box P has an
ordinary de-equivariantization B of dimension

    D_s=s(1+(s+sqrt(s^2+1))^2).

Since FPdim(C)=4s D_s, the de-equivariantizing group must have order
h=4s m. The fully faithful forgetful image of its Tannakian category
Rep(K) is pointed: its simple objects have integer FP dimensions, whereas
the only nonintegral simple dimensions of C box P are the irrational
near-group dimension. Hence K is abelian, and its h simple objects form an
isotropic subgroup H of the central invertibles.

Put L=Inv(Z(P)), k=|L|, and let R_L be the radical of the polarization of
its self-braiding quadratic form, of size t. Nondegeneracy of Z(P) gives
k*t<=m^2: its pointed subcategory has dimension k and its centralizer has
dimension m^2/k, and contains its own radical. This is the standard
centralizer dimension formula, not an assumption that L itself is modular.

By Section 2, H embeds into the finite premetric group G x L. Its radical
is G[2] x R_L. In the C4 case this has size 2t. In the C2 x C2 case
the quadratic form is nontrivial on the radical (already on G[2]), and
therefore its kernel has size 2t. In either case |H intersect rad|<=2t.
For an isotropic subgroup in any finite premetric group A,

    |H|^2 <= |A| * |H intersect rad(A)|,

because H is contained in its annihilator, whose order is
|A| |H intersect rad(A)| / |H|. Consequently

    h^2 <= (4s^2) k (2t) <= 8s^2 m^2.

But the required h=4s m has square 16s^2 m^2, a contradiction.

For s=7, this excludes every direct ordinary de-equivariantization from
a Radchenko–Wheeler parent with |G|=196, including its noncyclic parents,
even after tensoring by an arbitrary pointed category. Passing to a Morita
partner after such a quotient cannot repair the dimension contradiction.
There are no other RW parent orders with the right rational dimension
scale: the near-group global dimension E_N has trace N(N+4) and norm
N^2(N+4), so its scale-invariant trace-squared/norm ratio is N+4. For D
the same ratio is 1400^2/9800=200, forcing N=196. The near-group
dimension is genuinely quadratic because N(N+4) lies strictly between
(N+1)^2 and (N+2)^2 for every positive N.
It does NOT exclude arbitrary near-group categorifications with different
bicharacter data, a prior nontrivial Morita change of the parent, generalized
Haagerup parents, or arbitrary chains of category constructions.

## 5. A remaining construction direction (not a certified result)

The inversion-action candidate suggests a higher-multiplicity near-group
route rather than an RW parent. If its pointed associator can be trivialized
and a coherent inner C7 action is supplied, conjugation would act transitively
on its seven noninvertibles. The expected equivariantization would have 49
invertibles and one noninvertible of dimension 7delta, hence near-group
multiplicity 98. This points toward C7 x C7 + 98 under the expected untwisted
pointed data. The necessary coherence, invertible-group identification and
relation to the prescribed rank-two modules have NOT been independently
certified here. This is not part of the proved theorem or verifier.

In particular, no blanket assertion that multiplicity greater than group
order is impossible is justified: Izumi's *A Cuntz algebra approach to the
classification of near-group categories*, Section 10, treats m=2|G| and
Theorem 10.19 gives the known unitary C3+6 examples. The C*-specific
nonexistence results for C4 and C2 x C2 in that section do not apply to
C7 x C7 or to arbitrary pseudo-unitary categories.
