non-group-like simple integral fusion rings of Lie type*, Theorem 5.1
(arXiv:2102.01663v4, page 9), to this ring. That theorem applies to every
nonpointed simple complex fusion category; integrality is not a hypothesis.
The existing general sphericalization and pseudo-unitary Galois reduction
in `manuscripts/rank8/paper/m3_research_20261022.tex` is an input to the
nonbraidedness argument. It is not re-proved by these small tensor checks.

simple has irrational FP dimension, because every nonunit source simple
has a positive coefficient of sqrt(2), and a nontrivial simple center
cannot be generated by an object forgetting to copies of the unit.

The integral prime-power simple-object obstruction in Remark 5.15 of the
cited paper is not applicable: the hypothetical center is not integral.
The center-simplicity theorem and this caveat do not exclude R3472.
