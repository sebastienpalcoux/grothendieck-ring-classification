# A two-row induction obstruction for R7_2_160

**Concluded exclusion of general fusion categorification over C.**
The independent audit `AUDIT_R160_FS.md` approves the argument and replays
the exact premise verifier under ordinary and optimized Python.
All numerical premises are verified by
`verify_r160.py`. No large induction matrix is enumerated.

## 1. Exact source data and categorical inputs

The ring has positive dimensions `(1,1,2,6,6,6,6)`, total dimension 150,
all simples self-dual, and whole adjoint subring. Write its basis as
`1,g,E,X1,X2,X3,X4`. Its invertibles are `1,g`, and

    g²=1, gE=E, EXi=2Xi, gXi=Xi,
    E²=1+g+E.

Its formal codegrees, with multiplicities, are
`150,3,2,25,25,25,25`. If a categorification C existed, it would be integral
and hence pseudo-unitary. The center is integral and modular. Every
central simple has integer dimension dividing 150: the modular formal
codegree formula says that `150²/d²` is an algebraic integer, so `150/d`
is an integer.

For the forgetful functor F and its right adjoint I, the commutative-ring
induction theorem gives seven distinct multiplicity-one constituents of
`I(1)`, of dimensions

    1,50,75,6,6,6,6.

Here each constituent contains the unit exactly once under F. The usual
induction formula `F I(V)=sum_i Xi V Xi*` gives

    <I(1),I(g)>=5,
    <I(1),I(E)>=9,
    <I(g),I(g)>=7,
    FPdim(I(g))=150.

These are four small tensor sums, not additional assumptions.

## 2. A central invertible is forced

Let Y be any one of the four central dimension-6 constituents. Since F(Y)
contains the unit once and cannot contain a dimension-6 source simple,

    F(Y)=1+b g+c E,    b+2c=5.

In particular b is positive and odd. The total g multiplicity over all
nontrivial constituents of `I(1)` is 5. Thus all four dimension-6
constituents have `b=1,c=2`. Write `b50,b75` and `c50,c75` for the g and E
multiplicities of the other two constituents. The displayed induction
inner products give

    b50+b75=1,   c50+c75=1.

Their restriction dimensions modulo 6 give
`b50+2c50=49 mod 6`; with both coefficients zero or one this forces
`b50=1,c50=0`. Consequently `b75=0,c75=1`.

Therefore `I(g)` shares exactly five multiplicity-one constituents with
`I(1)`: the four dimension-6 constituents and the dimension-50 constituent.
Their total dimension is 74. Since the squared norm of `I(g)` is 7, its
remaining part consists of exactly two distinct multiplicity-one simples.
Their dimensions sum to 76 and divide 150. Among the positive divisors
of 150 the only pair summing to 76 is `(1,75)`. Hence the center contains
an invertible object whose restriction is g.

## 3. The forced invertible is bosonic

The subcategory generated by `1,g,E` has the Rep(S3) ring. The action on
the rank-one linear category generated by any Xi defines a fiber functor:
g acts by one copy and E by two copies. Semisimple Hopf reconstruction
and the dimension-6 Hopf classification therefore identify this actual
subcategory with Rep(S3), not merely its based ring.

Its universal grading is trivial, because `E²=1+g+E`. A half-braiding on
an invertible object, if it exists, is unique when the universal grading
is trivial: two choices differ by a tensor automorphism of the identity.
The restriction of the central lift of g is thus the usual sign object
in the symmetric Rep(S3) category, with self-braiding `+1`. Also the
central lift squares to the unit, since the whole category has trivial
universal grading, so there is no nontrivial central invertible lying
over the source unit. It generates a central Tannakian Rep(C2).

Consequently `C=E75^{C2}` with `FPdim(E75)=75`. The equivariantization
orbit formula yields E75 of type `1³,3⁸`: each sign-fixed source simple
of dimension 2 or 6 comes from a free orbit of two simples of dimensions
1 or 3, respectively, while the source units form the two lifts of the
neutral unit.

## 4. The descent combines to an impossible free S3 action

Every dimension-3 simple Z in E75 is fixed by all three invertibles. If
its invertible stabilizer were trivial, `ZZ*` would have one unit and
remaining dimension 8 divisible by 3, which is impossible. Thus `ZZ*`
contains the three units and two dimension-3 constituents. The adjoint
subcategory contains all units and a nonunit. A subcategory of type
`1³,3^k` has dimension `3+9k`; for positive k the only such number
dividing 75 is 75 itself. Thus the universal grading of E75 is trivial.

ENO's two-prime theorem makes E75 solvable. The final prime step of a
solvability sequence must therefore be an equivariantization, and its
prime is 3 because the invertible group is C3. The canonical central
Rep(C3) in E75 is unique on its underlying invertibles. It is preserved
by the outer C2 action.

Equivariantizing this central subcategory embeds a central symmetric
category of dimension 6 into `Z(E75^{C2})=Z(C)`. It has a fiber functor
obtained by forgetting equivariance, so it is Rep(H) for a group H of
order 6. Its number of invertibles is two: it contains the canonical
Rep(C2) and is embedded in C, which has exactly two units. Thus H=S3.
Direct de-equivariantization gives

    C = (Vec_A^omega)^{S3},    |A|=25,

because every fusion category of prime-square dimension 25 is pointed.

For a nonunit element of A in an orbit of length l<6, a simple lift has
dimension `l*dim(pi)`, where pi is a projective representation of the
stabilizer of order `6/l`. This is at most `sqrt(6l)<6`. Such a lift
cannot be one of the four source nonunits of dimension 6. It also cannot
be the dimension-2 source E, because E belongs to the embedded Rep(S3)
over the neutral element of A. Hence S3 must act freely on every nonunit
element of A.

The group A is either C25 or C5². The automorphism group of C25 has
order 20, so an order-three subgroup cannot act freely. In C5², a
fixed-point-free involution is necessarily `-I`: its minimal polynomial
divides `(t-1)(t+1)`, and the +1 eigenspace must vanish. Therefore all
three involutions of S3 would act by the same matrix `-I`. Their products,
the elements of order three, would act trivially. Again the action is
not free. This contradiction excludes R7_2_160 as a general complex
fusion category.
