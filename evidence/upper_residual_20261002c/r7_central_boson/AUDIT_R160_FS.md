as `d` is an integer, `150/d` is an integer too.

## Actual Rep(S3) and the boson

The full additive subcategory generated by any `Xi` is preserved by the
left action of the subcategory with simples `1,g,E`. It is a rank-one
module category, hence yields a tensor functor into `End(Vec)=Vec`.
The associativity constraints come from the hypothetical ambient fusion
category. This is an actual fibre functor, not merely a dimension
character. Hopf reconstruction and Etingof–Gelaki Theorem 6 in dimension
six identify this subcategory with `Rep(S3)`: the abelian group and
function-algebra alternatives are pointed and have incompatible fusion
data.

For an invertible object, two half-braidings differ by a half-braiding
on the unit, equivalently by a tensor automorphism of the identity.
EGNO Proposition 4.14.3 identifies these with characters of the universal
grading. The grading of the `Rep(S3)` subcategory is trivial, so the
restricted half-braiding on `g` must equal the transported ordinary sign
half-braiding. Its self-braiding is therefore `+1`. The source's whole
adjoint subring also makes the ambient universal grading trivial; this
ensures that the central lift has order two. Consequently the generated
central subcategory is Tannakian `Rep(C2)`, fully embedded under
forgetting. The first de-equivariantization is legitimate.

## The two equivariantizations combine centrally

For the outer `C2` equivariantization, every fixed underlying simple has
two inequivalent equivariant lifts interchanged by tensoring with the
sign character: `H^2(C2,C*)=0`. Thus each sign-fixed source simple `E`
or `Xi` comes from a free orbit of two underlying simples. The quotient
has type `1^3,3^8`, as claimed.

An invertible stabilizer of a dimension-three simple in this quotient
has order one or three. Order one would leave dimension eight outside
the unit in `ZZ*`, impossible for a sum of dimension-three simples.
Thus the adjoint subcategory contains all three units and a nonunit.
Its possible dimensions are `3+9k`; divisibility by 75 forces it to be
the whole category. ENO Theorem 1.6 and Proposition 4.4 now force the
last prime step to be equivariantization, since an extension would give
a nontrivial grading. The embedded invertibles force that prime to be
three.

The resulting canonical central `Rep(C3)` is the full pointed
subcategory under forgetting. Its half-braidings are unique, again
because the quotient's universal grading is trivial. Therefore the
outer `C2` action preserves it, with the inherited coherent action on
its central lifts.

For completeness, the central embedding after equivariantization can
be seen directly. For an equivariant central object and an equivariant
ambient object, use the original half-braiding on the underlying
objects. Naturality with the action makes this map equivariant and the
half-braiding hexagon is inherited. On this `Rep(C3)` subcategory every
underlying morphism is already a central morphism, so the resulting
functor into `Z(C)` remains fully faithful after forgetting to `C`.
The braiding is symmetric. Forgetting equivariance and then using the

The category is therefore `Rep(H)`, with `|H|=6`. It contains the
canonical `Rep(C2)` and has exactly two invertibles, since it embeds in

## Final action contradiction

Central `Rep(S3)` de-equivariantization has dimension 25. ENO
Proposition 8.32 gives pointedness for this odd prime square, without
any additional integrality hypothesis. Write it as `Vec_A^omega` with
`|A|=25`. The neutral unit has precisely the three lifts with dimensions
`1,1,2`, filling the embedded `Rep(S3)`. The orbit/projective-stabilizer
formula bounds the dimension of a lift over any other orbit of length
`l<6` by `sqrt(6l)<6`. Such a lift has no possible place in the source
list, so all 24 nonidentity elements must have free `S3` orbits.

The tensor action induces an honest group homomorphism
`S3 -> Aut(A)` on simple objects, even when its tensor coherence is
nontrivial. For `A=C25`, an order-three element acts trivially because
`|Aut(C25)|=20`. For `A=C5^2`, a fixed-point-free involution is `-I`:
in characteristic five its minimal polynomial divides the product of
the distinct linear factors `t-1,t+1`, and its `+1` eigenspace vanishes.
All transpositions would therefore act by the same `-I`, forcing their
order-three products to act trivially. Both groups contradict freeness.

The obstruction is consequently valid for general complex fusion
categories. No promotion from a unitary-only classification occurs.
