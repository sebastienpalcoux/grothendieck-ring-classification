# R191 and R299: the odd component forces an A4 Morita model

**General complex exclusion, independently audited.**

The neutral ring alone has several categorifications, including the
non-group-theoretical Jordan--Larson possibilities. The odd component
supplies the additional module-category constraint that eliminates these
possibilities. No classification of all neutral models is assumed.

## 1. Exact source structure

Both rings have type `1^4,2^2,(sqrt(6))^2`, of FP dimension 24. Their
integral simples form the even component D of a faithful C2 grading,
of FP dimension 12. D has invertible group V4 and two simples X,Y with

    gX=Xg=X, gY=Yg=Y for every g in V4,
    X*=Y, X^2=2Y, Y^2=2X, XY=sum_{g in V4} g.

Thus D is faithfully C3 graded, with neutral pointed category B on V4
and rank-one components generated by X and Y. Either rank-one component
is a rank-one B-module category, so the pointed associator on B is a
coboundary. We may therefore take `B=Vec_V4` with trivial associator.

The odd component M has two simple objects. V4 acts transitively on
them, with an order-two stabilizer H. In R191 the stabilizer is [0,5];
in R299 it is [0,7]. Thus M restricted to B is an indecomposable
rank-two module N. Its module cocycle is trivial in H^2(C2,C*)=0.

The exact source checker verifies the complete neutral subring, both
gradings, the stabilizers and transitivity, and the full positive dimension
character in `Z[sqrt(6)]`.

## 2. The module N has pointed dual Vec_V4

For `B=Vec_V4`, the module N is the module of a subgroup H=C2, with
trivial cocycle. Its dual is pointed, with invertible group

    H-hat times (V4/H) = C2 times C2.

One may choose a direct-product decomposition V4=H times K to see this
directly: the endofunctors are translations of K with an H character,
and their composition multiplies these labels independently. Hence
`B*_N=Vec_V4` as a tensor category, including its associator.

M is an actual D-module category by tensor multiplication in the putative
ambient category. Its restriction to B is exactly N and remains
indecomposable. Consequently N has a D-module extension in the sense of
Galindo's *Clifford theory for graded fusion categories*.

Let

    L = D tensor_B N

be the **induced** D-module category. Galindo's Theorem 1.3 gives that

    D' = D*_L

is a semidirect C3 product whose neutral component is `B*_N=Vec_V4`.
In particular D' is pointed. This theorem is about the dual of L, not the
dual of the original two-object module N. The latter dual would generally
be an equivariantization, and is not asserted to be pointed.

## 3. The pointed Morita group is A4

The original C3 Brauer--Picard action on B is nontrivial: its generator
is the rank-one bimodule component generated by X, whereas the regular
B-module has rank four. Morita transport by N conjugates this action and
therefore preserves its nontriviality and order three. The semidirect
product D' has this conjugate action.

For `Vec_V4`, equivalence classes of tensor autoequivalences have the
standard exact sequence with kernel `H^2(V4,C*)=C2` and quotient
`Aut(V4)=S3`. Since the action has order three, it cannot lie in the
cohomology kernel. It induces a nontrivial order-three automorphism of
the invertible group V4. Therefore the group of simples in D' is

    G = V4 rtimes C3 = A4.

Thus every neutral categorification compatible with the odd module in
either source is Morita equivalent to `Vec_A4^omega`. This conclusion
uses the existing coherent module structure, rather than merely
invariance of an abstract module class. It bypasses possible obstruction
classes for extending a module category.

## 4. Extension integrality contradicts sqrt(6)

The only normal abelian subgroups of A4 are 1 and V4, of orders 1 and 4.
The exact checker independently enumerates all subgroups of
`(F2)^2 rtimes C3` and verifies this assertion.

Apply the extension-integrality lemma proved and independently audited
in `R53_R93_GENERAL_PROOF.md`: if B is Morita equivalent to `Vec_G^omega`
and every normal abelian subgroup of G has square order, every graded
extension of B is integral. Its proof covers arbitrary omega using
Gelaki--Naidu's twisted double-coset formulas, normalizes the one-sided
module dimensions as `sqrt(|H|)`, transports the entire graded extension
through the Brauer--Picard groupoid, and proves the needed Morita
invariance of integrality by center induction.

Here a putative ambient category would be a C2 extension of D. The lemma
would make it integral, contrary to its two odd simples of dimension
`sqrt(6)`. This excludes both rings over the complex field.
