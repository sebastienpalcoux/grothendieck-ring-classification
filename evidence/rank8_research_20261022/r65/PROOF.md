# R8_3_65: pointed induction and Fourier integrality

This note supplies one unified obstruction covering every pointed associator
class of R65. With the published two-dimension Galois reduction, its six exact
certificates exclude general complex categorification. It uses the existing
central-induction cone and its explicitly listed identities, but does not rely
on the previous class-zero subgroup-dual argument or its lower-rank/R1453
exclusions. It does not assume unitarity, braiding, or a solution to any
general pivotality conjecture.

## A uniform pointed-induction lemma

Let C be pseudo-unitary, with its canonical positive spherical structure,
and write D=FPdim(C). Let g be an invertible simple of order n. Let I denote
the adjoint of the central forgetful functor and write

    I(g) = direct sum_Z m_Z Z,       z_Z = FPdim(Z) > 0.

The pointed restriction gives |nu_n(g)|=1, whereas nu_j(g)=0 for 1<=j<n.
The Ng--Schauenburg central-induction formula gives

    sum_Z m_Z z_Z theta_Z^j = D nu_j(g),
    sum_Z m_Z z_Z = D.

The twists are roots of unity. Equality in the triangle inequality at j=n
therefore forces

    theta_Z^n = nu_n(g)        whenever m_Z>0.                 (1)

Choose alpha with alpha^n=nu_n(g), and let W_a be the sum of m_Z z_Z
over the central simples with theta_Z=alpha exp(2 pi i a/n). The first
n-1 vanishing indicator identities and the dimension identity form a
discrete Fourier transform. Its inverse gives

    W_a = D/n          for every a in Z/n.                    (2)

In particular this conclusion holds fibre by fibre, before taking any
relative traces of twists or making any linear relaxation.

Suppose additionally that all noninvertible source simples have the same
irrational FP dimension d, and that the central inductions of distinct
source invertibles have disjoint simple supports. Then any Z in I(g) has

    F(Z) = m_Z g + sum_noninvertible_X b_(Z,X) X,
    z_Z = m_Z + b_Z d,     b_Z = sum_X b_(Z,X) >= 0.

If D/n=A+B d, with A and B rational, comparing the two coefficients in
(2) yields the exact nonnegative integer constraints

    sum_in_fibre m_Z^2 = A,
    sum_in_fibre m_Z b_Z = B.                                (3)

These constraints are a useful uniform refinement of a moment cone: the
integrality information is imposed before relaxation. No upper bound on
possible twist orders was used.

## Exact finite obstruction

`pointed_phase_cone.py` imports the previously certified all-order
root-trace, full-character-field S-symmetry, real CM-STS central-induction
cone read-only. It has 283 rational equality rows. It removes only columns
forbidden by the pointed trace table, and, in the Fourier variant, by the
proved multiplicity/bound restrictions. All other central columns and all
rotation columns are retained.

The inherited exhaustive second-indicator possibilities, in source indices
1 through 7, are `(0,0,0,0,1,e,f)` with e,f in {+1,-1}. The pointed class
fixes f=(-1)^k. The following six exact rational separators cover all

| 2 | -1 | `pointed_phase_k2.json`, branch 0 | 2222 |
| 2 | +1 | `pointed_phase_k2.json`, branch 1 | 2222 |

The thirteen center-twist trace types are complete at powers 1 through 4.
The categorical Cauchy theorem and Norm(D)=2000 restrict the prime divisors
of the center twist order to 2 and 5. For an order 2^a 5^b, the relative
traces at all four powers vanish if a>=4 or b>=2: the remaining power of 2
has a vanishing primitive-root trace, or the relative trace down the
5-power tower vanishes. The finite box a<=3,b<=1 is represented by divisors
of 40; adjoining the zero tuple covers all higher supported orders.
Thus the metadata value `order_bound:40` is a compression bound, not a
bound on actual twist orders. Order-three rotation eigenvalues on
Hom(1,X^3) belong to a different auxiliary domain: all such rotation
columns remain present and are not restricted by the center's prime support.

For each separator y, the verifier checks y dot column >=0 on EVERY
allowed column, and y dot right_hand_side <0. It does this twice: through
the factorized exact oracle and by explicit sparse-column arithmetic.
Numerical optimization proposes y; all conclusions use integer/rational
replay only. Missing branches, changed tensors, changed domain metadata,
or incorrect signs fail verification. No timeout or failed family search
is interpreted as nonexistence.

For transparency, `pointed_phase_k1.json` records an exact feasible witness
for the weaker odd-class relaxation before Fourier integrality. That
surviving witness demonstrates why the new fibre argument is essential;
it is not a categorification and does not satisfy the strengthened domain.

## Completing the general exclusion

1. The six certificates above exclude every class k=0,1,2,3 for every
   pseudo-unitary categorification.
2. Schopieray's published Lemma 3.1 says any categorification whose source
   dimensions have just the values 1,d is Galois conjugate to a
   pseudo-unitary categorification. A hypothetical categorification of R65
   would therefore produce a pseudo-unitary categorification of the same
   integral fusion rules, contradicting step 1.

Thus R8_3_65 has no categorification by a complex fusion category. In
particular no pivotal, spherical, pseudo-unitary, unitary, or braided
categorification remains.
