# A realized twisted quadratic precursor for R8_3_1768

This note constructs an actual complex fusion category with the higher
Haagerup rules `GH(C6, multiplicity 2)` and pointed associator class 3 in
`H^3(C6,C*)=Z/6`. It does **not** yet construct the connected separable
algebra `1+sigma` needed to obtain R8_3_1768. That last step is an independent
categorical problem; the earlier based-module completions do not solve it.

## 1. Actual near-group input

Use Radchenko--Wheeler, arXiv:2609.21892v1, Proposition 10 and Theorem 10,
with `gamma=[[145,12],[12,1]]`. Its determinant is 1 and its trace is 146.
The quotient `Z^2 / im(gamma-1)` is `G=C12 x C12`. Their Gaussian and
bicharacter reduce to

```
B((x,y),(a,b)) = exp(2 pi i (yb-xa)/12),
q(x,y) = (-1)^(x+y) exp(2 pi i (y^2-x^2)/24).
```

In particular this supplies the specific metric datum, not merely an
existence theorem for some metric on the abstract group. RW constructs a
complex near-group category with `rho^2=sum_G alpha_g+144 rho`. Write
`u=6+sqrt(37)`; its positive noninvertible dimension is `12u`.

Use the actual Leavitt-algebra endomorphisms, complete basic Hom spaces,
and identities listed in the project's algebraic orbifold proof
`../reference/ALGEBRAIC_ORBIFOLD.md`. This is a construction from RW's
specific realization; it is not a classification of arbitrary spherical
categories and does not assume unitarity.

## 2. A twisted subgroup of order 24

Put

```
h(x,t)=(x,x+6t),       x in C12, t in C2,
H={h(x,t)},            |H|=24,
omega(h(x,t),h(a,s))=(-1)^(ta).
```

Then `omega` is a normalized two-cocycle and

```
B(h(x,t),h(a,s))=(-1)^(xs+at)
                =omega(h(x,t),h(a,s))/omega(h(a,s),h(x,t)).
q(h(x,t))=(-1)^(xt+t).
```

The radical of the restricted bicharacter is
`K={h(2z,0):z in C6}`, of order 6; it is also `H-perp` in G.
The diagonal subgroup `L={h(x,0)}` has order 12, `L=L-perp`, and `q|L=1`.

Form the algebraic twisted crossed product
`R=direct_sum_{h in H} A lambda_h`, where A is RW's Leavitt algebra,
`lambda_h a=alpha_h(a)lambda_h`, and
`lambda_h lambda_k=omega(h,k)lambda_{h+k}`.
Extend the RW endomorphisms by

```
beta_g|A=alpha_g,  beta_g(lambda_h)=B(g,h)^(-1)lambda_h,
tau|A=rho,        tau(lambda_h)=q(h)U_h lambda_h.
```

The cocycle factors appear equally on both sides of the product and
covariance checks in the untwisted algebraic proof. Thus these formulas
define unital endomorphisms. Since `B|H` is real and is the commutator of
omega, `beta_h=Ad(lambda_h)` for h in H, and each lambda_h commutes with
tau(R). Coefficient comparison in the displayed direct sum gives

```
Hom(beta_g,beta_k)=C lambda_(k-g) if k-g in H, and zero otherwise;
End(tau)=C^omega[H];
Hom(beta_g,tau)=Hom(tau,beta_g)=0.
```

The complete decomposition on all of R remains

```
tau^2(a)=sum_g S_g beta_g(a) S'_g + sum_g T_g tau(a) T'_g.
```

For its check on lambda_h use `q(h)^2=1`, which holds for every h in H.
The proof is otherwise exactly the coefficient computation in the
referenced algebraic orbifold note. The original maps `S_0,d S'_0`
still satisfy both snake identities. Consequently the full additive
Karoubi completion generated by beta and tau is a rigid semisimple
complex tensor category with simple unit and finitely many simples.
This proves actual fusion-category existence.

## 3. Primitive projectors and complete fusion rules

Let `X=lambda_(1,1)` and `T=lambda_(0,6)`. In the twisted group algebra,

```
X^12=T^2=1,     TX=-XT.
```

Its center is generated by X^2, so
`C^omega[H] = direct_sum_{eta in C6} M2(C)`.
With `zeta=exp(2 pi i/12)`, the projectors

```
p_eta=(1/12)sum_(l=0)^11 zeta^(-eta*l) X^l
```

are primitive; `p_eta` and `p_(eta+6)` are equivalent via T. Define
`sigma_eta=(p_eta,tau)`, eta=0,...,5. Then
`tau=2 sum_eta sigma_eta` and all six noninvertibles have dimension u.
The six invertibles have classes `G/H=C6`, represented by `(r,0)`.
Their action on sigma_eta is free and transitive. The identities
`beta_g tau=tau` and `tau beta_g=Ad(U_g)tau` give the inversion mixed rule.

Here are direct rank calculations proving the square rule, including its
multiplicity. Put `p=p_0`, `sigma=sigma_0`, and `P=p tau(p)`.

For a fixed invertible class r=0,...,5,
`Hom(beta_(r,0),tau^2)` has complete basis
`v_(x,t)=S_((r,0)+h(x,t)) lambda_h(x,t)`.
Left multiplication by p averages x over C12, separately at t=0,1.
On the t-summand, multiplication by tau(p) averages with character
`l -> zeta^(rl) (-1)^(lt)`. Thus P has rank one exactly when
`r+6t=0 mod12`. There is precisely one such pair: r=t=0.

For each eta=0,...,5, a complete basis of
`Hom(sigma_eta,tau^2)` is
`w_(g,t)=T_g T^t p_eta`, g=(a,b) in G, t=0,1.
The p factor selects precisely

```
b-a=eta+6t mod12.
```

There are twelve g for each t. On the selected subspace, the q, B,
and projective-character factors cancel because q|L=1; tau(p) is the
average of the twelve shifts `g -> g-(l,l)`. There is one free orbit
at each t, so P has rank two for every eta. Therefore

```
sigma^2=1+2 sum_(r in C6) beta_r sigma,
beta_r sigma=sigma beta_(-r).
```

In particular sigma is self-dual; the inversion rule implies every
beta_r sigma is self-dual. The global FP dimension is
`6(1+u^2)=444+72sqrt(37)`, exactly that of R8_3_1768.

## 4. The pointed associator is the required nontrivial one

Choose the generator beta=beta_(1,0). Then

```
beta^6=Ad(lambda_(6,0)),
beta(lambda_(6,0))=B((1,0),(6,0))^(-1)lambda_(6,0)
                  =-lambda_(6,0).
```

The cyclic associator invariant is therefore -1, the element 3 of
`H^3(C6,C*)=Z/6` (inversion gives the same class). Its restriction to C2
is nontrivial and its restriction to C3 is trivial. This exactly matches
the anomaly forced by the previously computed R1768--GH6 based bimodule.
Unlike the cyclic C144 source, the present source has a suitable twisted
subgroup of order24. The previous obstruction to a nondegenerate C2^2
subgroup of a two-generator order144 group does not apply: H is C12xC2,
and its alternating form has a six-element radical.

## 5. Precise remaining task

The construction establishes the twisted GH precursor and its entire
based ring. It does not establish a connected separable multiplication
on `1+sigma`, nor the actual R1768 Morita equivalence. The old two based
module completions become candidates over an actual category, but this
is not sufficient for promotion of R8_3_1768 from unresolved.

There is a useful obstruction to a tempting shortcut. The canonical
near-group algebra on `1+rho` transports to the ordinary matrix algebra
`End(sigma)`, whose underlying object is
`A=1+2 sum_r beta_r sigma=sigma^2`, of dimension `u^2`.
The independent audit gives the full-corner proof of this algebra
identification; it is stronger than a dimension comparison.

The same A cannot be the internal end of a simple in either of the old
rank-eight module completions. The six small module simples have internal
ends `1+beta_(2g) sigma`, of dimension `1+u`. Each large module simple
has three pointed summands (its C3 stabilizer) and coefficients 5,6
alternating on the six noninvertibles, of dimension `3+33u`.
Neither internal-end vector equals A. A primitive idempotent in
`End(F(rho))` does not automatically cut out a subalgebra `1+sigma`.
The matrix-algebra route therefore gives the original category again.

Izumi arXiv:1512.04288, Theorem12.17 treats a nondegenerate elementary
two-group special case. The direct calculation above supplies the
degenerate twisted case needed here in the explicit RW algebraic model.
Grossman--Izumi, Quantum Topology13, section6, has related de-equivariant
rules, but its Q-system normalization is not imported into this proof.
