## Classification result

**One of the eleven rings is now modeled: R10_1_189. No new ring is excluded; ten remain unresolved.**

The new unitary construction is the full fusion subcategory generated by
`g=tau_2` and `x=tau_5 F(010)` in the category of modules over the actual
conformal-inclusion algebra for `SU(4)_6 ⊂ SU(10)_1`. It has relations

```
g^5=1,   gx=xg,   x*=x,
x^2=1+(g+g^2+g^3+g^4)x.
```

Existence comes from the known conformal inclusion and its module category.
The argument uses ground-state branching, two small Pieri products and
dimension exhaustion. All 1,000 census tensor coefficients match under the
explicit basis map. The categorical construction and its rank-fourteen
modular-category corollary were independently audited.

Read `rank10/rank10_conformal_model.pdf` (five pages), its LaTeX source,
and `rank10/CONFORMAL_MODEL.md`. The model is an identification inside
known constructions, not a claim that a new tensor category was discovered.

## Other new theoretical results

### Rank eight

The split near-group input from Radchenko--Wheeler, with group `C12 x C12`
and multiplicity 144, admits an explicit twisted crossed-product
construction. It produces an actual complex fusion category with
`GH(C6,2)` rules and pointed associator class 3. These are exactly the
parameters required by the earlier R1768 route. This fills one actual
existence gap; it does not yet give R1768.

The remaining step is a connected separable algebra on `1+sigma`. Its
associativity problem is reduced to two scalar variables in an explicit
linear/quadratic morphism system. The canonical near-group algebra instead
descends to the ordinary matrix algebra `End(sigma)`, and therefore does
not provide this missing step. The cubic-rotation calculation is also
recorded in the independent audit. See `quadratic8/` and
`global/R1768_TWISTED_PRECURSOR_AUDIT.md`.

For R1154, the obstruction now covers arbitrary pointed factors attached
to five specified quantum-group constructions, followed by a connected
etale condensation. This rules out these construction families only.
See `quadratic8/R1154_POINTED_EXTENSION_OBSTRUCTIONS.md`.

### Rank nine

A new general module-functor argument forces a sign in the left/right
stabilizer actions whenever right tensoring by the stabilizing involution
moves another simple object in the same module component. Applied to the
three commutative rank-nine cases, it gives, for every large simple X,

```
nu_3(X) in {-1, -zeta_3, -zeta_3^2}.
```

This argument was independently audited twice. It requires no promotion
of a based-central element to an actual half-braiding. A separate
cochain-independent argument in the D8 Morita dual gives an aggregate
restriction for all four cases, including R1975.

The full discrete projected choices, not just their convex upper bound,
were inserted into the necessary center systems. Exact rational feasible
witnesses remain for all four commutative source/TY-sign possibilities.
They demonstrate the limits of these particular finite relaxations;
they are not models. In the positive-TY R137 branch and negative-TY R1976
branch, exact separating certificates force every projected third
indicator to be 1/2, and consequently force 9 to divide the FS exponent.
The matrix semantics and all required relabellings passed an independent
audit. Details are documented in `rank9/` and
`quartic/AUDIT_DISCRETE_CLIFFORD.md`.

### Rank six and the quartic pair

The even cyclic Clifford criterion is new. All 1,144 prescribed R155
blocks through order 24 have exact nonnegative integer decompositions,
with an independent standard-library replay. Thus this test gives no
R155 exclusion. The R1660 Ising-bimodule route was found to be already
present in the project's manuscript and is not counted as new progress.

For R928/R941, a proper fusion-subcategory inclusion has FP index at
least two. This rules out the SU(4)_8 exceptional module category, whose
index over the target lies strictly between one and two. Further
dimension checks rule out the indicated F4_3/G2_4 direct-subcategory
routes. They do not rule out arbitrary constructions.

## Evidence, scope, and replay

`classification_delta.json` records the sole status change and its exact
source tensor hash. Source profiles in the adjacent
`unresolved11_20261024/inputs/` directory are frozen historical inputs;
their old unresolved status has deliberately not been rewritten.
The earlier research directory is included to preserve its proofs and
dependencies. Its earlier zero-resolution summary is superseded by this
README and the rank-ten proof.

Run `python unresolved11_retry_20261024/verify_retry.py` from the extracted
archive root for the portable exact replays. The theorem proofs remain
necessary: numerical identities or modular data alone never substitute
for categorical existence. The rank-nine necessary-system builders are
included separately with their dependencies; rebuilding those systems
requires SymPy, NumPy, SciPy and python-flint. Their saved certificates
can be replayed using the Python standard library.

This investigation does not establish that every possible theoretical
approach has been exhausted. It supplies one complete categorification,
new categorical restrictions, a realized precursor for another case,
and explicit remaining premises.
