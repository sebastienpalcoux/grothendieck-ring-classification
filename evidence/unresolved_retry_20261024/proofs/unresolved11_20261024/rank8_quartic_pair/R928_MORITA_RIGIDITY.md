## Rank bound proved by a determinant, not assumed

Set B_(i,n)=dim Hom(X_i tensor m,n). Adjunction gives

    (B B^t)_(i,j) = sum_k a_k N_(j,k)^i.

Every column is nonzero: m generates the indecomposable module category.
The columns for m and g m are a and g a. All remaining columns occur in
g-pairs and vanish at source indices0 and7. Group the other source rows in
the three pairs (1,2),(3,4),(5,6). Let u be the three pair sums of one
column and d its three pair differences. Then u>=0, u is nonzero,
|d_i|<=u_i, and u_i=d_i mod2. Conversely these conditions reconstruct the
paired nonnegative columns.

After removing the distinguished two columns, the plus and minus Gram
matrices satisfy P=sum u u^t and M=sum d d^t. In all four cases M=I3 and
det(P)=1. The exact P matrices are

    P0  = [[1,0,0],[0,1,0],[0,0,1]],
    P7  = [[9,10,14],[10,13,18],[14,18,25]],
    P9  = [[13,16,18],[16,21,22],[18,22,25]],
    P14 = [[13,18,20],[18,25,28],[20,28,33]].

Cauchy--Binet says the sum of the squares of all3-by-3 minors of the
integral factor U is one. Exactly one minor has determinant +/-1. Its
three columns form a basis. Replacing any basis column by any extra
column must give determinant zero, so every extra column is zero. Since
all u columns are nonzero, there are exactly three residual column pairs.
Thus every M has rank8, with no imposed rank cutoff.

## Two categorical corollaries

There are exactly four connected separable algebras in C up to algebra
isomorphism. Every such algebra is an internal matrix algebra X tensor X*
for a simple X, because its module category is regular. Right tensoring X
by an invertible object does not change this internal matrix algebra:
(Xg)(Xg)* contracts canonically to XX*, compatibly with multiplication.
The eight simples form four right-g orbits, and their four underlying
internal-end objects are distinct. This gives both existence and uniqueness
of these four algebra classes *within any hypothesized categorification*;
it does not supply that categorification.

Every tensor functor from C to a fusion category is fully faithful onto its
fusion image.
To prove this, replace the target by its fusion image, so F:C->D is
dominant. D is an indecomposable semisimple C-module category and hence is
regular. If its tensor unit corresponds to a simple X of C, uniqueness
of the positive module dimension vector gives FPdim_D(Y')=FPdim_C(Y)/
FPdim_C(X) for corresponding simples. In particular the simple
corresponding to 1_C has dimension 1/FPdim_C(X), which must be at least1.
Thus X is invertible, internalEnd_C(1_D)=1, and induction adjunction makes
F fully faithful. Dominance then makes F an equivalence. This rules out
proper tensor quotients as well as alternative Morita models, without a
normality assumption on the functor.
