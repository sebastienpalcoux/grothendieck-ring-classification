# Pointed anomaly in the cyclic maximal-isotropic orbifold route

This note blocks a particular proposed second de-equivariantization; it
does not exclude R8_3_1768 and does not assert existence of a near-group
source at any newly proposed parameters.

Assume an actual cyclic near-group realization and isotropic crossed
product with the precise endomorphism identities already proved in
`rank9_m2_research/phase5/orbifold_scope/ALGEBRAIC_ORBIFOLD.md`.
Take `G=Z/(m^2)`, the nondegenerate bicharacter
`B(a,b)=exp(2 pi i r ab/m^2)` with `gcd(r,m)=1`, and the maximal isotropic
subgroup `H=mZ/(m^2)`.
The relevant identities are

```
 beta_a beta_b=beta_(a+b),
 Hom(beta_a,beta_b)=C lambda_(b-a)  if b-a in H,
 beta_a(lambda_h)=B(a,h)^(-1) lambda_h,
 lambda_h lambda_k=lambda_(h+k).
```

Consequently the pointed quotient has group `G/H=C_m`, but its
associator is **not** trivial. Choose representatives `0,...,m-1`.
For a product `a+b`, replace `beta_(a+b)` by `beta_([a+b]_m)` using
`lambda_(-m floor((a+b)/m))`. Comparing the two composites for a triple
gives, up to inversion from the choice of associator direction, the
carry cocycle

```
 omega(a,b,c)=exp(2 pi i r a floor((b+c)/m)/m).
```

Its class is a generator of `H^3(C_m,C*)=Z/m`: on the subgroup of order
`d` generated by `h=m/d`, the coboundary-invariant cyclic product is

```
 product_(j=0)^(d-1) omega(h,[jh]_m,h)=exp(2 pi i r/d).
```

This has order `d`. A coboundary has cyclic product one, as is seen by
telescoping its defining two-cochain. Thus the restriction is nontrivial
on **every** nonidentity subgroup. The invariant also directly checks
the standard cyclic-cohomology description without relying on an
implicit identification of generators.

For `m=12`, the C2 subgroup generated by 6 has
`omega(6,6,6)=(-1)^r=-1`. Therefore the pointed quotient does not admit
the algebra `1+beta_6`; in particular, an ordinary second C2
 de-equivariantization cannot be performed. The obstruction is the
module pentagon at a stabilizer, equivalently Ostrik's Example 2.1 in
[arXiv:math/0202130](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0202130).

This is relevant to the dimensional proposal

```
 NG(C144,144) --isotropic C12 quotient--> rank24 quadratic category
             --proposed C2 quotient--> rank12 higher GH candidate.
```

Even conditional on the first near-group category and its specified
realization existing, the proposed second arrow is obstructed. Its
existence cannot be inferred from an order-two element of the quotient
based ring. A nontrivial pointed associator can remain after an isotropic
orbifold.

The original first-arrow theorem for the explicit algebraic models is a
separate dependency: Radchenko–Wheeler, arXiv:2609.21892v1, Section 5.3,
Theorem 10, plus the project's verified algebraic crossed-product proof.
Izumi's [arXiv:1512.04288](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.04288), Theorem 12.5,
is the analytic predecessor. Neither reference is being used here as a
classification of arbitrary spherical near-group categories. In
particular this note makes no assertion that `(C36,72)` is realized or
that the `m=2|G|` version of the needed orbifold has been reconstructed.

`verify_cyclic_anomaly.py` verifies the full cocycle identities modulo
`m`, all subgroup restriction invariants, and the C2 sign for `m=12`.
