\subsection{A convex relaxation of central induction}
\label{subsec:m3-convex-induction}
The following relaxation avoids enumerating all simultaneous choices of
restriction columns for the constituents of $I(\mathbf1)$. It uses the
central-induction and relative-trace identities already established above.
\begin{proposition}[Convex central-induction obstruction]
Let $R$ be a commutative fusion ring, let $D$ be its Frobenius--Perron
dimension, and suppose that its formal codegrees split in a real field $K$
for which complete joint relative traces of the required roots of unity
have been obtained. For each non-Frobenius--Perron character $E$, let
$\mathcal C_E$ be the complete set of possible restriction columns of the
central constituent of dimension $\beta_E=D/f_E$ of $I(\mathbf1)$.
Replace the choice of one column by variables
\[
 y_{E,c}\geq0,\qquad \sum_{c\in\mathcal C_E}y_{E,c}=1.
\]
Retain the Gram, first and second central trace, and tensor-rotation
equations, using $y_{E,c}cc^t$ for the low Gram contribution and
$y_{E,c}c_i\beta_E$ for each low twist moment. Fix the central unit column
separately. For high columns, use every nonnegative integral column
allowed by the full nonunit Gram block and the necessary central-dimension
conditions, paired with every joint relative-trace type.
If, for every admissible second-indicator assignment, the resulting
rational system $Ax=b$, $x\geq0$, has an exact integer separator
$A^tu\geq0$, $b^tu<0$, then $R$ has no pseudo-unitary categorification.
If the complete spherical-character and oriented-sphericalization checks
also succeed, then $R$ has no complex fusion categorification.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A hypothetical pseudo-unitary category selects one actual column from
each $\mathcal C_E$, hence supplies a one-hot solution for the corresponding
simplex variables. Since $R$ is commutative, each character labels a
distinct simple constituent of $I(\mathbf1)$ with multiplicity one and
twist one. Thus its Gram and trace contributions are exactly those stated.
Every actual high column is admitted by the full Gram block, whose
complement is a sum of column squares. The complete relative-trace tables
include every possible twist and cyclic-rotation spectrum. Consequently
the category supplies a nonnegative solution of all the displayed
equations. An exact separator contradicts $u^tAx=u^tb<0$.
The final assertion uses the separately checked complete lifting argument;
the convex relaxation itself does not assert pivotality or unitarity of an
arbitrary fusion category.
\end{proof}
At height four this criterion excludes $R_{8,3,1453}$ generally. The
relaxation has $15\,777$ variables and $127$ equations; all sixteen
second-indicator assignments have exact separators, and the full oriented
lifting succeeds. Three fixed-low specializations reproduce the frozen
Gram, moment, and rotation equations exactly. Known realized controls
$R_{8,3,334}$, $R_{8,3,342}$, and $R_{8,3,475}$ survive. The proof,
implementation, and validation receipts are stored in
\texttt{criteria/GLOBAL\_CONVEX\_INDUCTION.md},
\texttt{criteria/global\_induction.py}, and
\texttt{criteria/global\_specialization\_R8\_3\_1453.json}.
The same relaxation over $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ excludes
$R_{8,3,920}$ generally.  Its height-four system has $97\,768$ variables
and $197$ equations; both admissible second-indicator assignments have
exact separators and complete signed lifting succeeds.  Three fixed-low
specializations, selected from all $6891$ low frames, reproduce $182$
inherited equations each.  See
\texttt{criteria/global\_specialization\_R8\_3\_920.json}.
The biquadratic dimension filters are additionally checked on $324$
exact fixtures against independent rational multiplication-matrix
characteristic polynomials, including the coprime-radicand fields used
in the generalized trace adapter.
\subsection{A uniform split-module polynomial test}
\label{subsec:m3-split-polynomial}
\begin{proposition}[Fixed-point difference-space obstruction]
Let $R$ be a commutative fusion ring, $g$ a nontrivial invertible basis
element of order two fixing at least one basis element, and $X$ a self-dual
object. Let $F$ be the basis elements fixed by $g$, and let $p$ be any
integer polynomial with $p(N_X)=0$. A categorification of $R$ requires a
symmetric integer matrix $S$, indexed by $F$, satisfying
\[
 S_{V,W}\in\{-N_{X,V}^{W},-N_{X,V}^{W}+2,\ldots,N_{X,V}^{W}\},
 \qquad p(S)=0.
\]
Consequently, exhaustive failure of these equations, even after reduction
modulo a prime, excludes a complex fusion categorification.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A simple object fixed by $g$ supplies a rank-one module over the pointed
subcategory generated by $g$, trivializing its associator class. Hence
$A=\mathbf1\oplus g$ is a connected separable algebra. In $\mathcal C_A$,
each fixed simple $V$ splits into two module simples $V_+,V_-$, while each
free $g$-orbit induces one module simple. The induction image is spanned by
the free-orbit simples and the sums $V_++V_-$. The action of self-dual $X$
is symmetric, so its orthogonal complement, spanned by $V_+-V_-$, is
invariant. Induction and symmetry force the block between two fixed pairs
to have the form
$\left(\begin{smallmatrix}u&a-u\\a-u&u\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
where $a=N_{X,V}^{W}$ and $0\leq u\leq a$ is an integer. Its action on
differences has entry $2u-a$. The faithful regular representation makes
$p(N_X)=0$ a ring identity, which holds on every module and hence on this
invariant difference space. This proves the necessary condition.
\end{proof}
Swapping the two labels of a fixed pair conjugates $S$ by a diagonal sign
matrix. On a spanning forest of the odd-entry graph, all entries are
nonzero and can therefore be made positive. This gives a complete sign
reduction. The implementation verifies the source polynomial identity
and every finite case exactly; a separate replay uses unbounded integer
arithmetic and a different spanning forest. It gives new exclusions
$R_{8,3,j}$ for
$j\in\{1019,1057,1067,1197,2392,2836\}$, and independently recovers the
applicable dimension-$84$ and dimension-$280$ obstructions. See
\texttt{noncommutative/SPLIT\_MODULE\_POLYNOMIAL.md} and its source-bound
certificates.
