\subsection{Negative subring transfer and simultaneous quadratic fields}
\label{subsec:m3-transfer-compression}
The new census is first compared with the current, general-scope negative
rank-six and rank-seven catalogues.  Each transfer certificate gives a
dual-closed proper basis subset, verifies product closure, and supplies a
unit-preserving permutation identifying every multiplication coefficient
with the cited negative tensor.  The tensor and its full published decision
are hash bound.  Any categorification would contain this full fusion
subcategory, so the transfer excludes a general complex categorification.
The recovered scan reproduces fourteen such transfers from catalogues of
531 rank-six and 1396 rank-seven negatives of multiplicity at most three.

\begin{proposition}[Simultaneous quadratic compression]
Let $R$ be a fusion ring and $B=\sum_iN_iN_i^t$ its regular Casimir matrix.
For each real quadratic field $L=\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$ appearing among its
irreducible quadratic characteristic-polynomial factors, write
\[
 Q_d=f_d\sum_j m_j\frac{t_j^2-4n_j}{n_j^2},
 \qquad (x^2-t_jx+n_j)^{m_j}\mid\det(xI-B),
\]
where the sum includes exactly the factors generating $L$ and $f_d$ is
the fundamental discriminant of $L$.  Let $r$ be the rank over
$\mathbb F_2$ of all these quadratic squareclasses.  A complex fusion
categorification requires
\[
                 2^{r-1}Q_d\leq4
\]
for every displayed field.  If a categorification would admit a
Galois-conjugate pseudo-unitary form, the stronger bound is
$2^{r-1}Q_d\leq1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The full-$I(\mathbf1)$ compression theorem gives
$[K:L]Q_d\leq1$ for a spherical categorification with dimension field $K$.
On a simple representation $E$, the Casimir eigenvalue is
$(\dim E)f_E$ and its regular multiplicity is $(\dim E)^2$; hence the
displayed spectral multiplicities already give the correct contribution,
also for a noncommutative ring.  Each formal codegree, and therefore each
Casimir eigenvalue, lies in $K$.  The compositum of the displayed quadratic
fields has degree $2^r$, so $[K:L]\geq2^{r-1}$.
For an arbitrary category, pass to its sphericalization.  Its original
fusion-algebra sector has Casimir $2B$, dividing the known reciprocal-square
compression contribution by four.  This proves the first bound without
assuming pivotality.  A Galois-conjugate pseudo-unitary realization is
spherical and yields the stronger bound directly.
\end{proof}
The implementation checks the stronger hypothesis by the existing
weak-integrality or two-distinct-Frobenius--Perron-dimensions reduction.
It computes every polynomial factor, rational ratio, squareclass rank, and
gate from the source tensor.  In particular a scoped spherical obstruction
alone is never promoted to general nonexistence.  The proof source and
replay are \texttt{criteria/COMPRESSION\_AND\_TRANSFER.md} and
\texttt{criteria/quadratic\_compression.py}.

\subsection{Weakly integral quadratic trace fields}
\label{subsec:m3-weak-quadratic}
The relative-trace and tensor-rotation engines are extended to the case
where the object dimensions generate a real quadratic field but the total
Frobenius--Perron dimension $D$ is rational.  Weak integrality supplies
pseudo-unitarity.  The prime support of the Frobenius--Schur exponent is
the prime support of $D$; the quadratic conductor must be supported there.
Write every dimension in the basis $1,\sqrt d$, retaining both coordinates
even though $D=(D,0)$.  The central first- and second-moment and cyclic
rotation equations remain identities in this field, with the same
complete conductor-bounded relative-trace tables.  The extension uses no
division by the irrational coordinate of $D$.

All restriction-column choices and second-indicator assignments are
retained.  Numerical optimization only proposes a separator; exact integer
replay verifies every inequality and the negative right-side pairing.
The complete signed lifting remains required outside the independently
checked general-to-positive gates.  The additive adapter is
\texttt{criteria/weak\_integral\_quadratic.py}; the existing frozen engines
are left unchanged.
