# Rank-eight multiplicity-three continuation

The uploaded cumulative census has 4,500 rings: 96 of exact multiplicity 1,
877 of exact multiplicity 2 and 3,527 of exact multiplicity 3. Historical
multiplication tables remain an identical ordered subsequence of the new
source, but are not a contiguous prefix. The existing 973 decisions and all
historical identifiers remain unchanged. New IDs `R8_3_i` are assigned by
lexicographically sorting flattened exact-multiplicity-three tensors.

The immutable source is
`supplements/rank8/data/rank8_m3_tables.txt.gz`. The uncompressed SHA256 is
`7baaa0a2018e11a0464c3f19da9030195421f04a73806c22363cf9d5eeb92a84`.
The historical decision SHA256 is
`7f50213174f1020bb9d90d5981892c332448cf909a56bf60b29e1143228f3f24`.
`source_transport.json` connects current input lines to unchanged IDs and
historical source lines. Completeness of enumeration belongs to the
supplied census and its independent all-permutation verification; the
categorification pipeline checks the supplied tensors and its own claims.

The baseline verifies every tensor's unit, duality, Frobenius reciprocity
and associativity and applies known exact exclusion criteria. It yields
3250 exclusions and 277 residuals, with no timeout or error. Every exclusion
contains an exact witness; `verify_m3.py` rechecks all 3,527 tensors and all
3,250 witnesses. It makes no positive categorification assertion.

Subsequent decisions come from the regenerated, source-bound
`criteria/accepted_exclusions.json`, root scalar/weighted certificates and
`models/consolidated_models.json`. The default `finalize_m3.py` writes a
preview, retaining unresolved status whenever there is no accepted general
obstruction and no complete model. Unitary-only obstructions are recorded
separately. Ordinary finite-group model searches, weakly-group-theoretical
Frobenius diagnostics and bounded quantum-family searches have their stated
scopes; their failures are not general nonexistence claims.

The residual effort inventory records completed known and new exclusion
attempts, explicit construction attempts, numerical/heuristic searches,
implementation ineligibility, timeouts and interruptions separately.
`residual_audit.json` supplies operational coverage flags for research
review. Its coverage label does not establish existence, novelty or an
automatic mathematical definition of an exotic category.

Reproducible entrypoints from the repository root:

- `python supplements/rank8/software/verify_m3.py`
- `python supplements/rank8/software/finalize_m3.py`
- `python supplements/rank8/software/verify_m3_continuation.py`
- `python -O supplements/rank8/software/verify_m3_continuation.py`
- `python supplements/rank8/software/finalize_m3.py --publish`

The last command refuses publication until both full mathematical replay
receipts match the current ledger, all source/proof hashes, and their
section logs. Optimized invocation explicitly reexecutes inherited proof
routines with assertions active; new checks use explicit exceptions.
Custom `--output` locations isolate their logs and do not overwrite the
publication logs. `--metadata-only` checks a published result against both
full receipts; it is not a mathematical replay.

The D84 structural proof and its finite group audit are in adjacent files.
`literature/f660_match.py` independently transfers all 512 printed constants
of the published F660 to **R8_3_722**, preventing misidentification with the
unresolved R8_3_731. The one-spectrum criterion is applied to all 277
baseline residuals using an exact C++ search and direct Python witness
checks.

The workspace loss during this session required source reconstruction and
fresh replay. No earlier mathematical receipt has been recreated from its
remembered outcome. The committed receipts, when present, are generated by
actual completed executions of these recovered sources.
