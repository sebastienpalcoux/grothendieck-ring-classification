# Exact unitarity reduction

Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik, *Weakly group-theoretical and solvable fusion
categories*, arXiv:0809.3031, prove weak group-theoreticity for integer
dimension below84, solvability for dimensions with at most two prime factors,
and closure of the weakly group-theoretical class under extensions
(Proposition4.1). Galindo–Hong–Rowell, *Generalized and quasi-localizations of
braid group representations*, Theorem5.20, prove that this class is completely
unitary. The latter theorem concerns the tensor category, not merely a chosen
braiding.

The script checks one of these dimension hypotheses exactly, or checks a
faithful C2 grading whose neutral part has integer dimension below84. The
grading is checked on every nonzero structure constant. Its neutral dimension
and the ratio two are computed in the exact positive dimension field.

Only after this reduction may an exact negative primary-three quadratic form
exclude general categorification. Without the reduction the same quadratic
form excludes unitarity only. In particular, no assertion that all weakly
integral fusion categories are unitarizable is used.
