# A unified fixed-point split-module polynomial obstruction

Let R be a commutative fusion ring, g a nonidentity invertible basis element
of order two fixing at least one basis element, and X a self-dual object.
Suppose R has a complex fusion categorification.

The pointed subcategory generated by g has trivial associator class: a simple
fixed by g supplies a rank-one module for Vec(C2,omega), whose module pentagon
trivializes omega. Thus A=1+g is a connected separable algebra. In C_A, each
two-element g-orbit induces one simple, while each fixed simple V induces two
distinct simples V+ and V-. The induction adjunction gives an endomorphism
algebra of dimension two; semisimplicity forces two multiplicity-one summands,
each with underlying object V. Every module simple occurs in an induction.

The action matrix M_X is symmetric by rigidity and self-duality. Induction
is a module functor, so its image, spanned by orbit simples and V++V-, is
invariant. Its orthogonal complement, spanned by V+-V-, is invariant too.
For fixed V,W and a=N[X,V,W], the corresponding two-by-two block has both
row and column sums a, hence is [[u,a-u],[a-u,u]], where 0<=u<=a is integral.
The difference-space matrix S_X is therefore symmetric and its entry is in
{-a,-a+2,...,a}. A polynomial p(N_X)=0 gives p(X)=0 because the regular
representation is faithful. Hence p(M_X)=0 and p(S_X)=0.

The finite search uses the squarefree characteristic polynomial only after
explicitly verifying its value at N_X is zero. Swapping V+ and V- conjugates
S by a diagonal sign matrix. Every edge of the odd-bound graph is nonzero;
its signs can be made positive on a spanning forest. This reduction retains
at least one representative of every possible matrix. Discovery evaluates
p(S) modulo1009. Failure for every allowed matrix modulo1009 implies failure
over the integers. Batched signed64 arithmetic is exact because each reduced
matrix product accumulator is at most8*1008^2+1008. A modular survivor or an
enumeration budget is explicitly inconclusive.

The independent verifier reconstructs the blocks, uses a breadth-first forest,
and computes all polynomials with unbounded Python integers, without numpy
or modular reduction. Both verifiers retain all checks under python -O.
The new cases are1019,1057,1067,1197,2392,2836; the test also recovers76,735,
738,1805,1051,2415. The largest negative enumeration has34992 matrices.

The categorical inputs are induction/forgetful adjunction, pointed module
categories classified by subgroup/two-cochain, and module rigidity; see
EGNO, Tensor Categories, Chapter7, and Ostrik, arXiv:math/0111139.
No braiding, pivotality, sphericality or unitarity assumption is made.
