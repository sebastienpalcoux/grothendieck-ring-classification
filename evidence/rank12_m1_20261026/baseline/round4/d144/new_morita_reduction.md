## 1. The dimension24 subcategory has a pointed regular-algebra dual

Let B be the original subcategory on {1,z,E2,P,Q}, with Rep(S4) fusion rules and dimension24. We do not assume it is tensor-equivalent to Rep(S4). Let M be the original odd component restricted to B. Its four simples have equal normalized module dimension sqrt6, and their B-action is indecomposable. Both facts follow from the exact multiplication tensor.

The restriction of M to E=Rep(S3) has two blocks of rank2. In each, z exchanges the two simples and E2 acts by I+swap. By the classification of Rep(S3) module categories, this is Rep(C2); there are no nontrivial multipliers on a cyclic stabilizer. Under Morita equivalence E--Vec_S3 by A, each such block becomes Vec_(S3/C2), a transitive three-object Vec_S3 module.

Let B'=B_AA and M' be the transported module. Thus M' has six simple objects, the canonical Vec_S3 subcategory has two orbits of size3, and all six normalized dimensions are2. For explicit scaling, each new simple has underlying original module object of dimension2sqrt6; the Morita normalization divides by sqrt(FPdim A)=sqrt6.

The previous C2 then C3 condensation shows that B' can only have type1^24 or1^6,3^2. The intermediate possibility1^15,3 is impossible because the pointed dimension15 does not divide24. Suppose B' has type1^6,3^2. Then the stabilizer of each simple of M' under all its invertibles is the C2 stabilizer just computed. Its internal endomorphism algebra has dimension2²=4 and exactly two invertible summands. The remaining dimension2 cannot be supplied by objects of dimension3. Contradiction.

Hence B' is pointed of dimension24. Write B'=Vec_G^omega. The canonical subgroup H=S3 remains embedded.

## 2. The pointed group is S4, and omega is trivial on A4

The original B is the group-theoretical dual C(G,omega,H,1). Its identity double coset contributes the three H-irreducibles of dimensions1,1,2. Its two remaining simples have dimension3. Their supports must constitute one nonidentity double coset of size18 with intersection subgroup of order2. Consequently H acts transitively on the three other cosets of G/H: G acts doubly transitively on four points.

The kernel of this action is normal in H=S3. The possible doubly transitive images have orders12 or24, so a nontrivial kernel would have order2, impossible as a normal subgroup of S3. Thus G=S4 and H is a point stabilizer.

The original three dimension-four objects form another indecomposable B-module N. Its restriction to E has z=I and E2 equal to the off-diagonal adjacency matrix on three points; with equal dimensions it is Rep(C3). Transporting by A gives a rank2 Vec_S4 module, so its stabilizer is A4. The defining cochain of this module trivializes omega|A4. In particular the pointed subcategory on the normal V4 is genuinely untwisted Vec_V4.

The odd transported module M' corresponds to a subgroup of order4 in S4. Its S3 orbit pattern3+3 rules out transitive C4 and the normal V4; its subgroup is a nonnormal V4 generated by two disjoint transpositions. Both possible H²(V4,C*) choices remain allowed. No cochain choice is discarded here.

## 3. The full Morita dual has type1^24,2^12,(sqrt2)^36

Let D be the full original category condensed by A, so FPdim D=144. The integral part has24 invertibles and the remaining dimension48 comes from the original W sector. The previously enumerated C3-double-orbit possibilities are twelve dimension-two simples, or three dimension-two simples and one dimension-six simple. The latter is impossible: the group of24 invertibles acts on the three dimension-two objects, but an orbit has size at least24/4=6. Thus the integral type is1^24,2^12.

The odd part initially has type(sqrt2)^(9u),(3sqrt2)^(4-u). A sqrt2 simple has stabilizer2, so its S4 orbit has size12. Therefore u=0 or4.

We use the following slightly stronger stabilizer lemma. If a finite subgroup L of invertibles fixes a simple X, chosen isomorphisms l tensor X=X define a rank-one Vec_L^omega module and a matching cochain psi. The corresponding connected twisted regular algebra A_L^psi acts on X. The actual relative tensor product X* tensor_(A_L^psi) X has dimension FPdim(X)²/|L|. This quotient is therefore an algebraic integer. Cyclicity is unnecessary when the algebra cochain may be chosen from X; it was needed earlier only to prescribe the untwisted regular algebra.

If u=0, the S4-action on four odd objects cannot have an orbit of size1 (stabilizer24 exceeds dimension18). An orbit of size2 has stabilizer12 and is impossible by18/12=3/2. An orbit of size3 would leave a forbidden singleton. Thus the left S4 action is the natural faithful transitive action on four points. The commuting right S4 action must then be trivial, because that permutation action has trivial centralizer. This would give a right stabilizer of order24, again exceeding18. Hence u=0 is impossible and u=4.

## 4. The adjoint subcategory is exactly Vec_V4

The following chain has been independently audited; its exact module-action premises are checked by verify_reductions.py.

The odd part of D is an indecomposable Vec_S4-bimodule category. Indeed, the original odd B-bimodule was already indecomposable as a left B-module, and Morita transport on both sides preserves bimodule indecomposability. The S4 left action on its36 sqrt2 objects has three orbits of size12. The right S4 action must act transitively on these three orbits. Its image is S3, with kernel the normal V4 (S4 has no quotient C3). The same holds with left and right reversed.

A stabilizer L in S4 x S4 of an odd simple has order16. Its two projections have order8 and hence are Sylow D8 subgroups. Each projection kernel is an order-two normal subgroup of D8, hence its center. Thus every left/right stabilizer of a sqrt2 object is generated by a double transposition in the normal V4.

Every dimension-two simple Y is contained in Z tensor X* for some sqrt2 simples X,Z, because the odd component is an invertible bimodule over the integral part. Then YY* is a subobject of

    Z (X*X) Z* = ZZ* + Z g Z*,

where g is a double transposition. The normal V4 preserves every left S4-orbit of odd objects, so Zg=hZ for some invertible h. Both terms are pointed. Thus YY* is pointed for every dimension-two Y; the same was already true for every sqrt2 simple. Therefore D_ad is pointed.

Its subgroup K is normal in S4 and contains all three double transpositions, hence V4. The stabilizer of a dimension-two object now has order4. A subgroup of order4 contained in A4 must be its normal V4. Any other order4 subgroup contains an odd permutation, whose normal closure together with V4 is S4. Thus K=V4 orS4.

If K=S4, every odd universal grading component is an invertible Vec_S4-bimodule category with twelve sqrt2 simples. Its commuting left and right S4 actions are transitive, which forces their stabilizers to be normal subgroups of S4. But those stabilizers are order-two double-transposition subgroups, not normal in S4. Contradiction. Consequently D_ad=Vec_V4.

## 5. A finite extension-theory reduction

The universal grading group U has order36. Its homomorphism into BrPic(Vec_V4) is injective: the kernel consists of regular bimodule components, hence lies among the pointed grades K0=S4/V4=S3; on K0 the induced conjugation action on V4 is faithful.

The standard identification BrPic(Vec_V4)=O^+(4,2)=(S3 x S3) semidirect C2 gives order72. U is therefore an index-two subgroup. The subgroup of pointed bimodule classes is diag(S3) x C2, order12. Requiring exactly six pointed grades leaves two possible index-two subgroups:

    U_base=S3 x S3;
    U_alt={(a,b,epsilon): sign(a)sign(b)=(-1)^epsilon}.

The third index-two subgroup contains all twelve pointed classes and is impossible here. The normal odd-order subgroup C3² has quotient C2² in U_base and C4 in U_alt. These statements now have a standalone exact replay in verify_brpic.py and brpic_replay.json. The quadratic-space convention is the orthogonal sum of two anisotropic F2² planes, q(x,y)=[x!=0]+[y!=0]. This convention must not be silently mixed with the determinant-matrix coordinates, in which the same abstract parabolic has a different presentation.

## 6. Existence of alternate extensions, without identifying their reverse fusion rings

The extension-theory input is Etingof--Nikshych--Ostrik, *Fusion categories and homotopy theory*, arXiv:0909.3140v2, Corollary1.2 and Theorem1.3. Write M=Inv Z(Vec_V4)=F2^4 with the actual orthogonal action used in verify_brpic.py. The normal subgroup N=C3² has no fixed vectors in M. Since |N| is odd, averaging kills its higher cohomology on M, and the Lyndon--Hochschild--Serre spectral sequence gives H^i(U,M)=0 for every i>=0, for either retained U. Thus O3 vanishes and the multiplication system is unique up to its usual equivalence.

The known U_base source is concrete: if F is an S3-crossed three-fermion triality category, the Morita dual of its gauging F^S3 by Fun(S3) is F semidirect S3. Its grading group is S3 semidirect_Ad S3, identified with S3 x S3 by (g,h)->(gh,h). The two factors act on the two anisotropic planes of Z(Vec_V4), so its restriction to N is the same N->BrPic homomorphism used above. Equivalently, the N restriction is F_C3 semidirect C3. This binds the reference restriction categorically, not merely by a dimension count.

For U_alt, restrict O4 to N. The restriction is zero because the preceding actual U_base extension restricts to N, and H²(N,M)=0 makes the multiplication system unique there. A Sylow2 subgroup of U_alt is cyclic C4; H4(C4,C*)=0, so the restriction of O4 there is also zero. Restriction followed by transfer multiplies by the subgroup index. The N restriction kills the 3-primary part, and the C4 restriction kills the 2-primary part. Thus O4=0 and actual U_alt extensions of Vec_V4 exist.

The pointed grades are H=diag(S3), with faithful natural action on V4. Their pointed group is S4: H²(S3,V4)=0 by the same normal-C3 argument, so the extension of V4 by S3 splits uniquely. In particular there is a point-stabilizer subgroup H0=S3 in the actual invertible group.

The associator choices form an H³(U_alt,C*) torsor. Its 2-primary part is Z/4, inflated from U_alt/N=C4. Restriction to H0's C2 is reduction mod2. Its 3-primary part is the invariant line spanned by x²+y² in H4(BC3²,Z)=F3{x²,xy,y²}; the quarter-turn action sends (x,y) to (-y,x). Restriction to H0's diagonal C3 sends x²+y² to2x², hence is an isomorphism. Therefore restriction H³(U_alt,C*) -> H³(S3,C*) is surjective with kernel of order2. Any initial associator can be twisted to trivialize its H0 restriction, and the admissible marked associator classes form a two-element torsor. Each then has an actual twisted-regular S3 algebra; H²(S3,C*)=0 removes a further algebra-cochain choice.

This establishes precursor existence and existence of reverse algebras. The unique admissible 3-primary parameter is an affine statement, whose absolute identification requires the next argument. The complete reverse tensor is identified separately in Section10.

### A Galois anchor for the affine 3-primary parameter

The following argument supplies the missing absolute origin and has passed two independent audits, including a fresh primary-source check saved in ../c6/audit_d144_galois_anchor.md. Choose a field automorphism sigma of Qbar which fixes Q(zeta8) and inverts zeta3. No claim is made that sigma has order2 on Qbar. The neutral category Vec_V4, its invertible bimodule classes, and their marking are fixed by sigma: the bimodule classes can be represented using subgroups and order-two cocycles with values +/-1. All categories may be defined over Qbar by rigidity.

For the fixed N=C3²->BrPic map, the extension classes form a torsor under H³(N,C*)=(C3)^3, since H²(N,M)=0. Galois acts affinely on this torsor, with linear part multiplication by-1. Therefore this affine action has exactly one fixed point: solving v=-v+t is possible and unique because2 is invertible modulo3.

Consider the actual known m=0 triality source restricted to N. Its Galois conjugate is another U_base-restrictable N-extension. The difference class therefore lies in the span of x²,y² (the U_base/N=C2² invariants). Its restriction to the first C3 axis is fixed by sigma: the complete intrinsic-triality F-symbols at m=0 in Teo--Hughes--Fradkin TableVI, or Lu--Sun arXiv:2208.06077 Section6.2/Table4, belong to Q(i,sqrt2). In particular the apparent quaternion exponential is (I+A1+A2+A3)/2, with no hidden cube root of unity. Thus the x² coefficient of the difference is zero. The canonical reverse diagonal C3 subgroup has trivial associator before and after Galois conjugation, forcing the y² coefficient to vanish too. Hence this known m=0 N-extension is the unique sigma-fixed point. Notice that no field-of-definition assertion about the full crossed-action tensorators was needed.

Here the fixation is marked: the source first-axis inclusion is literally F_C3 -> F semidirect S3, X -> (X,1). The common integral grading subgroup is U_int=N semidirect C2, where C2 inverts both C3 factors. ## 8. Exhaustion of the U_base branch

The following scalar-twist argument has been independently audited. For U_base=S3 x S3, the restriction kernel H³(U_base,C*) -> H³(diag S3,C*) has order12: its 2-primary part is C2² and its 3-primary part is C3. The six actual triality gaugings indexed by m=0,1,2 and s=0,1 provide two marked extension classes for each m. Distinct m are detected on the first C3 axis. The two s choices are detected by the Frobenius--Schur sign of a first-axis order-two defect (Teo--Hughes--Fradkin Eq6.15), while their full fusion tensors agree (Eq6.53 is independent of s).

It remains to verify that central zests provide the other two classes for each m without leaving U_base. Put eta(a,b)=sign(a) and epsilon(a,b)=sign(a)+sign(b), valued in F2. Twist the precursor associator by the normalized cup-product cocycle

    omega(u,v,w)=(-1)^[eta(u) epsilon(v) epsilon(w)].

It restricts trivially to H=diag S3. It equals1 whenever two inputs lie in H, so the A_H-bimodule objects and their left/right associativity equations are unchanged. The balanced tensor quotient is unchanged because omega(u,h,v)=1, and its right action is unchanged because omega(u,v,h)=1. The left action on a product of degrees u,v gains omega(h,u,v)=sign(h) exactly when both degrees are odd. Thus the reverse dual tensor product is precisely the central zest by the sign charge z. The charge has its actual central lift since sign|H extends to the character eta of U_base.

For each m, the s difference is a nonzero order-two extension class, while the zest class cannot equal it: the s choices have equal fusion rings, and the zest gives a different fusion ring, as verified by all1728 structure constants. Therefore the two classes generate the full four-element 2-primary kernel. Together with the three m classes they exhaust all12 admissible marked U_base extensions. Their reverse fusion rings are exactly R12_1_79,R12_1_80,R12_1_322,R12_1_323. The m=1 and m=2 categories have the same based ring, but remain distinct marked extension classes; this distinction is retained in the counting argument.

Consequently none of the four target rings can arise in the U_base branch.
