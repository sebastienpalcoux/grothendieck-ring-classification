# The full bimodule stabilizer forces the R351 neutral associator to vanish

Let G=C2^3 and let M be the actual invertible neutral bimodule component
of a hypothetical categorification of R12_1_351. It has two simple objects.
For one such simple X the original tensor gives left stabilizer
{0,9,10,11} and right stabilizer {0,7,8,11}. Both are index-two subgroups,
and they are distinct.

Write their quotient characters as p_L,p_R:G -> C2. Left and right
translations are the nontrivial transitive two-point actions. Consequently
the stabilizer of X for the joint G x G action is exactly the fiber product

    S={(g,h): p_L(g)=p_R(h)}.

The bimodule category is a module category over Vec_G^omega tensor its
opposite. Its one-dimensional identifications at X give a genuine cochain
trivializing the restriction of the product associator to S. In particular,
restriction to every cyclic order-two subgroup generated by a pair (g,h)
in S is trivial. Write q(g) in F2 for the cyclic restriction of omega at g.
The opposite-associator restriction has the same order-two sign, so this
gives q(g)=q(h) whenever p_L(g)=p_R(h).

Taking h=0 shows q vanishes on ker p_L, and taking g=0 shows it vanishes
on ker p_R. The matched nonzero fibers show it is a constant lambda on
both complementary cosets. Therefore q=lambda*p_L=lambda*p_R. Since the
two characters differ, lambda=0 and q vanishes on all G.

Finally cyclic restrictions detect H^3(C2^3,C*) injectively. The usual
three type-I, three type-II, and one type-III generators have restriction
functions x_i, x_i*x_j, and x_1*x_2*x_3 on G. These seven zero-constant
Boolean monomials form a basis of all functions on G vanishing at zero.
Thus all seven restriction signs vanish only for the zero cohomology class.

Hence the neutral category is tensor equivalent to untwisted Vec(C2^3).
This conclusion uses the full joint stabilizer rather than only separate
left and right restrictions, and includes all matching bimodule cochains.
