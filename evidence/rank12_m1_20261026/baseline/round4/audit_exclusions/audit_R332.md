# Independent audit of the new R12_1_332 exclusion

Conclusion: the proposed exclusion is valid for arbitrary complex
categorifications. The proof below and `audit_R332.py` independently
check its source, action, necessary fusion rules and norm obstruction.
The script passes under `python -O`. This result is separate from the
425 inherited exclusions summarized in `results.json`.

## Categorical action: no trivial-associator assumption

The full input has invertibles generated by alpha=input3 and b=input5,
with alpha³=b²=1 and b alpha b=alpha⁻¹. The actual fusion subcategory on
(1,alpha,alpha²,rho,alpha rho,alpha² rho), where rho=input9, has
rho alpha=alpha⁻¹rho and rho²=1+rho+alpha rho+alpha²rho.
Conjugation by b fixes rho and swaps each of the other two pairs. All
these identities and the closure of the subset are checked directly.

For clarity, the coherent action is not obtained by asserting an algebra
1+b or a tensor functor Vec(C2) into the original category. Use the
monoidal groupoid of invertible objects. Conjugation sends u to the
tensor autoequivalence A_u(X)=u tensor X tensor u*, and an isomorphism
f:u→v to f tensor id_X tensor (f⁻¹)*. Canonical rigidity isomorphisms
identify A_u A_v with A_(u tensor v), coherently. Every scalar
automorphism f=lambda id_u maps to the identity, since the two outside
scalar factors cancel.

Choose representatives and multiplication maps for a finite subgroup
of invertible classes. Their two triple products differ by a scalar
after the ambient associator is included. Applying A kills this scalar.
Thus the multiplication maps induce monoidal compositors satisfying
the group-action coherence equation exactly, even if the original
pointed associator is nontrivial. Applying this to <b> and restricting
to the preserved subcategory yields a genuine C2 action. In particular
the possible associator value −1 on (b,b,b) causes no obstruction.

The entire original ring has exactly two positive FP values, 1 and
d=(3+sqrt(13))/2, verified by exact multiplication modulo d²−3d−1 and
rational root isolation. Published Schopieray Lemma 3.1 therefore permits
replacing any putative category by its pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate.
The neutral subcategory is pseudo-unitary. Its canonical positive
pivotal structure is preserved by each tensor autoequivalence by
uniqueness, hence descends to a positive spherical structure on the
equivariantization. No assertion of unitarity is used.

## Exhaustive fusion consequences

For a C2 action, each fixed simple has two equivariant lifts, because
H²(C2,C*)=0, and each two-element orbit gives one simple. Label the
equivariant simples (1,g,U,X,Y,V), forgetting to
(1,1,alpha+alpha²,rho,rho,alpha rho+alpha²rho). The sign object g lies
in the central Rep(C2), so g²=1, Y=gX, gU=U and gV=V.

The forgetful images and duality force U²=1+g+U and UX=XU=V. Therefore
UV=X+Y+V. Initially X²=a+W+V for a in {1,g} and W in {X,Y}; these
are all possible coefficient allocations. If a=g, duality exchanges
X and Y. But Y²=(gX)²=X², whereas taking duals would exchange the
unique X/Y summand of X². This is impossible. Thus a=1 and both X,Y
are selfdual. Relabeling them makes W=X.

The ring is generated by g,U,X, which commute, and every other product
now follows. The independently reconstructed complete table passes
associativity and every coefficient of the forgetful-ring map. This
also shows no choice of action compositor or equivariant structures
was silently fixed to remove an alternative ring.

For the exclusion one needs even less than the complete table. In the
central idempotent summand e=(1−g)/2, U and V vanish, Y=−X, and the
two-dimensional algebra generated by eX satisfies (eX)²=e+eX.
Consequently the full ring has a character
(1,−1,0,phi,−phi,0), with phi²=1+phi. Its formal codegrees are
f=5±sqrt(5). The code checks every character product directly.

## Contradiction

The equivariantization has positive global dimension
D=2(3+3d²)=12+18d=39+9sqrt(13), of quadratic norm 468. Each codegree
f above has quadratic norm 20. The fields Q(sqrt(13)) and Q(sqrt(5))
are distinct, so in their biquadratic compositum

    Norm(D/f)=468²/20²=13689/25.

This is not an integer. Ostrik Theorem 2.13 gives a center simple of
dimension D/f in any spherical categorification of this ring; its
dimension must be an algebraic integer, contradiction. This excludes
the putative original R12_1_332 category. Matching the equivariant
table to the prior R6_4_260 census is a useful cross-check but is not
a premise, and no old rank-six signed-exhaustion certificate is used.

The sources checked are Schopieray, published Lemma 3.1,
<https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12789>, and Ostrik, Theorem 2.13,
<https://arxiv.org/html/1309.4822v1>. The coherent-conjugation argument
is explicitly proved above. The orbit/stabilizer description is also
given in Burciu–Natale, *Fusion rules of equivariantizations of fusion
categories*, <https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.6625>.
