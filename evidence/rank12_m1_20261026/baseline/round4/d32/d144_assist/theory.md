Since zWi=Wi, every 2x2 block of Wi is constant. The equations (I+Z)Wi=Wj+Wk force W0=W1=W2=:W. Let P_+ and P_- denote the restrictions of P to the two-dimensional +1 and -1 eigenspaces of Z. In the bases given by pair sums and differences these are integer symmetric matrices. From P^2=2I+Z+(I+Z)P,

P_+^2=3I+2P_+, and P_-^2=I.

Since P_+ is nonnegative with row sum three it is [[a,3-a],[3-a,a]], and its second eigenvalue 2a-3 is either 3 or -1. Thus a is 3 or 1. Also P_- is an integer symmetric orthogonal matrix, hence a signed permutation matrix.

Therefore a=1. The diagonal entries of P_- are odd, so each is +/-1 and its off-diagonal entries vanish. The equation PW=3W forces W=J4, the all-ones matrix.
