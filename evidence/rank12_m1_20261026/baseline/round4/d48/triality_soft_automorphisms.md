# Soft automorphisms of the non-group-theoretical dimension-twelve categories

This lemma addresses a categorical branch. It does not itself classify or exclude any census ring. It needs independent review before being combined with the parent argument that a particular dimension-48 category is an equivariantization of one of these categories by a group of order four.

## Statement

Let T be any non-group-theoretical integral complex fusion category of dimension twelve. Then a tensor autoequivalence of T fixing every simple is tensor naturally isomorphic to the identity. Its group of tensor autoequivalence classes embeds into S3. Consequently, for every action of a group P of order four on T, the equivariantization has a fusion coefficient at least two.

## The neutral category and finite linear algebra

Jordan–Larson, Theorem 1.1, gives all such T: they have a faithful C3 grading with neutral category A=Vec(V4), and one simple rho or rhobar of dimension two in each nontrivial component. Their rules include rho²=2 rhobar, rhobar²=2 rho, and rho rhobar=sum(a in V4) a. The pointed subcategory A and the C3 grading are canonical.

Write B=Inv Z(A)=V4 plus V4*, with quadratic form q(x,y)=(-1)^(x dot y). The extension homomorphism is generated by c in O^+(4,2), of order three. The group-theoretical criterion for extensions says that c has no stable Lagrangian, since T is not group-theoretical.

The following small linear-algebra facts suffice:

1. Such a c has a two-dimensional fixed subspace B^c, which projects isomorphically onto V4. In particular B=B^c direct-sum V4*.
2. The nontrivial alternating-cocycle shear s(x,y)=(x,y+Jx), with J=[[0,1],[1,0]], does not commute with c.
3. Inside the parabolic subgroup preserving V4*, the normalizer of the subgroup generated by c has order six and projects isomorphically onto GL(V4)=S3. Its three involutions conjugate c to its inverse and induce the three transpositions on the nonzero elements of V4.

The complete exact check in `triality_orthogonal.py` enumerates the 72 isometries and the six Lagrangian planes. Among the eight elements of order three, exactly four have no stable Lagrangian. With bit coordinates (x1,x2,y1,y2), their ordered column vectors are

    (4,12,15,6), (6,15,3,2), (12,8,9,15), (15,9,1,3).

Their fixed sets are either {0,7,10,13} or {0,5,11,14}; projection to the two low bits is a bijection onto V4. Direct multiplication shows that none commutes with the shear (9,6,4,8). The same check computes the stated six-element normalizer for each matrix. This is a sixteen-element linear-space check, not a fusion-category classification search. Alternatively, noncommutation follows immediately from the invertible off-diagonal block b: the upper-left blocks of cs and sc differ by bJ.

## The soft kernel

Let F fix all simples. Its restriction to Vec(V4) fixes all invertibles, so its tensor-equivalence class is in H²(V4,C*)=C2. The nontrivial class induces the shear s on B. Since F fixes rho and rhobar, it fixes the C3 grading pointwise, and graded-extension compatibility requires this induced isometry to commute with c (Edie-Michell, Lemma 6.8). Fact 2 rules out the nontrivial class. Choose a tensor identification F|A=Id_A.

For this fixed identification, the component bimodule equivalences of F differ from those of the identity by a normalized cocycle b in Z¹(C3,B). This is precisely the systems-of-equivalences torsor of Edie-Michell, Lemma 6.9 / Theorem 8.1. Since 3 is invertible on B, H¹(C3,B)=0; hence b_g=t-c_g(t) for some t in B.

One must not stop at H¹=0: arbitrary coboundaries can represent inner tensor autoequivalences, which need not be trivial. Here Fact 1 removes that issue. Write t=u+chi with u in B^c and chi in V4*. Then b_g=chi-c_g(chi). The element chi is a tensor natural automorphism of Id_A. Changing the chosen neutral identification by chi modifies the component bimodule equivalences by exactly this coboundary. Concretely it changes their left and right A-module constraints by the character chi; transporting the right constraint across the component applies c_g, so the discrepancy is chi-c_g(chi). Therefore, after changing the neutral identification, the system of component equivalences is the identity system.

For a fixed system the remaining tensorators, if they exist, form a torsor under H²(C3,C*) (Edie-Michell, Lemma 7.5 / Theorem 8.1). This group is zero. The identity functor supplies one such tensorator, so F is tensor naturally isomorphic to the identity. The argument applies to all three Jordan–Larson associator choices; it does not need to compute a third-cohomology obstruction, since the functor F under consideration already exists.

## Actions of a group of order four

Every tensor autoequivalence preserves the canonical pointed subcategory and the universal C3 grading. Its neutral restriction gives an element p of the parabolic subgroup above, and its grading permutation is phi in Aut(C3). Compatibility requires p c p^(-1)=c^(phi). The grading permutation is determined by p. The kernel of this map to the parabolic normalizer fixes all simples and is trivial by the preceding proof. Fact 3 therefore embeds Aut_tensor(T) into S3. In particular every homomorphism from P has image either trivial or C2. In the nontrivial case the involution exchanges rho and rhobar.

The group of tensor natural automorphisms of Id_T is Hom(U(T),C*)=Hom(C3,C*)=C3. For any action of a 2-group on this group, its positive-degree cohomology vanishes. The obstruction to lifting the C2 homomorphism to a categorical action is in H³(C2,C3)=0; choices form a torsor under H²(C2,C3)=0. Likewise, actions of P lifting its fixed homomorphism are unique up to equivalence, since H²(P,C3)=0. Thus in the nontrivial case the given P action is equivalent to the pullback of the genuine C2 action. In the trivial-image case it is equivalent to the trivial action, and T^P=T tensor Rep(P) already has coefficient two. This is the ordinary obstruction theory for actions by tensor autoequivalences, as in ENO, Section 7 (applied to the categorical group Eq(T)).

Let K be the kernel P->C2. In a pullback presentation K acts by identity functors with trivial coherence. The orbit {rho,rhobar} has stabilizer K=C2 and produces two simple equivariant objects X_+ and X_- of dimension four, indexed by the two characters of K. In X_epsilon tensor X_eta, the kernel acts everywhere by the single character epsilon eta. Forgetting equivariance gives

    (rho+rhobar)^2 = 2(rho+rhobar) + 2 sum(a in V4) a.

Only one of X_+, X_- has the required kernel character. Hence its coefficient in that tensor product is two. Thus such an equivariantization cannot have multiplicity one. The same argument works for P=C4 and P=V4.
