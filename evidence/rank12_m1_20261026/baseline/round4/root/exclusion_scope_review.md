## The dimension-120 converse

The twelve IDs are 161,211,262,265,312,362,388,453,469,472,503,516, with
prefix R12_1_. The four positive controls are 25,142,361,444.

The categorical argument in the frozen third-round d120/theory.md was reread,
including both actual internal-end constructions, the subgroup order-ten
recovery, the finite-group restriction, relative cohomology coverage, and
the coprime-twist lemma. None requires a general classification of arbitrary
dimension-120 categories, a braiding, or unitarity of a hypothetical category.

The first condensation needs the COMPLETE left and right stabilizer of every
dimension-two simple to be the common C2, rather than merely the existence of
a common involution. The older check explicitly tested the latter weaker
condition. The fresh audit_d120_premises.py checks the complete stabilizers
on every one of the sixteen inputs; all equal the required C2. This strengthens
the executable premise check and does not change any classification.

For the second condensation, the internal end has dimension5, includes the
unit, and cannot include a dimension5 noninvertible. Its support is therefore
the unique Sylow-five subgroup of the order20 group. This supplies the needed
commuting left action; a bare based-ring stabilizer would not suffice.
The reverse algebra is obtained from the simple B-R bimodule with dimension
10/sqrt(5*2); its internal end is connected of dimension10. Thus the source
is genuinely group-theoretical with an order10 subgroup, not just Morita
equivalent to an unspecified small category.

The normalizer and Hall-subgroup arguments force A5 x C2 with the graph D10.
The relative 2-primary cohomology is generated by the two explicitly realized
classes u+v and w+v; the restriction/invariant dimension upper bounds match
the independent classes. The 5-primary part vanishes by restriction-transfer.
The 3-primary part is fusion-invisible because all subgroup and stabilizer
orders are units in characteristic3. The stated DVR proof uses separability
only at these subgroup orders, not at the ambient group order120.

The four exact constructions have also been freshly rebuilt in
d120_independent/ using the frozen independent monomial Hom-space checker.
This checks actual cocycles, subgroup algebra, simple modules, and all1728
tensor coefficients for each output. The final full comparison with all16
inputs is recorded separately in d120_exhaustion_binding.json.

## R12_1_350

The independent frozen verifier was copied into this directory and freshly
run under optimized Python. It reconstructed all30 low frames, four PSD
survivors, 45 joint cyclotomic trace types, and all32 integer Farkas systems.
No optimizer or rank-six implementation was imported. The output is
audit_R350.json and the execution log is R350_fresh.log.

The categorical premises were checked separately: the PUBLISHED Schopieray
Lemma3.1 gives a pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate for any two-FP-value
categorification. It does not say that the original category is necessarily
pseudo-unitary. The center is then modular; the induction theorem uses
representation degrees1,1,1,1,2,2 and codegrees D,D',12,12,6,6. In particular
the matrix-block codegree is6, not the Casimir eigenvalue12. The finite trace
image allows arbitrary prime-power exponent layers; 7200 is a trace-box
representative bound, not an assumed categorical exponent bound. Every
possible second-indicator sign assignment is retained. These facts support
the general-complex scope of the exclusion.

## Correction to a research note, without a classification change

The second-round family_integral/central_quotients.py and findings.md called
every near-group quotient NG(G,2), |G|=8, impossible. This statement omitted
the nonabelian possibilities D8 and Q8. Those categories exist; the small
parameter abelian theorem cannot be applied to them.

No accepted exclusion used that label. ng24_branch.py restores the branch,
enumerates all order-two automorphisms of both nonabelian groups with four
fixed elements, and checks the invariant orbit tensors against every affected
source. The branch is compatible with unresolved R216 (D8) and R239 (D8 or
Q8), as well as known controls R30 and R417. It is incompatible with the
specific quotient branches of R175,R319,R407,R523. These are restrictions on
central lifts, not exclusions of those whole rings. The newly proved
odd-Lagrangian lemma in ../d48/ subsequently shows that every actual
NG24 C2-equivariantization is group-theoretical.
