# Integral rank-twelve families: offline second pass

## Accepted results

Two previously unresolved dimension-48 rings have exact unitary models:

| ID | Construction |
| --- | --- |
| R12_1_30 | C(S4 × C2, 1, H, 1), with H generated by ((34),0) and ((12),1), hence H ≅ V4. |
| R12_1_417 | C(S4 ×_{C2} C4, 1, H, 1), with H generated by ((1324),1), hence H ≅ C4. Here the fiber product imposes sgn(s)=(-1)^j. |

The categories are bimodules over the actual subgroup algebra A=⊕_{h∈H}h in the unitary pointed category Vec_G. The finite subgroup algebra is connected and separable, with its standard unitary Frobenius structure. Thus the constructions establish actual unitary fusion categories, without a proposed tensor action, a conjectural associator, or a classification assumption about an arbitrary original category.

`order48_models.json` contains the complete group tables, subgroups, full fusion tensors and unit-preserving based bijections to the supplied rings. `verify.py` checks all 1728 structure constants for each model. It independently recomputes the tensors by ordinary equivariant-bundle character convolution and by explicit graded A-bimodules with balanced relative tensor products. The second method checks every module identity, descent relation, projective-character orthogonality and completeness. Both normal and optimized-Python replay receipts pass.

These are standard group-theoretical constructions with newly identified parameters in this input. No novelty up to tensor equivalence is asserted.

## A useful conditional reduction of the other dimension-48 rings

All 24 dimension-48 rings supplied as unresolved have trivial universal grading, eight invertibles, two simples of dimension 2 and two of dimension 4. Every complex categorification would be solvable, since 48=2^4·3 (Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik, arXiv:0809.3031, Theorem 1.6). By the extension/equivariantization characterization of solvability, a final nontrivial step cannot be an extension when the universal grading is trivial. It must be a prime cyclic equivariantization. Its canonical Rep(Cp) subcategory forces p to divide eight, hence p=2.

Consequently some central involution b must admit the actual central boson lift of this equivariantization. A central involution in the based ring is only a necessary candidate; no such lift is inferred from the based ring alone.

If b fixes both dimension-2 and both dimension-4 simples, de-equivariantization has type (1^8,2^4) and rank twelve. Its four noninvertible simples occur in free pairs under the dual C2 action. This forces multiplicity one: for a noninvertible target Z, any product coefficient in the de-equivariantization is bounded by the coefficient of its unique dimension-4 parent in the original category, which is at most one. Coefficients to invertibles, and products involving an invertible, are always at most one by rigidity.

We enumerated the involutive based automorphisms of the sixteen dimension-24 rings in the supplied rank-twelve list that have four fixed invertibles and no fixed noninvertibles. Their invariant orbit-sum rings were compared exactly with the corresponding quotient of the dimension-48 source by b−1. There are only two compatible target IDs among those sixteen supplied tensors:

- R12_1_351 for source IDs 30, 130, 146, 151, 216, 239, 244, 277, 289, 296, 303, 410, 417, 424, 433 and 538.
- R12_1_513 for source IDs 175, 319, 407, 414, 421, 523, 526 and 531.

The complete permutations, orbit tensors and based maps are in `central_quotients.json`; the scan takes less than a second. This is a conditional based-ring reduction and a construction guide. It does not prove uniqueness of the de-equivariantization category, does not construct the required tensor action, and does not by itself exclude any source. Any use as an exhaustive negative criterion would additionally require the completeness premise for the input census. No negative conclusion here uses that premise.

Other possible central involutions yield dimension-24 quotients of types (1^4,2,4), (1^4,2^5), or (1^8,4). The last type would be a near-group ring with eight invertibles and parameter two, so it is impossible by the usual small-parameter near-group theorem. The first type is a uniquely determined free-orbit quotient, and its d-number test passes. The rank-nine case has unresolved splitting data. These statements rule out some central lifts; they do not exclude a dimension-48 source having another candidate lift.

## Additional constructive attempts and their precise limits

The following are positive discovery attempts. Their failure never contributes an exclusion.

1. All conjugacy classes of order-four subgroups in S4×C2, S4×_{C2}C4 and GL(2,3) were checked by exact group-theoretical convolution. They give the two accepted models above.
2. All order-four subgroup conjugacy classes in S3×K, for all five groups K of order eight, were checked. No additional target was obtained.
3. Adapted Schur-cover cup-product cocycles were constructed and all 2-cocycle, subgroup-algebra and module equations were checked. These reproduce the accepted models or have a different object pattern; no new target was identified.
4. For G=(V4⊕V4)⋊C3, with C3 irreducible on the first summand and trivial on the second, diagonal H≅V4, three relative invariant trilinear cocycles were constructed. The nontrivial type-III classes give precisely the desired count of eight invertibles, two dimension-2 simples and two dimension-4 simples. Actual balanced bimodule calculations nevertheless give a coefficient greater than one, for both subgroup 2-cochain classes. Thus these are genuine unitary categories of the desired dimension pattern, but their based rings do not match the multiplicity-one input. All 48^4 cocycle equations and all subgroup associativity equations were checked exactly using finite integer arrays. No failed scalar-fiber search was mistaken for a nonexistence proof; the nonscalar projective V4 stabilizers were explicitly reconstructed with two-dimensional monomial actions and complete character orthogonality.
5. Fourth-root 3-cocycles on a Sylow D8 were obtained by short exact normalized cochain linear algebra (343 variables and 2401 equations), then transferred along the index-three subgroup of S4 and inflated to the two order-48 groups. All resulting group cocycle identities were checked. Discovery sampled one representative per cyclic-restriction fingerprint, and therefore is not claimed to exhaust cohomology classes. No additional target was obtained.

The symbolic constructor and attempt inventories are retained. These computations are small exact certificates and explicit group constructions, not an associator or modular-data search.

## Dimension 120

All sixteen dimension-120 rings have type (1^4,2^4,5^4), trivial universal grading, and a proper dimension-20 subring of type (1^4,2^4). The unique order-two invertible fixing all four dimension-2 objects acts freely on the four dimension-5 objects. All paired and arbitrary-triple stabilizer integrality checks give no new exclusion in these sixteen cases.

The parent investigation independently found the model R12_1_25=C(A5×C2,1,graph(D10→C2),1), using the reflection-sign character of the dihedral subgroup D10⊂A5. That result and its verification are recorded in the parent's separate `root/group_graph_models.json`; it is not counted among this subtask's two accepted dimension-48 models.

The untwisted candidates C(SL(2,5),Dic5) and C(S5,D10) have the desired type but fusion multiplicity two. Arbitrary twists of the S5,D10 candidate retain a sign C2 grading with neutral category C(A5,ω|A5,D10,ψ), whose type is (1^2,2^2,5^2). That neutral category cannot have multiplicity-one rules: a dimension-5 simple has square dimension 25, greater than the sum of all simple dimensions 16. This rules out that construction family, not all possible categorifications of the supplied rings.

A precise remaining constructive target is the nontrivial 3-primary cocycle class on SL(2,5), with H=Dic5. Its restriction to H is cohomologically trivial because 3 is coprime to |H|=20, so actual subgroup-algebra categories exist after choosing a trivializing cochain. Their object dimensions remain (1^4,2^4,5^4). A full twisted tensor calculation has not been completed; no supplied ring is modeled merely from this object pattern. Neither a classification of all dimension-120 fusion categories as group-theoretical nor a lift of the known unitary model to every possible original category is assumed.
