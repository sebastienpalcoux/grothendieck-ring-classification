The six exclusions are R12_1_309, R12_1_383, R12_1_467, R12_1_470, R12_1_501 and R12_1_514. The complete noncommutative D40 family consists of these six and the two positive rings R12_1_156 and R12_1_263. The ninth, commutative D40 ring R12_1_449 has its separate positive model.

## Categorical premises checked independently

1. **The first algebra exists for every original category.** The exact source tensors have a common central involution g fixing every dimension-two simple. That fixed simple supplies the algebra A=1+g, including its associator trivialization. No replacement of an arbitrary category by a known unitary model is used.

2. **The integral Morita dual is pointed.** There is an especially clean proof which avoids any classification of categorifications of a dihedral representation ring. Every simple right A-module in the integral subcategory has underlying dimension two: either a free pair of invertibles or one of the two A-actions on a fixed dimension-two simple. Its normalized module dimension is sqrt(2), so its internal endomorphism algebra has dimension two and is 1+v for a nontrivial invertible v. The v summand fixes the module as an A-module. The stabilizer of its underlying object is exactly {1,g}; hence v=g. Therefore g fixes every right A-module as a module, not merely as an object label. A right-A-linear isomorphism gM->M can be rescaled to satisfy the fixed multiplication of A; its square is a nonzero scalar by simplicity. Its two signs give the two left A-actions. All resulting simple bimodules have normalized dimension one, so the integral dual is pointed of dimension twenty.

3. **The reverse algebra is tracked.** The original simple A-module A has normalized dimension sqrt(2). Its internal endomorphism algebra in the dual has dimension two. Since all noninvertibles there have dimension sqrt(5)>2, the reverse algebra lies in the pointed part and is 1+c for an involution. This excludes the possibility that an untracked, different module category is being substituted when passing back to the source category. Its multiplication is unique up to isomorphism because H^2(C2,C*)=0.

4. **The pointed group is forced for every associator.** The four original invertibles force the normalizer of <c> in the pointed group S of order twenty to have order four. The group list then reduces to F20 or D10 x C2. There is an associator-independent way to exclude the latter. In D10 x C2, every nonnormal <c>-double coset is inverse-stable and has trivial stabilizer, so the corresponding dimension-two bimodule is unique and self-dual for every pointed associator. The four invertibles form an abelian group; the kernel consisting of the two <c>-characters fixes the dimension-two labels. The remaining normalizer coset is represented by the extra central involution, so left and right actions agree and factor through C2. Consequently products of dimension-two objects commute: their dimension-two output multiplicities agree by duality, while an invertible output has multiplicity delta_{Y,gZ}, symmetric in Y,Z because g acts involutively. Thus every such D10 x C2 dual ring is commutative. All eight source integral subrings are noncommutative, as checked directly, so S=F20 for every associator. This avoids needing a pointed-associator classification in the main argument. The six explicit integral dual models are retained only as a cross-check. Alternative noncentral involutions are related by group automorphisms, and H^2(C2,C*)=0 removes additional algebra choices.

5. **The graded action is forced, including all bicharacter choices.** The adjoint component of the feudal dual is Vec_C5, and its faithful universal grading has eight components. The C4 quotient of F20 acts faithfully on C5 by multiplier two or its inverse. In BrPic(Vec_C5)=O(C5 plus C5*,q), this is the order-four diagonal subgroup. A rank-one lord component is an off-diagonal reflection. Every such reflection squares to the identity and inverts that diagonal rotation. Therefore the universal group must be D8, with sigma=inversion and p=0. This proves the required restrictions directly, without making a finite phase ansatz. Changing the base lord by a rotation runs through all four reflection/bicharacter choices; the relabeling preserves c=r^2.

6. **The ENO classification applies to arbitrary complex associators.** Its coefficient group Inv Z(Vec_C5) is C5 squared, so all positive-degree D8-cohomology with those coefficients vanishes by averaging. In particular both the lower obstruction and the H^2 product-system freedom vanish. Existence of the actual reference category D0 handles the remaining obstruction. Every possible category with this action is therefore a grading H^3(D8,C*) twist of D0. The primary reference is Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik, *Fusion categories and homotopy theory*, arXiv:0909.3140, Theorems 1.1 and 1.3; this is an exhaustion theorem, not merely a construction theorem for selected examples.

7. **Fourth roots exhaust all relevant complex twists.** An index-two V4 subgroup has H^3(V4,C*) of exponent two. Restriction followed by corestriction is multiplication by two, hence every H^3(D8,C*) class is killed by four. The coefficient sequence mu4 -> C* -> C* therefore makes H^3(D8,mu4) surject onto H^3(D8,C*). On the reverse C2 subgroup, H^2(C2,C*)=0 makes the corresponding map in degree three injective. Thus a complex class with trivial C2 restriction has a mu4 lift whose restriction is already zero. The normalized condition is precisely omega(c,c,c)=1. No inadmissible algebra is constructed, and no admissible complex class is omitted. Sixteen mu4 classes need not mean sixteen distinct complex classes; duplicates after changing coefficients are harmless.

8. **Equivalences preserve the reverse-algebra possibilities.** The allowed grading relabelings preserve the degree r^2. The possible actual involutions in its F20 coset are conjugate. Conjugation by invertible objects transports the algebra, and its C2 multiplication is unique up to isomorphism. Thus passing to an ENO-equivalent representative cannot hide an additional reverse algebra that changes the source fusion ring.
