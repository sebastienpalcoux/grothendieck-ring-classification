# Four quadratic-C2 exclusions

The excluded input IDs are R12_1_63, R12_1_64, R12_1_215, R12_1_365. No unitarity or pivotality assumption is made on an alleged original category.

## General-to-spherical reduction by a small linear sign space

In the canonical sphericalization, write ε for the central sign object. Choose dual-compatible lifts of the original simples, and the positive-second-indicator lift for each self-dual simple. These choices are possible: otherwise a self-dual original simple's two lifts would be exchanged by duality, contradicting their opposite nonzero spherical dimensions. Tensoring by ε changes the second indicator's sign. For every object Y, the object Y⊗Y* has its canonical symmetric self-duality; consequently every skew self-dual simple occurs in it with even multiplicity. If a self-dual input simple has chosen indicator +1 and multiplicities a+ and a− in Y⊗Y*, then N−T=2a− is divisible by four, where T=a+−a− is the signed quotient coefficient.

For multiplicity one every nonzero signed coefficient equals (−1)^x with x in F2. Cyclic trace symmetry and dual reversal identify its variable orbit. Associativity modulo four is a LINEAR equation in these bits: for each i,j,k,l, XOR the two sign variables in every nonzero term of (b_i b_j)b_k and b_i(b_j b_k). Ordinary associativity equates the numbers of terms, so this XOR must be zero. Coefficients at the unit are fixed. Apply the indicator normalization just proved as additional zero-bit equations.

For the four rings the numbers of variables are respectively 189,190,179,169. The resulting kernels have dimensions 1,1,2,4, hence contain only 2,2,4,16 possibilities. Testing the remaining exact integer associativity equations leaves respectively 2,2,4,8 signed tensors. Every one is obtained from the original tensor by changing signs of simple basis elements, with ε_0=1 and ε_{i*}=ε_i. The certificates supply each gauge explicitly; the independent audit uses a different orbit construction, opposite elimination convention and separate enumeration.

This proves sphericality of any hypothetical categorification. Indeed, change each selected lift by the central sign object according to its gauge. All signed coefficients are then the original nonnegative multiplicity-one coefficients. Thus no negative lift occurs in their products; the chosen lifts form a full spherical fusion subcategory. Its forgetful functor to the original category is a tensor equivalence.

## All possible spherical dimension characters

A spherical dimension function is a one-dimensional ring character with nonzero values and equal values on dual pairs. We compute all such characters exactly, also for the two noncommutative rings. In a semisimple regular algebra, the common annihilator of all commutators is precisely the direct sum of its one-dimensional blocks: in a matrix block of size greater than one the commutators have no common kernel. Restrict the fusion matrices to this rational subspace and choose a square-free separating element. Exact polynomial coordinates give all character Galois orbits and verify every product. The independent audit instead constructs the commutator-ideal quotient.

All four Casimir polynomials contain t²−720t+2592. Every actual formal codegree belongs to the spherical dimension field, since its associated distinguished central simple has dimension dim(C)/f and forgetting it gives an integer sum of simple dimensions. Multiplication by an irreducible representation's integer degree does not change this field condition. Hence character fields of degrees one or three cannot work. Any zero or dual-incompatible character is already impossible. The only remaining Galois orbit has degree two and dimensions

1 (twice), d=4+3√2 (eight times), d+1 (twice),

with d²−8d−2=0. Its global dimension is D=24+84d=360+252√2. The dimension order generated by all simples is exactly Z+Zd=Z+3√2 Z.

The Casimir eigenvalue 36 has multiplicity one in each ring, so it comes from a one-dimensional irreducible representation and is the actual formal codegree 36. The associated central simple would have dimension

D/36 = 2/3 + (7/3)d = 10+7√2,

which does not belong to that dimension order. The other real embedding gives the same nonintegral order coordinates. This excludes the final character orbit and therefore every complex categorification.
