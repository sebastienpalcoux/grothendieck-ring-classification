# D48 third-round outcome

Two of the 22 inputs are now modeled: `R12_1_414` and `R12_1_531`. There are no new general-complex exclusions; twenty remain unresolved. The full proof is `theory.md`, the exact witnesses are `positive_witnesses.json`, and `verify.py` replays the constructions without relying on previous rounds. An independent graded-Hom implementation passed both models.

The new idea is a relative-cocycle adaptation along a subgroup retraction. In the two relevant order-48 groups the selected subgroup of order four maps isomorphically onto the abelianization. Subtracting the inflation of a cocycle's subgroup restriction makes it admissible. The earlier search had discarded such nontrivial restrictions instead of canceling them. This produces the two actual group-theoretical models and explains why they were missed.

Additional work, all offline:

- Checked ordinary subgroup-algebra models across all 52 groups of order 48 and all 1722 subgroup conjugacy classes. Exactly six pairs have the required simple-dimension pattern; no unresolved input matches them.
- Extended the ordinary search to all relevant subgroup Schur twists. For the only eligible ambient groups, 19 exact twisted calculations give two multiplicity-two tensors and 17 tensors isomorphic to the already-known models `R12_1_30` or `R12_1_417`. A scan across all 52 groups adds only one ordinary quaternion-subgroup case already covered in the untwisted scan.
- Tested six additional explicit order-48 constructions, all subgroup classes of orders 8,12,16 in the earlier and new constructions, and combined transferred associators with adapted Schur cup cocycles. These produced no additional accepted input.
- Adapted the transferred fourth-root associators along the actual subgroup retractions. The initial 70 parameter checks produced the two accepted models. A subsequent expanded finite check retained all generated cochains, rather than deduplicating by cyclic restrictions: 310 parameter checks gave 96 successful matches, all to those same two rings. Its receipts record the precise tested scope. Neither search is claimed to classify all third cohomology.
- Derived a necessary double-coset restriction for any group-theoretical model of the target type. It leaves 23 subgroup classes before further theory. A uniform argument excludes eleven order-16 pairs with normal Sylow-three subgroup from this parameter search; the other two order-16 pairs are covered by the exact subgroup-twist calculation. This leaves only two order-four and eight order-eight subgroup classes for a complete relative-associator treatment.

The principal remaining categorical gap is that an arbitrary category with a remaining D48 input has not been proved group-theoretical. Solvability only forces a final C2-equivariantization; its existence does not provide an arbitrary categorical action on a chosen model. There are also untreated relative-cocycle mechanisms for the eight order-eight subgroup pairs. In particular, abelian order-eight stabilizers require a nontrivial normalizer-sector projective multiplier, which ordinary untwisted calculations cannot detect. These are reasons to retain the twenty unresolved labels.

References for the categorical construction are Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik, *On fusion categories*, Annals of Mathematics 162 (2005), §8, https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v162-n2-p01.pdf, and Etingof–Gelaki–Nikshych–Ostrik, *Tensor Categories*, Chapter 7, https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/egnobookfinal.pdf. The coprime-primary reduction used in the conditional order-16 argument is proved in the sibling D120 notes; it is not an appeal to an unproved invariance assumption.
