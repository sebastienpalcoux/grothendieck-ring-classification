# Two dimension-48 models by relative cocycle adaptation

## Accepted conclusion

The rank-12 multiplicity-one fusion rings `R12_1_414` and `R12_1_531` have unitary group-theoretical categorifications. The construction is uniform: take the category of subgroup-algebra bimodules in a pointed category with an adapted fourth-root associator. This is an existence result for ordinary complex categorification, with the stronger conclusion of unitarity. It assumes no braiding, pivotal structure, fiber functor, or classification of all categories with the given fusion rules.

The other twenty D48 inputs remain unresolved. No failed model search is used as an exclusion.

## The two finite group pairs

Let `epsilon:S4 -> C2` be the sign homomorphism. The first group is

`G_0=S4 x C2`.

Let `H_0` be generated by `((34),0)` and `((12),1)`. This is a Klein four subgroup; its two generators project to disjoint transpositions. The quotient `q_0=(epsilon,id):G_0 -> C2 x C2` restricts to an isomorphism on `H_0`. Its inverse supplies a group-homomorphic section `s_0`.

The second group is

`G_1={(g,z) in S4 x C4 : epsilon(g)=z mod 2}`.

Let `r=(1324)` be a four-cycle and `H_1=<(r,1)>`, a cyclic group of order four. Projection `q_1:G_1 -> C4` restricts to an isomorphism on `H_1`, with inverse section `s_1`.

Write `p_i:G_i -> S4` for the projection. Both groups have order 48. The chosen group tables and subgroup embeddings are frozen in `positive_witnesses.json`.

## Adaptation lemma

Let `q:G -> Q` have a group-homomorphic section `s` with image `H`, and let `p:G -> K` be a group homomorphism. For any normalized additive 3-cocycle `v` on `K`, put

\[
 w(a,b,c)=v(p(a),p(b),p(c))
          -v(p(s(q(a))),p(s(q(b))),p(s(q(c)))).
\]

Both summands are pullbacks along homomorphisms, so `w` is a normalized 3-cocycle. For `a,b,c in H`, the two summands coincide; hence `w|H=0` identically. Thus the ordinary subgroup algebra

\[
 A_H=\bigoplus_{h\in H} h
\]

exists in `Vec_G^(exp(2 pi i w))` with its ordinary multiplication. It is connected and separable: the usual averaging splitting has denominator `|H|`, and all associators involving three subgroup degrees are trivial. Its bimodule category is a fusion category of dimension `|G|`.

If the cocycle is unitary, `Vec_G^omega` is unitary and `A_H` has the ordinary finite-group Q-system normalization; therefore its bimodule fusion category is unitary as well.

The cancellation is essential. A cocycle whose restriction to `H` is nontrivial need not be discarded: it can be canceled by the pullback of exactly that restriction along the retraction `s q`. The previous discovery search omitted this possibility.

## The actual cocycle

A normalized fourth-root cocycle `v` on `S4` is obtained by cohomological transfer from a normalized fourth-root cocycle on its Sylow dihedral subgroup `D8`. The discovery program constructs the latter by solving the normalized cocycle equations modulo 4, and transfers it using the three right cosets of `D8`. The earliest accepted generator has discovery bits `[0,0,0,0,1]` in `transfer48_models.py::transfer_generators`.

Use the adaptation lemma with `G_i,H_i,p_i,q_i,s_i`. The saved additive table `W`, with modulus 8, is twice the resulting modulus-4 table. The associator is `omega(a,b,c)=exp(2 pi i W(a,b,c)/8)`. The subgroup cochain is identically zero. No additional Schur cup cocycle is required.

Transfer is an explanation of discovery, not an unverified premise of existence. The full normalized table `W` is supplied. Both verification implementations directly check all `48^4 = 5,308,416` cocycle equations, together with normalization and the trivial subgroup restriction. Thus the proof does not depend on a claimed generating set for cohomology, cyclic-restriction detection, or completeness of any transfer search.

## Computation of the fusion rules

For each double coset `H z H`, an `A_H`-bimodule consists of fibers supported on that double coset with left and right subgroup actions satisfying the associator equations. The saved action matrices are monomial matrices with eighth-root entries. In the two accepted models all fibers have dimension one. Stabilizer projective characters show that the listed simple bimodules are complete and pairwise inequivalent.

The tensor product is the actual balanced quotient `M tensor_A N`. The supplied implementation checks the balancing relations, proves that the induced left and right actions descend, and checks all resulting module and bimodule identities. Exact projective-character projection gives the multiplicities. Its tensor unit is the subgroup algebra, and the output simple dimensions are `(1^8,2^2,4^2)`.

The first group pair gives `R12_1_414`; the second gives `R12_1_531`. The saved basis bijections match every one of the 1728 tensor entries, including the unit. The matching is exact integer equality.

An independent implementation in `../independent/audit_d48.py` reconstructs the complete graded Hom spaces of all tensor products. It verifies each simple module and every multiplicity without using the discovery code's character-projection formula. Both models pass that independent audit (`../independent/d48_independent.json`).

## Conditional reduction of all possible group-theoretical models

This section restricts a group-theoretical search. It does not assert that an arbitrary category with one of the remaining rings is group-theoretical.

Suppose `C(G,omega,H,psi)` has the required type and trivial universal grading. Necessarily `|G|=48`. The subgroup `H` normally generates `G`: otherwise supports induce a nontrivial faithful grading by `G/normal_closure(H)`. The identity double coset contributes the ordinary category `Rep(H)`, regardless of the subgroup cochain. A simple in the double coset `HzH` has dimension `[H:K] dim(pi)`, where `K=H intersect zHz^-1` and `pi` is an irreducible projective representation of `K`.

These observations leave only `|H|=4,8,16`. Subgroups of order one, two, three, or six cannot produce dimension four with their available stabilizers and projective degrees. An order-12 subgroup must have no irreducible degree three and at most eight linear characters, hence is one of the dihedral or dicyclic types with character degrees `(1^4,2^2)`. The dimension-two allowance is already exhausted. A normalizer coset can supply the missing four linears, but an outer double coset cannot supply exactly the two fours: stabilizer index four requires a cyclic order-three stabilizer, which supplies three fours; the other indices give prohibited degrees or additional twos. An order-24 subgroup is normal and therefore violates normal generation. For `H=G`, the possible category is `Rep(G)` as a based ring; none of the 52 groups has the required character degrees.

The exact small-group check in `possible_twisted_types.g` independently implements a necessary condition allowing *every* projective character degree pattern of each stabilizer separately. Thus it even permits mutually incompatible local cocycles, making it a safe superset test. It produces 23 subgroup-conjugacy classes:

| Subgroup order | Ambient SmallGroup(48,i) | Subgroup type and number of conjugacy classes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 30 | C4, one |
| 4 | 48 | V4, one |
| 8 | 28 | Q8, one |
| 8 | 29 | D8, one |
| 8 | 30 | C4 x C2, two |
| 8 | 48 | D8, two; C4 x C2, one; C2^3, one |
| 16 | 10,12,13,14,19,34,36,37,38,39,43 | One class in each group |
| 16 | 30,48 | One class in each group |

For both order-four pairs the normalizer has order eight, and the double-coset stabilizers are `H,H,C2,1,1`. For every order-eight pair, the normalizer has order sixteen and the stabilizers are `H,H,C2`. Therefore the outer double coset supplies exactly two dimension-four objects. If `H` is D8 or Q8, the second normalizer sector must have an ordinary-type projective character pattern `(1^4,2)`; if `H` is abelian, it must instead have two irreducible projective characters of degree two. This isolates the remaining untested relative-cocycle mechanism in the abelian order-eight cases.

For every order-sixteen pair the normalizer is `H` and the stabilizers are `H,K8`. The identity character degrees are `(1^8,2^2)`; the outer stabilizer would have to possess exactly two projective degree-two characters.

The eleven order-sixteen pairs outside ambient groups 30 and 48 can be removed from this *group-theoretical parameter search* uniformly. Each ambient group has a normal Sylow-three subgroup `N=C3`, and `G=N semidirect H`. The order-eight intersection for a representative `z in N minus {1}` is `K=ker(H -> Aut(N))`, so `z` centralizes `K`. Since `H` is Sylow two, restriction on the two-primary component of `H^3(G,C*)` is injective (restriction followed by corestriction is multiplication by the odd index three). Admissibility on `H` therefore removes that component. Three-primary cocycle changes do not affect the based fusion ring here by the coprime-twist invariance lemma proved in `../d120/theory.md`, because `3` does not divide `|H|`. With associator trivial, the stabilizer multiplier is the ratio of a subgroup cocycle and its conjugate. Conjugation by `z` is the identity on `K`, so that ratio is trivial. Ordinary `K` representations include linear characters and would create dimension-two simples, contradicting that all twos were already supplied by `Rep(H)`.

The two remaining order-sixteen pairs, in groups 30 and 48, were checked with every subgroup Schur class. They yield no new unresolved target. This reduces the unresolved group-theoretical route to the two order-four and eight order-eight pairs, with genuinely relative two-primary associator classes still requiring a complete treatment. The present accepted models settle two of the census rings without presupposing such a classification.
