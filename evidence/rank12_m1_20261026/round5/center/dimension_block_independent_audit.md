# Independent audit: equality in the dimension-block bound forbids the metric C3

## Verdict

The proposed final obstruction for R12_1_561 and R12_1_628 is valid. The last
step can be formulated entirely in the actual modular representation of the
Drinfeld center. It does not require a separate assertion about a projective
congruence level of the pointed factor. This note independently checks that
step and the equality case of the norm argument.

The input categorical statements are the already established pseudo-unitary
Galois reduction and the forced braided central C3 lift. The final section
also removes the need to import an older rank-four exclusion for the Tannakian
case. No braided structure on the original fusion category is
assumed or deduced by a spurious splitting argument.

## Primary source and its scope

Andrew Schopieray, *The dimension block of a spherical fusion category*,
arXiv:2609.35287v1, September 28, 2026,
https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35287v1 .

The source's Lemma 3.1 supplies the twist-fixed, torus-character vector and its
S-matrix coefficient. Lemma 4.1 gives the local compression norm. Theorem 5.1
assembles those bounds by the Chinese remainder theorem. Its proof applies to
spherical categories; the modular representation is unitary without assuming
that the original fusion category is a unitary tensor category. I checked the
displayed proofs of these three statements, including the induced-representation
calculation proving Lemma 4.1. The equality implication below is an additional
deduction, not a quotation from the theorem statement.

## 1. Exact saturation

Put d=3+sqrt(10), D=120+36sqrt(10), D'=120-36sqrt(10). Then DD'=1440,
D/D'=d^2, and

    (1/D - 1/D')^2 = 1/40.

The nontrivial character chi of Q(sqrt(10))/Q has conductor 40. Both the field
generated by simple dimensions and the global-dimension field are Q(sqrt(10)),
so the dimensional grading index in the source is one.

Let A be the simple summand of I(1) corresponding to the conjugate dimension
character; dim(A)=d^2 and theta_A=1. In the center's modular representation set

    w=e_1-e_A.

Lemma 3.1 gives ||w||^2=2, Tw=w, torus character chi, and

    |<w,S w>| = 2/sqrt(40).

Here S is normalized. This is equality in the full compression bound.

## 2. Equality forces invariance at every prime outside the conductor

Write the exact Frobenius-Schur exponent as N=product p^{a_p}. The representation
factors through product SL2(Z/p^{a_p}), with local unipotent projections P_p and
local Weyl operators S_p. Let B_p=P_p S_p P_p. The full compression is the
product of the commuting B_p.

Fix p not dividing 40. On w's unipotent-fixed torus-character subspace, the
product B_rest of the other local compressions has norm at most c=1/sqrt(40),
and B_p has norm at most one. The exact matrix coefficient gives

    c||w||^2 = |<w,B_rest B_p w>|
              <= ||w|| ||B_rest B_p w||
              <= c ||w|| ||B_p w||
              <= c ||w||^2.

All inequalities are equalities. Thus ||P_p S_p w||=||w||, so S_p w is fixed
by the local upper unipotent subgroup U_p. Since w itself is U_p-fixed, it
is fixed by both U_p and S_p^{-1}U_p S_p. These upper and lower elementary
unipotents generate SL2 over Z/p^{a_p}; hence w is fixed by the whole local
group. In particular this holds at p=3 whenever 3 divides N.

This argument works with direct sums of tensor-product constituents as well;
no assumption that w is a pure tensor in the CRT decomposition is made.

## 3. Why w is a pure tensor for the pointed modular factor

Suppose the central C3 lift is nondegenerate, with generator twist zeta^r,
r=1 or 2 and zeta=exp(2pi i/3). Müger decomposition is applied inside Z(C):

    Z(C) = B(C3,q) Deligne-product E.

Equation (5) of the primary source gives S_unnormalized(A,h)=dim(A) for each
h in this C3, because the conjugate dimension character takes the underlying
invertible h to one. Since h is invertible, its monodromy with A is therefore
one. Thus A belongs to the centralizer E and, in the displayed product,

    w=e_0 tensor (e_1-e_Y)

for the corresponding simple Y of E. This is a nonzero pure tensor.

## 4. Explicit lower-unipotent contradiction: no projective-level gap

The pointed twists force 3|N. Put n=N/3^{a_3}; then gcd(n,3)=1. The element

    L = S T^n S^{-1}

belongs to the local 3-primary subgroup: T^n is the identity at every other
prime-power factor. Section 2 therefore forces Lw=w.

Normalized modular matrices and actual twists factor exactly under Deligne
product. Consequently L acts on the two displayed factors as

    (S_B T_B^n S_B^{-1}) tensor (S_E T_E^n S_E^{-1}).

For the metric C3 pointed factor, T_B=diag(1,zeta^r,zeta^r), and S_B is its
normalized Fourier matrix. Direct multiplication gives

    S_B T_B^n S_B^{-1} e_0
      = ((1+2 zeta^{rn})/3, (1-zeta^{rn})/3, (1-zeta^{rn})/3).

Both latter coordinates are nonzero. The first tensor factor is therefore
not collinear with e_0, while the second is nonzero because its operator is
invertible. Hence Lw cannot equal w, contradiction.

The proof needs neither a bound on N nor a claim that an arbitrary factor's
projective modular representation has a specified congruence level.

## 5. The same bound directly excludes the Tannakian quotient

If the central C3 is Tannakian, genuine tensor de-equivariantization gives a
rank-four fusion category of dimension D/3=40+12sqrt(10). The free C3 action on
all original simples gives two pointed and two nonpointed simples; the latter
both have FP dimension d. Thus the same two-FP-dimension pseudo-unitary Galois
reduction applies to this actual quotient. Its simple-dimension field and
global-dimension field are again Q(sqrt(10)), and the nontrivial conductor is
again 40. The dimension-block theorem would require

    |3/D - 3/D'|^2 = 9/40 <= 1/40,

which is impossible. This argument needs no classification of rank-four
categories and no assumption that every original spherical category was unitary.

## Consequence for the two rings

The forced central C3 lift has only its Tannakian or its two metric possibilities.
The former is excluded by strict violation of the dimension-block bound after
actual de-equivariantization. Both metric possibilities contradict equality
rigidity in that same bound before de-equivariantization. Subject to the
already verified central-lift and pseudo-unitary-Galois input lemmas,
R12_1_561 and R12_1_628 admit no complex categorification.

## Secondary progress on R12_1_623

This audit also found a useful necessary condition, not an exclusion: writing
Z(C)=Semion Deligne-product E gives dim(E)=D^2/2. Any simple of E with dimension
4+12d would have rational formal codegree dim(E)/(4+12d)^2=9/2, impossible.
Thus the prior induction-profile type (p,t)=(2,2) is unavailable. This removes
profiles 1,7,11 after the independent removal of dimension-two central simples.
It is compatible with, and must not be used to deny, an actual construction
of R12_1_623 established by other agents.
