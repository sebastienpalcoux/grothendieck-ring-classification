# R8_3_1154: a simple center and a uniform obstruction to braided construction routes

29 October 2026. **Status: unresolved.** This note proves a new necessary
structural condition for this project. It does not claim a new theorem in the
literature or a categorification/nonexistence result for the source ring.

## 1. A general lemma

Let C be a complex fusion category satisfying:

1. C does not admit a braiding.
2. Every proper fusion subcategory of C is integral.
3. The only integral simple object of Z(C) is its tensor unit.

Then **Z(C) is simple as a fusion category**: its only fusion subcategories
are Vec and Z(C).

Proof. Write D=FPdim(C). For every nontrivial fusion subcategory E of Z(C),
choose a nonunit simple object W in E. By (3), FPdim(W) is nonintegral.
The forgetful tensor functor preserves FP dimension. Thus the fusion
subcategory of C generated by F(E) is nonintegral and, by (2), equals C.
Consequently F restricted to E is dominant, giving FPdim(E)>=D. Equality
would make this dominant tensor functor an equivalence, transporting E's
braiding to C, contrary to (1). In fact FPdim(E)>D.

Suppose E were proper as well as nontrivial. Since Z(C) is nondegenerate,
its centralizer E' is nontrivial and satisfies

    FPdim(E) FPdim(E') = FPdim(Z(C)) = D^2.

The preceding strict inequality applies to both E and E', contradicting
this equation. Hence no such E exists. QED.

Only Frobenius-Perron dimensions are used. Neither sphericality,
pseudo-unitarity, unitarity, nor a classification of modular data is assumed.
The ingredients are the dimension inequality/equality criterion for a
dominant tensor functor, nondegeneracy of the Drinfeld center, and DGNO's
centralizer dimension theorem (Theorem 3.14 of arXiv:0906.0620).

## 2. Exact application to R1154

Use the frozen source basis 1,X1,X2,g,X4,X5,X6,X7. Put a=6+3 sqrt(5).
The FP dimensions are

    (1,a+1,a+1,1,a,a,a+1,a+1),
    D = 540+240 sqrt(5),
    D^2 = 579600+259200 sqrt(5).

Each of X1,X2,X4,X5,X6,X7 generates the whole based fusion ring. Hence its
only proper nontrivial fusion subcategory is the pointed category on
{1,g}. The ring is noncommutative: gX1=X7 while X1g=X6. These facts verify
conditions (1) and (2).

For completeness, condition (3) has the following direct proof. If W is an
integral-dimensional simple of Z(C), positivity of all six noninvertible
sqrt(5) coefficients forces F(W)=m*1+n*g. This class must be central in the
source ring. Noncentrality of g forces n=0. The source adjoint subring is
all of C; hence its universal grading group is trivial. A half-braiding on
m*1 gives a representation of that universal grading group on its
multiplicity space, so it is the trivial half-braiding. Simplicity gives
m=1. This repeats the short general-complex integral-center argument from
the accepted 27 October record, without relying on the center enumeration.

**Therefore every complex categorification of R1154 has a simple Drinfeld
center.** This is stronger than the previously established trivial integral
center and excludes additional broad construction routes.

In the positive spherical representative supplied by the separate existing
existence reduction, the retained center ranks are **21, 23, or 29**.
Those ranks are inherited from patterns 8766, 6971, and 8772, respectively;
the simple-center theorem itself is independent of that enumeration.

## 3. Consequences throughout the Morita class

Centers are invariant under categorical Morita equivalence, so simplicity
holds for the center of every category Morita equivalent to C.

* **No braided Morita representative exists.** A braiding on a nontrivial
  fusion category B embeds B into Z(B). Since FPdim(B)=D, this is a proper
  nontrivial fusion subcategory of the center, impossible here. This does
  not assert that every Morita representative has a noncommutative ring;
  a commutative ring without any braiding remains possible.
* **No nontrivial Deligne product is a Morita representative.** If
  B=B1 tensor B2 with both factors nontrivial, its center contains the
  proper nontrivial factor Z(B1).
* **Every nontrivial central tensor functor from a braided category P to C
  requires FPdim(P)>=D^2.** Its braided lift to Z(C) has a nontrivial fusion
  image and therefore is dominant onto Z(C). The dominant-functor dimension
  inequality gives the bound. The same statement holds with any Morita
  representative of C as target.

Here a central tensor functor means one with a specified compatible braided
lift to the target's center. An arbitrary tensor functor or an arbitrary
fusion-subcategory inclusion into a larger category is not covered.

## 4. All direct condensations from nondegenerate braided categories

Suppose P is nondegenerate braided, A is a connected étale algebra in P,
and the fusion category of all right A-modules P_A is equivalent to C or
to a category Morita equivalent to C. DMNO Corollary 3.30 gives

    Z(P_A) = P tensor (P_A^0)^rev.

Simplicity of the left side, with P nontrivial, forces P_A^0=Vec and
P equivalent to Z(C). Thus necessarily

    FPdim(P)=D^2,      FPdim(A)=D,

and A is Lagrangian. In particular, this construction cannot start with a
smaller modular category, with a nontrivial product of modular categories,
or with a non-Lagrangian algebra. This replaces separate dimension tests
for numerous small braided ambients by one categorical obstruction.

This does not exclude a genuinely new simple modular category of dimension
D^2 with an appropriate Lagrangian algebra. Such a pair is exactly another
presentation of the missing categorification problem. It also does not
exclude a proper fusion subcategory extracted after condensation, noncentral
bimodule constructions in larger categories, or other non-braided ambients.

## 5. Other directions checked in this pass

The all-six-trivalent-rotations-identity result was revisited. It controls
six cyclic operators, not the full associator. In the rank-ten anomaly-
cancelled category, x*=ux and the odd-indicator decomposition gives
nu_3(x)=1. For either selfdual orbit object z=y or vy, the same C2
character decomposition gives

    nu_3(F(z))=nu_3(z)+nu_3(uz)=2 nu_3(z),

because uz=z. The source theorem and F(y)=X1+X2,
F(vy)=X6+X7 yield nu_3(y)=nu_3(vy)=2. Each cubic vacuum space has
dimension 8, so the multiplicities of the three cube-root eigenvalues are
(4,2,2). In particular the quaternionic y, whose second indicator is -1,
does not yield an odd-dimensional alternating block from this spectrum.
No pentagon contradiction follows merely from the cubic identities.

The forced canonical algebra attached to C's pointed subcategory was also
examined as a potential nonpointed construction. Its dimension is D/2;
the known distinguished center columns allow the object 1+B+E, with
FPdim(B)=161+72 sqrt(5) and FPdim(E)=108+48 sqrt(5). This dimension identity
is not an algebra-existence proof or a way to construct C. We do not use
it as a new model or a new exclusion.

Simple modular categories can have connected étale and Lagrangian algebras.
Thus simplicity of this hypothetical center is not itself a contradiction.
No rank-21/23/29 exhaustive classification applicable here was found; absence
from a numerical catalogue is not used as evidence of nonexistence.
