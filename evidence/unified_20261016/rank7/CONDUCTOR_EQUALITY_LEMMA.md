# Equality in the local compression bound forces conductor level

Status: theoretical lemma proved below; applications to induction data are under
independent review. This is a necessary condition, not an existence theorem.

Let `rho` be a finite-dimensional unitary representation of
`SL_2(Z/NZ)`, let `U=<T>`, and let `P` be the orthogonal projection
onto the `U`-fixed space. Suppose `w` is `U`-fixed and transforms by a
Dirichlet character `chi` under the diagonal torus. Write `f=cond(chi)`.
If

    ||P S w||^2 = ||w||^2/f,

then the cyclic subrepresentation generated by `w` factors through
`SL_2(Z/fZ)`. In particular, `T^f` acts trivially on `S w`.
If `chi` is trivial (`f=1`), `w` is invariant under the entire group.

## Proof

We refine the proof of Lemma 4.1 of A. Schopieray, *The dimension block of a
spherical fusion category*, arXiv:2609.35287v1.

First suppose `N=l^a` and `chi=alpha` is nontrivial of conductor `l^c`.
Every irreducible representation with a nonzero `U`-fixed vector embeds in
`C[Omega_a]`, where `Omega_a` is the set of primitive vectors over
`Z/l^aZ`. In the source's notation its torus eigenspace is

    C F_alpha direct_sum Sigma_alpha,

where `F_alpha(p,q)=alpha(q)[q is a unit]`, and `Sigma_alpha` is supported
on `q` nonunit. The compression maps `F_alpha` to

    G_alpha(p,q)=alpha(-p)[q=0 mod l^c],

and maps `Sigma_alpha` to the one-dimensional span of `F_conjugate(alpha)`.
The latter map has norm `l^(-c/2)`, and its adjoint has image the span
of `G_conjugate(alpha)` (a nonzero central scalar is immaterial).
Consequently equality in the compression norm holds exactly in the
span of `F_alpha` and `G_conjugate(alpha)`. Both functions are pullbacks
of functions on `Omega_c`; the space of such pullbacks is a group
subrepresentation factoring through `SL_2(Z/l^cZ)`. If an irreducible
summand contains a nonzero equality vector, that whole summand lies in
this subrepresentation by irreducibility.

For a trivial local character the norm bound is one. Equality means
`S w` is `U`-fixed. Thus `w` is fixed by both upper and lower unipotents.
These generate `SL_2(Z/l^aZ)`, so `w` is group invariant. In an
irreducible summand this forces the trivial representation.

For arbitrary `N`, use the Chinese remainder decomposition and decompose
unitarily into irreducible tensor products. As in the cited proof,
the `U`-fixed torus-character space and its compression are tensor
products of their local counterparts. The squared norm deficits are
nonnegative. Equality of the total norm forces every nonzero
irreducible component of `w` to attain each local bound. More explicitly,
the maximal singular subspace of a tensor product of contractions is
the tensor product of their maximal singular subspaces; this follows
by diagonalizing their positive operators `A^*A`. At primes dividing
`f`, the local irreducible factors through its character conductor;
at the other primes it is trivial. Hence every irreducible summand
supporting `w` factors through level `f`, as claimed.

## Useful corollary for a twist-one block

Suppose a known collection of twist-one basis vectors spans `L`, contains
`w`, and its compression satisfies

    projection_L(S w) = lambda w,  |lambda|^2=1/f.

If `w` has torus character of conductor `f`, the global compression
bound and this displayed equality force `P S w=lambda w`. Thus the
lemma applies even if there are additional unknown twist-one simples.
Every coordinate `Y` of `S w` which is nonzero has twist satisfying
`theta_Y^f=1`.

For the two formal-codegree-five constituents in the rank-eight rings
R8_2_820 and R8_2_869, the difference vector has torus character `(5/.)`
and compressed eigenvalue `delta/sqrt(5)`, where `delta=+1` or `-1`.
The lemma therefore forces every simple center object whose restriction
multiplicities on basis labels 6 and 7 differ to have twist of order
dividing five. Strengthening this to a specified quadratic-residue class
uses the explicit degree-three constituents of the level-five induced
representation and is being checked separately.

## A sharper elementary consequence at conductor five

Let `chi=(5/.)` and suppose `P S w=delta*w/sqrt(5)` for
`delta in {+1,-1}`. Then

    (T-1)(T^2 - ((-1+delta*sqrt(5))/2)*T + 1) S w = 0.

Indeed the equality lemma reduces the cyclic representation to level five.
In the primitive-vector model, the indicated eigenspace of the compression
is spanned by `F+delta*sqrt(5)*G`. Put `zeta=exp(2*pi*i/5)`. For each
nonzero second coordinate `q`, write `x=p/q`. The function obtained by
applying `S` is

    chi(q) * (chi(x) + delta*sqrt(5)*1_{x=0}).

Its discrete Fourier coefficient at `k!=0` is
`sqrt(5)*(chi(k)+delta)`, up to the harmless common factor `chi(q)`;
this is the elementary quadratic Gauss sum over F5. Hence the only
nonconstant Fourier modes have `chi(k)=delta`. The `q=0` orbit contributes
only a `T`-fixed vector. The two surviving nontrivial eigenvalues are
`zeta^a,zeta^(-a)`, where `chi(a)=delta`, whose sum is
`(-1+delta*sqrt(5))/2`. This proves the displayed polynomial.

For a high center simple in the rank-eight application, the known low
block already attains the entire allowed norm of the twist-one
projection. Thus a nonzero coordinate outside that block cannot have
twist one. Such a coordinate has a primitive fifth-root twist in exactly
the stated quadratic-residue class. Its normalized relative trace to
Q(sqrt(5)) is therefore `(-1+delta*sqrt(5))/4`; at power `k` it is
`(-1+delta*chi(k)*sqrt(5))/4` if `5` does not divide `k`, and one otherwise.

This restriction has been used only as a necessary condition in exploratory
linear systems. No categorification is asserted or excluded by those systems.
