# R7_3_1022: a general exclusion by inverse Morita duality

## Statement and scope

The fusion ring **R7_3_1022 has no categorification over C**. The argument
uses the already verified C3/V4 Morita reduction and closes its last case.
No pivotality, sphericality, pseudo-unitarity, or unitarity is assumed.
No uniqueness theorem for a Haagerup–Izumi category is needed.

The new ingredient is a general form of Izumi's commutation-cocycle
argument. Its final obstruction is Schopieray's classification of
near-group fusion rings for elementary abelian 2-groups, which applies
to arbitrary complex fusion categories.

## 1. A commutation lemma for the Klein-four Haagerup–Izumi rules

Let E be a complex fusion category with invertibles H=V4, trivial pointed
associator, and four other simples h rho, h in H, where rho is self-dual and

    rho h = h rho,       rho^2 = 1 + sum(h in H) h rho.

Choose coherent multiplication isomorphisms for H and isomorphisms
w_h: h rho -> rho h. Their failure to respect multiplication is a scalar
2-cocycle xi on H. Its class is independent of the choices. It is Izumi's
invariant c_{1,2}, since the action on H is the identity.

**Lemma.** The class [xi] in H^2(V4,C*) is zero.

**Proof.** Take the regular algebra P=sum_{h in H}h and its Morita dual
E_P^*. In the bicategory of separable algebras, write i for the canonical
P-module 1-morphism and i-bar for its adjoint, with i-bar i=sum_h h.
Set Z=i rho i-bar. Frobenius reciprocity identifies End(Z) with the twisted
group algebra C_xi[H]; multiplication is obtained by moving the h strings
past rho using w_h. This is an algebraic calculation: it uses only
associativity and rigidity, and is exactly the calculation in Izumi,
Proposition 9.6, before his final appeal to a C*-classification theorem.

Suppose [xi] is nonzero. A nontrivial twisted group algebra of V4 is M_2(C),
so Z=2 sigma for a simple sigma of the Morita dual. The pointed dual of
Vec_H by P is Rep(H)=Vec_(H-hat), giving four invertibles chi. The fusion
calculation is

    Z^2 = i rho (sum_h h) rho i-bar
        = 4 i rho^2 i-bar
        = 4 sum_chi chi + 16 Z.

Dividing the corresponding equality of Grothendieck classes by four gives

    sigma^2 = sum_chi chi + 8 sigma.

The dual has exactly these five simples: its pointed part consists of the
four chi, and the free H-orbit of noninvertibles has the one projective
stabilizer representation of degree two. Thus it is a categorification of
the near-group ring R(V4,8). Schopieray's Theorem 1.1 says that the only
categorifiable levels for V4 are 0 and 4. Contradiction. This proves the
lemma. In particular, the C*-hypothesis in the statement of Izumi's
Proposition 9.6 is unnecessary when its last step is replaced by this
published general theorem. QED.

For either 2-cocycle beta defining a regular H algebra, the same lemma
implies that its noninvertible bimodule orbit splits into four simples.
Indeed the stabilizer of rho in H x H is the diagonal H. The two algebra
twists restrict to beta and beta^{-1} on this stabilizer, so cancel; the
remaining projective class is [xi]=0. Each of the four simple projective
representations has degree one. Its underlying object is sum_h h rho and
its dimension in the Morita dual is FPdim(rho).

## 2. The already established reduction for R7_3_1022

Suppose C categorifies R7_3_1022. Its dimensions are

    1,1,1,3,d,d,d,       d=4+2 sqrt(5),       d^2=4+8d.

The existing C3/V4 reduction constructs a Morita category D with twelve
invertibles K=A4, trivial pointed associator, and three noninvertibles of
dimension d. The latter have stabilizer H=V4. For one of the two algebra
structures B on sum_{h in H}h, the Morita dual D'=D_B^* has twelve
invertibles forming untwisted A4 and twelve noninvertibles of dimension

    t=d/2=2+sqrt(5),       t^2=1+4t.

The exact quadratic A4 ring classification leaves only

    rho g = g rho  (g in A4),
    rho^2 = 1 + sum(h in V4) h rho.

The other normalized k=4 ring contains the forbidden rank-two ring
rho^2=1+4rho. Thus the fusion subcategory E generated by V4 and rho has
precisely the rules in Section 1, with trivial pointed associator.
Consequently its commutation class [xi] vanishes.

## 3. The inverse algebra is forced inside the pointed part

View M=D_B as the category of right B-modules, and let m=B, the simple
regular right B-module. It is simple because
End_B(B)=Hom_D(1,B)=C. The category D'=B-bimod(D) acts on M by relative
tensor product. Its internal end Q=End_{D'}(m) is a connected separable
algebra and the Morita double-centralizer equivalence gives

    (D')_Q^* equivalent to D (up to the harmless opposite convention).

For a simple B-bimodule X, the multiplicity of X (or X* according to
convention) in Q is

    dim Hom_M(B, B tensor_B X)
      = dim Hom_{right B}(B,X)
      = dim Hom_D(1,X).

Exactly four simple B-bimodules contain 1 in their underlying object:
they are the four invertibles supported on H. Each has underlying object
B and contains 1 with multiplicity one. This remains true for either
algebra cocycle on B: the pointed dual of Vec_H is Rep(H), with its four
one-dimensional characters. Every other bimodule has support in a
nonidentity H-double coset or in a noninvertible orbit, so does not
contain 1. Therefore Q is the sum of these four invertibles, each once.
They form the unique subgroup H'=V4 of the invertible A4 of D'.

In particular Q is a regular H' algebra with one of its two 2-cocycle
structures. This conclusion follows from the actual Morita context; it
is not an assumption that an arbitrary algebra object has pointed
support.

## 4. Contradiction in the inverse dual

The twelve noninvertibles of D' form a free A4-orbit. Under H' on the
left and right they divide into three orbits of size four, indexed by
A4/H'. On an orbit represented by g rho, the H' x H' stabilizer is a
graph of conjugation by g. Its projective class is the transport of xi,
together with the quotient between beta and its conjugate by g, where
beta is the 2-cocycle of Q.

The first class vanishes by Section 1. The second vanishes because
H^2(V4,C*) has two elements and hence every automorphism of V4 acts
trivially on it. Thus **all three stabilizer classes are trivial**,
including for the nontrivial algebra structure on Q. Each orbit gives
four simples of (D')_Q^*, each with underlying dimension 4t and dual
dimension t after division by FPdim(Q)=4.

The inverse dual therefore has twelve noninvertible simples of dimension
t. But it is equivalent to D, which has exactly three noninvertible
simples, each of dimension d=2t. Since t>1, neither set is pointed, and
the rank and dimension multisets cannot agree. This contradiction
excludes R7_3_1022 over C.
