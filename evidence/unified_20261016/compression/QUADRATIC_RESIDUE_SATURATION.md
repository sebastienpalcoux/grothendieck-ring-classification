# Primitive twist residue classes under quadratic saturation

Let rho be a unitary congruence representation of SL2(Z), S=rho(s), T=rho(t), and P the projection onto the T-fixed space. Let chi be a primitive quadratic Dirichlet character of positive fundamental discriminant f, so chi(-1)=1 and its Gauss sum is sqrt(f). Suppose w is T-fixed, has torus character chi, and

    P S w = delta w/sqrt(f),  delta=+1 or -1.

Then the conductor equality lemma makes the cyclic representation generated by w a level-f representation. Moreover, if the component of S w with T-eigenvalue exp(2 pi i k/f) is nonzero and gcd(k,f)=1, then

    chi(k)=delta.

Only this primitive-f statement is asserted here; nonprimitive twist orders can also occur.

Proof. Write f as the product of its prime powers f_i. The primitive quadratic chi is the product of local characters chi_i of conductor f_i. The conductor equality lemma embeds the relevant local irreducible constituents in the primitive-vector permutation representation at precisely that level, and its maximal-singular subspace is spanned by

    F_i(p,q)=chi_i(q) [q unit],
    G_i(p,q)=chi_i(-p) [q=0].

On this space P_i S_i sends F_i to G_i and G_i to chi_i(-1)F_i/f_i. Its eigenvalues are lambda_i=epsilon_i tau_i/f_i, where epsilon_i=+1 or -1 and tau_i is the local quadratic Gauss sum, tau_i²=chi_i(-1)f_i. An eigenvector is F_i+c_i G_i with c_i=1/lambda_i=epsilon_i chi_i(-1)tau_i.

A primitive f_i-th T-eigenvalue can only be supported on q-unit orbits: if q is not a unit, translation of p by q has order strictly less than f_i. On a q-unit orbit put x=p/q. Up to a common nonzero scalar, S_i(F_i+c_i G_i) restricts to chi_i(x)+c_i 1_{x=0}. Its Fourier coefficient at a unit k is

    chi_i(-1) tau_i chi_i(k)+c_i
      = chi_i(-1) tau_i (chi_i(k)+epsilon_i).

Thus its primitive local modes have chi_i(k)=epsilon_i.

Globally the maximal-singular space is a tensor product of these two-dimensional spaces. It has a basis of their S-compression eigenvectors. The global eigenvalue hypothesis restricts this basis to products with

    product(epsilon_i) product(tau_i)=delta sqrt(f).

Put C=product chi_i(f/f_i). The Chinese-remainder Gauss-sum formula gives sqrt(f)=C product(tau_i), so product(epsilon_i)=delta C. For a primitive global mode write its eigenvalue as product exp(2 pi i k_i/f_i). Its exponent satisfies k= sum k_i(f/f_i) mod f, hence

    chi(k)=C product chi_i(k_i)=C product epsilon_i=delta.

Linear combinations preserve this common support condition, proving the assertion.

For f=12 the allowed primitive roots have normalized relative trace delta sqrt(3)/2 to Q(sqrt(3)); for f=24 the trace is delta sqrt(6)/4 to Q(sqrt(6)). These follow from the character decomposition of the primitive root sum, using mu(12)=mu(24)=0 and the positive quadratic Gauss sums.

Scope: this is a necessary representation-theoretic restriction. It does not establish a central lift, construct a category, or itself change the status of any census case. Each application must verify the torus character and exact saturation independently.
