# A nonsplit extension realizes R8_2_37

Status: theoretical construction, with exact source-tensor identification.
The category constructed below is unitary. No classification of nonunitary
2D2 categories is used or asserted.

## Input theorems and exact normalization

1. Grossman–Izumi–Snyder, *Graded extensions of generalized Haagerup
   categories*, arXiv:2201.11901v1, Theorem 4.1 (printed p. 24), constructs
   extensions of the known generalized-Haagerup category for G = C4.
   The factor realization in §3, Lemmas 3.1–3.2, gives automorphisms
   alpha_j, beta, an endomorphism rho, and unitaries u,v with

       beta alpha_j = alpha_j beta,
       beta rho = Ad(u) alpha_1 rho beta,
       beta² = Ad(v) alpha_{1+z},  z in {0,2}.

   Here the alpha_j form an honest C4 action; alpha_2 is outer.
   Lemma 3.2 gives characters chi,mu with alpha_j(u)=chi(j)u and
   alpha_j(v)=mu(j)v. Equation (3.18), printed p. 17, gives
   chi(2)=epsilon_2(1)=-1. Equation (3.10) gives mu(1)²=chi(1)²,
   so mu(2)=-1. Thus alpha_2(u)=-u and alpha_2(v)=-v.

2. Grossman, *Fusion categories associated to subfactors with index
   3+sqrt(5)*, arXiv:1612.07185, §4.1 (printed pp. 37–38), describes the
   2D2 category Q inside End(P), where P=M crossed_{alpha_2} C2. Write
   lambda for the implementing involution. The extensions are

       alpha_tilde_j(lambda)=(-1)^j lambda,
       rho_tilde(lambda)=lambda.

   They generate Q with simple sectors 1,g,r,gr, where g=[alpha_tilde_1],
   r=[rho_tilde], and

       g²=1,  gr=rg,  r²=1+2r+2gr.

The second construction applies to the neutral C4 Haagerup category in
the first realization: it is tensor equivalent to the same known category,
and the crossed-product construction is intrinsic to the algebra
1+alpha_2. The relation alpha_2²=id guarantees that P is a factor.

Primary sources:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.11901 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.07185.

## Transport the odd automorphism through the crossed product

Because beta commutes with alpha_2, it extends to an automorphism b of P
by b|_M=beta and b(lambda)=lambda. The two crucial equalities are

       b rho_tilde = Ad(u) alpha_tilde_1 rho_tilde b,
       b² = Ad(v) alpha_tilde_{1+z}.

They already hold on M by the input relations. On lambda the first
right-hand side is u(-lambda)u*=lambda, since alpha_2(u)=-u; the
second is v(-lambda)v*=lambda, since 1+z is odd and alpha_2(v)=-v.
Thus these are actual endomorphism equalities on the generators of P,
not merely proposed fusion-rule relations.

Let a=[b]. Because [alpha_tilde_{1+z}]=g, the equalities imply

       a²=g,  a⁴=1,  ara^{-1}=gr=a²r,

or equivalently ra=a^{-1}r. In particular the simple sector a has order
four. The full additive, subobject-closed tensor category generated by
b and rho_tilde is a unitary fusion category. Its simple sectors are

       1,a,a²,a³,r,ar,a²r,a³r.

For completeness, these are eight distinct sectors. The four invertibles
are distinct since a²=g is nontrivial. The four noninvertibles are
distinct because a^i r=a^j r would, by Frobenius reciprocity, put
a^{j-i} in rr*=1+2r+2gr, whose only invertible constituent is 1.
The four noninvertibles have dimension 2+sqrt(5), so cannot coincide
with invertibles. The displayed relations show the list is closed under
tensor product and duals. Hence it gives the entire category.

All associativity, unit, duality and positivity constraints are inherited
from End_0(P). There is no need to select a coherent order-two tensor
autoequivalence: the extension is nonsplit, with a²=g rather than 1.

## Complete multiplication and census identification

For i,j modulo 4 the multiplication is

       a^i a^j = a^{i+j},
       a^i(a^j r)=a^{i+j}r,
       (a^i r)a^j=a^{i-j}r,
       (a^i r)(a^j r)=a^{i-j}+2a^{i-j}r+2a^{i-j+2}r.

The ordered model basis (1,a,a²,a³,r,ar,a²r,a³r) maps to zero-based
census labels

       (0,1,7,2,5,3,6,4).

`verify_r37_model.py` compares all 512 structure constants with the
restored census tensor and independently checks its ledger tensor hash,
the duals, associativity, and the faithful C2 grading. It also binds the
structural hypotheses for the scoped R170 obstruction in the companion
note. These finite checks identify the established category; they do
not substitute for the categorical construction above.

Conclusion: R8_2_37 admits a unitary categorification.
