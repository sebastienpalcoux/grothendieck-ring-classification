# A theoretical Galois pseudo-unitarity reduction for the Fibonacci pair

**Theorem.** Every complex fusion categorification of R8_2_190 or R8_2_489 admits a spherical structure and is Galois conjugate to a pseudo-unitary category. For R8_2_489 its pointed C2 subcategory has trivial associator; hence 1+g is a connected separable algebra. This theorem does not decide categorifiability of either ring.

Both rings have the commutative presentation

    g^2=1, tau^2=1+tau, x^2=g^epsilon+tau(x+gx),

with basis g^i tau^j x^k (i,j,k in {0,1}). Here epsilon=1 for R190, epsilon=0 for R489, and x*=g^epsilon x. All products g times a basis element and tau times x or gx are simple.

## Spherical section, without a sign search

Let C be a hypothetical categorification and S its canonical sphericalization. Its central sign object e has spherical dimension -1. Work in the signed based algebra K(S)/(e+1). Every original simple has two lifts with opposite, nonzero spherical dimensions.

Choose the lift g of the original invertible with dimension +1. Then g^2=1. Choose a lift tau; it is self-dual because the dual has the same nonzero spherical dimension. Thus its square has unit coefficient +1 and has the form tau^2=1+s tau, s=+1 or -1. Replacing tau by its other lift makes s=+1.

Choose either lift x. Define the other lifts by the actual simple products g tau, gx, tau x, g tau x. All these products have source multiplicity one. Left and right products by g, and the products x tau and tau x, have the same underlying simple and the same nonzero dimension, so they coincide as lifts. In particular the signed generators commute, and

    x*=g^epsilon x.

The lift equality in this last formula follows from equality of dimensions and the source duality. The unit coefficient of xx* is +1, so the coefficient of g^epsilon in x^2 is +1. The signed ring is therefore generated by

    g^2=1, tau^2=1+tau,
    x^2=g^epsilon+a tau x+b tau gx,  a,b in {+1,-1}.

There is no unenumerated sign parameter: the eight chosen basis elements are the displayed monomials and the three relations determine all their products.

If epsilon=1, duality fixes x^2 and g but exchanges tau x and tau gx. Hence a=b.

Suppose epsilon=0 and a=-b. Put t=dim(tau), z=dim(x), and delta=dim(C), the canonical global dimension. Since dim(g)=1,

    t^2=t+1, z^2=1,
    delta=2(1+t^2)(1+z^2)=4(t+2).

Take a source ring character with g-value 1, tau-value t, and x-value d satisfying d^2=1+2td. Its formal codegree is

    f_E=2(t+2)(1+d^2).

The source formal-codegree divisibility theorem applied inside the sphericalization requires delta/f_E to be an algebraic integer: both its global dimension and the epsilon=+1 source formal codegrees acquire the same factor two. This is exactly the signed-sphericalization use of Ostrik, Pivotal fusion categories of rank 3, arXiv:1309.4822, Theorem 2.13.

But y=delta/f_E=2/(1+d^2) is not an algebraic integer. Indeed d has irreducible polynomial

    f(X)=X^4-2X^3-6X^2+2X+1,

irreducible modulo three. Moreover

    d=((d^2)^2-6d^2+1)/(2(d^2-1)),

so Q(y)=Q(d^2)=Q(d), of degree four. Elimination gives its primitive minimal polynomial

    5Y^4-20Y^3+25Y^2-10Y+1,

whose leading coefficient is not one. The excluded alternative a=-b is therefore impossible.

We have a=b for both rings. If a=b=-1, replace x and its three g/tau translates by their other lifts. This makes a=b=+1. The signed multiplication tensor is now exactly the source tensor. For every channel, equality T=N together with N=N^++N^- implies N^-=0. Thus the eight chosen lifts form a full fusion subcategory of S, mapped equivalently to C by the forgetful functor. This is the required spherical section.

## Galois pseudo-unitarity

The selected section has dim(g)=1, t^2=t+1, and z^2=1+2tz. Hence z is a root of f, and t=(z^2-1)/(2z). Irreducibility shows that all four possible dimension characters form a single Galois orbit. The positive character is

    dim(tau)=phi, dim(x)=phi+sqrt(phi^2+1).

A field automorphism sending the selected character to this positive one makes the spherical category pseudo-unitary. No unitarity assertion is made.

## R489: the actual regular C2 algebra

In the resulting pseudo-unitary category, all simples are self-dual. The exact ring congruences are

    x^3=x+tau+g tau (mod2),
    (g tau)^3=g (mod2).

A self-dual V with indicator nu has a homogeneous nondegenerate form on V^n of sign nu^n; a simple with the opposite indicator occurs with even multiplicity. Since tau^2=1+tau, its indicator is +1. The first cube identity therefore gives nu(x)=nu(g tau)=+1, and the second gives nu(g)=+1.

For Vec_(C2)^omega with positive spherical dimensions, the nontrivial associator cohomology class has indicator -1 on g. Thus omega is trivial and 1+g has the regular connected separable algebra structure. Existence of this algebra persists under inverse Galois conjugation.

This conclusion does not give a half-braiding on g. The right-module category and its Morita dual can be used, but a tensor de-equivariantization cannot be assumed.
