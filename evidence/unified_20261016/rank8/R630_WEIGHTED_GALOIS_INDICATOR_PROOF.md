# A weighted Galois third-indicator obstruction for R8_2_630

**Theorem.** R8_2_630 has no complex fusion categorification.

The contradiction is the strict triangle inequality 4>1+1, obtained from a saturated quadratic dimension block. There is no enumeration of possible center rows, optimization, assumed center identification, or assumption that a one-dimensional cubic rotation has eigenvalue one.

## 2. The quadratic block saturates at conductor forty

Work with the canonical positive spherical structure of the hypothetical pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate. By Ostrik's Theorem 2.13, the constituents A_E of I(1) have dimension D/f_E; because the source ring is commutative each has multiplicity one. Let J be the constituent for f_E=D'. Then

    dim J=D/D'=19+6s.

Every constituent of I(1) has twist one. Its multiplicity vector m satisfies Sm=m for the normalized center S-matrix with S_00=1/D. Consequently its support is Galois-stable, with positive Galois signs on that support. The image of the central unit under a cyclotomic automorphism sigma has dimension D/sigma(D). The unique constituent of dimension1 is the central unit; the unique constituent of dimension D/D' is J. Thus the central unit has exactly the two-point Galois orbit {1,J}, and

    w=e_1−e_J

transforms under the diagonal torus by the primitive quadratic character chi_40 of K=Q(sqrt10).

In the known twist-one I(1) block, the 1,J submatrix has diagonal1/D and off-diagonal1/D'. The remaining entries in the unit column are 1/f_E with rational f_E; Galois symmetry shows that the entries in the J column agree. Hence

    projection_I(1) S w = (1/D−1/D')w
                        = −s w/20 = −w/sqrt40.           (S)

The squared compression norm is exactly1/40. Schopieray's local compression bound (arXiv:2609.35287v1, Lemma4.1) and the proved equality refinement in ../compression/CONDUCTOR_EQUALITY_LEMMA.md imply that the cyclic modular representation generated by w factors through level40. In particular T^40 S w=S w. This refinement applies to the prime-power factors8 and5, including their nontrivial primitive quadratic characters, and to all other prime factors with trivial local character; no prime-conductor assumption is made.

For a center simple A of dimension z, its coordinate in S w is

    z/D−sigma(z/D),

where sigma flips K. Therefore every A with z/D nonrational satisfies theta_A^40=1. This is precisely the hypothesis needed for the saturated Galois indicator lemma. The argument below would only need the assertion outside I(1).

## 3. One weighted Galois transformation

Set

    ell(a+b s)=3a−10b.

Then ell(D)=0 and the source weights are

    (ell(d_i))_(i=0..7)=(3,3,−4,−1,−1,−4,2,2).

The primitive quadratic character chi_40 takes value+1 at3: equivalently the automorphism zeta_40→zeta_40^3 fixes Q(sqrt10). Apply SATURATED_GALOIS_INDICATOR_LEMMA.md with f=40 and k=3. It gives the exact complex identity

    sum_(i!=0) ell(d_i) nu_3(X_i)=0.                    (G)

Explicitly, the weighted first induction trace is

    sum_A ell(dim A) dim A theta_A = 3D.

Take a cyclotomic automorphism tau that fixes K and raises 40th roots to their third powers. Such an automorphism extends to the full twist field by the Chinese remainder construction in the lemma, even when that field has a factor divisible by3. It fixes the coefficients of this identity. Rational-ratio terms have ell(dim A)=0; all remaining high terms have theta_A^40=1 by (S), and low terms have theta_A=1. Applying tau therefore replaces the weighted first trace by the weighted third trace exactly. Ng–Schauenburg's indicator formula gives (G).

## 4. The strict triangle contradiction

For each simple X_i, nu_3(X_i) is the trace of an order-three pivotal rotation on Hom(1,X_i^3). Equation (C) shows that the only nonzero nonunit terms in (G) are labels3,4,5, each on a one-dimensional space. Thus each of nu_3(X3),nu_3(X4),nu_3(X5) is a cube root of unity and has modulus one. The weighted identity reads

    nu_3(X3)+nu_3(X4)+4nu_3(X5)=0.

Its first two terms have sum of absolute value at most2; the last has absolute value4. This is impossible. The hypothetical pseudo-unitary category does not exist, and the audited Galois scope bridge proves the stated general-complex exclusion.

No reality claim about these third indicators occurs in the proof. In particular it does not assert that a self-dual object with a one-dimensional cubic invariant space has third indicator1.
