# General exclusion of R8_2_342 by a forced PSU(3)_5 Morita dual

**Theorem.** No fusion category over C has based ring R8_2_342.

This theorem is independent of pivotality, sphericality, pseudo-unitarity and unitarity. Its finite input is a complete reconstruction of the Morita dual, not a list of isolated necessary Gram conditions.

## 1. A forced regular C2 module

Suppose C is a categorification. In the zero-based census labelling, g=5 is an involution and fixes simples 3 and 7. The subcategory generated by g has the form Vec_C2^ω. Tensoring it on either fixed simple gives a one-simple-object module category, so ω is a coboundary. Consequently A=1⊕g is an actual connected separable algebra. Let M=C_A and let E be its Morita dual, with composition convention chosen for the right action on M.

The module simples are

\[
(0,0),(1,0),(3,+),(3,-),(4,0),(7,+),(7,-).
\]

The labels 0,1,4 stand for free g-orbits; 3 and 7 split into two modules each. On the two split objects, the antisymmetric parts of the action matrices form a signed two-dimensional representation T of the source ring. Each entry has the same parity as its corresponding source multiplicity and absolute value at most that multiplicity; duality takes T_i to T_i^t. The complete signed representation domain has one class modulo changing signs on the two split labels. A representative is

\[
\begin{aligned}
T_0=T_5&=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},&
T_1=T_2&=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},\\
T_3&=\begin{pmatrix}-1&1\\1&-1\end{pmatrix},&
T_4=T_6&=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},&
T_7&=\begin{pmatrix}-1&-1\\-1&-1\end{pmatrix}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The source-bound verifier derives the seven-object module matrices from these and checks every module equation. Completeness is established by certified annihilating polynomials of the source fusion matrices and the three ring generators 1,4,6: their bounded signed domains leave eight generator tuples, whose reconstructed full representations give exactly the one stated gauge class. No two-generator cutoff is used.

## 2. Complete Morita reconstruction

Write L_i for the seven-by-seven left action matrices and m_0 for the module corresponding to A. The action of g on M is the identity. Hence the distinguished induction Gram matrix is 2I_7. A nonnegative integral factorization of 2I_7 must have exactly two copies of each standard basis column: off-diagonal zero entries force every column to have support one, and the diagonal two can only be 1²+1². No simple dual object has zero restriction. It follows that E has exactly fourteen simples, two above each of the seven module labels. Their dimensions are

\[
1^2,(1+\sqrt2)^4,(2+\sqrt2)^4,(2+2\sqrt2)^2,(3+2\sqrt2)^2.
\]

Every dual action matrix R_y is integral and nonnegative and commutes with all L_i. Its first column is a standard basis vector e_j. For each module label a, the linking-category associativity identity is

\[
Q_a:=\sum_i (L_i)_{aa}L_i
   =\sum_{y\in\operatorname{Irr}(E)}
      R_y[:,a]R_y[:,a]^t.
\]

In particular every entry satisfies the finite exact bound

\[
0\le (R_y)_{ba}\le\lfloor\sqrt{(Q_a)_{bb}}\rfloor.
\]

The common matrix commutant has dimension eleven. Specifying a first column leaves four free physical matrix entries. Exhausting those entries within the displayed integer bounds gives candidate counts

\[
(2,4,2,2,2,2,2)
\]

for the seven standard first columns. The first domain contains the identity and a unique nontrivial permutation P with P²=I. This is the action of the second invertible u in E. Multiplication on the right by P pairs the candidates freely in each first-column domain; hence multiplication by u pairs the two actual dual simples above that column. After fixing the unit, there are exactly two joint candidate tuples. Exactly one satisfies all seven linking Gram identities. Thus the entire set of fourteen action matrices is forced, up to the already accounted label choices.

We next reconstruct the full fusion tensor, despite the three-dimensional kernel of this action. For a product yz, any coefficient vector n satisfies

\[
\sum_w n_wR_w=R_yR_z,\qquad
n_1=\delta_{z,y^*},\qquad
n_u=\delta_{zu,y^*}.
\]

The first column bounds each coefficient by the corresponding entry of R_yR_z. The coefficient equations with the two reciprocity conditions have rank twelve; complete bounded enumeration leaves unique products whenever the first factor has label

\[
0,1,4,5,6,7,10,11,12,13.
\]

These ten forced fusion matrices generate the entire fourteen-dimensional based algebra: the fourteen words

\[
1,u,4,5,6,7,10,11,12,13,44,46,4\,11,4\,13
\]

have linearly independent class vectors. Associativity therefore determines every other fusion matrix as the unique rational linear combination of the corresponding word matrices. The verifier checks all resulting entries against their original complete nonnegative product domains and checks all 14^4 associativity equations.

In the final certificate labelling the subset

\[
B_0=(0,2,5,6,9,10,13)
\]

is closed and is the neutral component of a faithful C2 grading; its complement consists precisely of its products with u. The restricted module is regular, since these seven objects carry m_0 bijectively to the seven module simples (using dual labels if the opposite matrix convention is adopted). Conjugation by u permutes the local neutral labels by

\[
(0,1,3,2,4,6,5).
\]

This is the duality permutation of the neutral ring.

## 3. Identification of the neutral category and the action

Let B⊂E be the actual fusion subcategory corresponding to B_0. Its ring is PSU(3)_5. To make this identification independently checkable, the certificate compares the complete fusion matrix of Y_1 with Bonderson–Rowell–Wang, *On Realizing Modular Data*, §2.4, under the map from their basis (1,W,Y_1,Y_2,V,U_1,U_2) to our local neutral basis

\[
(0,4,5,6,1,3,2).
\]

The seven vectors obtained from the unit by successive powers of that matrix are independent. Thus one matched generator fixes the entire rank-seven tensor, rather than merely its dimension profile. The checker also verifies that the neutral based automorphism group is exactly C2, generated by duality, and that its adjoint subcategory is all of B. In particular U(B)=1.

Kazhdan–Wenzl reconstruction applies without any braiding or pivotality hypothesis. A precise general-complex statement is Feng–Ming–Rowell, arXiv:2202.00121v2, Theorem 5.1: every rigid monoidal semisimple C-category with SU(N)_k fusion rules is a quantum type-A category with a grading three-cocycle twist. Apply it to B⊠Vec_C3, whose based ring is SU(3)_5. Its adjoint subcategory is B. Restricting the resulting equivalence to the adjoint subcategory removes the C3 associator twist. Hence B is a Galois conjugate of the standard quantum category PSU(3)_5.

Edie-Michell, *Auto-equivalences of the modular tensor categories of type A, B, C and G*, arXiv:2002.03220v2, Theorem 1.1 and §3, proves that the gauge-autoequivalence kernel of SU(3)_5 is trivial. The standard modular category SU(3)_5 factors as PSU(3)_5⊠P, where P is a nondegenerate pointed C3 category. A tensor autoequivalence of PSU(3)_5 fixing every simple label extends by the identity on P. Its extension fixes every SU(3)_5 label, hence is isomorphic to the identity; restriction proves the same assertion for PSU(3)_5. Galois transport gives this assertion for every B under consideration.

Therefore the based duality permutation determines a unique tensor autoequivalence class of B. Since U(B)=1, the group of monoidal natural automorphisms of Id_B is trivial. All comparison isomorphisms and coherence isomorphisms for an action of C2 realizing this class are consequently unique when they exist. Such an action exists in the present situation. Thus it is equivalent, after a Galois conjugation, to the charge-conjugation action occurring in the known rank-eight centralizer construction of R8_2_666.

## 4. Reflection back gives a contradiction

The regular restriction M|_B≃B identifies m_0 with the regular generator. Its internal endomorphism algebra over E is exactly 1⊕u: the first-column multiplicities show that these are the only two dual simples admitting a nonzero map at m_0, each once. This is an actual associative connected separable algebra, so the associator on the pointed subcategory generated by u is trivializable. The grading E=B⊕Bu is therefore the crossed product for its coherent conjugation C2 action. The dual category of its regular extension module is the corresponding equivariantization B^C2. This is the usual crossed-product/equivariantization Morita equivalence, here applied to the forced internal-end algebra, rather than inferred from a based-ring grading alone.

The existing R8_2_666 category has Tannakian central boson and its C2 de-equivariantization is a Galois conjugate of PSU(3)_5, by Bonderson–Rowell–Wang §2.4. The two swapped pairs of de-equivariantized simples show that its action is precisely the same nontrivial based duality action. The uniqueness just proved therefore implies that the reflected category has based ring R8_2_666. Galois conjugation preserves its integer fusion coefficients.

But R8_2_342 has a non-self-dual pair, labels 1 and 2, while every simple of R8_2_666 is self-dual. The rings cannot be isomorphic. This contradicts Morita reflection back to C and proves the theorem.
