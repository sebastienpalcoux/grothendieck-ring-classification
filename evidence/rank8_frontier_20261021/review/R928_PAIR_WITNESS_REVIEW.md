# Independent review of the R928 paired-semion campaign

The full correlated-pair height-six necessary system survives. Four of the
surviving patterns also have exact nonnegative integer solutions to every
equation. These are positive witnesses for the stated necessary constraints,
not fusion categories.

The already reviewed central-orbit theorem forces a semion over the source
invertible g. Its action on central simples is free. On each of the forty
fixed restriction types the simples occur in pairs with twists theta and
plus or minus i theta. The two orientations give the same complete unordered
pair domain. The exact relative-trace enumeration in
`semion_pair_traces.py/.json` supplies thirty joint six-moment types, including
the zero tail beyond the complete order bound 288. Counts are counts of
actual pairs; their Gram mass is two. Normalizing the field trace does not
divide the number of pairs, so integer pair counts are necessary.

The independent reviewer imports neither `pair_rotation.py` nor
`integer_counts.py`. Starting from the approved complete noncommutative
rotation/STS operator, it reconstructs all 65 exact high multiplicities,
the second/sixth moment zero constraints, and the six forced moments of
each of the five known translated low objects. It then sums full original
columns for every true paired trace type. Auxiliary rotation columns are
retained exactly once. All 873 rows and 2,227 paired columns match the
producer's bound matrix hashes; every rational witness and integer witness
is checked directly against the reconstructed rows, without a solver.

The complete initial fifteen-pattern domain has exact separators for
2, 6, 61, 64, and 199 and exact rational solutions for the other ten. All
five separators are independently checked against every original column.
All ten stronger correlated-pair systems still have exact rational
solutions. Patterns 40, 49, 186, and 191 additionally have exact nonnegative
integer solutions, including all auxiliary counts. The other six integer
feasibility questions remain unresolved; bounded numerical failure is not
treated as a certificate. Modular consistency is likewise not existence.

Canonical independent replay is `verify_r928_pair_witnesses.py`, with
source-bound receipt `r928_pair_witness_review.json`. It can run normally or
with `python -O`. This review endorses the exact positive survival and the
five finite branch eliminations only, not a global exclusion or model.
