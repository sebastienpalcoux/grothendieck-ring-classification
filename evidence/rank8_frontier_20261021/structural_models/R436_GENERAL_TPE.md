# A general-complex obstruction for R8_3_436

This is new frontier evidence. It does not alter the published, correctly scoped
unitary-only argument in `evidence/rank8_m3_20261021`.

**Claim.** No complex fusion category has the source tensor `R8_3_436`.
The new step is that every complex near-group category of type `(C3,3)`
is Galois conjugate to a unitary category. The proof uses the full spherical
parameter theorem of the October 2026 TPE preprint, not an extension of
Izumi's C*-classification assumed without proof.

## 2. Reduction to the positive spherical branch

Thornton, *On braided near-group categories*,
[arXiv:1102.4640](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1102.4640), Theorem 1.3, applies to
near-group categories without a braiding assumption. It supplies a spherical
structure and a field automorphism making its dimensions positive. Apply that
field automorphism to the hypothetical category together with its neutral
subcategory and conjugation functor. All these structures can be expressed by
finite scalar associators, dualities, tensor-functor maps and coherence
equations in a skeleton. Field conjugation preserves those equations and
invertibility of the maps. It preserves the integral fusion tensor and the
permutation of labels induced by the functor. Consequently it suffices to
rule out inversion when the neutral object's spherical dimension is

\[
d=(3+\sqrt{21})/2>0,\qquad d^2=3+3d.
\]

The Fuchs--Runkel--Schweigert theorem in Liu--Palcoux--Ren--Vercleyen,
*Triangular Prism Equations and Categorification*,
[arXiv:2203.06522v5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.06522), Theorem 3.9,
printed p.11, forces `nu_2(X)=+1`: the multiplicity of the self-dual object
`X` in `X* tensor X` is 3. The unit has indicator +1, and the two remaining
simples are a non-self-dual pair. This verifies every self-dual indicator
hypothesis needed below.

## 3. Exhaustive algebraic TPE reduction for C3

Use He--Liu--Lu--Palcoux--Ren, *Triangular prism equations for near-group
categories: algebraic solutions and a unitary conjecture*,
[arXiv:2610.11652v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.11652), Theorem 2.3 and
Sections 4--5. In particular, Theorem 4.12 gives the complete tetrahedral
table in the normalized gauge. This necessity/complete-table assertion is
essential: an existence theorem constructing some categories from scalar
solutions alone would not suffice for the present argument.

Here is the complete parameter reduction, including both bicharacters, both
dimension roots and all choices of the rotation scalar. Write `w` for either
primitive cube root. Every nondegenerate symmetric bicharacter on C3 is
`chi(g,h)=w^(gh)`. The even refinement has `a(0)=1` and `a(1)=a(2)`;
the refinement equation at `(1,1)` forces `a(1)=a(2)=w` since these numbers
are nonzero. Put

\[
t=\beta(0)=-1/d=(3-d)/3,\quad x=\beta(1),\quad y=\beta(2),\quad q=1+w.
\]

The boundary normalization follows from Proposition 2.7, which says the
remaining scalar system is equivalent to its cubic equation (2.7). The
product relation and the two cubic instances `(g,h)=(1,0),(2,0)` give

\[
xy=w/3,\qquad y^2+qtx=tw,\qquad x^2+qty=tw.
\]

Subtracting the last two relations gives
`(y-x)(x+y-qt)=0`. If `x=y`, substitution and squaring the resulting linear
equation give `6t^2-6t+1=0`. But the dimension equation gives
`t^2-t-1/3=0`, so the same expression equals 3. Therefore

\[
x+y=qt,\qquad xy=w/3. \tag{1}
\]

The rotation scalar is forced, rather than chosen:

\[
c=\frac{t}{t+x+y}=\frac1{2+w}=\frac{1-w}{3}.
\]

The executable computes the ideal of all nine cubic equations over Q with
`d^2-3d-3` and `w^2+w+1` adjoined. Its reduced Gröbner basis is exactly
the basis generated by (1) and these two quadratic polynomials. It also
checks all Fourier, product, refinement and rotation equations after this
reduction. No equation is tested numerically. Before gauge equivalence there
are eight scalar points: two choices each for `d`, `w`, and the order of the
two distinct roots in (1). The discriminant identity below proves the last
two roots never coincide. There are no further `a` or `c` branches.

## 4. Every positive-dimensional scalar point is unitary

Since `q^2=w` and `|q|=1`, the normalized variables `u=x/q`, `v=y/q`
are the roots of the real polynomial

\[
z^2-tz+1/3.
\]

Its discriminant is `t^2-4/3=-d/3<0`. Thus `v=conjugate(u)` and
`|u|^2=|v|^2=1/3`. Consequently

\[
\overline{x}=y/w,\qquad \overline{y}=x/w,\qquad \overline{t}=t.
\]

These are exactly the Hermitian scalar conditions (2.9) of the TPE paper.
Corollary 5.5 now unitarizes the complete associator table. Because the table
was obtained from the original category by the complete normalized-gauge
description, this unitarizes that category up to tensor equivalence; it does
not merely produce an unrelated category with the same fusion rules.

This proves the new intermediate result: **every complex `(C3,3)`
near-group category is Galois conjugate to a unitary one.**

## 5. Contradiction with the forced inversion

Izumi, *A Cuntz algebra approach to the classification of near-group
categories*, [arXiv:1512.04288](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.04288),
Theorem 9.1 and Example 9.4, identifies the unitary `(C3,3)` categories
with the known Izumi--Xu categories, up to complex conjugation.

For the final obstruction we use a result about ordinary complex tensor
autoequivalences, so no assumption that the transported functor preserves
a chosen star operation is needed. Grossman--Jordan--Snyder,
*Cyclic extensions of fusion categories via the Brauer--Picard groupoid*,
[arXiv:1211.6414v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.6414), defines `Eq` and `Out`
as ordinary tensor autoequivalences and their quotient by inner ones
(Definition 3.3, Corollary 3.7, and Section 5). Section 4.2, printed p.10,
after Remark 4.10, proves that either known Izumi--Xu category has trivial
`Out`. Inner conjugations fix all labels because its based ring is
commutative. Therefore every ordinary tensor autoequivalence fixes `g`
and `g^2`. The inversion forced in Section 1 is impossible.
