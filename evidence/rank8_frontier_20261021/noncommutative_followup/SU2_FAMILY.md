# A complete all-level quantum-group product/Morita family test

**Result.** None of R8_3_721, R8_3_928, R8_3_941, R8_3_1154 has the global
FP dimension of an integer multiple of a finite Deligne product of
standard SU(2) quantum-group categories and their adjoint subcategories,
at any positive levels. Therefore none can arise from those products by
finite sequences of finite-group extensions, equivariantizations, Deligne
products with integer-FP-dimension categories, and Morita equivalences.
Galois variants with the same fusion rules are covered as well.

This excludes a construction family, not arbitrary categorification.
It does not cover other Lie types, arbitrary algebra condensation,
dimension-decreasing de-equivariantization, or products with additional
nonintegral factors outside the stated family.

The baseline `models/model_search_audit.py` compared three rank-eight
SU(2)/adjoint tensors, while `models/quantum_model_attempts.py` performed a
bounded numerical WZW scan. The present search is different: it gives an
exact bound valid at **all levels**, for **arbitrary finite products** and
**all Morita duals**, using global dimension rather than rank.

## Why the infinite domain reduces to a finite exact calculation

Put n=k+2, where k>=1. The SU(2)_k global dimension is

    D_n = n / (1-cos(2 pi/n)).

Its faithful parity grading makes the adjoint global dimension

    A_n = D_n/2 = n / (2-2 cos(2 pi/n)).

Every full SU(2) factor can thus be replaced by the corresponding adjoint
factor and an integer multiplier2. We may test the larger dimension
family m product A_n, with an arbitrary integer m>=1. For n=3,4,6,
A_n=1,2,6, so these factors are absorbed into m.

The field Q(A_n) is exactly the maximal real cyclotomic field
Q(cos(2 pi/n)), of degree phi(n)/2. Every conjugate of A_n is positive,
and A_n itself is the largest conjugate: the denominator is minimized at
the primitive angles plus or minus2 pi/n.

**Field-of-products lemma.** Suppose a_i are totally positive elements of
abelian number fields and each a_i is a largest conjugate at the chosen
real embedding. In a common Galois extension, any automorphism fixing
product a_i satisfies product sigma(a_i)=product a_i. Each positive factor
sigma(a_i) is at most a_i, so equality forces sigma(a_i)=a_i for every i.
Thus the stabilizer of the product is the intersection of the individual
stabilizers. Consequently the field generated by the product equals the
compositum of the individual fields. A rational multiplier does not alter
this conclusion.

Apply this lemma to a proposed equality D=m product A_n. Every factor
field must lie in Q(D), hence in the exactly reconstructed FP field of
the census tensor. Those fields have degree2 or4 in the four original targets and are checked to be
Galois by splitting their defining polynomial inside the field itself.
Therefore phi(n) divides twice the target field degree.

There is no arbitrary level cutoff in the enumeration. If p divides n,
then p-1 divides phi(n), so p is among the primes with p-1 dividing2d.
The p-adic exponent is bounded by phi(p^a)<=2d. Exhausting these finite
prime-power products and checking phi(n) dividing2d gives every possible
n. For d=2 the list is 3,4,5,6,8,10,12; for d=4 it additionally includes
15,16,20,24,30.

For each n, the exact real cyclotomic polynomial is obtained from
Res_z(Phi_n(z),z^2-u z+1), which is its square. Substitution u=2-n/t gives
the minimal polynomial of A_n. Exact factorization over the target field
tests containment. Since the target field is Galois, containment is
equivalent to complete splitting. The largest real root is selected by
the exact isolating embedding, with no numerical tolerance.

Finally, A_n>1 for every remaining nonrational factor. Because m>=1,
A_n^e<=D bounds its exponent. The verifier checks both the last allowed
power and the first excluded power exactly, exhausts the product box,
and tests whether D/product A_n is a positive rational integer.

## Extension to all34 original frontier rings

`su2_all_frontier.py` applies exactly the same finite arithmetic to all34
source-bound baseline rows. It reconstructs the unique positive scalar
character directly, without requiring a noncommutative Wedderburn type.
All target FP fields are checked to be Galois. Using the entire FP field
rather than Q(D), when it is larger, enlarges the candidate domain and is
therefore safe for an exclusion.

The complete result is30 family exclusions and four retained dimension
matches. R8_3_439 and R8_3_567 have D=2A_12, the dimension of SU(2)_10.
R8_3_731 and R8_3_3344 have integer dimensions660 and360, respectively,
and pass through the empty product with that integer multiplier. No
categorical model is asserted for any dimension match. These four cases
are not excluded by this family test.

## Primary mathematical inputs

* Coquereaux, *Quantum McKay correspondence and global dimensions for
  fusion and module-categories associated with Lie groups*, p.8,
  gives the SU(2) dimension formula:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.6621.
* Etingof--Nikshych--Ostrik, *On fusion categories*, Corollary8.14 proves
  Morita invariance of FP dimension; Proposition8.20 gives equal dimensions
  of faithful grading components:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0203060.
* A finite-group equivariantization has FP dimension |G| times that of
  the original category; equivalently it is Morita equivalent to the
  corresponding crossed-product extension. The scope above needs only
  this integer dimension multiplier, not a classification of actions.
