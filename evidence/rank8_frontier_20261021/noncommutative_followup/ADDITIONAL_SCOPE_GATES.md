## The extra dimension-field obstruction

The earlier restricted gate accepted only zero, duality and norm
obstructions. On each of439 and567 this left one nongauge class with a
rational dimension character and signed global dimension delta=24.
This branch is excluded by the following standard induction consequence.

Let K be the field generated by the signed spherical simple dimensions.
Every simple of the sphericalization has dimension in K. The forgetful
functor from its center expresses each central simple dimension as an
integer linear combination of those dimensions, hence in K. For an
ordinary-sector irreducible representation with original formal codegree
f, the sphericalization has formal codegree2f and global dimension2delta.
Ostrik, *Pivotal fusion categories of rank3*, Theorem2.13, therefore gives

    dim(A_E) = (2delta)/(2f) = delta/f in K.

Since delta is nonzero and belongs to K, necessarily f belongs to K.
This argument requires sphericality of the sphericalization, which is
automatic; it does not assume that the original category is spherical
or pseudo-unitary.

In both remaining branches K=Q, delta=24, but an ordinary scalar formal
codegree has irreducible polynomial t^2-96t+576. Exact irreducibility,
all rational simple dimensions, delta=24, and the required codegree's
occurrence are checked in the receipt. Thus the field condition is
impossible. All remaining signed tensors are gauge-original. The existing
spherical-section argument then constructs a spherical structure on any
hypothetical category; the unique ordinary FP Galois orbit gives its
pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate.
