# General applications of character-labeled atomic STS

The theorem and exact tensor-unit normalization are in ATOMIC_MODULAR.md.
The new system uses independent character-labeled low simplices, not a complete
joint-frame enumeration. Its low domain therefore contains every actual joint
frame, and a full-domain separator is sufficient. A positive solution would only
establish survival of this relaxation.

For R8_3_3258, all eight characters split in the real FP field Q(sqrt5).
The exact partial-S filter retains all low columns and removes479 high vectors,
leaving1471. The low simplex sizes in verified character order are
2359,84,155,84,84,84,24. At height4 the all-order compression has bound45 and
ten joint phase-trace types. The final cone has241 rows and17619 columns.
Its sole admissible indicator assignment has an exact full-domain integer
separating ray. The saved general certificate checks the spherical character
alternatives and its single normalized oriented signed branch, gauge equivalent
to the original ring. Thus the exclusion is over arbitrary complex fusion
categories, without assuming pivotality.

The source-bound certificate is atomic_results/R8_3_3258_h4.json.
Canonical exact-only replay is verify_accepted_atomic_sts.py. The independent
positive-control and oracle comparisons are verify_atomic.py/atomic_validation.json,
with separate reviewer receipts. No numerical solver is needed for replay.
