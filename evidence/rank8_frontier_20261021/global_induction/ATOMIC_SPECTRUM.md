all eight characters and let K be the FP field. All character values, formal
codegrees, the embedded FP dimension character, orthogonality and duality are
verified against the source tensor. The algebraic-integrality test for

not membership in the generally nonmaximal order generated by a primitive root.
An element is integral exactly when that monic characteristic polynomial has

low restriction columns for every non-FP character E, of dimension betaE=D/fE.

symmetry equations are automatic from the dimension condition. Each genuine
