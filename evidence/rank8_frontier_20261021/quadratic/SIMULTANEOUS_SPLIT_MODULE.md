# Simultaneous fixed-point module representations exclude four residuals

The rings `R8_3_1203`, `R8_3_1350`, `R8_3_2842`, and `R8_3_2885` do not
admit a fusion categorification over the complex numbers. No pivotal,
spherical, braided, or unitary structure is assumed. This strengthens the
previous unitary-only conclusions for 1350 and 2885 and resolves the other
two previously open cases. The proof below uses the complete multiplication
tensor, rather than only a single-generator polynomial.

## Categorical reduction

Let a commutative fusion ring have an invertible basis object `g` of order
two and at least one `g`-fixed simple object. In any categorification the
pointed subcategory generated by `g` is `Vec(C2, omega)`. The orbit of a
fixed simple is a rank-one module category over this pointed subcategory.
Its module associativity is a two-cochain whose coboundary is `omega`.
Consequently the associator class is trivial and `A = 1 + g` admits a
connected separable algebra structure.

Consider the semisimple left module category of right `A`-modules. A free
two-element `g`-orbit induces a single simple module. A fixed simple `V`
induces exactly two nonisomorphic simple modules `V+` and `V-`, each with
underlying object `V`. Indeed induction adjunction gives endomorphism
dimension two, so semisimplicity gives two multiplicity-one summands;
forgetting the induction gives `V + V`. Every simple module appears in
some induced object.

For each ring basis object `X`, let `M_X` be its integral nonnegative
action matrix. The span of induced objects is invariant. Its orthogonal
complement is invariant as well because `M_X^T = M_(X*)` by module
rigidity and the span is invariant under both. The complement has basis
`V+ - V-`, one for each fixed simple. Write `S_X` for the action there.
Induction compatibility and rigidity imply that the two-by-two block
from a fixed `V` to a fixed `W` has both row and column sums
`a = N[X,V,W]`. It therefore has the form
`[[u, a-u], [a-u, u]]`. In particular:

* `S_1 = I`, `S_(X*) = S_X^T`;
* `(S_X)[V,W]` belongs to `{-a, -a+2, ..., a}`;
* `S_X S_Y = sum_Z N[X,Y,Z] S_Z` simultaneously for all `X,Y`.

For `X=g` the fixed block is the identity, so `S_g` is diagonal with
entries `+1` or `-1`. If `gX=X`, then `S_g S_X=S_X`. Thus if, for a
fixed vertex `V`, some fixed `X` has odd `N[X,V,V]`, the diagonal entry
`(S_X)[V,V]` cannot vanish and `(S_g)[V,V]=+1`. If this odd-diagonal
coverage holds at every fixed vertex, **`S_g=I`**. This is the decisive
extra condition: the simultaneous representation factors through the
rational quotient `R_Q/(g-1)`.

These categorical inputs are standard semisimple module-category theory:
V. Ostrik, *Module categories, weak Hopf algebras and modular invariants*,
Transformation Groups 8 (2003), 177–206,
[arXiv:math/0111139](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0111139), and
P. Etingof, S. Gelaki, D. Nikshych, V. Ostrik, *Tensor Categories*,
Chapter 7, [author-hosted book](https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/egnobookfinal.pdf).
The odd-diagonal quotient specialization and its exhaustive application
here are deductions from these inputs.

## Complete finite search

The fixed indices are `[4,5,6,7]` for all four tensors. The involution is
index 3 for 1203 and 1350, and index 1 for 2842 and 2885. Odd-diagonal
coverage is checked directly from each tensor. The quotient has rank six
and is generated as a rational algebra by the self-dual object of index
4. Its characteristic polynomial is:

| Rings | Quotient polynomial for index 4 |
| --- | --- |
| 1203, 2842 | `(t^2-14t+4)(t^4+2t^3-6t^2-12t-4)` |
| 1350, 2885 | `(t^2-12t-14)(t^4-10t^2+12t-2)` |

Every other `S_X` is consequently a uniquely specified rational
polynomial in `S_4`. The evidence stores the exact numerator lists and
positive denominators. Each identity is independently checked in the
original rank-eight regular representation by multiplication by `I+N_g`;
thus the verification does not trust the quotient construction.

Enumerate every symmetric integral `S_4` with the required entry bounds
and parities. The primary enumerator removes only a rigorously justified
sign gauge: swapping `V+` and `V-` conjugates all matrices by a diagonal
sign matrix. On a spanning forest of odd-bound off-diagonal entries these
entries are nonzero, so every system has a representative with the chosen
forest entries positive. The independent verifier makes **no gauge
reduction** and enumerates the entire finite box.

| Ring | Gauge-reduced matrices | Ungauged matrices | Exact polynomial survivors, reduced / ungauged | Simultaneous survivors |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1203 | 13,824 | 110,592 | 4 / 32 | 0 |
| 1350 | 7,776 | 62,208 | 2 / 16 | 0 |
| 2842 | 13,824 | 110,592 | 4 / 32 | 0 |
| 2885 | 7,776 | 62,208 | 2 / 16 | 0 |

For every polynomial survivor, at least one reconstructed matrix has a
nonintegral entry or violates its necessary bound/parity. The primary
evidence gives a specific failing entry for each survivor. The independent
verifier checks all 345,600 ungauged matrices using unbounded Python
integer arithmetic. Its preliminary trace filter follows from the full
factorization of the squarefree annihilating polynomial and exhausts all
possible degree-four characteristic-polynomial factor multiplicities.
No cutoff, numerical spectral calculation, or optimization tolerance is
used. Explicit runtime checks remain enabled under `python -O`.

## Positive control and replay

The known model `R8_3_342 = Rep(C13 semidirect C6)` has an involution with
fixed indices `[5,6]` and satisfies the odd-diagonal hypothesis. Its full
simultaneous bounded representation is checked: all six invertible basis
elements act by `I_2`, while indices 5 and 6 act respectively by
`[[-2,-1],[-1,1]]` and `[[1,1],[1,-2]]`. Every tensor multiplication,
duality relation, entry bound and parity is verified. The additional
cyclic-self-dual quotient specialization is inapplicable to this control;
the existence of a bounded module representation is not itself asserted
to construct a fusion category.
