# A uniform rank-sixteen Morita reduction

This is a necessary structural theorem, **not an exclusion or a construction**. It applies to all five unresolved quadratic rings R10_1_114, R10_1_115, R10_1_152, R10_1_153, and R10_1_179. No pivotal, spherical, braided, or unitary hypothesis is used.

Write δ=1+√2. In every ring the invertibles form a group G of order four. There is a unique nontrivial involution h fixing precisely the two simples Y₀,Y₁ of dimension 2δ. The other two elements of G interchange Y₀,Y₁. Multiplication by h exchanges the four simples of dimension δ² in two pairs and the four invertibles in two pairs. Moreover

N(Yᵢ,Yⱼ;Yₖ)=1 for every i,j,k∈{0,1}.

The supplied verifier checks these assertions directly in the original tensor and verifies the dimension homomorphism exactly in Q(√2).

## The algebra and its right modules

Suppose C categorifies the ring. Since h⊗Y₀≅Y₀, the associator on the pointed subcategory generated by h is cohomologically trivial: this subcategory acts on the single isomorphism class Y₀, giving a rank-one module category. Equivalently, the stabilizer summand 1⊕h in the internal endomorphism algebra Y₀⊗Y₀* inherits its twisted group-algebra multiplication. Thus A=1⊕h is a connected separable algebra.

The simple right A-modules are the four free modules associated with the length-two h-orbits and two modules Yᵢ⁺,Yᵢ⁻ over each h-fixed simple Yᵢ. The rank is therefore eight. One can derive this directly from free-module adjunction: End_A(X⊗A) has dimension one for a length-two orbit and dimension two for a fixed X. Over C the latter semisimple algebra is C⊕C, and the two summands both have underlying object X.

Let τ be the automorphism of A acting by −1 on h. Twisting a right action by τ fixes each free simple and interchanges Yᵢ⁺ with Yᵢ⁻. The action of C on its right A-modules commutes with this twisting. The τ-anti-invariant subspace W of their complexified Grothendieck group therefore has basis

w₀=[Y₀⁺]−[Y₀⁻],   w₁=[Y₁⁺]−[Y₁⁻],

and is a two-dimensional representation ρ of K₀(C).

For any simple X, the (k,j) entry of ρ(X) is a−b, where a and b count the two split outputs Yₖ⁺ and Yₖ⁻ in X⊗Yⱼ⁺. Forgetting the right action gives

a+b=N(X,Yⱼ;Yₖ).

In particular **every entry of ρ(Yᵢ) is odd**, hence is nonzero.

## The key parity argument

Left tensoring by h either fixes the two modules over Yᵢ or interchanges them. Thus ρ(h)=diag(ε₀,ε₁), with εᵢ∈{±1}. Choose g∈G interchanging Y₀,Y₁. Its action on W is an invertible signed permutation interchanging the two basis vectors. Since gh=hg in K₀(C), ρ(g) commutes with ρ(h). Consequently ε₀=ε₁.

If both signs were −1, the fusion identity hYᵢ=Yᵢ would imply

−ρ(Yᵢ)=ρ(h)ρ(Yᵢ)=ρ(Yᵢ),

so ρ(Yᵢ)=0. This contradicts the odd-entry assertion. Therefore **ρ(h)=I**: left tensoring by h fixes every simple right A-module, including each Yᵢ⁺ and Yᵢ⁻ individually.

## The Morita dual

An A-bimodule is a right A-module with a coherent left H={1,h} action. Hence its underlying semisimple category is the H-equivariantization of C_A for left tensoring. Every one of the eight right-module simples is H-fixed. Its obstruction is a projective character of C₂, and H²(C₂,C×)=0; it therefore has exactly two inequivalent one-dimensional equivariant structures. There are **sixteen** simple A-bimodules.

The FP dimension in the dual category D={}_A C_A is the underlying FP dimension in C divided by FPdim(A)=2. The two invertible h-orbits contribute four dimension-one simples. The two h-orbits of δ²-dimensional simples contribute four δ²-dimensional simples. The four modules Yᵢ± contribute eight δ-dimensional simples. Thus

rank(D)=16,   FPdim-type(D)=(1⁴,δ⁸,(δ²)⁴),   FPdim(D)=96+64√2.

Its invertible group fits an exact sequence

1 → Ĉ₂ → G(D) → G(C)/⟨h⟩ → 1.

At this stage G(D) may be C₄ or C₂×C₂; the original fusion tensor alone has not determined the extension/coherence. The eight δ-dimensional simples form two free G(D)-orbits. Indeed, if X has dimension δ, write the decomposition of XX* by dimension as n·1+m·δ+l·δ². The equation δ²=1+2δ and the obligatory unit summand force n=1,m=2,l=0. Hence XX* contains no nontrivial invertible, so X has trivial invertible stabilizer.

## What this does and does not establish

The five categorification problems reduce to particular Morita descents of rank-sixteen categories with the displayed dimension profile. The profile agrees with PSU(2)₆⊠PSU(2)₆, but this agreement alone does not identify the fusion category or even its fusion ring. Pseudo-PSU(2)₆ has the same rank-four dimension profile and nonselfdual noninvertibles, so it must also be considered. No product factorization, braiding, unitarity, or equivalence to a known category is asserted here.

The proof is independent of the previously obtained spherical/Galois reduction. It can therefore be used safely as a complex-categorifiability reduction.

# The module action and dual fusion ring are forced

The rank-sixteen reduction can be strengthened to a complete based-ring determination.

Normalize the two split signs so that an invertible g interchanging Y₀,Y₁ acts on the anti-invariant two-space by the ordinary swap matrix. This is possible because g²∈{1,h} and ρ(h)=I. Every ρ(X) commutes with this swap. Its plus eigenspace gives an integer-valued character trivial on all invertibles. Transitivity on Y and Z makes its values constant, say y on Y and z on Z. The exact tensor yields

y²=2+2y+2z,   yz=y+4z.

Hence (y−2)(y²−4y−4)=0. Since y is rational, y=2 and z=−1. Each entry of ρ(Y) is ±1; its row sum is two, so ρ(Y) is the all-ones matrix. The fixed-sector matrices of every Z are either the identity or the swap, and parity now forces ρ(Z) to be its negative. The invertible actions are already fixed. Thus the complete eight-simple module based action is unique up to the harmless relabeling of split signs. `module_action.py` constructs it and verifies all module fusion relations and duality exactly.

Let T be right-module twisting by the sign automorphism of A. It fixes the four free modules and swaps the two pairs of split modules. For each of the eight right modules M_m, a simple A-bimodule over it has an action matrix B satisfying:

- B has nonnegative integer entries;
- B commutes with all ten left C-action matrices;
- B e₀=e_m, where M₀=A.

The Perron vector w of this module action, normalized at A, has coordinates in {1,δ,δ²}. The sum of the ten left action matrices is symmetric, since the action respects duality, and irreducible, since A generates this indecomposable module category. Commutation therefore preserves its one-dimensional Perron line on both the left and right: Bw=λw and wᵀB=λwᵀ. The prescribed first column gives λ=wᵀBe₀=w_m. Nonnegativity then gives B_ij w_j≤λw_i. Thus every entry is bounded by δ²·δ²=δ⁴=17+12√2<34, so is at most 33.

The exact commutant has dimension 12. Prescribing the first column leaves four parameters, each literally an entry of B. Linear nonnegativity reduces their ranges to 0 or 1 (one parameter can be forced to 1); for each m there are precisely two matrices. `commutant_candidates.py` checks that every free parameter is an actual matrix entry, exhausts the derived finite bounds, and verifies B T exchanges the two matrices without fixed points.

Why both matrices must occur: the invertible A-bimodule ε={}_σ A, with its left action twisted by the sign automorphism σ and regular right action, acts on right A-modules by T. For a fixed underlying right module, the two left-H equivariant structures differ by ε⊗_A−. Right module action reverses composition, so their matrices are B and B T. Since this exchange has no fixed point, both distinct candidates occur. Therefore all sixteen dual simple action matrices are forced.

Nonnegative decomposition of their matrix products determines 240 of the 256 dual fusion products uniquely. The remaining sixteen are products between the four δ²-dimensional simples. Each of these four simples is itself a simple product of two δ-dimensional simples. Associativity therefore determines the remaining products from the already determined ones. The complete integer tensors are stored as `R10_1_<id>_dual_tensor.json`. Every one passes all 4096 associativity equations, the unit and duality constraints, and all 256 matrix-action equations. This determines a necessary based ring, not its associator or existence.

**Product convention.** The stored tensor uses B_i B_j, with column vectors. Therefore it is the fusion tensor of End_C(C_A), equivalently the opposite of the literal A-bimodule tensor product under these labels. Either is a fusion category, and passing to the opposite does not change the adjoint subcategory, the commutative rank-four subcategories, or conjugation by the involution ε. All later categorical arguments use this stored convention consistently.

The adjoint rank-eight subrings are matched coefficient-by-coefficient to the completed lower-rank census in `rank8_matches.json`: R8_2_557 for inputs 114 and 115, and R8_2_313 for inputs 152,153,179. Their maximum coefficient is two. Both lower-rank rings have known unitary models, so the descent by itself does not exclude these cases.
