# Cyclic-module divisibility obstruction

This argument concerns arbitrary fusion categories over the complex numbers. It assumes neither a pivotal structure nor unitarity. It applies to the source tensors in ranks seven and eight without constructing associators.

**Lemma.** Let H be a cyclic group of invertible objects of prime order p in a fusion category C. Let X, Y, Z be simple objects such that hX is isomorphic to X, Yh is isomorphic to Y, and hZ and Zh are isomorphic to Z, for every h in H. If FPdim(Z)/p is not an algebraic integer, then p divides the fusion coefficient N(X,Y;Z).

**Proof.** The additive subcategory generated by X is a rank-one module category over the pointed subcategory Vec(H,omega). Choose nonzero isomorphisms hX → X. Their compatibility scalars give a two-cochain whose coboundary is omega. Consequently the restriction of the pointed associator to H is cohomologically trivial. Thus the regular object A = direct sum of h in H admits a connected separable algebra structure. Fix such a structure.

The obstruction to making X a left A-module is a scalar two-cocycle on H. Since H is cyclic and the ground field is the complex numbers, H²(H,C×)=0. Explicitly, rescale an isomorphism gX → X so its p-fold composite agrees with the A multiplication; a p-th root of the resulting nonzero scalar exists. This gives a left A-module structure on X. The same argument gives a right A-module structure on Y. Their ordinary tensor product X tensor Y is therefore an A-bimodule, with A acting on its two factors.

Consider A-bimod(C), with tensor product over A. It is a fusion category. Its simple objects supported on a fixed simple Z correspond to irreducible projective representations of H × H with a single scalar two-cocycle. Here the second factor acts on the right; replacing it by the opposite group makes no difference because H is abelian. To see the correspondence directly, any object supported on Z is V tensor Z for a vector space V. Choosing isomorphisms hZ → Z and Zh → Z translates the left/right module axioms into a projective representation on V. Thus the dimension of the projective representation is exactly the underlying multiplicity of Z.

For H × H = C_p², the commutator pairing of a two-cocycle is an alternating form on a two-dimensional vector space over F_p. It is either zero or nondegenerate. The corresponding complex twisted group algebra is therefore either C^(p²), with p² simple modules of dimension one, or M_p(C), with one simple module of dimension p. In the first case A-bimod(C) has simple objects with underlying object Z and hence FP dimension FPdim_C(Z)/FPdim_C(A) = FPdim_C(Z)/p. This contradicts algebraic integrality of simple FP dimensions. Only the second case remains, and every simple A-bimodule supported on Z has underlying object pZ.

The isotypic component of Z in the A-bimodule X tensor Y is an A-bimodule because Z is fixed by H on both sides. Semisimplicity now forces its multiplicity to be divisible by p. This is exactly p | N(X,Y;Z). QED.

The division by FPdim(A) is essential: the FP dimension of an A-bimodule in the dual fusion category is its underlying FP dimension in C divided by FPdim_C(A). The proof does not assume that the left-right stabilizer cocycle is trivial. Its nontrivial possibility is precisely what gives the divisibility conclusion.

## Primary-source context

The regular cyclic algebra and the left/right bimodule argument are the same elementary method used by P. Etingof, S. Gelaki, V. Ostrik, *Classification of fusion categories of dimension pq*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2004, 3041–3056, arXiv:math/0304194v2, Lemmas 6.1–6.2. The statement above extends that argument to a nonintegral simple-dimension obstruction and gives the complete proof. For its applications after a dimension-ten Morita change, the proof of Theorem 6.3 in that source identifies the pointed dual group as the dihedral group of order ten.

## Applications and scope

The rank-seven evidence records witnesses for R7_4_1212, R7_4_1213, R7_4_1438, R7_4_1440, R7_4_2731. The rank-eight evidence records a direct witness for R8_2_260 and applications after a Morita change for R8_2_380 and R8_2_673. Each source tensor, pointed action, exact FP character, and required nonintegrality witness must be checked in the corresponding rank-specific verifier. The abstract lemma alone does not certify any unchecked tensor.
