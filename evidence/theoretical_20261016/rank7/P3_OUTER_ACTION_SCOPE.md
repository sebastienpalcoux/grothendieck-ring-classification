# The P3 outer-action route does not produce a rank-seven category

Research note; the frozen classification and publication are unchanged.

## Primary sources

- P. Grossman, *Fusion categories associated to subfactors with index 3+sqrt(5)*, arXiv:1612.07185, §§4.1–4.2, especially Lemmas 4.1–4.3, the definition of P3 following Lemma 4.6, and Corollary 4.14. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07185.
- P. Grossman, M. Izumi, N. Snyder, *Graded extensions of generalized Haagerup categories*, arXiv:2201.11901v1, Lemmas 3.1–3.2, equations (3.10) and (3.18), and Theorem 4.1. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11901.

The arguments below use actual endomorphism realizations and tensor autoequivalences, not just symmetries of a fusion ring.

## The exact P3 fusion rules

Write d=2+sqrt(5). Realize P1 in End(M) in generalized-Haagerup standard form, with invertibles alpha_g, g in Z/4, and rho. Put

    P = M ⋊_{alpha_2} C2,
    lambda² = 1,   lambda x lambda = alpha_2(x).

As in Grossman §4.1, extend the endomorphisms by

    alpha_tilde_g(lambda) = (−1)^g lambda,
    rho_tilde(lambda) = lambda.

Let a=[alpha_tilde_1], r=[rho_tilde], and let b=[phi], where phi fixes M and sends lambda to −lambda. Grossman's P3 is the category generated by Q1=<rho_tilde> and phi. Its simples are

    1, a, b, ab, r, ar, br, abr.

Here a²=b²=1 and ab=ba: alpha_tilde_2=Ad(lambda), phi²=id, and phi commutes exactly with alpha_tilde_1. Moreover a and b commute with r at the level of sectors, and

    r² = 1 + 2r + 2ar.

These relations give every product. For g,h in {1,a,b,ab},

    g(hr) = (gh)r,
    (gr)(hr) = gh + 2(gh)r + 2(gha)r.

All simples are self-dual. The four invertibles form C2×C2, not C4. The noninvertibles all have dimension d. The neutral component of the displayed C2 grading is Q1={1,a,r,ar}.

## Constructing the nontrivial outer autoequivalence

Use GIS Theorem 4.1 for the known generalized-Haagerup C4 category P1, with p=1 and z in {0,2}. It gives a realization on a factor M with an automorphism beta and unitaries u,v satisfying

    beta alpha_g = alpha_g beta,
    beta rho = Ad(u) alpha_1 rho beta,
    beta² = Ad(v) alpha_{1+z}.

GIS Lemmas 3.1–3.2 give the last relation and character eigenvalues alpha_g(u)=chi(g)u, alpha_g(v)=mu(g)v. Equation (3.18) gives chi(2)=epsilon_2(1)=−1. Because chi and mu are characters and (3.10) gives mu(1)²=chi(1)², also mu(2)=−1. Thus

    alpha_2(u)=−u,   alpha_2(v)=−v.

The first relation permits beta to extend to P by beta_tilde(lambda)=lambda. Let F=Ad(beta_tilde). The extensions beta_tilde, alpha_tilde_1, and phi commute pairwise where claimed: beta_tilde commutes with alpha_tilde_1 and phi, as checked on M and lambda. Further,

    beta_tilde rho_tilde
        = Ad(u) alpha_tilde_1 rho_tilde beta_tilde.

On M this is the GIS relation. On lambda the left side gives lambda; the right side gives Ad(u)(−lambda)=lambda, because alpha_2(u)=−u implies u lambda u*=−lambda. Therefore F is an actual tensor autoequivalence of P3 with

    F(a)=a,   F(b)=b,   F(r)≅ar.

It swaps r with ar, and br with abr, while fixing all four invertible classes. Since the ring of P3 is commutative, inner autoequivalences fix every simple-object class. Thus F is outer. Grossman Corollary 4.14 states Out(P3)=C2, so F represents its unique nontrivial element. Every tensor autoequivalence of P3 is inner or inner times F, and consequently every tensor autoequivalence fixes all four invertible-object classes.

This construction can use the GIS factor realization without identifying it with the specific factor in Grossman's paper: its neutral generalized-Haagerup category is tensor equivalent to P1, and the unique algebra 1+alpha_2 gives the same Morita-dual category P3. The crossed-product description is the endomorphism realization of that dual.

## Rank obstruction

In any genuine C2 action on P3, all four invertibles are fixed. A fixed invertible has a projective C2 stabilizer representation; H²(C2,C×)=0, so it has two one-dimensional equivariant lifts. The equivariantization therefore contains at least eight invertible simples. Its rank cannot be seven, independently of the action on the four dimension-d simples.

This excludes P3 as the base of a C2-equivariantization model for any of the residual rank-seven rings. It does not exclude those rings as fusion rings and does not exhaust other possible base categories or constructions.

## A stronger coherence obstruction for the outer class

On M the GIS identity gives beta_tilde²=Ad(v) alpha_tilde_{1+z}. It holds on lambda as well: alpha_tilde_{1+z}(lambda)=−lambda, and alpha_2(v)=−v implies Ad(v)(−lambda)=lambda. Consequently, as tensor autoequivalences,

    F² ≅ Ad(a).

Ad(a) is not tensor naturally isomorphic to the identity on P3. Indeed its restriction to Q1 transposes the two nonisomorphic Q-systems on 1+r by Grossman Lemma 4.1; an identity tensor functor cannot do this.

Any representative H of the nontrivial outer class has the form Ad(t)F up to tensor natural isomorphism, for an invertible t. Since F fixes t and t²≅1,

    H² ≅ Ad(t F(t)) F² ≅ F² ≅ Ad(a) ≄ Id.

Thus the nontrivial outer class has no order-two lift to the group of tensor autoequivalences modulo tensor natural isomorphism. In particular it cannot be the image of the nonidentity element in any coherent C2 action. This is an obstruction before the remaining associativity/coherence choices for a group action.
