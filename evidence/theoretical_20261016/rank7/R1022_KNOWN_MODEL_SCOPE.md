# The known A4 crossed-product model does not realize R7_3_1022

The general Morita reduction in `A4_C3_GENERAL_PROOF.md` leaves one
possible 24-simple based ring for R7_3_1022. It has invertibles A4 and
noninvertibles g rho of dimension t=2+sqrt(5), with

    rho g = g rho,    rho² = 1 + sum(v in V4) v rho.

There is a known unitary realization: if C is the generalized Haagerup
V4 category, its coherent cyclic order-three symmetry gives the crossed
product C2=C crossed C3. Grossman, *Fusion categories associated to
subfactors with index 3+sqrt(5)*, arXiv:1612.07185, §§2.5 and3.2,
describes this category and its Morita class:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07185.

A natural attempted reconstruction of the rank-seven source uses one of
the two algebra structures on the sum of all twelve A4 invertibles.
These correspond to the two classes of H²(A4,C*). This construction does
not give rank seven. Grossman §3.2 and Lemma3.15 identify the duals for
both 12-dimensional algebras with C1=C^(C3), the rank-eight4442 category.
The surrounding discussion explicitly treats both algebra structures;
choosing the nontrivial class does not produce a different rank-seven
output.

This closes the proposed construction from that known crossed product.
It does not prove that every complex categorification of the same
24-simple ring is equivalent to the known one. Nor does the based-ring
reduction alone supply the necessary module associators to reconstruct
the original source. Therefore R7_3_1022 remains unresolved, with neither
an exclusion nor an explicit model claimed.
