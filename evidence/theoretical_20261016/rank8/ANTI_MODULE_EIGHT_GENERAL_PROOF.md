# Eight general rank-eight exclusions from an involution module

The rings R8_2_318, R8_2_319, R8_2_320, R8_2_368, R8_2_370,
R8_2_735, R8_2_758 and R8_2_760 have no categorification over the
complex numbers. No pivotal, spherical, or unitary structure is assumed.
The proof uses the following module-category construction and elementary
spectral inequalities. It does not depend on exhaustive matrix search.

## The anti-invariant module

Let g be an invertible simple of order two in a fusion category C, and
suppose right multiplication by g fixes exactly f simple objects x_a.
Assume f>0. The additive subcategory generated by a fixed simple is a
rank-one module category over the pointed category generated by g.
Its existence trivializes the associator class on C2. Therefore the
regular connected separable algebra A=1+g exists.

Each fixed simple has two simple right A-module structures m_a^+ and
m_a^-, with underlying object x_a. A free right orbit gives one simple
module, whose underlying object is the sum of its two members. This is
also seen by decomposing the induced module: its endomorphism algebra
has dimension two or one, respectively; the twisted group algebra of
C2 splits over C. Twisting the right action by the algebra automorphism
of A that is -1 on g exchanges the two fixed structures and fixes the
free-orbit module. Twisting commutes with left tensoring by C.

The anti-invariant part of the module Grothendieck group consequently
has integral basis v_a=[m_a^+]-[m_a^-]. If T_X is left tensoring by X on
this basis and B_X=(N_{X,x_a}^{x_b}), then

    |(T_X)_ab| <= (B_X)_ab,    (T_X)_ab = (B_X)_ab mod 2.

Indeed, the two lift multiplicities have sum B_ab and difference T_ab.
Rigidity gives T_X^t=T_{X*}. The difference vectors all have squared
norm two, so this transpose formula has no hidden dimension weighting.
Every fusion relation is preserved. Thus T_X is normal whenever
XX*=X*X in the source ring, and every source polynomial identity for X
holds on T_X. Commutativity of the whole ring is unnecessary.

This construction and its general categorical scope were independently
audited in `../RANK7_THREE_FIXED_CATEGORICAL_AUDIT.md`. The usual
classification of modules over a pointed category and the regular
cyclic algebra argument also appear in Etingof--Gelaki--Ostrik,
*Classification of fusion categories of dimension pq*, Lemmas 6.1--6.2,
arXiv:math/0304194. No claim about existence follows from a matrix
solution; nonexistence of this necessary module is the obstruction.

## Spectral contradictions

Write T=T_X. A normal real matrix satisfies
||T||_F²=sum |lambda_j|². Since T is integral, its characteristic
polynomial is rational; an irreducible quadratic eigenvalue must occur
with its conjugate and with the same multiplicity.

For A and B, entry bounds and parity force ||T||_F² to be 12 or 16.
The pair 3+/-2sqrt(2), the roots of q, contributes 34 to this sum, and
therefore cannot occur. For cases 319,320,370,760 every remaining root
has modulus one. Four eigenvalues would give ||T||_F²=4, a contradiction.

For cases 318 and 735 the extra permitted pair -1+/-sqrt(2), the roots
of r, contributes 6. The possible total squared norms after excluding
q are 4,8,12, according as zero, one, or two copies of this pair occur.
Thus two copies are forced, giving squared norm 12 and trace -4.
But squared norm 12 forces the sole even nonzero diagonal bound, B_44=2,
to have T_44=0. The other nonzero diagonal bounds are two ones, so the
trace lies between -2 and 2. This is the required contradiction.

For C, entry parity gives squared norm in {12,16,20}. The pair
3+/-sqrt(10), the roots of h, contributes 38, so cannot occur. All
remaining roots for cases 368 and 758 have modulus one, again forcing
squared norm 4. This proves all eight unrestricted exclusions.

The former spherical/pivotal compression exclusions of 318 and 735
remain valid, but these module arguments now exclude their general
categorifications as well.
