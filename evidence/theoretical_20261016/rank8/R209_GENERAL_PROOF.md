# Frobenius–Perron subcategory index excludes R8_2_209

Suppose a complex fusion category C has this ring. Put d=(5+sqrt(29))/2. Its positive dimension character, checked against every tensor product, is

    (1,1,1,d,d−1,d+1,d,d).

The basis elements0,1,2 form a pointed fusion subcategory of FP dimension3. On the other hand

    FPdim(C) = 3 + 3d² + (d−1)² + (d+1)²
             = 5(1+d²) = (145+25sqrt(29))/2.

Etingof–Nikshych–Ostrik, *On fusion categories*, Ann.Math.162(2005), Proposition8.15, requires FPdim(C)/3 to be an algebraic integer. Its field trace is145/3, not an integer. Contradiction.

This is a general complex obstruction and involves no categorical dimension or pivotality assumption. In particular no Galois pseudo-unitarization is required. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0203060, Proposition8.15.
