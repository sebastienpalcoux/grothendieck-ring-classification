# Bibliography additions for the rank-eight manuscript

Existing keys `SchNear`, `ENO`, `Ostrik2` already supply the Galois bridge, FP index theorem, and rank-two obstruction. Add these three entries (used in `theoretical_20261016.tex`):

```tex
\bibitem{SchDimensionBlock}
A.~Schopieray, \emph{The dimension block of a spherical fusion category},
arXiv:2609.35287v1 (28 September 2026), Theorem~5.1.
\bibitem{EGODimPQ}
P.~Etingof, S.~Gelaki and V.~Ostrik,
\emph{Classification of fusion categories of dimension $pq$},
Int. Math. Res. Not. \textbf{2004} (2004), 3041--3056;
arXiv:math/0304194v2, Lemma~6.2 and Theorems~6.3 and~7.1.
\bibitem{DongSunNear}
J.~Dong and H.~Sun,
\emph{Structure, examples and classification for generalized near-group fusion categories},
J. Algebra \textbf{568} (2021), 386--407;
arXiv:2004.08735v1, Propositions~3.3 and~3.6.
```

The relevant statements have now also been checked in the matching Dong–Sun preprint, arXiv:2004.08735v1: Proposition 3.3(1) gives normality of the noninvertible stabilizer, and Proposition 3.6(1), including its proof, says the adjoint subcategory is nonpointed and contained in every nonpointed fusion subcategory. The earlier single-author arXiv:1903.08791v1 is superseded as the citation used here.
