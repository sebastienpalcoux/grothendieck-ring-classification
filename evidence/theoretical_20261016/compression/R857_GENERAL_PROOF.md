# A general exclusion of R8_2_857

**Theorem.** The ring R8_2_857 is not the Grothendieck ring of a complex
fusion category. No pivotality assumption is made.

The argument combines a conductor-32 Fourier calculation with three
elementary signed Jacobi-matrix branches. It does not enumerate associators
or signed multiplication tables.

## 1. Exact source data and Fourier calculation

Reorder the source labels as `(0,1,7,2,5,4,3,6)` and call them
`X0=1,X1,...,X7`. All are self-dual. Multiplication by X1 is the symmetric
tridiagonal matrix J with off-diagonal entries 1 and diagonal

    (0,1,1,1,1,1,1,2).

Writing Mj for multiplication by Xj, the source satisfies

    M0=I, M1=J, M[j+1]=(J-I)M[j]-M[j-1]  (1<=j<=6).

In particular X1 generates the algebra. Put zeta=exp(2*pi*i/32) and
L=Q(zeta+zeta^-1), of degree eight. Its positive dimension character is

    d_j = sum_{r=-j}^j zeta^r = sin((2j+1)*pi/32)/sin(pi/32).

All these dimensions are positive. The eight distinct Galois conjugates
give all characters, with formal codegrees sigma(D), where

    D = sum_j d_j^2 = 4*csc^2(pi/32),
    1/D = (2-zeta-zeta^-1)/16.

Let chi be any primitive even Dirichlet character modulo 32. Regarding chi
as a character of Gal(L/Q), its reciprocal Fourier sum is

    ghat(chi) = sum_sigma chi(sigma)/sigma(D) = -tau(chi)/16.

Indeed summing zeta^a+zeta^-a over representatives modulo sign gives the
full Gauss sum tau(chi). The constant term vanishes because chi is
nontrivial. The primitive Gauss-sum identity gives

    |ghat(chi)|^2 = 32/256 = 1/8.

An entirely exact alternative calculation is included in the verifier:
Gal(L/Q) is generated by 3 modulo 32 up to sign; for chi_k(3)=zeta^(4k),
k=0,...,7, the squared Fourier norms are respectively

    1, 1/8, 0, 1/8, 0, 1/8, 0, 1/8.

The four odd k characters have conductor 32.

The full-I(1) compression theorem rules out every spherical categorification.
In fact, if its dimension field K contains L with index e, the eight
constituents corresponding to these formal codegrees contribute
`8*e^2*(1/8)=e^2` to the squared Fourier norm, whereas its upper bound is
`[K:Q]/32=e/4`. This is impossible for e>=1.

## 2. A necessary field for any sphericalization

Suppose an arbitrary categorification C exists and take its canonical
sphericalization Ctilde, with central sign object epsilon of dimension -1.
The epsilon=+1 sector of its fusion algebra is the original algebra, and
its formal codegrees are `2*sigma(D)`. Formal codegrees belong to the
spherical dimension field K, by the induced-unit dimension formula
`dim(A_E)=dim(Ctilde)/f_E`. Thus L is contained in K.

Put e=[K:L]. For a primitive conductor-32 character pulled back to Gal(K/Q),
the known eight epsilon=+1 constituents contribute

    8*e^2*(1/8)/4 = e^2/4

to the full-I(1) squared Fourier norm. Its upper bound is e/4. Consequently
e=1: **the spherical dimension field of Ctilde must be exactly L**.
Only this degree-eight conclusion is needed below. Equality also forces the
epsilon=-1 formal codegrees into Q(cos(pi/8)), but that stronger consequence
is unnecessary for the proof.

## 3. Three signed branches

Both lifts of every original simple are self-dual: duality preserves a
nonzero spherical dimension, whereas switching the lift negates it.
Choose the positive-second-indicator lift of each Xj. In the quotient
epsilon=-1, denote the signed structure constants by T. The established
sphericalization and pivotal-parity constraints give

    |T_ij^k|<=N_ij^k,  T_ij^k=N_ij^k (mod 2),
    T_ii^j=N_ii^j (mod 4).

All simples are self-dual, so the trace tensor T is fully symmetric.
For j=1,...,6, reciprocity identifies T_1j^j with T_jj^1. Its original
multiplicity is one; the square-channel congruence therefore forces it to
be +1. The last diagonal entry t is, using only the weaker parity bound,
one of -2,0,2. All off-diagonal entries of multiplication by X1 are +/-1.

Change the signs of the basis X2,...,X7 successively to make the seven
off-diagonal entries +1. The first off-diagonal entry is already +1, so
the unit and X1 remain unchanged. Such diagonal changes of basis preserve
the diagonal entries of multiplication by X1. The resulting signed
Jacobi matrix is therefore

    J_t = tridiagonal(1; 0,1,1,1,1,1,1,t; 1),  t in {-2,0,2}.

The source zeros force every other entry to vanish. Its first seven rows
give the same recurrence for the signed multiplication matrices Mj as
above. In particular all simple dimensions are integer polynomials in
the dimension of X1, so that one number generates K.

* If t=-2, M2=J_t^2-J_t-I has bottom-right entry 6. The corresponding
  original multiplicity is N_27^7=2. This contradicts the signed bound.

* If t=0, the characteristic polynomial of J_t is

      (x-1)(x+1)(x^2-2x-1)(x^4-4x^3+2x^2+4x-1).

  The dimension of X1 has algebraic degree at most four. Since it generates
  K, this contradicts [K:Q]=8.

* If t=2, J_t is the original multiplication matrix. The recurrence
  therefore makes every signed multiplication matrix equal to the original
  nonnegative one. For each fusion coefficient the signed difference of
  the two lift multiplicities now equals their original sum. The negative
  lift multiplicity is consequently zero. The chosen eight lifts are
  closed under tensor product and duality, and form a spherical fusion
  subcategory equivalent to C. This contradicts the spherical exclusion
  in Section 1.

All branches are impossible, proving the theorem.

## Dependencies and reproduction

The full-I(1) compression proof is `full_I1_compression.tex`, using
Schopieray, arXiv:2609.35287v1, Lemma 4.1 and the proof of Theorem 5.1.
The canonical sphericalization and positive-indicator square-channel
conditions are the established inputs used in the rank-seven proofs:
Ostrik, arXiv:1309.4822, Section 5.1, and Liu--Palcoux--Ren--Vercleyen,
arXiv:2203.06522v5, Theorem 3.9.

Run `python verify_R857_general.py`. It checks the source tensor hash,
all 512 dimension-character products in Q(zeta_32), all eight exact Fourier
norms, the source recurrence, and the three branch identities. It uses no
floating-point tests or searches. The field identification and three-case
argument above are theoretical; the script verifies their finite inputs.
