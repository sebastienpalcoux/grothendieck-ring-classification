RESULT
The reduction is logically complete and applies to an arbitrary complex fusion categorification, with no assumed pivotal, spherical, braided, unitary, or pseudo-unitary structure. Every such category admits a spherical structure and has a pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate with the same fusion rules. Consequently, nonexistence in the pseudo-unitary case implies nonexistence of any complex fusion categorification. The original category need not itself be pseudo-unitary.

1. SPHERICALIZATION AND INDICATOR NORMALIZATION
Ostrik, Pivotal fusion categories of rank 3, arXiv:1309.4822, section 5.1, constructs the sphericalization of an arbitrary fusion category. Each source simple has two lifts, differing by the central sign object of dimension -1. All eight source simples here are selfdual, and both lifts of each are selfdual (section 5.1(a)). Their second indicators are opposite signs: changing the pivotal isomorphism alpha to -alpha changes the defining second-indicator rotation by -1. Choose the lift with indicator +1 for every simple; the unit choice is forced.

Liu--Palcoux--Ren--Vercleyen, Triangular prism equations and categorification, arXiv:2203.06522v5, Definition 2.14, Lemma 2.16 and Theorem 3.9 supply the needed indicator facts. Theorem 3.9 applies in an arbitrary pivotal fusion category: a selfdual simple occurring with odd multiplicity in X* tensor X has indicator +1. Applied to the negative lift (indicator -1), this makes its multiplicity in every positive-lift square even. Thus the signed quotient tensor satisfies T_jj^i = N_jj^i modulo 4, in addition to |T_ij^k| <= N_ij^k and T_ij^k = N_ij^k modulo 2. No braiding or positivity is used here.

T=N means the negative-lift multiplicities (N-T)/2 all vanish. The positive lifts are therefore closed under tensor product and duality and form a spherical fusion subcategory tensor-equivalent to the original category, by Ostrik section 5.1(c). This proves sphericality of the original category.

3. SPHERICAL DIMENSIONS AND GALOIS TRANSPORT
The exact ordinary character calculation has eight characters. Two are the FP character and its sqrt(2)-conjugate. The other six are the roots of two cubic factors; one triple vanishes on objects 5,6,7 and the other on objects 1,2,4. A simple spherical dimension cannot vanish, so only the FP pair can be the spherical dimension character. In the canonical ordering, FP dimensions are

Their global dimension is 700+490sqrt(2).

The manuscript cites Etingof--Nikshych--Ostrik, On fusion categories, arXiv:math/0203060, Theorem 2.30 and the following remark, for a number-field model. A direct argument avoids any question about separately descending a chosen pivotal structure: extend the identity or the sqrt(2)-flip to an automorphism of C, and apply it simultaneously to the finite skeletal associator, unit, rigidity, and spherical/pivotal structure matrices. All coherence and trace axioms are algebraic identities, so the transported data define a spherical fusion category with the same fusion rules and positive FP dimensions. Its categorical global dimension equals its FP dimension, hence it is pseudo-unitary. No unproved pivotal-existence theorem or unitarity assumption enters.
