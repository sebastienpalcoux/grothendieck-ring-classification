# Rank-six fusion-ring counts

These are counts of all based fusion rings, regardless of categorifiability.
The eight exact-multiplicity terms are

**39, 154, 384, 872, 1582, 2768, 3814, 6999**, with offset 1.

Their sum is 16,612. Rank includes the unit. Multiplicity is the maximum
structure constant, including unit coefficients. Every duality type and
noncommutative case is included.

The sole counting source is
[Fusion Ring Census](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/fusion-ring-census),
revision `b86eb6dc6eeeafe5fc5f6906d7b074e0322e9726`, specifically
`results/census.json` and `results/rank6/tables.txt.gz`. The repository supplies
the enumeration and coverage evidence. The accompanying categorification paper
does not run a separate census.

- `counts.csv`: exact and cumulative counts.
- `bfile_rank6_unassigned.txt`: exact terms in b-file format.
- `proposal.txt`: sequence definition and source information.

No accession is presumed and no online submission is claimed. The partial
categorification results in the remaining higher bands do not affect these counts.
