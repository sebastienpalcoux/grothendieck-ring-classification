NAME
Number of based-isomorphism classes of fusion rings of rank 6 and exact multiplicity n.

DATA
39, 154, 384, 872, 1582, 2768, 3814, 6999, 8809

OFFSET
1,1

COMMENTS
The rank is the number of distinguished basis elements, including the identity. The multiplicity is the largest structure constant, including coefficients involving the identity. See A348305 for the definition of a fusion ring.

Rings are counted up to isomorphisms that map the distinguished basis bijectively to the distinguished basis. All duality types and noncommutative rings are included. No categorifiability condition is imposed.

The first four terms agree with the rank-six counts of Vercleyen and Slingerland. The Fusion Ring Census supplies the enumeration through multiplicity 9. The number of classes with multiplicity at most 9 is 25421; the number with exact multiplicity 9 is 8809.

EDIT-LOG / SUBMISSION EXPLANATION
Extend the rank-six fixed-rank draft by a(9)=8809, preserving a(1) through a(8). The cumulative count increases from 16612 to 25421. These are exact-multiplicity counts of all based fusion rings, not counts of categorifiable rings. The supplied bound-nine enumeration reports completion of all three duality strata. The published independent table check reports 25421 distinct rings, zero duplicates and verification of associativity, using all 120 unit-fixing permutations for each table. This table validation is distinct from rerunning the complete enumeration.
