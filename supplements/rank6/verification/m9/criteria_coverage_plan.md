# Multiplicity-nine criteria coverage audit

Audit target: `work_m9/Rank6_Categorification_M9`, inherited implementation as of 2026-09-30. This is an applicability and execution plan, not an exclusion certificate. No mathematical conclusion follows from a timeout, failed search, unmet implementation gate, or feasible necessary system. Every proposed new exclusion must replay exactly with numerical optimizers disabled.

The initial campaign applies all standard `classify.STAGES` conditions to all 8,809 new tensors, omits vacuous retained-certificate completion, and adds dual-module Gram. The table separates additional mathematics from alternative implementations and refinements already inside a standard stage.

Cost codes describe a proposed **per-process** allowance, not measured complexity: C = 30–60 seconds; M = 120–180 seconds; H = 300–840 seconds, only on a matched residual. Every process and descendants needs an external deadline. No job may exceed 840 seconds.

## Safe reuse and execution order

1. Transfer existing exact proofs/models only under a full unit-preserving based-ring isomorphism. Maximum structure coefficient is invariant, so **no exact-M9 rank-six tensor can equal or be based-isomorphic to an M≤8 tensor**. Equal FP dimensions, codegrees, Casimir polynomials, or module profiles are not sufficient. Historical tensor-parametric criteria remain applicable.
2. Enumerate all proper dual-closed subrings before expensive searches. Their maximum coefficient can be lower: transfer the rank-two/rank-three theorems, complete supported rank-four/five classification, and any full-tensor matched constructive model. A subring positive model does not imply an ambient positive model.
3. After standard residuals are available, run direct general arithmetic and full Morita/C4 criteria first. Record gate failures distinctly from completed non-exclusions; preserve errors and deadlines.
4. Compute and exactly verify one reusable general-to-PU reduction per eligible surviving tensor. Cache with the full tensor digest. Probe stronger PU center conditions on matched structural/field gates in increasing cost, accepting only exact full-branch certificates.
5. Retain source-bound per-ring records, elapsed time, deadline, discovery function, verifier, mathematical scope, finite completeness, and exact checker outcome. Never silently promote historical proof files or conditional profile feasibility to a new general result.

Implementation audit notes: standard general induction already invokes balanced higher traces and integer Gram; `module_dual_gram` and ramified field certificates require local replay; the NC commutative-sector default entry is misspelled; family-specific split/center/profile code must not be applied based on filename similarity. This coverage is exhaustive over inherited implemented criterion families, not a claim that all conceivable categorical nonexistence theorems have an algorithmic test.

## Independent continuation audit refinements

The continuation/model audit independently checked all 11 positive registries: their 64 constructor records have maximum coefficient at most 7. They cannot identify an exact-M9 tensor. Parameterized ring formulas such as `cyclic_near_group(n,m)`, `sign_swap_equivariantization(m)`, `inversion_rules(n)`, and `quadratic(m,n)` must not be extrapolated beyond their individually cited categorical parameter values. Rank-six TY, SU2, dihedral, ADE and Izumi constructors have rank-forced parameters already in the registry.

Cheap complete-tensor projection recognizers are `round4_higher_fast.half_e6_subrings`, `.cyclic_three_subrings`, `round4_m8_ising.ising_labels`, and the elementary Fibonacci square `a²=1+a`. The C3 wrapper specifically accepts only `{'kind':'two_FP_values'}` and uses ambient `(height=3,F=24)`; the half-E6 verifier uses `(3,60)` and has no discovery generator. `round4_higher_mass.certificate` returns `(certificate, partial)` and has only PU replay. Ising and Fibonacci `run` return reports; only `outcome='pseudo_unitary_excluded'` contains an exclusion certificate. Their general wrappers require a full `galois_pseudo_reduction`, not a dummy two-FP gate.

`round4_m8_ising_verlinde.probe` handles one branch and its CLI fixes an old tensor/labelling. Importing its `run` does not replace the base Ising module's system. `round4_quartic_profiles`, `round4_quartic_twists`, `round5_1204`, `round5_1660` and pointed-profile replays can return verified survivors; a passed verifier is not a nonexistence result. The last two `run`/CLI routes hardcode old tensor IDs. These are specialist necessary-data engines, not missing generic one-call criteria.

General wrapper kinds with payload `{version:1,scope:'general',reduction,pseudo}` are `general_stabilizer_rotation`, `general_stabilizer_rotation_higher`, `general_cyclotomic_stabilizer_trace`, `general_integral_morita_rotation`, `general_low_verlinde_restriction`, `general_ambient_full_character_rotation`, `general_fibonacci_subfield_rotation`, and `general_ising_projection_rotation`. The higher-stabilizer wrapper alone additionally accepts an exactly checked two-FP-value reduction. For efficiency, first obtain a complete PU certificate; compute a costly Galois reduction only when there is a PU exclusion to promote, or when several promising matched center probes will reuse it.
