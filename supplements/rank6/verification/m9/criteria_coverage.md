# Multiplicity-nine final criteria coverage

The exact-nine input has 8,809 tensors. The completed main campaign accepts 8,798 independently replayed general exclusions and retains eleven general existence questions. Its actual 17-stage order, individual budgets, and per-ring outcomes are authoritative in `classification/summary.json` and `classification/stages.json`. The separately recorded ten-stage advanced campaign has exactly those eleven inputs: 110 attempts, 55 searches returning no certificate, 54 unmet gates, and one unfinished general signed reduction. It accepts no additional general proof. “No certificate” does not assert satisfaction of every necessary condition.

Six residuals have independently verified **pivotal** obstructions: R6_9_7018, R6_9_7741, R6_9_7742, R6_9_8099, R6_9_8101, R6_9_8200. Complete spherical proof trees plus the tensor-checked self-duality of every simple give this scope: pivotal left/right traces obey dim_left(X)=dim_right(X*), and equality on simples suffices in a fusion category. Thus pivotal, spherical, pseudo-unitary and unitary existence are no; general existence remains unknown. The other five new residuals are R6_9_529, R6_9_1060, R6_9_2455, R6_9_3748, R6_9_8785. R6_9_3748 separately has no braided categorification.
