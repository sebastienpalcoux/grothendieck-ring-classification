\begin{abstract}
We study complex categorifiability of the $\CensusCount$ rank-six fusion
rings of multiplicity at most eight in the \emph{Fusion Ring Census}.
Combining arithmetic and subring obstructions with a unified
central-induction framework, we obtain $\NegativeCount$ general
exclusions, $\PositiveCount$ categorifiable rings, and $\UnresolvedCount$
unresolved cases. Categorifiability is completely classified through
multiplicity three: $46$ of $577$ rings admit spherical models, including
$45$ with unitary models; the other $531$ rings are generally excluded.
Our necessary criteria and model identifications are applied
with exact certificates and a stepwise exclusion table. The continuation
adds spectral constraints on involution modules, rank-three subring
descent, a Morita--grading obstruction, and a biquadratic dimension-field
trace test, and reapplies the stronger
induction criteria throughout multiplicities four to eight. The final
multiplicity-three obstruction links a local involution commutator to
cubic pivotal rotation. Exhaustive sphericalization first reduces
arbitrary existence to pseudo-unitary existence; the forced module
action then determines two third indicators, and exact separating
certificates exclude every remaining induction branch. Earlier feasible
rank-$22$ modular and restriction-ring data are retained to show why
those relaxations alone did not decide the ring. Matching modular data
or a positive ring map does not supply a categorical model.
The present continuation develops forced cyclic-associator constraints,
local isolation of signed dimension fields and noncommutative signed
Casimir saturation, with further exact induction refinements at
multiplicities four through eight.
\end{abstract}
