\begin{abstract}
We study complex categorifiability of the $\CensusCount$ rank-six fusion
rings of multiplicity at most eight in the \emph{Fusion Ring Census}.
Arithmetic and sphericalization criteria reduce unrestricted existence to
exact finite problems in central induction and module categories.
A common framework combines restriction Gram matrices, character and
Galois symmetry, pivotal rotation, projection to fusion subcategories,
and simultaneous product moments. Positive constructions include three
neutral components of conformal extensions, identified by exact tensor
reconstruction.
The resulting classification has $\NegativeCount$ generally excluded
rings, $\PositiveCount$ realized rings, and $\UnresolvedCount$ unresolved
rings; the exact multiplicity bands $\CompleteBands$ are complete.
Every exclusion and model identification has an exact reproducible
certificate.
\end{abstract}
