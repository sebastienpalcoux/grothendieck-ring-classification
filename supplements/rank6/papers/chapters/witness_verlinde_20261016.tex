\section{A Verlinde obstruction to the rank-37 witness}
\label{sec:1660-witness-verlinde}

The preceding necessary conditions do not suffice for modular data. We now
exclude the particular rank-$37$ restriction, twist, and representation
witness specified below. This does not exclude
$\RR{8}{1660}$ or every refinement of its retained profiles.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:1660-witness-verlinde}
The specified rank-$37$ witness cannot be the modular data of a center:
it forces a fusion coefficient to equal $1/2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Use the source order $(1,g,X,gX,\sigma,T)$, where $T=\sigma X$.
The witness has a low simple $A$ and exactly two simples $B_+,B_-$ of
twist $\zeta_{48}^{4}$, with
\[
 F(A)=1+X+gX+2T,\qquad F(B_\pm)=X+gX+2T.
\]
Among its active modular irreducibles, precisely $W=(61,5)$ and
$H=(0,1)$ contain that twist, each with multiplicity one. Here $W$ has
dimension $12$, simple twist spectrum, and a one-dimensional twist-one
space; $H$ is one-dimensional. These assertions follow directly from
the frozen local character tables.

Let $e_W=w_6/2$ be the normalized twist-one vector of $W$, as in
\S\ref{sec:1660-forced-third-indicator}. Reciprocity gives equal nonzero
coordinates of $se_W$ at the two repeated restriction rows. Hence
\[
 b=\frac{e_{B_+}+e_{B_-}}{\sqrt2}\in W,\qquad
 h=\frac{e_{B_+}-e_{B_-}}{\sqrt2}\in H.
\]
For a center simple $E$ with restriction row $v_E$, put
\[
 a_E=|\langle e_E,se_W\rangle|^2
     =\frac{L_6(v_E)^2}{96},\qquad
 L_6=(-1,-1,-1,-1,-2,2).
\]
The last equality follows from $sw_6=4\sqrt6\,\ell_6$ and
$48\ell_6(F(E))=L_6(v_E)$. Write
\[
 r_E=\frac{s_{A,E}}{s_{0,E}},\qquad
 q_t=\frac{\sum_{\theta_E=\zeta_{48}^t}r_Ea_E}
              {\sum_{\theta_E=\zeta_{48}^t}a_E}.
\]
Since every twist eigenspace of $W$ is one-dimensional, $q_t$ is the
expectation of the Verlinde spectral diagonal on that line. The exact
transition probabilities are
\[
 p_t=\operatorname{Tr}_W(P_4sP_ts^{-1}).
\]
Expanding the two projectors gives character averages. For a local
factor of modulus $m$, with local target phase $c$, the average is
\[
 \frac1{m^2}\sum_{j,k\bmod m}
 \zeta_m^{-cj-tk}\operatorname{char}_W(T^jST^kS^{-1}).
\]
The local phases of $4$ are $(12,1)$; CRT reconstructs a global phase
as $3a+16b\pmod {48}$. Exact evaluation gives
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 t&0&16&6&22&12&28\\\hline
 p_t&1/12&1/24&1/6&1/12&1/12&1/24\\
 q_t&15&3&-1&-1&-1&-1
\end{array}
\qquad
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrr}
 t&18&34&24&40&36&4\\\hline
 p_t&1/6&1/12&1/12&1/24&1/12&1/24\\
 q_t&-1&-1&15&3&-1&-1
\end{array}
\]
and therefore $\langle b,N_A b\rangle=\sum_t p_tq_t=2$.
Here $N_A=s\operatorname{diag}(r_E)s^*$ by Verlinde.

On the one-dimensional summand $H$, the matrix $s$ acts by a scalar.
Moreover $r_{B_+}=r_{B_-}=-1$. Thus $N_Ah=-h$, and its mixed matrix
coefficients with $b$ vanish. Consequently
\[
 N_{A,B_+}^{B_+}=\langle e_{B_+},N_Ae_{B_+}\rangle
                =\frac{2-1}{2}=\frac12,
\]
contradicting integral fusion multiplicities.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope and replay.}
The proof uses no numerical modular completion. The standard-library
checker in
\filename{evidence/unified_20261016/rank6/verlinde\_obstruction/}
binds the source tensor, the two certified local character tables, and
the specified witness. It checks all $1536$ equations
and $1447$ variable bounds of the linear necessary system, which
pass, and then obtains the coefficient $1/2$ exactly. Its normal and
optimized receipts and an independent categorical audit are included.
Other representation and twist refinements are not covered by this
proposition; both rank-six categorification questions remain unresolved.
