\subsection{The finite centralizer frontier for $R^\prime(4)$}
\label{sec:frontier-155}

The ring $R6\_4\_155$ remains unresolved.  The new certificate replaces its
previous necessary-data witness by a complete finite list of six
induction profiles, each with a forced pair of low fusion matrices in a
semion centralizer.  This is a reduction of the categorification problem, not an
existence claim for any profile.

Write the basis as $\mathbf1,X,X^*,h,g,hg$, where the invertibles form
$C_2^2$, $h$ fixes $X,X^*$, and $g$ exchanges them.  The exact dimension
and products are
\[
 d=4+3\sqrt2,\qquad D=72+48\sqrt2,\qquad
 X^2=g+hg+4X+4X^*,\quad XX^*=\mathbf1+h+4X+4X^*.
\]
The previously certified sphericalization reduction applies to every
categorification of this ring.  We therefore use its pseudo-unitary
Galois representative throughout the necessary-data computation.

First apply the pointed version of
Proposition~\ref{prop:central-subcategory-projection} to all three
involutions.  If a central simple restricts with a nonzero coefficient of
an involution $k$, its twist has square $\nu_2(k)$.  Of the ten ordinary
induction frames, only one survives the resulting exact systems.  Its six
low columns are
\[
 (1,0,0,0,0,0),\quad (1,2,2,0,0,0),\quad
 (1,2,2,1,0,0)\ \text{twice},\quad
 (1,1,1,0,0,0)\ \text{twice}.
\]
Its four character labellings differ by permutations of equal columns;
the verifier checks the permutations explicitly.  The surviving second
indicators, in the displayed basis order after the unit, are
$(0,0,1,-1,-1)$.  This first reduction uses twenty-eight exact Farkas
certificates.

The exact right-$(\mathbf1\oplus h)$ module enumeration has one
anti-invariant representation, in which $h$ exchanges the two module
structures on both $X$ and $X^*$.  Proposition~\ref{prop:stabilizer-rotation}
applies to these non-self-dual objects: its hypothesis concerns left and
right stabilizers and does not require self-duality.  Since
$[X^3:\mathbf1]=[(X^*)^3:\mathbf1]=4$, the third indicators have the form
$-(a+b\zeta_3+c\zeta_3^2)$ with $a+b+c=2$.  Their normalized relative
traces to $\mathbb Q(\zeta_8)$ belong to
$\{-2,-\tfrac12,1\}$.  Duality gives the same value for $X$ and $X^*$.
Eight further exact Farkas certificates exclude the first two values in
all four labellings.

The remaining bounded Gram--Galois lattice has exactly $1066$ integral
profiles; an independent optimizer-free recursion enumerates all of them.
The unique central object restricting to the unit makes the image of
central invertibles injective.  Subgroup closure and their tensor
permutation of restriction types leave $874$ profiles.  The unified joint
restriction--word Gram criterion, using all fifteen quadratic and
thirty-five cubic low words, excludes $772$ profiles by an empty exact
coefficient domain and four by exact Farkas inequalities.  Coupling the
remaining coefficient vectors to the same twist-trace variables and
imposing ribbon balancing excludes another $39$ profiles.  For the
non-self-dual low pair the balancing target is $S_{A_a^*,A_b}$, as in
Proposition~\ref{prop:projected-product-balancing}.

All $59$ remaining profiles force unique central lifts of $h,g,hg$.
Their twists satisfy $q(g),q(hg)\in\{i,-i\}$.  The lift of $h$ is
fermionic: a bosonic lift would allow de-equivariantization by
$\mathbf1\oplus h$, and the fixed simple $X$ would split into objects of
dimension $d/2$; its norm is $-1/2$, contradicting algebraic integrality.
If $q(g)=q(hg)$, the pointed monodromy form has trivial radical; otherwise
its radical is $\langle h\rangle$.  The exact divisibility test in
Proposition~\ref{prop:pointed-stabilizer-divisibility} leaves respectively
seven and twenty-five profiles, with the former contained in the latter.

Finally the central lift of $g$ generates a nondegenerate semion
subcategory, so the \emph{center} decomposes as its product with a modular
centralizer $\mathcal E$.  Restriction columns occur in pairs $(v,gv)$.
The low monodromies select the low objects $\mathbf1,A_1,A_2$ in
$\mathcal E$, hence the restricted adjoint algebra is
$\mathbf1\oplus A_1\oplus A_2$.  For every endpoint orientation the
restricted adjunction identity is
\[
 V^{\mathsf T}V=I+N_{A_1}+N_{A_2}.
\]
Exhaustive endpoint enumeration, closure under duality, and restriction
compatibility leave fifteen profiles and thirty orientations.  The
possible center ranks are
\[
 30,32,36,38,40,42,44,46,48,52,54,60.
\]
No decomposition of the original unbraided category is asserted.

The remaining adjunction matrix splits by
Corollary~\ref{cor:graded-adjunction-splitting}.  The monodromy charges of $A_1,A_2$ against $h$ are respectively
$+1,-1$.  For any assignment $\epsilon_i\in\{\pm1\}$ to the centralizer
simples, $N_{A_1}$ is the same-charge block of $V^{\mathsf T}V-I$, and
$N_{A_2}$ is the complementary block.  Equal restriction columns are
permuted freely, so the verifier enumerates charge counts within each
type rather than labelled sign vectors.  It enforces the known low
charges, duality, restriction products, and commutation.  In the opposite
quadratic form the centralizer contains the fermion $h$, which preserves
charges and cannot fix a charge-$+1$ simple.  In the equal quadratic form
it contains the semion $hg$, whose tensor action reverses charges.
These exact finite tests leave six profiles and nine low-matrix pairs.

Complex conjugation of the entire pseudo-unitary category preserves its
positive dimensions and restriction columns and conjugates all twists.
The checked low-labelling permutations therefore allow the normalization
$q(g)=i$.  Three exact simultaneous-pointed-action and ribbon-balancing
rays eliminate the remaining equal-form pairs.  The final six profiles
have center ranks $44,46,48,52,54,60$, and each has one forced pair
$(N_{A_1},N_{A_2})$ in the opposite quadratic form.  The surviving profile
indices in the independently reconstructed enumeration are
$275,277,279,296,298,306$.


The compact certificate is
\texttt{research/round5/m4\_155/frontier\_certificate.json}; its independent
reconstruction is \texttt{replay\_frontier.py}.  The final packet
reconstruction is \texttt{replay\_packets.py}, with output
\texttt{packet\_frontier.json}.  The packet replay also reconstructs the charge splittings and the three
final rays.  Both replay programs disable numerical
optimizers and use explicit checks that remain active under
\texttt{python -O}.  Full fusion associativity and modular completion of
this finite frontier remain necessary before any categorification can be
claimed.
