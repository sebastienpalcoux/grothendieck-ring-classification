\subsection{Completion at exact multiplicity nine}
\label{sec:multiplicity-nine}
The stratum contains $8\,809$ new rank-six based rings whose largest
structure coefficient is exactly nine: $8\,808$ commutative rings and one
noncommutative ring. None admits a complex fusion categorification.
The initial exact sieve excludes $8\,798$ rings. The following arguments
exclude its eleven residuals, without assuming pivotality or braiding.
The only remaining general existence questions through the census bound
are $\RR{4}{155}$ and $\RR{8}{1660}$.

The original and advanced campaign records retain their source-bound
attempts, including unfinished searches. They supply no exclusion beyond
their accepted certificates. The completion is a separate, disjoint set
of eleven general certificates with normal and optimized exact replays.
No census tensor, identifier, earlier decision, or counting term changes.
