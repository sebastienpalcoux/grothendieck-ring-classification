# Limits of the current R155 reduction

The canonical proof consists of the two replay programs and their compact certificates. The final six profiles remain unresolved; no associator, categorification, or full modular datum has been constructed.

Several further automated searches did not close this gap:

- Adding all quartic low words to the simultaneous restriction/word Gram domains left every tested profile with a nonempty domain. Separate feasibility statements do not establish a common categorical model.
- Coupling low-word monodromy support, twists, coefficient vectors, pointed tensor actions, and ribbon balancing produced no further exact profile exclusion. Some integer optimizers reported infeasibility or exhausted their time budgets; these reports are not used as proofs.
- Exact congruence searches modulo 2,3,5 and local Smith elimination modulo 64 found no additional contradiction on the final six profiles.
- In the semion centralizer, the two low fusion matrices are forced after the certified charge splitting. Adding every compatible fermion permutation and seeking the smallest noninvertible generator still produced bounded linear systems whose exhaustive enumeration exceeded the 100000-node discovery limit. Every such branch was retained.
- A conductor 24 discovery refinement keeps the original cubic Clifford indicator rather than its conductor 8 average. Among the three possible values 1,−2ζ3,−2ζ3², exact rays were found for the two nonreal values in each of the six profiles, while the real-value systems remained numerically feasible. This supplementary discovery was not added to the canonical certificate because it did not reduce the profile frontier.

The earlier rank 36 necessary-data witness fails the stronger joint cubic-word domain and is superseded. General claims that a fully faithful central semion embedding forces the original fusion category to split are false; only the Drinfeld-center factorization is used here.
