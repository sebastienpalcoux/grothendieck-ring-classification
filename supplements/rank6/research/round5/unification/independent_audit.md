# Independent mathematical and coverage audit

This audit concerns necessary-data certificates, not existence of fusion
categories. Canonical checkers rebuild their systems and verify exact outputs;
feasible fusion and modular data must retain an unresolved categorical status.

## Common word/twist balancing

`software/round5_balancing.py` correctly refines each old restriction/twist
count by a simultaneous word coefficient vector. Both old trace equations and
new Gram/restriction equations use these same refined count variables. For a
binary word `A_a A_b`, the target is the unnormalized entry
`dim(A_{a*}) chi_{a*}(F(A_b))`, namely `D S_(a*,b)`. This is essential for
complex low characters. The convention follows BNRW, arXiv:1310.7050v3,
equation (2.3): `theta_i theta_j S_ij = sum_k N_(i*,j)^k theta_k d_k`.
Applying the relative field trace is valid since dimensions and integral
word coefficients lie in the retained field; a trace type is not an actual
twist root.

## Independent categorical controls

`pointed_center_control.py` checks the actual centers of Vec(C2) and Vec(C3),
including complex low duality for C3. The full-center C3 ribbon identities
pass in all 81 cases; omitting the required dual fails 48 equations. Those
pointed low words have zero high coefficients, so the complementary

satisfy the 28 Gram/count and 49 coupled rows, including six nonzero product
balancing rows. Replacing the actual twists by one violates two balancing
rows. These are categorical positive controls and a concrete mutation control,
not tests that merely reconstruct the implementation's own formulas.

For the rank-35 complement, exact finite-group class algebras enumerate all

## Individual projection capacity

Actual-root projection masses constrain each individual central simple.
For an Ising sigma root the capacity is D*sqrt(2)/4, although a projected
trace bucket combining two roots has twice that capacity. A column whose
single-object mass exceeds the first quantity is impossible; its integral
multiplicity cannot be divided between the two actual roots. This bound
applies directly to restriction profiles, before any word-factorization or
twist-witness choice. The current multiplicity-eight domain incorporates it.

When an occupied relative trace coordinate is itself a root of unity, equality
in the triangle inequality forces the corresponding actual twist power to
equal that coordinate. This justifies finite actual-root lift enumeration
without treating an ambient field or a search cutoff as an exponent bound.

## Saturated dimension-block bounds

The six-input exact Fourier audit uses Schopieray arXiv:2609.35287v1,
Theorem 5.1. All six inequalities pass; the new information is in equality.
In the universal local induced representation the entire maximal singular
space is spanned by the big-cell vector and the adjoint small-cell vector.
Both are functions of the primitive column modulo the character conductor.
Thus equality forces the generated cyclic representation to factor through
that conductor. A trivial local character attaining norm one gives a vector
fixed by both upper and lower unipotents, hence a trivial local representation.
Orthogonal decomposition and CRT prove the global assertion. Consequently
a nonzero Fourier coefficient L_chi(v) forces theta^f=1.

For real characters, the cyclic representation embeds in Ind_B chi by
Frobenius reciprocity and the multiplicity bound supplied by its single
generating vector. Its generator lies in the specified compression eigenspace.
Every exact matrix moment relation on that whole eigenspace transfers to the
weighted twist moments; no particular irreducible constituent is assumed.
Distinct primitive conductors force inequivalent occupied constituents, so
their cyclic modules are orthogonal and their mixed twist moments vanish.
These equality-case deductions were independently checked by three agents.
The realized Fibonacci center controls both saturated root support and the
universal moment equations; replacing its nontrivial twists by one preserves
root support but violates all four nontrivial universal moment equations.

## Full rotations, prime indicators and finite representation spectra

For n dividing the proved exponent, grouping rotation powers by
`d=gcd(n,r)` gives `tr(E_n^r)=sigma_(r/d)(nu_(n/d)(X^d))`. Applying a consistent
unit lift modulo the exponent to the entire indicator is valid even when its
individual center summands lie in a larger field. Exact Fourier inversion
therefore yields nonnegative integer eigenvalue multiplicities. The actual48
rotation adapter includes the full Galois action on dimension ratios as well
as twists; its fixed-space-only option is a weaker necessary condition.

The large-prime engine uses exact modular binary powers and verifies the
row-sum cutoff and the sharper positive dimension embedding. For p coprime
to the exponent, rational cyclic characters force `nu_p == [X^p:1] (mod p)`;
the positive induction bound makes the representative unique when p>2B.
Primes in the same exponent residue must agree. The Vec(C3), Fibonacci,
wrong-exponent and binary-power controls test these deductions independently.
The canonical higher Gauss formula is
`tau_n = D^2 / sigma_(n^-1)(D)`; the inverse must not be silently replaced by
n outside the multiquadratic case.

For SL2(Z/48), exact class algebra homomorphisms, positive degrees and
orthogonality reconstruct every irreducible of SL2(Z/16) and SL2(Z/3).
The CRT spectrum index is `3*x+16*y mod48`. Merging identical signed spectra
preserves the integer cone because every merged type has an actual irreducible
representative. On each T eigenspace charge conjugation contributes one plus
and one minus for a dual pair, and one plus for a self-dual simple. Therefore
its signed multiplicity difference is nonnegative and is at least the known
self-dual low count. These are necessary representation constraints only.

## Modular subcategory projection and signed complements

The additive central projection is not assumed to preserve tensor products.
Its multiplicity matrix nevertheless intertwines the canonical normalized
center S and T matrices. The proof identifies its entries, rather than only
its dimensions, with the full center of the D–C bimodule C: right-module
naturality is determined at the unit, and left-module naturality is exactly
the restricted half-braiding equation. The coefficient convention is
W tensor Z-dual; the diagonal inclusion gives the identity matrix, fixing
both the dual and reverse-center conventions. The unit column then proves
the individual target-simple mass and capacity bound. The new statement is
pseudo-unitary; the earlier signed-spherical Fourier-mass corollary retains
its separate proof and broader hypotheses.

The exact subgroup control derives both modular matrices and the projection
from finite-group character formulas for Vec(C2), Vec(C3) inside Vec(S3).
All rectangular S/T identities and individual masses hold. The C3 target
has rank nine but the projection has rank five, so general surjectivity
cannot be inferred. A changed unit multiplicity fails the S identity.

If every projection column is zero or a standard basis vector, B B^T is
positive diagonal and commutes with S. Its unit column has no zero entries,
forcing B B^T=rI. This proves the full target modular representation embeds
isometrically, and permits subtracting its T spectrum. Charge conjugation
on the complement is not a permutation: its signed trace bound is the
full-space bound minus the known subrepresentation trace. The R1660 adapter
has the correct signs in both the T-count and signed-trace equations; its
complete-profile projection-shape check is a required prerequisite.

The quartic projection test exhausts every twist-compatible nonnegative
integral forgetful column. Its reduction to bounded integer-segment

bounded free-coordinate enumeration are complete. Every resulting matrix
is rechecked against every S, T, unit and forgetful equation. A surviving
