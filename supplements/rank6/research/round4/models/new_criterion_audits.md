# Independent audits of round-four criteria

## Fibonacci subfield exclusion, R6_8_3664

`software/round4_m8_subfield.py` and its general certificate were independently
reviewed. The character-field calculation verifies that the complete character
table lies in K=Q(sqrt(5),sqrt(6)), that K is generated by the global dimension,
and that every automorphism of K has square equal to the identity. The exact
embedding and normalized relative trace from Q(zeta_120) to K are checked in
the implementation. The 43 phase types are a complete bounded relative-trace
domain, rather than a list of selected twist orders.

The fixed-trace Galois balancing is valid specifically because Gal(K/Q)
has exponent two. The modular Galois permutation acts on twists by sigma²,
so the normalized relative phase is fixed, while the joint eigenvalue type
transforms by sigma. Rational, nonnegative rotation variables are a relaxation
of sums of integer eigenspace multiplicities; no integrality assertion is
made for an individual relative trace.

Fibonacci projection removes low columns with c_a>c_1. For high columns with
c_a>0 it restricts the actual twists to zeta_5² and zeta_5^-2, then projects
these allowed twists into the same relative-trace coordinates. The proof uses
the canonical positive pivotal structure after the general reduction.

An independent replay ran `verify_general` under Python optimization (`-O`)
with both `scipy.optimize.linprog` and `milp` replaced by functions which raise
an exception. It passed all 13 low frames, both exact Farkas rays, and the
general signed reduction. The recorded result is
`fibonacci_subfield_replay.json`. The replay used no optimizer. No gap was
identified between the pseudo-unitary obstruction and its general reduction.

## Odd relative cyclotomic orbits, R6_4_629

The proposition in `papers/chapters/round4_629_orbits.tex` and implementation
`software/round4_629_orbits.py` were independently reviewed.

For a modular Galois automorphism fixing K, the normalized S entries in every
low induction row A_chi lie in K. The row therefore remains fixed, and the
positive entry in its unit column removes the possible negative Galois sign.
The unit row is fixed for the same reason. Applied to column permutation,
positivity of the unit row removes every column sign. The fixed values
S_(A_chi,Z)=chi(FZ)/f_chi then determine FZ because the complete character
table is invertible. Thus every restriction-column class is preserved.

For odd F and twist order n supported on primes dividing F, the relative
Galois group Gal(Q(zeta_lcm(F,n))/Q(zeta_F)) has odd order
product_p p^max(0,v_p(n)-v_p(F)). It is abelian, so squaring is bijective.
The modular square action therefore has the full ordinary relative orbits;
each primitive n-th-root orbit has the stated size. Uniform multiplicities
on each orbit imply the exact count semigroup used by the code.

The implementation correctly bounds the total number of copies of a nonzero
high column v by min_i floor(G_ii/v_i²). This bounds every orbit size and
makes the prime-valuation enumeration exhaustive. Removing an orbit size
which is a positive multiple of another retained size leaves the additive
semigroup unchanged. The high-column variable ordering agrees with the
underlying ambient system, including spectral pruning. Auxiliary rotation
variables retain the ambient relaxation. No odd-orbit claim is made for an
even relative Galois group.

The proposition is a necessary condition only. An exact feasible integer
witness is not a center, modular datum, or categorification.

## Half-E6 projected third-rotation variables

The table, all six x-masses, and the exhaustiveness of the two positive
half-E6 branches in `papers/chapters/round4_higher.tex` agree with the source
classification and center calculation. The normalized trace pullback in
`software/round4_higher_real.py` is checked coordinate by coordinate, and
the exponent-two automorphism condition justifies its fixed-phase Galois
balancing.

Both auxiliary coordinates for every third-rotation block may be required
to be nonnegative integers. This includes the blocks whose coefficients
are fractional. For an ordinary cubic invariant space, let (a,b,c) be the
integer multiplicities of (1,zeta_3,zeta_3²). Its two projected coordinates
are (u,v)=(a,b+c), with u+v=m and projected trace u-v/2. For a forced
Clifford block, use the integer multiplicities on the Clifford multiplicity
spaces in Proposition `prop:stabilizer-rotation`; then (u,v)=(a,b+c),
u+v=m/2, and projected trace -u+v/2. Since the field Q(sqrt(3),sqrt(5)) is
real, normalized relative trace pairs zeta_3 with zeta_3² in both cases.
The values 1/2 and -1/2 are trace coefficients, not fractional counts.

Thus the ten rotation variables appended by `round4_higher_real.system`
admit the integrality refinement. Imposing it does not assert that b=c;
the projected equations retain only their integer sum. No such conclusion
is asserted for an arbitrary auxiliary variable introduced by a different
normalization or relaxation.

## Cyclic-three exclusion, R6_6_49

The proof and verifier `round4_higher_c3.verify_general` were independently
audited. The general reduction checks the exact positive dimension character
has precisely the values 1 and d. It invokes Schopieray's published Lemma
3.1 in *Categorification of integral group rings extended by one dimension*,
J. London Math. Soc. 108 (2023), DOI 10.1112/jlms.12789: a categorification
has a pseudo-unitary Galois conjugate. The older arXiv v1 overstates this as
positivity of the original category; the release correctly cites the
published Galois-conjugacy version. No assumed pivotal structure is needed
for this general reduction.

The exact certificate covers 353 low frames: 154 ordinary failures,
198 without a compatible character labelling, and one remaining frame.
Its four character labellings, three pointed associator classes and two
second-indicator tuples give all 24 recorded Farkas systems. The new cached
Galois helper retains the original transformed eigenvalues, squared twist
action, row ordering and low-column correction. It only reuses exact
automorphism evaluations.

The independent optimized audit `c3_independent_audit.py` sums the relative
Galois action directly in Q(zeta_72) and checks all 18 source twists over the
two nontrivial fluxes and all three associator classes. It checks the detector
under all 120 unit-preserving basis permutations of the target and Vec(C6).
Eight negative mutations are rejected, including deleting a nontrivial
associator branch and negating an actual separating ray. Numerical optimizers
are disabled. `c3_independent_audit.json` records the certificate hash and
results. This targeted audit supplements the case agent's complete normal
and optimized replays; it is not described as a third full replay.

## Ising exclusion, R6_8_311

The phase and mass conditions in `software/round4_m8_ising.py` were audited
independently. Over Q(zeta_24), each pair of opposite primitive sixteenth
roots merges under normalized trace from Q(zeta_48) to the type
(0,zeta_8^a,0). Its mass is twice the single-twist mass, hence D*sqrt(2)/2.
The condition zeta_8^a+zeta_8^-a=nu_2(sigma)*sqrt(2) selects precisely the
two possible projected types for either Ising associator sign. Neither
positive spherical branch is omitted. The code explicitly checks that both
types exist in the complete ambient domain.

The low twist-one g-mass is D/2: a central object whose twist is one and
whose restriction contains g must also contain the unit by the projection
table, so it occurs among the low columns. A high column cannot mix g and
sigma, cannot contain either at twist one, and has the exact allowed phase
support used by the implementation. The ordinary and low-character equations
are retained, with the full coupled Galois action on relative phases.

The general certificate records all 96 unrestricted signed quotients in
eight sign orbits: one original gauge orbit, five commutative arithmetic
branches and two noncommutative branches. The latter are handled by the
oriented-extension checker, not by a commutative character assumption.
The only nonzero original dimension-character component is the FP orbit;
the other component has zero simple dimensions. The reduction verifier
reconstructs these domains before the Ising certificate is accepted.
The case agent supplies the complete normal/optimized replay and mutation
receipts; this independent audit focuses on the mathematical phase merging
and general-scope coverage.

## Final general half-E6 certificate, R6_6_1035

The final certificate `research/round4/higher/R6_6_1035_general.json` and
`round4_higher_e6.verify_general` were audited after completion. They use
the full conductor-60 system, not the exploratory real-field MILP or
Clifford refinements discussed earlier. The complete 12-frame domain has
two frames with a character assignment. Each has two retained second-
indicator choices, giving four exact Farkas systems. The general reduction
exhausts 448 unrestricted signed quotients in 14 sign orbits.

Checking just one of the two positive half-E6 source tables is valid here
because one may conjugate the **entire hypothetical category**. If C is
pseudo-unitary, its complex conjugate has the identical based tensor:
all fusion coefficients and duality permutations are integers. Its
canonical positive pivotal structure still has the same positive real
dimensions. Restriction to the chosen half-E6 subcategory conjugates its
monoidal and pivotal structure, exchanging exactly the two positive
branches classified by Hagge--Hong. The corresponding center twists all
conjugate. Consequently any realization in the omitted branch would give
a realization of the same full input tensor in the checked branch.
Conjugation can permute ordinary character labels, but every compatible
labelling is enumerated. Second indicators are real and are unchanged.
This argument does not hold by independently replacing selected twists
inside a fixed hypothetical ambient category; global conjugation is the
essential step.

The source table directly gives nu_2(g)=nu_2(x)=1 in both branches by the
central indicator formula, justifying the verifier's two fixed indicator
coordinates. `half_e6_independent_audit.py` verifies these identities in
Q(zeta_12), the exchange of source twists under conjugation, all 120
unit-preserving relabellings of the target and of the realized control
half-E6 times Vec(C2), and nine schema mutations. It ran with Python
optimization and disabled optimizers. The source-bound receipt is
`half_e6_independent_audit.json`. Complete normal/optimized general
replays are supplied by the case agent and canonical release checker.
No mathematical or branch-coverage gap was found in the final certificate.
