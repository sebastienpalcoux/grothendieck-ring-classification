# Independent audit of the third-continuation arithmetic reductions

## Inert quadratic isolation: R6_8_6524 and R6_8_6525

The theorem and implementation in `software/round3_arithmetic.py` and
`research/round3/arithmetic/proof.md` were checked independently. The field
degree deduction and the divisibilities by **original** formal codegrees
are valid for general complex categorifications, with the sphericalization
dimension-field lemma already stated in the manuscript.

Here are the points on which the scope depends.

1. For a commutative self-dual input, the chosen lifts of all simples in
   sphericalization are self-dual: the alternate lift has opposite nonzero
   dimension, whereas duality preserves spherical dimension. Thus the
   sphericalized fusion ring is commutative. The negative-sign quotient A
   is a commutative signed integral based algebra, with symmetric regular
   matrices. In particular its rational algebra is a product of totally
   real number fields; its dimension character defines a field quotient K.
2. Every original character extends to the positive-sign sector of
   sphericalization, with twice its original formal codegree f. The
   sphericalization has global dimension 2 Delta. Central induction gives
   a central simple of dimension Delta/f. Forgetting shows this lies in
   the integer span of the signed dimensions, hence in O_K. Since Delta
   is nonzero and belongs to K, this also puts f in K. The same argument
   applies to the negative-sign characters. These are sphericalization
   dimensions; no replacement by Frobenius–Perron dimensions is made.
3. A finite free Z_2 algebra with finite étale special fibre is finite
   étale. One may first use Nakayama on its finite module of relative
   differentials to obtain unramifiedness, and then finite freeness gives
   étaleness. Its factors are the unramified local extensions with the
   displayed residue degrees. Stacks Project, Tag 04GK, classifies those
   finite étale lifts over the henselian base Z_2. The tag was checked at
   https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04GK on 2026-09-29.
4. E=Q(sqrt(q)) is inert at 2 because q=5 mod 8. If E is contained in a
   number field F, every completion of F at 2 contains the unramified
   quadratic completion of E, so every corresponding local degree is
   even. There is precisely one even-degree local factor of A, of degree
   two. Applied to K, this proves K=E, including the assertion that K
   occupies all of that local factor.
5. Each remaining signed formal codegree belongs to E by induction and
   to its own character field F by definition. If irrational, it generates
   E, requiring an additional even-degree local factor for F. The dimension
   component has already used the unique such factor. Thus the other four
   codegrees are rational algebraic integers. Positivity and the reciprocal
   identity make each greater than one.

The finite arithmetic is also correctly normalized. If
`Delta=c(q y+x sqrt(q))/2`, its trace is `c q y`, its norm is
`c² q(q y²−x²)/4`, and the d-number test is
`(q y²−x²) | 4q y²`. The trace of its inverse is
`4y/[c(q y²−x²)]`. In the integral basis `1,(1+sqrt(q))/2`, its two
coordinates are `c(qy−x)/2,cx`; a rational integer f divides Delta
precisely when it divides both. This justifies the divisor content used
by the implementation. The upper bound `cqy <= T−5` is conservative:
four integer complementary codegrees greater than one actually sum to
at least eight. That weaker bound omits no possibility.

The case x=0 must not be discarded: the field generated by all signed
dimensions can be quadratic even if their squared sum is rational.
The implementation includes it. Counting Delta twice in the signed
Casimir spectrum is also correct in that case, since there remain two
distinct dimension characters.

For q=149,c=9,T=6741, the independent direct enumeration in
`arithmetic_audit.py` reproduces only x=+61 or −61,y=5 and four codegrees
equal to 9, with trace 6741. It uses a separate fourfold divisor loop,
not the recursive tuple routine being audited. Saturation therefore
forces every signed tensor coefficient to retain its original absolute
value. Exhausting signs and checking their gauges is then a sufficient
finite reduction to the previously recorded character checks.

## Cubic-factor extension: R6_8_6999

The proposed local-global extension is sound with the following explicit
additional ingredient. One-dimensional characters of a fusion category
have cyclotomic values (ENO's cyclotomicity theorem); a signed character
here is such a character of sphericalization with sign object sent to −1.
Its character field is therefore an abelian Galois extension of Q.

Suppose the reduction of A at 2 has local factor ranks 3,2,1, with the
rank-three factor the field F8 and the rank-two factor dual numbers.
Idempotents lift over Z_2, giving separate generic local blocks of these
ranks, even though the whole algebra need not be étale over Z_2. The
rank-three block is étale, hence its generic fibre is the unramified cubic
field. Let F be its global character field. All 2-adic completions of the
Galois field F have the same local degree, here three. Thus its degree is
three or six. Degree six would require two degree-three local factors;
the residual blocks of ranks two and one cannot supply another. Therefore
F is cubic. The remaining rational algebra has dimension three. As its
dimension field contains E=Q(sqrt(41)), that dimension field equals E.
Every other codegree lies in E intersected with either the cubic F or the
remaining rational field, so is an integer. The same quadratic spectrum
enumeration is consequently applicable. This argument does not presume
that the rank-two order is maximal or that its generic quadratic algebra
is a field at 2.

## Noncommutative rank-six proposal: R6_6_170

The proposed semisimple-algebra reduction has the stated normalization.
If the signed quotient remains noncommutative modulo two, it cannot be
commutative in characteristic zero. The positive involutive trace form
makes it semisimple. A noncommutative complex semisimple algebra of dimension
six admitting a dimension character is exactly C+C+M2(C). Therefore its
dimension character field has degree at most two. Original-codegree
containment supplies Q(sqrt(37)), so equality follows.

There is only one two-dimensional irreducible character. Galois action
preserves its degree, so its formal codegree h is Galois invariant. Formal
codegrees are algebraic integers; hence h is an integer. The regular
Casimir acts on that two-dimensional representation by 2h, with regular
multiplicity two, so its contribution to the regular trace is **8h**.
The reciprocal identity contributes **2/h**. Central induction produces
the dimension **Delta/h**, with no additional factor two: the degree two
appears in the multiplicity in induction, not in that simple object's
dimension. Thus the conditions

`Tr(Delta)+8h <= T`, `Tr(1/Delta)+2/h=1`, and `Delta/h integral`

are consistent. The original-codegree divisibilities `Delta/D`,
`Delta/D'`, and `Delta/4` use the positive-sign sector and remain valid
without a pivotal assumption on the original category. This audit covers
the proposed theorem and its numerical factors; the root's separate
signed enumeration and certificate replay establish its particular
application. No computational outcome is inferred merely from this note.
