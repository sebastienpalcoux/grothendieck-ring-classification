# A Morita-grading obstruction at multiplicity five

## Statement

Neither `R6_5_100` nor `R6_5_408` admits a fusion-category categorification over C. More generally, the obstruction applies to a rank-six commutative fusion ring with trivial universal grading if the exhaustive involution-module test forces its dual by a split involution algebra to have exactly six invertible simples and six simples of dimension d=2+sqrt(5).

The new implementation is `software/new_morita_order6.py`. It does not infer noncategorifiability from failure to find a model. It exhausts a finite list of possible fusion rings for a categorically forced Morita dual, then uses either exact inherited negative certificates or a grading contradiction.

## 1. The forced dual

Let C be a hypothetical categorification and let g be the unique nontrivial invertible (label 4 for R6_5_100, label 1 for R6_5_408). A g-fixed simple object supplies a rank-one module category for the pointed subcategory generated by g. Consequently its associator class in H^3(C2,C*) is trivial, and A=1+g has its usual connected separable algebra structure.

The involution-module reconstruction already established in the manuscript is categorical and requires no pivotality. The exact enumeration `anti_representations` gives one admissible anti-invariant module representation for each of these two input tensors. Its action of g on the two fixed-object splitting pairs is +I. The bimodule restriction Gram matrix therefore forces the dual category D=_A C_A to have dimensions

    1,1,1,1,1,1,d,d,d,d,d,d;   d=2+sqrt(5), d^2=1+4d.

The attached certificate records the unique representation and these dimensions in the exact number field supplied by the tensor. All fusion coefficients, multiplicities and signs are reconstructed on checking.

Every categorification with two distinct Frobenius-Perron dimensions is Galois conjugate to a pseudounitary category (Schopieray, *Categorification of integral group rings extended by one dimension*, J. London Math. Soc. 108 (2023), Lemma 3.1). Accordingly pseudo-unitary formal-codegree integrality is a general necessary condition for D.

## 2. A finite classification of the possible dual based rings

Let G be the group of six invertibles of D, and choose a noninvertible rho. The invertible summands in rho rho* are exactly its stabilizer, each with multiplicity one. Comparing rational and irrational coefficients in d^2=1+4d shows the stabilizer is trivial. Hence the six noninvertibles are precisely g rho, g in G; right multiplication likewise acts freely. There is an automorphism phi of G such that rho g=phi(g)rho. We have

    rho^2 = u + sum_g a_g g rho,  sum_g a_g=4.

Associativity implies phi^2=Ad(u), phi(u)=u, and invariance of the coefficient function under

    h -> phi^2(g) h phi(g)^(-1).

There are only two groups G of order six.

### G=C6

Its automorphisms are the identity and inversion. Inversion would make a invariant under multiplication by the square subgroup C3, forcing its total coefficient sum to be divisible by three. Thus phi=id. In additive labels 0,...,5, duality gives rho*=(-u)rho and

    a_k = a_(u-k).

There are exactly 60 nonnegative pairs (u,a) with u in C6, sum a=4, and this symmetry. The implementation enumerates all of them and constructs their complete 12^3 fusion tensors from the displayed relations.

* Forty-two fail the elementary formal-codegree norm divisibility condition. (The implementation first checks the general d-number condition as well.) Here FPdim(D)=60+24sqrt(5), with minimal polynomial x^2-120x+720. Each obstruction records an exact polynomial and an exact prime/coefficient witness.
* Six contain a closed rank-six based subring isomorphic to the previously excluded `R6_2_20`. The unit-fixed basis permutation and subring support are recorded, and the inherited categorical certificate is checked again.
* The other twelve have a faithful C3 grading, given by

      deg(g)=g mod 3,  deg(g rho)=g+2u mod 3.

  The checker verifies every nonzero structure constant against this grading. The unique involution b=3 has degree zero.

No assertion about the associators or a specific 2D2 model is needed in the last case.

### G=S3

Every automorphism of S3 is inner. Replacing rho by a suitable invertible multiple makes phi=id. Then u is central, hence u=1. The a_g are constant on conjugacy classes. Write (a_e,a_t,a_c) for the coefficients on the identity, transpositions and 3-cycles. Their sum condition is

    a_e+3a_t+2a_c=4.

There are four possibilities:

| Coefficients | Obstruction |
|---|---|
| (4,0,0) | Closed rank-two ring rho^2=1+4rho, forbidden by rank-two classification. |
| (2,0,1) | Closed rank-six C3-based subring `R6_2_20`. |
| (0,0,2) | Closed rank-six C3-based subring `R6_2_21`. |
| (1,1,0) | Formal-codegree norm divisibility. |

For the last row extend the sign character of S3 by a root lambda of lambda^2+2lambda-1. This gives a one-dimensional character of the full ring, whose formal codegree is 6(1+lambda^2)=24 plus or minus 12sqrt(2), with polynomial x^2-48x+288. The normalized 2-adic norm valuations are 4/2 for FPdim(D) (norm 720) and 5/2 for this codegree (norm 288). Hence their quotient is not integral. This eliminates the last nonabelian possibility.

## 3. Inverse Morita algebra and the grading contradiction

Keep the distinguished simple right A-module A in C_A. Under the dual-category action its internal end is a connected separable algebra B in D, and double Morita equivalence recovers C as D_B^* (up to the harmless opposite-category convention).

There are exactly two simple A-bimodule structures whose underlying right A-module is A: the standard one and its twist by the nontrivial character of C2. All other simple bimodules have underlying right modules in different restriction blocks. Thus B=1+b, where b is a nontrivial invertible involution. In the surviving cyclic case this is the unique involution 3, which has degree zero in the faithful C3 grading above.

A connected separable algebra B in the neutral component of a faithful G-graded fusion category D induces a faithful G grading of _B D_B: every B-bimodule decomposes by homogeneous support; tensor product over B multiplies degrees. Faithfulness follows because B tensor Y tensor B is a nonzero B-bimodule of the degree of any homogeneous nonzero Y. Therefore C admits a faithful C3 grading.

For each input ring, however, the based subring generated by all X X* is the entire ring. The checker computes this closure exactly. Thus the universal grading is trivial, a contradiction.
