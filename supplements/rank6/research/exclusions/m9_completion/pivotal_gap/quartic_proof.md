# General nonexistence for R6_9_8099 and R6_9_8101

These two rings admit no complex fusion categorification. Pivotality, sphericality, pseudo-unitarity, positivity of the categorical dimensions, and a bound on a conductor are not assumed.

## The general signed necessary data

Sphericalization of a hypothetical category supplies a rank-six signed integral algebra A. Since every original simple is self-dual, each spherical lift is self-dual: the two lifts have opposite nonzero dimensions, while duality preserves dimension. Its fusion ring, and hence A, is commutative. In a basis of chosen lifts, the multiplication coefficients t have |t_ijk|≤N_ijk and the same parity as N_ijk. Its matrices are symmetric. A dimension character has totally real field K of degree at most six and formal codegree Δ=Σ_i d_i². Every original or signed formal codegree f lies in K and Δ/f is integral. Indeed, the corresponding character of sphericalization has codegree2f, and central induction produces a simple central dimension Δ/f, which is an integral linear combination of the ±d_i. This is the dimension-field-containment lemma already proved in the release, papers/chapters/biquadratic_trace.tex.

The six signed formal codegrees are totally positive, each exceeds1, their reciprocals sum to1, and their sum is at most T=Σ_ijk N_ijk². They are d-numbers, since twice each is a formal codegree of sphericalization. A rational nonzero scalar does not affect the d-number property.

These statements use Ostrik, *Pivotal fusion categories of rank3*, Theorem2.13 and §5.1, https://arxiv.org/html/1309.4822v1, and Ostrik, *On formal codegrees of fusion categories*, Theorem1.2, https://arxiv.org/html/0810.3242v2. They are applied to sphericalization, never to an unproved pivotal structure on the original category.

## Exact common field and ideal

Both tensors have T=4525. Their original formal-codegree polynomials are F G_i, with

F=X²−4225X+21125,
G_8099=X⁴−300X³+5250X²−22500X+28125,
G_8101=X⁴−300X³+26250X²−562500X+703125.

Both irreducible quartics generate E=Q(r), where h(r)=r⁴−r³−4r²+4r+1=0. The verifier supplies explicit embeddings of all original codegrees. E has integral basis1,r,r²,r³ and discriminant1125, verified by the maximal-order algorithm. The substitution r↦−r³−r²+3r+2 gives an automorphism of order4. Thus E is cyclic quartic, with unique quadratic subfield Q(sqrt5), generated by1−6r+2r³ whose square is5. Since E⊂K and4 divides[K:Q]≤6, K=E.

Central induction puts Δ in the intersection of the principal ideals f O_E over all original formal codegrees. With columns in the displayed integral basis, these intersections are

The second lattice is contained in the first. These lattices are recomputed by rational multiplication matrices and Hermite normal form. Hence, in either case,

## A finite exact spectrum obstruction

Let t=Tr_E(θ). For the trace-dual basis b_j^vee, every embedding lies strictly between−2 and2. The verifier checks this by Sturm root counts on all four multiplication characteristic polynomials. Since θ is totally positive, a_j=Tr_E(θ b_j^vee) implies |a_j|<2t. Thus the integral box in the verifier is exhaustive. The trace relation determines d=t−4a−b−9c, and the ideal congruences reduce the box further.

Positivity uses rational isolating intervals of width10^-8 for the four roots of h. A derivative bound34t proves each sign; an ambiguous sign aborts the verifier instead of rejecting a candidate. No floating-point arithmetic occurs.

For the characteristic polynomial X⁴+c1X³+c2X²+c3X+c4 of θ, the necessary d-number tests are c4^j | c_j^4 forj=1,...,4. These remain necessary when θ belongs to a proper subfield: the polynomial is then a power of its minimal polynomial, or equivalently all four embeddings still generate the same ideal.

The remaining two signed codegrees f,g form a Galois-stable pair and belong to E. Thus either both are rational integers, or they form a conjugate pair in Q(sqrt5). Their reciprocal sum is

1/f+1/g=1−Tr_E(1/Δ)=1+c3/(65c4)=A/B>0

in lowest terms. Since S=f+g and P=fg are integers, S=Aj and P=Bj for a positive integerj. Exhaustive bounds and tests are

## Reproducibility

Run `python verify_quartic.py` or `python -O verify_quartic.py`. Both take about half a second and use explicit checks, not removable assertions. The script reconstructs the field, maximal order, ideals, bounds, and every finite candidate directly and verifies the full input tensors. `quartic_general_certificate.json` records hashes, bases, bounds, all39 d-number survivors and their empty complementary-pair lists. No existing release file is changed.
