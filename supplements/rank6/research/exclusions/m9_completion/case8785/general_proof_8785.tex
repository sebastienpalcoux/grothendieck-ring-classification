\subsection{The final general obstruction for \texttt{R6\_9\_8785}}
\label{sec:completion8785}
\paragraph{Theorem.}
The based ring \texttt{R6\_9\_8785} admits no fusion-category
categorification over $\mathbb C$, without a pivotal, spherical, or
pseudo-unitary hypothesis. The input tensor has SHA256
\texttt{7897af45c48cb1d416710f37ea9c4c11226490421317d193c85e3cc5022b3da8}.

\paragraph{Sphericalization and its signed algebra.}
Suppose a categorification $\mathcal C$ exists. Its canonical
sphericalization has two lifts $X_i^+,X_i^-$ of each simple object.
Both lifts of a self-dual simple are self-dual (Ostrik, Section~5.1(a)).
Choose the lift of the unit to be the unit. Quotient its Grothendieck ring
by $[1^-]=-1$, obtaining the signed integral algebra with basis $x_i$.
Its structure constants $T_{ij}^k$ satisfy
\[
 T_{0j}^k=\delta_{jk},\qquad T_{ij}^0=\delta_{ij},\qquad
 |T_{ij}^k|\le N_{ij}^k,\qquad T_{ij}^k\equiv N_{ij}^k\pmod2.
\]
Duality is an anti-automorphism fixing every basis vector, so this signed
algebra is commutative. Frobenius reciprocity then makes $T_{ij}^k$
totally symmetric in its three indices. Its six multiplication matrices
$A_i$ are real symmetric, and associativity is equivalent to the
vanishing of their pairwise commutators. Its Casimir is
$\sum_i A_i^2$, and
\[
 \operatorname{Tr}\Bigl(\sum_i A_i^2\Bigr)
 =\sum_{i,j,k}(T_{ij}^k)^2\le\sum_{i,j,k}(N_{ij}^k)^2=4325.
\]
Let $K$ be the field generated by its categorical dimension character,
and let $\Delta=\dim(\mathcal C)$ be the corresponding signed formal
codegree. All signed formal codegrees lie in $K$; this follows by
applying Ostrik's character--induction theorem to the sphericalization,
whose negative-sector codegrees are twice the signed codegrees.
The induction dimensions are $\Delta/f$ and belong to $K$.

\paragraph{The dimension field and finite quadratic spectra.}
The shared higher-field theorem, replayed by
\texttt{verify\_higher\_fields.py}, forces $K=E=\mathbb Q(\sqrt5)$.
It uses the full mod-two algebra $\mathbb F_4^3$, the ideal condition
$\Delta\in65\sqrt5\mathcal O_K$, and different/conductor trace bounds;
no assumption about indicators or pivotality of $\mathcal C$ enters it.
In particular, the Casimir polynomial alone must not be mistaken for
the decomposition of the mod-two algebra.

Write each conjugate pair of signed formal codegrees as
$(a\pm b\sqrt5)/2$, with norm $n=(a^2-5b^2)/4$.
All roots are at least one, $a\le4325$, $n$ is odd, and $n\mid a^2$
by the $d$-number property. They divide $\Delta$, their reciprocals sum
to one, their total trace is at most $4325$ and congruent to $4325$
modulo four, and their complete polynomial has reduction
$(X^2+X+1)(X+1)^4$.
The six roots partition into three such pairs, including repeated
rational roots: every rational component of the signed algebra has even
degree because its completion at two is a product of unramified
quadratic fields. Nonrational roots have equal Galois multiplicities.

The portable verifier \texttt{verify\_quadratic\_8785.py} enumerates
all $3001$ pairs meeting the universal trace, positivity, parity and
$d$-number bounds, and all $18$ possible pairs for $\Delta$ divisible by
$D=(4225+1885\sqrt5)/2$. Exact divisibility in $\mathcal O_E$ and the
reciprocal identity leave precisely the following four spectra:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|r}
 \Delta\text{ pair}&\text{second pair}&\text{third pair}&\text{total trace}\\\hline
 (1625\pm325\sqrt5)/2&(450\pm200\sqrt5)/2&(1170\pm520\sqrt5)/2&3245\\
 (4225\pm1885\sqrt5)/2&(5,5)&(5,5)&4245\\
 (4225\pm1885\sqrt5)/2&(5,5)&25\pm10\sqrt5&4285\\
 (4225\pm1885\sqrt5)/2&25\pm10\sqrt5&25\pm10\sqrt5&4325
\end{array}
\]
In the last three rows $\Delta$ is Galois conjugate to $D=\operatorname{FPdim}(R)$.
The corresponding Galois conjugate category is pseudo-unitary by
ENO Proposition~8.23. The independent character-reciprocity and
uniform twist-moment certificate \texttt{verify\_8785.py}, proved in
\texttt{proof\_8785.tex}, excludes every pseudo-unitary categorification.
It covers both possible second-indicator signs. Hence only the first
row remains, and its exact Casimir trace deficit is
\[
 \sum_{i,j,k}\bigl((N_{ij}^k)^2-(T_{ij}^k)^2\bigr)=4325-3245=1080.
 \tag{8785-D}
\]

\paragraph{Exhaustive positive-indicator normalization.}
This is a choice of lifts in the sphericalization, not an assumption
about a pivotal structure on the original category. Represent the two
lifts of a self-dual object by $(X,a)$ and $(X,-a)$ in the pivotalization.
Their evaluation and coevaluation maps are inherited from the original
category. Their one-dimensional spaces
$\operatorname{Hom}(1,X^\pm\otimes X^\pm)$ identify, since the tensor
structure map $a\otimes a$ is unchanged by $a\mapsto-a$.
Ng--Schauenburg, Definition~3.1, writes the second-indicator operator as
$A^{-1}TA$, where $T(f)=j_X^{-1}\circ f^\vee$.
Consequently changing $a$ to $-a$ negates that operator and its trace.
The two self-dual lifts therefore have opposite indicators $+1,-1$.
Choose the unique $+1$ lift for each basis simple, independently.
The unit already has indicator $+1$.

The Fuchs--Runkel--Schweigert parity theorem, in the full pivotal form
proved as Theorem~3.9 of Liu--Palcoux--Ren--Vercleyen,
\emph{Triangular prism equations and categorification}, says that a
self-dual simple of indicator $-1$ occurs with even multiplicity in
$Y^*\otimes Y$. It requires neither a braiding nor positivity of dimensions.
Thus the multiplicity $m_-$ of $X_i^-$ in
$(X_j^+)^*\otimes X_j^+$ is even. Since
$N_{jj}^i=m_++m_-$ and $T_{jj}^i=m_+-m_-$, we obtain
\[
 T_{jj}^i\equiv N_{jj}^i\pmod4. \tag{8785-P}
\]
No additional basis-sign gauge is imposed after this normalization.

\paragraph{The exact finite exhaustion.}
The standalone standard-library verifier \texttt{verify\_signed\_8785.py}
regenerates its domains and equations directly from the portable tensor.
There are $\binom{7}{3}=35$ variables, one for each unordered nonunit
triple. Each runs through every integer between $-N$ and $N$ congruent
to $N$ modulo two. For a repeated index impose (8785-P), equivalently
congruence to $N$ modulo four. This is the complete normalized domain.
The program constructs every entry of every commutator $[A_i,A_j]$,
deduplicating only identical polynomials up to an overall sign, leaving
$55$ equations. It searches the full domain, rejecting a branch only
when an already determined equation is nonzero or its accumulated
nonnegative deficit exceeds $1080$. At a full assignment it requires
exactly (8785-D). Variable ordering changes only the order of search.

The native completion report is
\[
 \texttt{nodes 2247571340 solutions 0}.
\]
All polynomial absolute-value bounds are at most $390$, and the deficit
fits in a signed 32-bit integer; polynomial arithmetic uses signed
64-bit integers. The unsigned node counter explicitly aborts before
overflow. A timeout or any nonzero exit status fails the verifier and
never creates a completeness certificate. All Python checks use
explicit exceptions and remain enabled under \texttt{python -O}.
The replay under that flag returned zero tables in about $30$ seconds;
the timing is not part of the proof. Altered tensor and altered deficit
negative controls are rejected. Therefore the only remaining spectrum
has no associative signed table, contradicting sphericalization and
proving the theorem.
