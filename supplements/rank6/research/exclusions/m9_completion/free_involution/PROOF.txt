====================================================================

Status: no complex fusion categorification. The general-scope reduction
below uses sphericalization and a 144-candidate exact dimension enumeration.
The pseudo-unitary obstruction has 128 exact Farkas rays, covering four
exhaustively enumerated center-induction frames and 32 indicator choices.
The scripts never write to the supplied classification package.

Reduction to four induction frames
---------------------------------
Let F:Z(C)->C, I its adjoint, and M the induction Gram matrix, M_ij=[FI(i):j].
For commutative self-dual input, M=sum_k N_k^2. Its first column is
(6,0,32,32,34,34). The six summands A_E of I(1) have multiplicity one,
restriction unit coefficient one and g coefficient zero; their dimensions
are D/f_E (Ostrik, Theorem 2.13).

In the five nonunit restriction rows, the totals in the X/gX and Y/gY pairs
are respectively
 A=(16,11,11,6,22), B=(17,11,11,5,22).
These follow by equating rational and z coefficients of D/f_E.
Let u_j be the X minus gX multiplicity, and v_j the Y minus gY multiplicity.
The difference-coordinate Gram for (1-g, X-gX, Y-gY) is exactly
 [[12,-4,-4],[-4,12,4],[-4,4,20]].
Consequently sum u_j=sum v_j=-2, sum u_j^2<=12, sum v_j^2<=20,
u_j congruent A_j mod 2, and v_j congruent B_j mod 2.
There are exactly 18 u-vectors and 59 v-vectors, hence 1062 pairs.
The exact requirement M-F_low^T F_low>=0 in both entrywise and PSD senses
leaves 369. PSD replay uses rational Schur elimination, no floating point.

Exclusion of the four frames
----------------------------
The inherited exact character_induction / tensor_rotation criterion at
height 6 is applied to these frames. The high restriction counts are
117,113,23,13; the counts pruned by full-character Galois compatibility are
31,30,7,5. For each frame all 32 second-indicator choices
(epsilon_g,epsilon_X,epsilon_Y,epsilon_gY,epsilon_gX), with all signs arbitrary,
are considered. Every system has an integer ray y satisfying y^T A>=0
and y^T b<0. The 128 rays are in certificate.json.

Pivotal upgrade
---------------
Any pivotal categorification here is spherical because all simples are
self-dual. Its categorical dimension homomorphism is a nonzero-valued
row of the displayed character table. The row with codegree 4 has zero
values and is impossible. The three remaining rational rows are impossible
by Ostrik Corollary 2.15: all formal codegrees must belong to the field
generated by the categorical dimensions, whereas D is irrational.
Thus the only possible categorical dimension homomorphisms are the FP
character and its quadratic conjugate. In the second case, Galois-conjugating
the category gives a positive-dimensional, hence pseudo-unitary category.
The pseudo-unitary exclusion therefore rules out pivotal and spherical
categorifications, and consequently also unitary categorifications.

Additional conditional obstruction, without an assumed pivotal structure
----------------------------------------------------------------
There cannot be a Tannakian central lift Rep(Z2)->Z(C) whose nontrivial
underlying object is g. Such a lift permits de-equivariantization. The free
g-action makes its simples exactly the three pair-orbits, since
End(FX)=Hom_C(X,X tensor (1+g)) has dimension one. Its rank-three based ring is
 X^2=1+16X+16Y, XY=16X+17Y, Y^2=1+17X+17Y.
This is quadratic in the sense of Ostrik Theorem 5.1; any categorification
would be spherical. It is absent from Ostrik Theorem 1.1, so it is not
categorifiable at all. Ring commutativity does NOT establish such a lift.
This result is conditional on the lift and is NOT a general exclusion.

General-scope reduction (joint derivation with the parent agent)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Suppose C is ANY categorification. Form its canonical sphericalization S.
Write epsilon for its kernel object of dimension -1. For every original
simple choose the unique lift with positive categorical dimension; this
exists because squared norms are positive. Since the originals are
self-dual, these positive lifts are self-dual: the dual has the same positive
dimension. All simples of S are therefore self-dual, so K(S) is commutative.

Pass only at the ALGEBRA level to T=K(S)/(epsilon+1). This is a rank-six
commutative integral signed ring; its constants t_ijk satisfy
 |t_ijk|<=N_ijk and t_ijk congruent N_ijk mod 2.
Its basis remains orthonormal for the coefficient-of-unit trace form,
so its multiplication matrices are real symmetric, mutually commuting,
and simultaneously diagonalizable. No categorical quotient or central lift
of g in C is asserted or used.

The chosen lift of g has positive dimension 1. Its product with a chosen
positive lift is the chosen positive lift of the corresponding paired
object, because the underlying product is simple and its dimension positive.
Thus multiplication by g swaps each pair with coefficient +1. The actual
positive dimension character on T has values (1,1,x,y,y,x), and factors
through the rank-three Q-algebra T/(g-1). Hence its image field K=Q(x,y)
has degree at most three.

On the other hand, every character of the original ring pulls back along
K(S)->K(C) and has twice its original formal codegree. By Ostrik Cor2.15
all of these formal codegrees belong to the spherical dimension field K.
In particular 2D belongs to K, so E=Q(sqrt(273)) is a subfield of K.
It follows that K=E.

Write x=(a+b*sqrt(273))/2 and y=(c+d*sqrt(273))/2, with integers a,b,c,d,
a congruent b mod2 and c congruent d mod2; these are the algebraic-integer
conditions for O_E=Z[(1+sqrt(273))/2]. The dimension character and its
nontrivial Galois conjugate are distinct characters of the real symmetric
rank-six signed algebra T. Character orthogonality gives
 1+Norm(x)+Norm(y)=0,
hence
 a^2+c^2+4=273*(b^2+d^2).
Put S0=b^2+d^2 and B=a*b+c*d. The global dimension of C is
 Delta=2(1+x^2+y^2)=273*S0+B*sqrt(273).

The trace of the signed Casimir sum_i T_i^2 equals the sum of its six
formal codegrees and is sum_ijk t_ijk^2<=sum_ijk N_ijk^2=4396.
Two of its characters are the dimension character and its conjugate,
contributing field-trace Tr_E/Q(Delta)=546*S0. (If Delta is rational,
the two distinct characters still contribute 2*Delta.) Each of the four
remaining formal codegrees is >1: it is 1 plus the sum of squares of the
nonunit character values, which cannot all vanish because X^2 has unit
coefficient one. Thus 546*S0+4<4396, and 0<S0<=8.

This bounds b,d to [-2,2], and a,c to [-46,46]. The exact dimension_reduction function in verify.py
exhausts all parity-compatible solutions of the norm equation. There are
144. For any fusion category, original formal codegrees divide Delta
(Ostrik Cor2.14), so in particular Delta/D and Delta/12 are algebraic
integers. These conditions leave 32 assignments and just four values:
 Delta=2184+/-108*sqrt(273), or 2184+/-132*sqrt(273).

Global dimensions of arbitrary fusion categories are d-numbers (Ostrik,
On formal codegrees, Cor1.4). For a quadratic integer with trace t and norm n,
this requires n dividing t^2. For the 108 cases, n=1585584 and t=4368,
and t^2 mod n=52416, excluding them. The surviving values are exactly D,D'.

Finally, Galois-conjugate C if necessary to obtain global dimension D,
equal to its Frobenius-Perron dimension. By ENO Prop8.23 it is pseudo-unitary.
This contradicts the fully replayed 128-ray obstruction. Therefore no
categorification exists, with no pivotal, spherical, or braiding hypothesis.
