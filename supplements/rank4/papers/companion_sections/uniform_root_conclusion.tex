\begin{corollary}\label{cor:all-quadratic-family}
For every $n\geq2$, the quadratic family $R_n$ defined in
\cite[Section~\ref{B-sec:quadratic-family}]{Finite} admits no pivotal
categorification.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The case $n=2$ is the conductor-forty boundary obstruction of \cite{Finite}.
For $n>2$, write $9n^2+4=t^2d$. Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform-quadratic-discriminant}
gives
\[
 nt\lambda\geq\frac{8n}{\sqrt{10}}\sqrt{9n^2+4}>7n^2+4.
\]
The strict comparison follows by squaring positive quantities:
\[
 \frac{64}{10}n^2(9n^2+4)-(7n^2+4)^2
 =\frac{(43n^2+20)(n^2-4)}5>0.
\]
Thus the parameter condition in \cite[Proposition~\ref{B-prop:quadratic-family-n3-n4}]{Finite}
always holds. Pivotality is sphericality on a self-dual ring, so the
spherical exclusion applies.
\end{proof}
