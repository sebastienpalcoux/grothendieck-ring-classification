\begin{abstract}
We classify the complex-categorifiable rank-four fusion rings whose maximum
structure constant is at most $\MultiplicityBound$. Exactly fifteen of the $\CensusSize$ rings are categorifiable, and all fifteen have
unitary realizations. The five existence classifications---general, pivotal,
spherical, pseudo-unitary and unitary---therefore coincide in this range.
The proof uses a fixed collection of arithmetic and central-induction
obstructions. Its principal additional ingredient is a positive measure of
squared central dimensions in a sphericalization, together with the central
sign object and the first two Frobenius--Schur indicators. A parity
constraint from skew self-duality supplies a further obstruction to
sphericalization. All finite applications are generated and verified automatically by exact
certificates. The checker exhausts every required signed
table and induction branch; no exclusion is assigned by a catalogue label.
The input is the rank-four census at a pinned repository commit. The exclusions use general
categorical results, the obstructions proved here, and an automatic
application of the published $C_3$ near-group classification.
The unrestricted classification and automatic sphericality are established
in the companion article \emph{Grothendieck rings of general fusion categories of rank four}. The present paper supplies an independent,
larger finite census and its exact certificates.
\end{abstract}
