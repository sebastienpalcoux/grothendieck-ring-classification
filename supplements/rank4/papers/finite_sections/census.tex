\section{Census input and tensor conventions}\label{sec:census}
The classification takes its exhaustive list of rank-four based rings from
\cite{Census}, pinned to the commit specified in Section~\ref{sec:finite-statement}.
The repository gives its enumeration method, canonicalization, counts,
and full tensor data. We recall the coefficient conventions needed to
reconstruct the local certificates and the signed search. All parameters
below are integers between $0$ and the multiplicity bound $M$.

\subsection{Self-dual basis}
Frobenius reciprocity gives the regular matrices
\[
N_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0&0\\1&a&b&c\\0&b&d&e\\0&c&e&f\end{pmatrix},\quad
N_y=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1&0\\0&b&d&e\\1&d&g&h\\0&e&h&i\end{pmatrix},\quad
N_z=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&0&1\\0&c&e&f\\0&e&h&i\\1&f&i&j\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Associativity is equivalent to their pairwise commutativity, or to
\begin{align}
 ad-b^2+bg+ch-d^2-e^2+1&=0,\label{eq:census1}\\
 ae-bc+bh+ci-de-ef&=0,\label{eq:census2}\\
 be-cd+dh-eg+ei-fh&=0,\label{eq:census3}\\
 af+bi-c^2+cj-e^2-f^2+1&=0,\label{eq:census4}\\
 bf-ce+di-eh+ej-fi&=0,\label{eq:census5}\\
 df-e^2+gi-h^2+hj-i^2+1&=0.\label{eq:census6}
\end{align}
The equivalence follows by comparing the first row of products of regular
matrices and then using commutativity to recover every other row. It is
also checked directly against the full associativity identities.
For the signed-cover Diophantine reduction, when $b>0$ define
\begin{align*}
 H_0&=e(d+f)+bc,&G_0&=b^2+d^2+e^2-1,\\
 A&=e(b^2-c^2)+bc(f-d),&C&=e(bf-ce),\\
 B_0&=b^3e-b^2cd+b(d-f)H_0-ebG_0+ecH_0.
\end{align*}
Eliminating $h,g,j$ from the six equations yields the necessary identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:census-linear}
 Ca+Ai+B_0=0.
\end{equation}
The signed verifier checks all six original equations after using this
identity, retaining every vanishing-coefficient branch.

\subsection{One nontrivial dual pair}
For $X^*=X$ and $Y^*=Z$, the regular matrices are
\[
N_X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0&0\\1&a&b&b\\0&b&c&d\\0&b&d&c\end{pmatrix},\quad
N_Y=\begin{pmatrix}0&0&1&0\\0&b&c&d\\0&d&e&f\\1&c&e&e\end{pmatrix},\quad
N_Z=N_Y^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Associativity is equivalent to
\begin{align*}
 ac-b^2+2be-c^2-d^2+1&=0,&ad-b^2+be+bf-2cd&=0,\\
 b(c-d)+d(e-f)&=0,&-c^2+d^2-e^2+f^2-1&=0.
\end{align*}
The pinned source contains $889530$ classes through $M=128$;
\path{data/parameters_128.txt.gz} is its compact snapshot and
\path{data/census_128_manifest.json} records the source commit and digests.
The verifier reconstructs each full tensor, checks its axioms and digest,
and tests its categorification certificate. The exhaustive census method
and its count certification are supplied by the source repository
\cite{Census}; no second rank-four census is required by the classification.
