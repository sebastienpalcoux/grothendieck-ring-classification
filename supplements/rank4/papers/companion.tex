\begin{abstract}
We study rank-four categorification without a multiplicity bound. Pivotal,
spherical and pseudo-unitary existence are equivalent for rank-four fusion
rings. We classify pivotal categorifiability for rings with a proper
nontrivial fusion subring and for rings whose Frobenius--Perron character
field has degree at most two. The remaining pivotal question concerns
self-dual rings with no proper nontrivial fusion subring and character
field of degree three or four; in the cubic case the rational formal
codegree is three or four. The proofs combine uniform twist-moment
estimates with explicitly bounded certificates. We also separate the
Diophantine descriptions of rank-four fusion rings from the question
of uniform completeness of categorification obstructions.
\end{abstract}
