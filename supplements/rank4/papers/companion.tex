\begin{abstract}
Exactly fifteen based rings occur as Grothendieck rings of complex
fusion categories of rank four. Every rank-four fusion category admits
a spherical structure, and each of the fifteen rings has a unitary
realization. No multiplicity bound or pivotal hypothesis is assumed.
A uniform theoretical argument using sphericalization, central induction,
dimension-field descent and indicator estimates first proves
$\FPdim(\C)<1000$ and $N_{XY}^{Z}\le31$. An exhaustive exact census
and categorical obstruction certificates then identify all surviving
rings. The resulting sharp bounds are $24+12\sqrt3$ for the global
Frobenius--Perron dimension and $6$ for the fusion multiplicities,
with equality precisely for the $C_3$ near-group ring of parameter six.
\end{abstract}
